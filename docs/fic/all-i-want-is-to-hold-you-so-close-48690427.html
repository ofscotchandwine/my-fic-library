But the beast followed them up too, and her arms were too weak from the climb after a few seconds, or minutes she wasn’t sure, she didn’t really care.

Before having to settle down and ascend to his throne. He was static when she told him who she was. Which maybe she shouldn’t have, but he was also a Prince, and he was the one, she was sure of it.

    Instead, the woman grabs her by the wrist and guides her to a fountain, or waterfall, maybe a strange mix of both. Something that Kara has never seen, and she’s been at the sanctuary thousands of times.

    “Just close your eyes, my darling, and make your wish.” The woman says, with her old woman’s voice. Kara obeys, if only so they could get this over with and she can marry her prince. Streaky hisses again, louder and the woman speaks again; “That's right. That's right. Lean in close. Are you wishing for something?”

    Kara takes a deep breath, and whispers, “and they both lived happily ever aft—”

    She doesn't get to finish, because she feels the strong hands of someone pushing her, and she loses her balance. She falls.

    And she falls, and falls.

    And she screams the entire time, until she simply stops falling. As if she had been gently dropped on the floor under her.

All she knew is that she was most definitely not in Krypton anymore. Kara does not stop running, until she’s  crashing over many people that scream some more at her. She couldn’t understand what they were saying, really. But now she’s really, really scared.

    “Oh, thanks Rao,” she says as she gets closer to him. “May I sit with you? I am very tired, and scared.” She mumbles, sitting next to him after the man nods, musingly. “I’ve never been away from home before, and I'm not sure at all where I am. If somebody could show me just a bit of kindness, a friendly ‘hello’ or even a smile, I'm sure that would lift my spirits so much.”

    He runs off into the darkness, and she loses sight of him. 

    “You are not a very nice old man!”

    A Lightning sound startles her and soon enough rain starts pouring down on her. Kara looks to her feet, cold and lonely. 

    Afraid and with nowhere to go, she hugs herself trying to warm up, and starts walking with the hope of finding someone, anyone who would help her. 

     

    The rain continues to fall, less stormy but more constant, when she sees it. Only accessible per a ladder (weird); a castle, exactly what she was looking for. A door that would undoubtedly lead her back to her prince. Kara climbs over a chariot, and the ladder and knocks on the door, “Hello! It's me, Kara Zor-El, from Krypton. Hello! Hello! Is there anybody home?” The rain starts pouring heavier, and she keeps knocking on the door. “Hello, please open the door. It's very cold out here. Is anybody there? Do you know Mon-El? Please be home.”

    “Hey, Lady!” Someone screams at her from below. 

    She turns around and can barely see a very beautiful woman, drenched in water too, before she slips, and ends up hanging from the rail in front of the castle. She screams, again. 

    “Hang on!” Kara hears the woman scream. She wants to tell her she has no intention of letting go, but honestly, the rail is very wet, and her hands are very slippery. Surprisingly it’s not her hands that slips, it’s the rail that gives out, and then… she falls.

    “I—” She starts, was she uncomfortable about something? She wasn’t aware, up until that moment, that she felt itchy, just as Alex said. And her feet felt stuffed on her own shoes, the same ones she was gladly wearing on her way to her wedding. They felt like two sizes smaller. 

    “I’m okay.” She declares instead. 

    “If you say so,” Alex turns to the children. “Well, it seems we will be going out, so everyone put on their shoes.”

     

    As promised, a carriage came to pick them up minutes later. And transported them to a seamstress; or so Kara thought at first. The shop was full of already made gowns and similar clothing, ready for a ball in the next hour, if needed. How extraordinary.

    Lexie leads them all to a very tall woman, who smiles at the child.

    “Hello, we are guests of Lena Luthor,” the little one says, as if that were something that she’s heard other people say. 

    The woman looks at Alex and Kara and nods. “The charity ball, right? Miss Luthor told us a few of you were coming. If you could follow me.”

     

    Kara tried at least 15 different gowns. Every single one of them was beautiful, and they all seemed to fit her… Until she actually put them on, and then they felt suffocating. Kara couldn’t explain it. She still had one on, it was light blue, and Kara’s sure she has one very quasi identical back on Krypton, but she cannot breathe in it. It’s constricting her lungs. The tall woman keeps telling her the dress fits her well, but Kara just cannot take a breath in that bodice. It’s too restrictive. 

    Alex keeps looking at her, like she’s some frog on the dissection table of one of her tutors. The three children sitting next to her, on the lounge, the tinier ones dangling their feet and drinking juice. The oldest rhythmically, moving her head completely distracted.

    “Kara,” Alex starts, when she cannot anymore and asks to be shown something else. “Were you comfortable the day we met?”

    She doesn’t really understand the question. When she met Alex, she was on a strange land, missing her fiancé and her parents, and didn’t really know anyone besides Lena and Lexie. 

    “I was a bit lost,” she mumbles.

    “I mean the pants.”

    Kara tilts her head, confused. “I think so?” She shrugs. “I had never used them before, but the gown I tried to use didn’t fit me.”

    Alex looks at her for a moment, and then turns to the tall woman; Diana, Kara reminds herself.

    “Maybe she should try a suit next.”

     

     

    It was a strange feeling. She felt free. And up until the moment she started trying dresses and gowns, she had never felt trapped. She was used to wearing her big puffy dresses, made with the finest fabrics the world has, and sewn by the best seamstresses in Krypton. And yet, she never felt better than right now. Wearing white breeches, and black boots up to her knee. A blue coat, with buttons adorned in gold. It was something Kal would wear, and she never felt jealousy of his wardrobe until now. She could jump, and actually move her arms. She could take long strides, instead of the demure ones she’s used to making in court. 

    “You look taller,” Lexie comments.

    Which is weird, because she’d been wearing heels before.

    “Because she’s confident and comfortable now.” Alex smiles at her. “You have not stopped smiling since you put that on. How do you feel?”

    “Free.” The word is right out of her mouth without her meaning to say it. But it was so incredibly true. “Is it normal for women to wear this here?”

    “Well, no. That is more of a costume to us. But pants and suits, yes. Of course.” Alex grins.

    Kara looks at Lexie, “What do you think?”

    “I think it suits you.”

    There is something in the way the child says it that makes Kara almost hear Lena’s voice, seeing her in her wedding dress, the night they met; “But there is something… on the way you carry it, that makes me see that you are somewhat uncomfortable in it.”. And Lena seeing her wearing a suit that very morning; "The clothes suit you. You look...Handsome.”

    She cannot help but blush at the memories. Maybe Alex was right, she’s comfortable in this type of clothes, more than she’s ever been in anything she’s worn. And she’d never realized because she didn’t know anything other than her dresses and frocks. 

    “Oh.” She mumbles. 

    “I know,” Alex whispers, and Kara feels like she’s telling the truth. As if it was a revelation she’s had before too.

    “Thank you.”

    “I’m not paying for it, kid. Thank Lena.”

     

     

    Alex is pacing the entire living room, the mirror stuck to her ear, “No, of course I understand.” 

    It was late now, very late. The carriage brought them back to Lena’s castle— Apartment, she corrected herself. Lexie told her it was an apartment, not a castle. And Alex ordered burgers and fries, (delicious food, Kara ate three, after Lexie told Alex she had a big appetite). But now it was hours later, and Lena had not arrived yet, and Alex had no news about Kelly either. Until now.

    “Kelly, are you seriously telling me that this meeting could go all night? Is LCorp suddenly falling apart?”

    Kara isn’t really sure what all the words she said meant, but it didn’t sound good.

    “No, they all fell asleep watching Encanto.” Alex comments, looking over the lounge, where the three children were sleeping. “Yes, even Ruby, you can tell Sam.”

     

    Alex walks to the kitchen once she finishes talking to the mirror. Kara follows her, curious. 

    "How does the mirror work?" She quietly asks.

    Alex looks at her, confused. "Mirror?"

    "In Krypton only witches can use mirrors to talk and spy. Is it the same here?"

    Alex shows her the mirror, it looks similar to the one Lena showed her a couple of days earlier, but not the same. "It's not a mirror. It's a phone." Alex explains. "We use it to communicate. It can take pictures too. And witches are not real."

    "You truly can talk with anyone with this?"

    "As long as you have their number…" Alex shrugs. "Kara, where are you from?"

    "Krypton," Kara smiles, and she explains everything to Alex, while they sip some orange juice. Who she is, how she was on her way to marry Mon-El, and how she ended up stranded in this weird place, until meeting Lexie and Lena.

    "That seems a bit fantastical," the redhead comments, and Kara knows she's skeptical of her. 

    "I guess our worlds seem very different. And in all honesty, I don't think I understand how everything happened, either. And since I got here I feel very different, as how I am in Krypton." She confesses.

    "Different how?"

    "Well… I am always hungry. I’ve always had a healthy appetite in Krypton, but here I feel I could eat forever." Kara nervously twiddles with her hands. "And I don't mean that I only find the food delicious. I feel hungry. But I don't want to be a nuisance." She shrugs. "And I can move very fast. Faster that I have ever moved. I have to be careful with how much force I touch things, because I have destroyed a few things already. But I fixed them, I promise." The memory of almost hitting Lexie by accident, with the fridge that very morning, still very fresh in her mind.

    "Are you hungry now?" Alex asks, worried.

    "A little?"

    Alex nods to herself and opens the fridge, as Lexie called it that morning.

    "You wouldn't be a nuisance, Kara. I think Lena and Lexie appreciate you too much. And being hungry it's nothing to be ashamed of."

    Kara ends up with a peanut butter and jam sandwich in front of her, a few minutes later. 

    "What do you mean fast and strong?" Alex is looking at her again like she's a frog. Ready to dissect her.

    Kara looks at her for a moment and then rushes to the other side of the apartment and comes back. Less than a second later, the only evidence of the movement is the way Alex’s hair was now sticking out.

    The woman looks at her, with big brown eyes, while Kara takes out a spoon from the rack the silverware was in, and with two fingers and no effort whatsoever, just twists it in a circle.

    “Fast and strong.” Kara points out, giving Alex the spoon.

    “Okay,” she mumbles, examining the silverware, and combing her fingers through her hair. “That could actually explain the hunger. And it’s fascinating, but better keep this between us. And Lena, definitely tell Lena.”

    “I don’t think she believes me,” Kara shrugs. “And I’m going back home, soon. There is no point in worrying her.”

    “You are looking like something it’s itching again,” Alex comments, leaving the twisted spoon on the island in the middle of the kitchen. “What is it?”

    Kara thinks about it, something truly is bothering her, but in all honesty she’s not sure what it is. This was a very different world than her own, but the more time she spent there, the less she wanted to go back. She looks at Alex, who had a very calm expression, just waiting for her to say anything, and Kara was hit with the wish she had when she was a child; a sibling. Back then she wished she had a sibling, just like her father, and uncle Jor-El. Less like her mother and aunt Astra. But they still had each other, regardless. If she’d had a sister, she wished it was someone like Alex, even with the little Kara knew her.

    And then, she realized that it was the first time she had thought about her family. And…

    “You told me that Sam and Steve didn't marry for love, but that didn’t mean that they didn’t love each other…” Kara starts and Alex nods. “How can you know the difference?”

    “That is a very complex question, Kara.” Alex says in a low voice, crossing her arms. “What brought this up?”

    Kara doesn’t answer. She doesn’t know how to answer, because she cannot think of words that can encompass the awkward feeling she got during her date with Mon-El, and how he seemed little interested in the conversation she was trying to have with him. How she felt like there was nothing connecting them, besides both being from roughly the same area of the world. Their world. Even in this strange one, when they only had each other, Kara didn’t feel anything else tethering her to him.

    Now she was wondering if her family was onto something, trying to dissuade her from marrying the Daxam prince.

    “Krypton doesn’t have divorces,” Kara mumbles. “I just want to make sure I’m not making a mistake, and mixing up love from friendship.”

    Alex smiles at her, “Well, in my personal opinion, the best love stories start like a friendship.” The red head touches her arm and squeezes it, in support. “Now, I'm gonna need the muscles to move the kids to bed. Or they will wake up cranky, because they slept wrong.”

     

    Kara had no problem moving Ruby and Lexie to the latter’s room. The bed was big enough for the three young ones. Alex left the door ajar, open enough so a little sliver of light entered the room. 

    “Is Lena not coming back? Or your wife?” Kara asks quietly, once they are back in the living room.

    “There is an investor that’s being a pain in the ass,” Alex shrugs. “They had the lenses demonstration next week with him, but he demanded it be today, and now he wants a full report for tomorrow morning. It threw a wrench in everyone’s plans.”

    Kara understood nothing.

    “I hope they can get away later. If not… I guess tomorrow is a new day.” Alex adds.

    “She doesn’t know I came back.” Kara mumbles.

    “Of course she knows, Kara.” The redhead laughs. “Do you really think Jess wouldn’t tell her? You were around town with her kid. Lena knows.”

    “Do you think she’s glad?”

    “Do you want her to be?”

    “Of course. She’s my friend.”

     

     

    Kara’s not sure when she fell asleep; or Alex for that matter. The other woman was slumped on a chair lightly snoring, while Kara was occupying the lounge. The apartment was starting to get the first rays of sunshine, and there was no sign of Lena. 

    Kara took a moment to appreciate the warmth the sun was giving her, so different from Krypton’s. It made her feel energized and alive. Soon enough the pitter-patter of Lexie’s steps alerted her that the little one was awake.

    “You are still here.” She whispers, making sure Alex is still asleep.

    Kara smiles and nods without saying anything else for a while. Enough time for Lexie to sit down next to her.

    “I really wish you wouldn’t have to go…” the child confesses in a whisper. “I will really miss you. I had a lot of fun with you.”

    “I’ll miss you too, little one. But perhaps you could convince your mom to visit me on Kryp—” Kara interrupts her own whisper. “On Daxam.” She pauses, and looks back at Lexie. “I don’t know Daxam, but if you come to visit, we could travel to my home; I would show you the royal gallery. And I could show you the Jewel Mountains, I think you would like them, they are beautiful. And we could go shopping in Argo’s market.” She keeps her voice low, trying to not wake Alex.

    “Is it fun being a princess?” Lexie asks, curious.

    Kara thinks for a moment. “It’s busy sometimes. When there is a treaty or something alike, I have to help my mother organize, and be the hostess. If not, most of my days are with my tutors. I learn math, grammar and science. I like learning.”

    Lexie scrunches her face, “it sounds like my day at school, and when mommy has to prepare galas. That’s not fun.”

    “Well, perhaps you need a subject you like. I like science, but I also enjoy art.“

    “I like science too.” Lexie smiles.

    Kara feels her stomach protest once again. Lexie giggles next to her. 

    “Let’s have cereal!” She says, standing up and pulling Kara by her hand. “I’m hungry too.”

     

     

    Kara is on her fourth bowl of cereal when Alex enters the kitchen. The phone, already glued to her ear. 

    “Babe, you know I would love to stay with the girls all day, but I have a surgery in two hours.” She opens the fridge to look inside. “I can drop them off at LCorp, until you are done.” She looks at Kara, without closing the fridge door. “Yeah, I don’t know if I’m on the ‘leaving my daughter alone with her’ stage, yet. Plus, I'm pretty sure she has to go see her fiancé.”

    Kara loses her appetite straightaway. She needed to go find Mon-El, make sure his clothes were presentable, and then attend the ball. After that they would go home. And then she would start her life in Daxam. 

    “I should go.” She mumbles to herself.

    Both Alex and Lexie look at her.

    “Kara, are you okay?” The child asks in a confidential whisper. “You look scared.”

    “Not scared,” she’s quick to say. “Excited. I can’t wait for the ball.” She stands up. “I think I have to go now, Lexie.”

    The child also stands up and hugs her waist. “I wish we could spend more time together, Kara.”

    Alex keeps looking at her, curious. Kara sometimes feels Alex can read her a little too well for someone she just met.

    “I will see you at the ball.” She says, as a goodbye.

    Alex nods, touching Lexie’s shoulder, making the kid take a step away from Kara.

    “Don’t forget your clothes.”

     

    After picking the bag with the clothes for the ball; Kara makes a quick exit. The more time she spends with these amazing people; the kids and Alex, the harder it is to say goodbye. The more time she spends with Lena and Lexie, the less she wants to leave. And saying goodbye to the little one is even more painful this time around.

    Mon-El told her where to find him after their date. It was a long distance from Lena’s apartment, and yet Kara does not rush to him. She needs time to think. 

    The warnings her family had told her for years about her relationship with the Daxamite Prince were at the forefront of her mind. Her date with him the day before, felt stilted, uncomfortable, and very different from the time they first met, something she remembers very fondly. He only wanted to talk about himself, and his hunts. And he was clearly bored of everything Kara explained and showed to him. He also dismissed her thoughts, correcting her about the stuff she’d learned about this world, or what she knew of their own. Kara’s not really conceited, she can accept that she can be wrong, but the way Mon-El laughed, it wasn’t a pleasant experience. It felt like he was laughing at her instead of with her.

    Kara stops walking for a moment, when Lena’s smile flashes in her mind, and the warm feeling she felt when the woman guided her in this new and scary world. Even when Lena felt angry with her, and Kara made a mess of her life, Lena was kind with her. She touches her own cheeks, knowing they had pinkened just by the thought.

    She wonders, just for a moment, how different she would feel if yesterday’s date had been with Lena instead.

    She remembers her confusion, about their dinner together, at the restaurant. And how she felt so comfortable, and appreciated with Lena, even when Streaky interrupted them. She shakes her head, batting those thoughts away. 

    She was going to marry Mon-El, and they would be happily ever after. 

    And Lena had Jack, anyway. She would be happily ever after with him too.

     

     

    It’s hours later, when she finds herself in the lodge Mon-El has been sleeping in. There is a contrast difference between the place and Lena’s apartment. 

    Lena’s was full of light, and there was a peaceful air about it. Mon-El’s room felt stuffy, and dark. And it was full of filth, with cartons of food and beverages everywhere.

    Once her fiancé opened the door to her, he smiles charmingly and immediately lays down in the middle of the bed.

    Kara’s cheeks pinken right away. He was just in his linen shirt and undergarments. The least clothed she has ever seen him. She wasn’t sure she liked it, regardless of his well built physique. As royals they both had a protocol of engagement to follow.

    “I know it’s not very welcoming, but it has served its purpose for the time we’ve been in this land.” He comments, crossing his arms behind his head.

    “We?” Kara asks, still standing close to the door, the bag with her clothes still hanging from her hands.

    “Oh, Winn came with me, to look for you. And Streaky.” He suddenly looks around the place. “Though I don’t know where he is. Winn left early today, but I don’t remember seeing Streaky.”

    “We have to look out for him, then,” Kara says straightaway.

    “I’m sure he will turn up before we have to go,” Mon-El dismisses her, nonchalantly.

    Kara purses her lips. She would not leave without Streaky, she cannot believe she didn’t ask him about her cat earlier, on their date. But she was really trying to connect with her fiancé.

    “Did you clean up your wardrobe?” She asks him, instead.

    “Indeed. Winn found out a contraption that cleaned it.” He smiles, while distractedly looking at the talking mirror. The Television, the TV, Kara corrects herself. She had learned a few things in the days on this land. “He told me to not wear it until we needed to leave.” Something in the reflective screen makes him laugh, Kara doesn’t know what, she’s distracted looking at him, because he never looked back at her. 

    Was that the future that awaited her? Trying to have a conversation with her husband only to be ignored by him, because he’s too busy looking someplace else?

    This is not the Mon-El she met in the woods. He was attentive, and hung onto every word she said, back then. Had they both changed so much since then?

    “Where is the shower room?” She asks, preferring to not dwell too much on those frankly depressing thoughts, and instead pamper herself, with something she discovered she enjoys very much.

    “Yes, just behind that door,” Mon-El points out, to his right; his eyes still glued to the TV. “You were right, it's amazing, it reminds me of bathing in Daxam’s cascades.”

    Kara smiles, without saying anything else, just locking herself in the shower room.

     

     

    The shower is not as pleasant as the one in Lena’s apartment; the pipes must be different, because it ran out of warm water rather quickly. But the colder water doesn’t really bother her, it actually helps her think. Think about how changed Mon-El is, from who she remembers from the forest, five years ago. How different he is from the idea she had from the letters he sent over the years. Yes, he came looking for her, to this strange, new world, and found her. And he remains as handsome as she remembers, but there is something not quite… enchanting about him now. However she feels she owes him, exactly because he waited for her, for five years. And because he came to National City, desperately looking for her.

     

    She’s not as proficient as her maids, the process is incredibly slow for her, but she manages to half-braid her hair, the best she can, making sure to keep her locks out of her face. She’s about to dress, when it dawns on her that it will be the first time she dances with Mon-El. 

    She’s finishing putting on her brand new boots, when she realizes she has no idea if he would appreciate her selected attire. She looks at the mirror, her own reflection staring back at her; with the dark blue jacket adorned with gold, and her hair half braided at her back; she looks a little like her father. A laugh bubbles out of her, just by that thought.

    She takes a deep breath and opens the door to join her boyfriend.

    “I was about to call out to you,” he says, without looking at her, instead sheathing his sword, finishing dressing too. The pompous balloon sleeves of his maroon jacket following every wild motion he makes. “Are you ready?”

    He finally looks at her, and he tilts his head, clearly confused. But the charming smile remains on his lips, “Blue looks great on you.”

    Kara returns the smile, “Thank you.” 

    “You surprise me, bride, I thought you would be keen on keeping big dresses you are accustomed to use. You kryptonians are so used to tradition.” 

    “It surprised me too,” Kara confesses. He was correct, her people are very adamant about following tradition and their roles. But she wasn’t on Krypton right now. And she has never felt freer. “Shall we go?”

    Mon-El smiles and opens the door, with a big flourish, and comically bows down to her. “After you, my bride.”

     

     

    Mon-El keeps looking at her with curiosity, the entire ride to the place the ball is being held. Both sitting in the back of a yellow taxi, something Alex had graciously explained how to use, with the money Lena had given her the day at the park. It was a new experience, similar to the black cars Lena uses to move everywhere, even if less sophisticated looking.

    “What a weird transport,” Mon-El mumbles with a smile. “I miss my horse, the wind in my hair. And it feels faster.”

    Kara doesn’t disagree, this feels more like a carriage than a horse, but she’s used to that on Krypton. Mon-El is used to being mostly by his lonesome, in open fields, traveling the land, whenever his mood took him. Just his horse and his squire. 

    She pays the driver once the man announces their arrival, and she lets Mon-El help her out of the carriage. He’s perfectly gentlemanly about the event, putting her hand in his arm and subtly guiding her around. 

    “There you are,” a familiar voice says when they get to the antechamber. Jess looks amazing with a big, puffy pale pink dress. “You are the last one to arrive, and you didn’t have the tickets.” The woman smiles at her, giving two pieces of paper to a man standing next to a door.

    “You look beautiful, Jess.” Kara compliments her. “This is my… This is Mon-El.”

    “Pleasure to make your acquaintance.” He curtsies to Jess.

    She curtly smiles, “I’m sure. I’ll see you around, Kara.” 

    And just like that, Jess disappears from view, behind the door.

    “Are you ready?”

    Kara nods, as a reply to his question, taking a deep breath.

     

     

    They enter arm in arm, someone asks for their capes, and Mon-El gallantly gives both of them to the footman. Kara is too busy searching for familiar faces on the dance floor. She sees Alex in her black jacket dress and trousers, looking very militaristic in Kara’s opinion, talking with a beautiful woman with dark skin in a vibrant yellow dress that complimented her features; both with a drink in hand. 

    She sees Jess close to them, laughing next to a very tall man in shades of gray.

    For the first time since Kara arrived in this world, everyone else looks like they belong in Krypton. It was a rather odd feeling.

    Kara and Mon-El are walking down the stairs when she finally spots Lena; in the middle of the dance floor, stark in contrast with her pale skin, a beautiful burgundy-red gown that seems to flow around her like the river; looking expensive. She loves the way she looks, perhaps because the skirt of her gown reminds Kara of the heavy leaves from the trees in the Scarlet Jungle. Lena’s features are delicate, her hair loose and falling at her shoulders, and the gown hugging her figure and fitting her like a glove, or rather like it was especially made for only her. 

    She looks happy, with a beautiful smile she shares with the tall man in front of her dressed head to toe in green; Jack.

    They twirl, making Lena’s dress flow around her like magic, and in that moment, Lena’s eyes catch hers. Mon-El and her are at the bottom of the stairs, now, and Kara clearly sees as Lena taps her companion’s arm and starts walking towards them with a blinding smile and interlacing her fingers with Jack’s.

    “I’m very happy you both decided to come,” Lena says as a greeting. Her eyes fixed on Kara’s.

    And Kara cannot tear her eyes away from her, smiling too.

    “Me too. I’m surprised, though; you told me you couldn't dance.”

    Lena laughs, her eyes crinkling with amusement, “I said I didn’t like dancing, not that I couldn't.”

    From his place beside her, Mon-El clears his throat.

    “Oh, excuse me,” Kara says, turning to Jack. “This is Mon-El. He's my... Prince.”

    “Yes, and this is Jack.” Lena presents her boyfriend to Mon-El. “He's my...” Lena doubts, and Kara frowns too at the long pause.

    “We're together,” Jack finishes for Lena, hugging her waist, to bring her closer to him. Kara tries to regain her smile.

    “And this beautiful lady is Kara,” Mon-El announces gallantly. “The love of my life, my intended. My heart's true desire.”

    “Wow.”

    “Is something wrong?” Mon-El asks Jack; who shakes his head no, with an impressed look on his face. 

    “Just the way you said that.” He mumbles. “So straightforward and unself-conscious. Not a hint of irony. It’s very… rare.” Jack concedes.

    “Well, thank you.” Mon-El gives him a small bow as the bandleader steps to the speaker.

    “Well, ladies, gentlemen and everything in between,” he announces to everyone. “It's the time of the night we look forward to all year long, So in keeping with tradition, I’d like to ask each one of you to turn to a person you did not accompany this evening and invite them to dance the King and Queen's Waltz.” The band strikes up a slow tune.

    Mon-El turns to Lena and opens his mouth, “May—”

    “Actually,” Kara interrupts him. “May I have this dance?” she asks the brunette.

    Lena blinks, her lips slightly parted by the surprise. But nods her acceptance anyways. Kara offers her her hand, giving Lena a small curtsy.

    They walk hand in hand until they are in the middle of the room.

    “May I say,” Lena starts, just as the band’s slow music fills the room. “I’m surprised by your attire. But you look very handsome in it. Blue brings out your eyes.”

    “Thank you,” Kara gulps, putting one of her hands on Lena’s waist, and bringing her close to her. 

    She’d never led a dance, and now she feels like it was a mistake to ask Lena to dance in such a public setting. It was an impulse, she couldn’t help it. She wanted to dance with Lena. She wants to dance with Lena.

    The music swells and Kara does her best, guiding Lena’s steps, not that she needed it, as Kara quickly learns. As they waltz in the middle of the room, Kara looks into Lena's eyes once again, and feels her heart flutter wildly in her chest. She cannot explain the feeling, it feels both like drowning, but also like flying at the same time. It’s like they are gliding around the room, with fast and secure steps.

    Lena smiles and rests her temple on Kara’s cheek while they twirl and sway with the rhythm of the music. Lena’s body feels warm and inviting under her hands, through the gown. And Kara cannot get enough, regardless of  her nervousness.

    Kara swears she can almost hear her own heart, and she wonders if Lena can too.

    Instead, the brunette voice is the only thing she can hear, over the music, and the idle conversation. Low, melodic, and in tune, even if a little raspy, next to her ear, along with the singer at the stage. Lena’s breath caresses her cheek with every word she says. “So close, was waiting; Waiting here with you; and now, forever, I know. All that I wanted; to hold you so close.”

    Lena didn’t sing. It was something she told Kara multiple times. Kara knows it must mean something that she’s doing it now. It must mean something that she’s singing to Kara.

    She moves her head, just enough to see Lena's eyes again. She wants to say something, anything, but words refuse to leave her throat, not even when she’s sure she has a dumb smile on her face. She wants to reply in song too.

    Then someone is calling her attention, by touching her free shoulder.

    “Mind if I cut in?" Jack’s voice asks her, nodding towards Lena, his expression solemn.

    The moment is shattered and Kara steps away, the smile wiping away from her face. “No,” she says, clearing her throat. “Of course.”

    Jack takes Lena in his arms and starts dancing with her. Green eyes fixed on her boyfriend’s face.

    Tears well up in Kara’s eyes, inexplicably; making the couple look blurry as they twirl away.

    Mon-El takes Kara’s hand, coming out of nowhere, and he stands in front of her ready for a dance.

    “You're sad?" he asks, baffled, looking at her face.

    Kara makes herself smile at him, trying to blink away the tears. “Oh, no, I'm fine.” She lies, not sure about how to tell him the truth. 

    She isn’t even sure what the truth is. That she loves Lena? How is that even possible? They are from different worlds. They met recently. Kara has Mon-El, and Lena has Jack. Their lives are completely different. There is no reason to break both relationships for her unrequited love that would lead anywhere. It isn’t even love, she tells herself.

The brave little princess coming to the rescue.” Queen Rhea says in a sneer, turning towards Lena again. “I guess that makes you, the damsel in distress, uh, darling?” Rhea looks back at Kara; “Keep up with me, dear! It's time we take this tale to new heights.” She takes a mighty leap and jumps to the top of the building. 

    Thunder, lightning and rain surrounds them when Rhea lets out a quite villainous laugh, that was so comically evil that if it weren’t because Lena’s literally in the overgrown hand of a dragon, she would tell the Queen it was too much.
    Lena tries to twist her body, looking for Kara, she needs to tell her to save herself, to go to safety.

    Instead the blonde is climbing after them, with a determined expression on her face.

    The Dragon Queen howls in triumph again from the building’s top spire. 

    “Rhea! I’m not going to let you take her away!” Kara screams as she pulls herself up onto a thin decorative spire.

    “I'm sorry,” the Queen mocks as she holds Lena high over her head. “We're coming to the end of our tale now.” 

    Kara valiantly strikes the sword in the dragon’s face direction but she’s a tad too far.

    “Aren’t you at the edge of your seat, Princess of Krypton?” She cracks her tail like a whip towards Kara, who rolls away just in time, her clothes drenched in rainwater. “Just dying to know how it ends!?” Rhea continues mocking Kara.

    “Go back, Kara!” Lena begs. “It’s too dangerous, it’s not worth it.”

    “She's right. you know?” Queen Rhea taunts Kara. “Playing the 'hero’ can be quite dangerous. You might chip a nail. Or get your hair mussed beyond repair.” The tail snaps once more, and Kara dodges it again with agility. She climbs toward the top of the building after Lena’s captor.

    A dark shadow dashes past Kara’s feet, and nimbly climbs the building, next to Rhea.

    The Queen doesn’t really notice it, too focused on mocking both Kara and Lena. “What about this? ‘And they lived happily ever after,’” the dragon’s tail cracks once more in Kara’s direction causing her to lose her footing and almost tumble over the edge. 

    Lena calls her name, but Kara manages to stay in place, her eyes fixed on Lena, the sword still in her hand.

    Rhea brings Lena towards her gigantic snout and almost conspiratorially she whispers; “Well at least I did.” She laughs, stretching herself in victory over the skies of National City.

    But something happens, something Lena didn’t really register at the moment. The spire the huge, gigantic dragon is balancing out off, starts to give out. Lena tries to look up but the rain doesn’t let her see. 

    Rhea tries to hold onto the building, but with her weight it was already a lost hope. The dragon slips and hurls Lena up onto the night sky.

    Lena screams, and falls, and screams. Until the blade of a sword pieces the sleeve of her dress and the bent building’s spire.

    She hears Rhea falling, but she’s too preoccupied with her own situation and how her delicate sleeve is already giving in. She’s not willing to look at whatever happens to the dragon, but if she’s going to have a few seconds before her death, she prefers to look at Kara’s handsome face and think of her daughter.

    Except Lena Luthor is not a quitter. And as great as Kara’s face is, she will not just accept death. 

    She tries to grab the twisted metal of the spire when her sleeve dress rips out, but the rain’s making her hands slip. From God’s knows where, a cat jumps into the metal, and with his tiny paws tries to help her stay. Lena honestly doesn’t know if she’s even screaming anymore.

    “Lena hold on!” Kara screams at her, but she’s not even finished the sentence, when the metal completely slips from her hands.

    She’s falling again, she’s screaming now, she’s sure of it. She thinks of Lexie, and she thinks of Kara.

    She can almost feel her arms around her. 

    She falls until she stops.

    Solid arms, around her waist, and the warped and bended roof of the hotel, making a sort of ledge, separates her from death.

    “What?” She asks in disbelief, as Lena registers that the arm around her waist is Kara’s, and that the ledge stopped their fall. 

    “I got you,” Kara whispers, the rain sliding down her face, one of her hands literally inside the roof, like holding onto the building itself. She looks at Lena’s eyes and smiles. “Is this a habit of yours? Falling out of stuff?”

    Lena smiles, she smiles because she remembers that same question coming out of her lips a few days earlier.

    She wraps her arms around Kara’s neck, without disturbing the hold the blonde has on the only thing keeping them safe. “Only when you are around to catch me.”

    Kara leans in and kisses her, like she never wants to let her go. Her tongue asking for permission, and somehow making Lena see stars.

    And if that is how Kara’s True love’s kisses are… Lena definitely doesn’t want to let her go either.

    Epilogue: Lexie’s tales

    Chapter Notes

    “I know. But mummy says you cannot sleep on my bed.” Lexie tries to keep her eyes closed. Looking to the ceiling in the almost complete darkness and ignoring the cries coming from the other side of the room.

    “Yes, I also think it is very unfair that Streaky gets to climb on the bed,” Lexie continues. 

    Now also ignoring the offended hiss coming from the black cat at the bottom of the bed, near her blanket covered feet. 

    “But mummy says he gets a pass because he saved her life…” Lexie finishes, but opens one of her eyes, and turns to the side to look at the white puppy whining in his fluffy bed.

    “Mummy said Streaky slayed a dragon to save her.”

    The puppy stands up, leaving his bed. 

    “Don’t ask me. I wasn’t there.” Lexie rolls from her bed and stands up only to sit on the floor.

    The puppy happily jumps on her legs.

    “But I’ll tell you all I know about it. It all started when Jeju arrived in National City.” Lexie thinks for a moment, all the times Jeju told her a story.  

    Now it was her turn and she wanted to do it right.

    They all started the same.

    “Once upon a time, there was a very far away place called Krypton,” She starts, petting the dog’s fur. “And Krypton had a beautiful princess, who fell in love with a prince.” 

    Lexie looks at the dog, when he whines. 

    “Yes, I know that she's not with the prince anymore, but I’m telling you how that happened. Be patient.” She looks at Streaky, positioning himself closer to her. “But the prince was from an enemy kingdom, and the Princess' parents didn’t like him very much. So they asked her to wait.

    And wait she did, for years, and she still insisted she was in love with the Prince. The day of the wedding came and the Princess was very excited to marry her Prince. But the Princess’ parents weren’t the only ones unhappy about the wedding. The Prince’s mother also hated the idea.”

    The puppy lets out a low growl, and Lexie nods. “You are starting to get it. The Queen disguised herself as an old woman, and pushed the princess into a funtain that got her into National City.

    The Princess was lost, for while, in a place she didn’t know. Desperate to find her prince and a way to get home. It started raining when she saw a castle. It was different to the rest of the ones she had been seeing, and more like the ones in Krypton. So she knocked at the door.

    But the castle was only a billboard, and with the rain, the princess slipped! And she was falling to the ground!” Lexie tells the dog excitedly. “But then! Then mommy caught the Princess.” She tilts her head to look at Streaky. “I think we need a better name for mummy than that one.” She thinks for a moment. “Mummy’s a CEO, does that work?”

    Streaky hisses.

    “Well, she also says she’s a scientist at heart, is that better?”

    Streaky meows, more calmly this time.

    “Okay, that works. The Princess was falling, but then the Scientist caught her. I was there for that part too. I will be the… karate-ka.” 

    Streaky hisses again.

    “I was wearing my karate gi!” 

    The cat touches her head with his paw and Lexie sighs. “Okay, I'll be just Lexie.”

    “Well, the important thing is that mummy caught the Princess.” She turns to the puppy, and repeats again. “The Princess was falling, but a very brave scientist caught her. The Scientist had a cool daughter called Lexie, and she loved the Princess as soon as she saw her.

    The Scientist didn’t trust the princess story, but at her heart the scientist is a good person, and decided to help the princess anyway. Giving her a place to spend the night.” Lexie keeps her voice low, petting the dog under his ears. “The next day, the Princess had cleaned the entire apartment of the Scientist, it was like magic.

    Lexie…” She grimaces at the use of her own name in the story, but continues still. “Lexie could tell there was something magical between the Princess and the Scientist, but like with every good story, there is obstables. Because the Scientist has a boyfriend, just as the Princess has a Prince she insists is looking for her.”

    Lexie sighs, “Jack’s not bad, but he didn’t make mummy happy. I need to say, I do not hate him, it’s just that he talked weird to me.” 

    She shrugs. “The boyfriend showed up at the scientist’s apartment and got a bit jealous of the Princess. But the Scientist still couldn’t let the Princess go alone in the world she did not know. I’m telling you, she’s a good person.” Lexie smiles. “The Princess and the Scientist went to work, and there the Princess met all of the Scientist's friends. Honestly they are too many so I’ll just name them when the story needs them. Streaky, I can’t think of more names.” She tells the cat when he meows again. “There was aunt Sam, and aunt Andrea. Jess and Ms. Kelly too.” She thinks for a moment, “I don’t know what happened at work, really. But then the Princess and the Scientist picked me— Lexie, from school. And went to eat Ice Cream!” She says excitedly.

    “And the more time Lexie spent with the Princess, the more she liked her. And she could tell that the same was happening to the Scientist.

    She had completely forgotten about her boyfriend, just enjoying her time with the Princess and, of course, with me. They went to see art, and then to eat. It was a magical night!”

    She smiles at both animals. “The princess told me… told Lexie a bedtime story, and then they all went to sleep. But then, the next day, when Lexie and the Princess were making breakfast, and the Scientist was just sitting at the table, there was a knock at the door. When Lexie opened it, she couldn’t believe her eyes! It was the Prince, looking for the Princess, and ready to take her away.”

    Lexie sighs. “The Scientist and Lexie didn’t think they would see the Princess again, and it was a very sad goodbye. But that same afternoon, the Princess came back! Asking Lexie for help, she needed clothes for a ball.”

    Lexie takes a breath because she got a bit too excited and feels a bit short-winded.

    “The Doctor,” she looks at Streaky to add in a whisper, “that’s Ms. Alex. The Doctor helped Princess Kara and Lexie to pick up something to wear. The next morning, Lexie and the Princess had an amazing time together, talking and playing, until it was time for the Princess to go again.

    I don’t really know what happened then, really. Mummy… was busy, and she sent me with Grandma, and I had a grand day with my friends. But the next day, the Princess was home! With mummy… with the Scientist! And she never left!” Lexie finishes excitedly. “I have to tell you what happened next, though. Aunt Sam and Ruby’s dad stopped living together. Ruby is happier that way, she said her parents fought a lot, and she hated it.” Lexie adds, thoughtful. “Sometimes Ruby goes to visit her dad, but she comes back quickly because she misses her mom.

    Aunt Sam and Aunt Andrea are together now; or so mummy says. I’m not sure what that means. But I’ve never seen Aunt Andrea smile so much, she always seemed so serious about everything, and she takes Ruby to lots of soccer games… No. Football, Aunt Andrea calls it football. They enjoy their time together. Aunt Sam is also happier.”

I’m excited for her.  The Doctor and the Princess are very good friends, so much that the Princess now has the same last name as the Doctor, they say they are siblings. Miss Kelly and mummy also spend a lot of time together. We all do right, Streaky?” She asks the black cat behind her.

    “The Prince and the Boyfriend became really good friends, for some reason,” Lexie tilts her head, curiously. “And the Boyfriend is dating the Prince's best friend now. He treats me better now, too, not like a child who doesn’t understand what he’s saying because he uses big words. The boyfriend spends a lot of time at Dra– Daxam.”

After the ball The Princess and the Scientist started dating. I still don’t know what that is for sure, but the Princess and the Scientist lived together with Lexie, and they did all sorts of stuff together. They made breakfast together, and went places together, and watched movies, like… like a family. The Scientist started spending more time with Lexie, instead of being at the office until very, very late.” 

    Lexie stands up trying to not yawn again, with the puppy in her arms and petting him.

    “The Princess told Lexie amazing stories about her home, and the people she knew there, and Lexie loved every second of it. Once a friend of the Scientists, the Queen of all media, heard one of the stories and asked if the Princess could write it down. That’s how the Princess became an author. She writes amazing stories about brave kids, having adventures.” She excitedly announces. ”The Princess and the Scientist were happy; they made each other happy. But the Princess missed her family, and both Lexie and the Scientist could tell. Streaky led us to the door to take us to Krypton, because the Princess didn’t remember where it was.

    It was in Flower Street, the Princess was right to forget, it’s one of the busiest streets in National City. Or so mummy says.”

    “Well, the Princess was right, when she described Krypton to Lexie. The sky was very different to the one in National City, it was more red. Maybe because the Sun there was also red. Kara called it Rao. The grass was more blue than green. And the water also felt different.”

    “But the Princess and Streaky took the Scientist and Lexie to the castle, where Kara introduced Mummy and I as her family.” Lexie smiles. “They were happy to see the Princess, but there was also sadness.”

    "Was he, now?" Jeju yawns and gets closer to Lexie, easily picking her and the dog up. "Would he be able to sleep in your bed?"

    "Mummy doesn't like that." Lexie whispers.

    "It will be our secret…" Jeju mumbles as she plops them all in the bed. Streaky protests with a hiss. "I know Lena's your favorite now, but Lexie needs sleep, Streaky. You can keep the secret once."

    "At what part of the story were you?"

    "When we got to Krypton…" Lexie mumbles, shy, all of a sudden. Maybe Kara didn't want their story told.

    "Well, go on. Krypto needs to know where his name comes from."

    "True, but it's normal for parents to worry about their children." Jeju says, kissing Lexie's forehead.

    "Is mummy the Scientist?" Jeju asks.

    “Maybe I am really more of a cat person after all.” Mummy jokes.
