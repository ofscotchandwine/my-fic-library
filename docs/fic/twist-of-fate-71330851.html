    twist of fate

    Mxy looked different. He acted different too. Not showboating nor pompous but seemingly, truly asking for forgiveness.

    He offered the perfect atonement for his actions the last time Kara saw him. He could make her go back and fix it all.

    Fix her cowardice to not tell Lena she was Supergirl early enough. Maybe if she did that, if she told her at the right moment and time, then she would still be there, with Kara and not working with Lex.

    It doesn't go well the first time, Mercy's attack on L-Corp

    Or the second, against Reign and the worldkillers during the eclipse, and after.

    "We have to go back to the very beginning," she told Mxy. "She didn't wanna let anyone in, and then, when she did, I made the mistake of not letting her in, too. This time, I'm gonna do it right."

    And with a snap from Mxy's fingers, Kara was now standing on Lena's office. Right before they truly became friends.

    She was so closed off, so serious, rigid, cold. When the Lena she got to know was smiley and free, even if only in private. Kara missed her.

    "I didn't come here to make friends—" Lena said, sitting at her desk.

    "I wanna show you something," Kara quickly interrupted her. She knew what she had to do.

    "Listen, I'm really busy, so, if we could just—"

    Kara didn't let her finish, she simply flew out the window and quickly returned, without even taking off her glasses.

    "It's you," Lena said in awe, standing up. "Kara Danvers is Supergirl."

    Kara nodded, biting her lip, expectant.

    "Can't believe I was fooled by a pair of glasses," Lena said with a huge smile on her face.

    There she was. The Lena she knew.

    "Don't worry, a lot people are," Kara smiled too.

    "Oh, God, I have to recall all those alien detection devices 'cause they clearly don't work."

    "You're not angry?" Kara asked, hopeful she had figured out the right moment.

    "No, it's incredible," Lena whispered. "I mean, you're incredible."

    And the air felt electric all of a sudden.

    New, exciting. A different kind of electric.

     

     

    It felt almost like watching a video tape being fast-forwarded. Except she was inside it, she was the camera's eyes.

    She knew she was missing a lot, but there were things she could perceive. She saw herself getting a new Supersuit made by Lena. It was similar to her new one, but with a longer cape. She had shorter boots, her suit was covered in Kryptonese. She loved it.

    She noticed the way Lena's hands lingered in hers. The way they shared looks and smiles across rooms, the laughs and secret jokes. The cuddles in the couch at her apartment as they watched movies, and had game nights.

    "I plead the fifth," Lena said, playing with her own hands, sitting at her mother's trial.

    And Kara couldn't help the way her face shaped into a smile, unbidden.

    It felt special, different.

    And then— then, Ben Lockwood tried to kidnap Lena as she was leaving L-Corp late at night.

    Kara didn't know how she did it. But the rapid pitter-patter of Lena's heartbeat infiltrated her ear. And she just knew she was in danger. She lifted in the air and flew as fast as she could, barely managing to not cause a sonic boom.

    She body slammed into Otis throwing him against the building and knocking him out, before pushing Lockwood away from Lena.

    "I should have known you'd show up for her," Ben said standing back up. "In a way you didn't show up for my family."

    Kara carefully took off the hood they'd used to cover Lena's face, "are you okay?"

    Lena nodded, her hand landing over Kara's at her own face. A relieved breath left Kara's lungs even if she didn't need to breathe.

    "I'm sorry I wasn't there for them," Kara said to Lockwood. "I'm sorry they believed Coville's words, I'm not immune nor omnipresent, I do my best but I fail sometimes," Kara took a step towards him. "But let me be very clear, if you go after Lena, after my allies— you will regret it."

    She turned around, and freed Thomas Coville from his bounds, "I've told you to stop your cult, you are hurting people. I will find a way to arrest you." She told him, and then she lifted Lena without much trouble, in a bridal carry, before addressing Lockwood again, as she started floating. "And it's not a threat, Ben. It's a promise."

     

     

    She landed on Lena's balcony slowly, carefully letting her stand on her own feet, without letting her go.

    "Are you sure you are okay?" Kara asked again.

    Lena quietly laughed, without moving away.

    "Yes, I'm okay. The same as the other six times you asked."

    "Sorry, just worried. That was a close one," Kara sighed.

    "Even if they had managed to take me, I trust you would have swooped in a nick of time to save me," Lena said, taking a step back, opening the balcony door to her penthouse. "You always do, my very own knight in red and blue."

    "Always," Kara repeated. "Whatever it takes."

    Lena kept her smile, "come on in, I got something I was hoping to show you during our next movie night."

    Kara didn't move. She stayed rooted at the balcony. She couldn't pinpoint what was different between this Lena and her best friend. There was a different spark on her eyes, a hint of something in her smile. A different feeling in her touch. Kara felt a different kind of draw to Lena, as well. The magnetism between them made the anticipation of seeing her again almost intoxicating.

    She, however, wasn't actually ready to see Lena again, as it turns out. Time had apparently slipped by, because Lena came back looking for her.

    "Why are you still standing there, dork?" She asked, wearing a satin floral robe, that let very little to the imagination. As Kara gawked at her, Lena frowned, worried. "Do you have to go?"

    Kara heard Lena repeat her name as her brain rebooted.

    "No," she finally said. "I can stay."

    "Is everything all right?" Lena questioned. "You are acting as if you haven't seen me with even less clothes."

    One thing she was sure, Kara had not seen Lena with less than that robe. And yet, she knew, whatever version of them she was now living in, most definitive had.

    "I'm okay," she said. "Just a bit shaken about what happened."

    Lena's eyes softened, as she walked closer to her. Putting her arms around Kara's neck. "I know it feels different now," she whispered. "But it's just the quarterly assassination attempt, it comes with the territory. And if you think about it, it wasn't that bad, it was just kidnapping."

    "I wish you'd stop diminishing how scary that is, Lena," Kara whispered, carefully touching the brunette's cheek. "Especially because we know who's behind it. It's unfair, it's scary… it's upsetting."

    Few seconds went by, before Lena finally conceded. "It is. But if I stop to think too much about it, I'll freeze. And I don't want to give him the satisfaction, okay?"

    Kara sighed, then nodded.

    "And I have the perfect remedy to make us forget the entire thing, at least for a few hours," Lena smiled. It was a new playfulness that Kara didn't know.

    Lena guided Kara's hand to the robe's tie around her waist, and then…. and then she kissed her.

    Right on the lips. And Kara, as much as she saw Lena lean in closer and closer almost in slow motion, didn't move away. She didn't, because she realized she did want to kiss Lena too.

    Her lips were soft, plump, and she should have been kissing Lena long time ago. Kara didn't want to stop. Lena bit her lip, teasingly, and Kara couldn't stop the urge of pulling the robe's tie, until it opened.

    Lena took a step back, opening the robe even more, as if to give her a show.

    The sight was delectable, divine, mouthwatering. Lena seemed pleased with herself, as she grabbed Kara's hands and pulled her inside the penthouse. Kara didn't stop her, almost hypnotized by what was happening.

    Lena kissed her again, more demanding, hungrier. Kara could feel her nails digging into her scalp, and Kara wasn't sure how she knew, but she was sure Lena was almost willing them to mark her, to leave a visible mark on her.

    Rao, Kara wished she could.

    "Wait… wait," she stopped kissing Lena, touching their foreheads instead. "I'm sorry. I— this is wrong."

    Lena looked at that phrase as if Kara had struck her.

    "No, I don't mean—" Kara started, but she did mean them. Because it wasn't really them. "I'm sorry, I know it's confusing. It's just… You know I love you, right?"

    Lena had a slight frown, but nodded anyways.

    "I know this seems— I don't feel like myself, right now," Kara continued. "And this version of me, who I am right now… I don't deserve you."

    "Kara, what—"

    Kara shook her head, kissed Lena in her cheek, lightly touching her lips again. "Don't worry I promise I'll fix it," she said. "I will do it right this time."

    She took a step back, again. "Mxy, get me home." And just like that, everything around her stopped existing.

     

     

    After sending off Mxy on his merry way, assuring him his debt was paid, she flew around National City to clear her mind. She wasn't sure how… but she was determined to fix everything with her Lena.

    And maybe… Maybe then they would have a shot at a future similar to the one universe she just went into.

    Regardless if that was even possible, she knew she still wanted Lena in her life. So, she softly landed a the penthouse's balcony. Identical to the one she was just at… and yet, it felt colder and emptier.

    Of course, Lena sensed her immediately.

    "Let me guess You're here to tell me once again that I should forgive you," she asked. "Or maybe that I shouldn't work with my brother?"

    Kara thought about it for a moment, and then took a step forward while making her suit disappear. "Yes, but more than anything I just wanted to see you." She said standing there in her socks and cable knit sweater. "I know I cannot snap my mistakes away, but I was wondering, if you were willing to talk to me?"

    And Kara… She was ready to grovel this time, if she had to, because now she knew the reason behind the reason. And she truly really wanted the best outcome possible.
