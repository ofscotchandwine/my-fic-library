Author's Note:

This is hopefully one in many one-shots I'll be writing for Supercorp that are inspired by Christmas Songs. To be clear, this isn't a music fic, I've just taken inspiration from the song to create the story. This one is obviously inspired by 'I Saw Mommy Kissing Santa Claus.' I love Esme and had to find a way to bring her into the story. So this is what I've come up with. The beginning is focused on Esme, to keep with the idea of the story, but then it switches to Lena and Kara.

This story also has hints to other stories I'll be writing. One of which is 'The Twelve Pains of Christmas by Bob Rivers'. The other one is mentioned at the end, and I'm fairly certain you can figure that one out. It's in a pretty popular animated movie. Most of the stories will fit in with each other. Some may detour into another universe, but most will fit together, and when they tie into each other, I'll leave hints like I have with this one.

Now, as I come up with story ideas, I will be updating my spotify playlist with the songs. So if you want to have a listen, you can go here: Supercorp Carols . If you have any song suggestions, feel free to leave them below and if I figure something out, I can gift it to you if you'd like.

Esme was so excited for Christmas this year. It was her first Christmas that she could remember where she wasn't going to be staying in a foster home. One where she might get one present if she was lucky. A present that was probably going to be a pair of socks. The cheapest of cheap white socks.

This year however, was the start of what she believed to be the best Christmas ever, and many more to come.

She had her moms Alex and Kelly, her Aunt Kara, her godmother Lena, and of course J'onn, Nia, Brainy and her grandmother Eliza. She was sure to get a couple presents this year. More than just socks. Not that presents were the only thing she was excited for. She had a family. A real life family that cared for and loved her.

Not to mention, they were all staying at the Luthor Mansion, a house so big that Esme hadn't even seen all of the rooms. She was still in the midst of exploring the entire place. There were too many rooms to count, so many bathrooms, a study, a library, a swimming pool room, a dining hall, a big kitchen, a game room, the list went on and on. She even found a secret room that led to a lab. Lena had told her she wasn't allowed back in that room. Which was fair, it had a lot of equipment lying around in it. Something about it being one of Lex's old labs.

They had spent the last couple days decorating the house, both inside and out. Aunt Kara had an incident of her own with the lights, but that's a story for another day. She did get them to work, but she had a time doing it. Esme on the other hand was inside helping put up the tree with Alex, Kelly and Lena. She was able to put the star on the top with J'onn's help.

The inside of the mansion looked like Santa's workshop had exploded with all the garland, lights, miniature houses, a train roaming the living room, there was even fake snow on the counters.

So when Alex had told her to go to bed early on Christmas Eve, so that Santa would come, she tried her best to follow instructions. Over the past month, they had convinced her that Santa really was real, even though Sammy at one of her previous foster homes had said he wasn't real. Alex, Kelly, and of course Kara, were pushing the idea that Santa Claus really was real, and that she needed to get to sleep early so that he would come with presents.

That's what Santa did right? He waited until you were sleeping, and then he would come to town. That's how the song went right?

Esme tried her best to keep her eyes shut and sleep, but she couldn't. She had to go out and see for herself. She was going to catch Santa in the act, or prove that they were lying. They said they'd never lie, because she was lied to so often growing up, but if it turned out that he wasn't real she wouldn't hold it against them. After all, Santa was created for kids to dream.

Esme made her way down one of the three staircases to the main floor, toward the big living room where the huge eight foot tree stood so she could wait for Santa Claus.

It was just before midnight, when her entire family was asleep, Esme was the only one awake, or so she thought.

As she came down the stairs, she heard a voice.

"Ho Ho Ho."

Esme's eyes opened wide at the deep voice saying those iconic Santa sounds. She had to stop herself from running into the room, afraid that she might be dreaming. So she slowly walked over to the room, kneeled down and peeked around the corner.

There, in a bright red suit, a big belly, and a big white beard was…

"Santa Claus…" Esme barely whispered as she was so surprised by what she was looking at. She rubbed her eyes thinking she had to be seeing things. But no, as she dropped her hands, he was still standing there. He was standing there eating the cookies, and drinking the milk that Alex helped her put out earlier that night.

She was about to stand up and go say hello when another voice joined the room.

"Well, Santa, you better be off to finish your runs. Wouldn't want some kids to wake up without any presents under the tree."

It was godmother Lena who had appeared next to Santa, holding what looked like his sack of presents.

She handed the bag over to Santa Claus, who gladly took it.

"Before you go, I found this little bit of mistletoe by the entrance," Lena wiggled the mistletoe between her fingers.

From what Esme could see, Santa smiled, and leaned in wrapping his arms around Lena's waist to pull her in for a kiss. Esme immediately turned away, back against the wall. What is happening!? She thought to herself. Thinking there's no way that Aunt Lena would cheat on Aunt Kara, she turned back around slowly, only to find that Santa was still kissing her aunt.

She looked one more time, realized it wasn't ending, and decided that she had seen enough and went back to her room. She would tell Kara in the morning.

Esme proved that Santa was real, but now she wished she wouldn't have made that discovery.

This included National City, Metropolis, Smallville, Midvale, and any other little towns in the area. Lena's foundation also donated a chunk of money so that even more kids would get presents from National City's very own Super Santa Claus.

Kara handed the bag to her, but before letting go, she leaned in to kiss her on the cheek, "I'm sorry I'm going to miss our Christmas Eve plans."
