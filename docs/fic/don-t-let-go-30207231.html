1. Chapter 1

Wow, Kara thought.
Wow. Wow. Wow. Wow. Wow.
Here she was just going for an afternoon jog in the park and she had been caught up short. Kara slowed to a walk, crawl really, as she was drawn in by the most heavenly sight imaginable.
There was nothing ordinary about the woman she had been swept up by. An airy, striped sundress covered the body of a goddess while her face, that of an angel leaned up, taking in the last of the late afternoon sun, black hair waving in the breeze. She was ethereal and Kara came to a stop, admiring the way the dark red lipstick contrasted with her alabaster skin. Nothing ordinary, indeed.
She watched as the woman gave a small smile toward the sun before turning to pick up the hat that sat on the bench next to her and putting it on. With a deep breath, she stood and Kara saw her chances of learning everything about her dwindling to nothing. Her heart squeezed in sorrow and she took a step forward, body drawn toward the brunette.
Before Kara could get very far, a gust of wind blew up and took them both by surprise. The woman's tan, broad-brimmed hat was lifted away from her head and went sailing away from her toward the pond. With a strangled sound of protest, the woman watched as it fell to the water just out of reach.
Kara jogged forward, seeing the forlorn look on the brunette's face.
"Don't worry," she said, "I've got this."
Kara didn't have anything but she shucked her shoes and socks anyway.
"It's o-" the woman began but Kara had already taken one step into the cold water.
What was she thinking? Kara asked herself. Just get the hat. She needs her hat and you're going to get it back for her. Kara took another tentative step, trying not to think about whatever was squishing under her feet. It was deeply unpleasant and she refused to look down, focusing entirely on her goal.
It was right there. Just a couple more inches forward and she could make a grab for it. Kara slid carefully along before bending down to retrieve the hat, feeling wet felt between her fingers.
Standing straight up, Kara held aloft her prize triumphantly, turning to see a beaming smile on the face of the gorgeous woman. This proved to be her downfall as she felt her feet slip out from underneath her on the unknown sludge. With a loud yell, Kara got a face full of disgusting pond water as she fell forward, the hat still in her outstretched hand. Lifting herself above the water, Kara found it nearly impossible to move forward without slipping further away from the edge of the concrete pond. Who had designed this? she wondered, her hands trying to find anything to grip onto. The smell was awful, the taste was worse, but Kara had to fight her way back, talking down her embarrassment and gag reflex. Arm muscles straining, she felt around and discovered cracks in the concrete that she fit her fingertips into to drag her body up, finally reaching the edge where an outstretched hand waited for her. Without thinking, Kara grabbed for it and was hauled from the stinking duck cesspool.
Exhausted and smelling foul, Kara rolled onto her back, trying to get air into her lungs. A face appeared above her, blocking out the sun. It was the angel, peering down at her in shock.
"I got your hat," Kara panted, hoarsely. The taste in her mouth was really awful.
"You didn't have to do that," the angel said.
"Of course I did."
"Why?"
"Because you looked sad. Couldn't have that." Wow, Kara must have swallowed more duck poop water than she thought.
The angel blushed but gave her a small smile. "You're my hero, you know."
"Then I have lived a worthy life and can die here in peace." Kara closed her eyes and went slack.
"No please. No dying. Time to get up." The brunette grabbed hold of Kara's cold hand and began to lift her up. She was just happy to follow the warmth. This woman's hand was soft and made Kara think of running through fields during summer.
"Mmm, your hands are nice."
"Um, thank you," she answered shyly.
The adrenaline was beginning to crash around Kara and she realized what she had said. "Oh, I'm sorry. I just-um, here's your hat." She held it out the soaking, crumpled thing and the woman took it from her with a wry smile.
"Can I have the name of my hero?"
"Yeah, it's Kara. Um, Kara Danvers." Kara tried to wipe her damp hands off on her running shorts before remembering that it wouldn't do any good. Instead, she gave a little wave and watched eagerly as she was given a smile in return.
"I'm Lena. Lena Luthor. Thank you, Kara. It was very brave of you to do that."
"Pshaw, it was nothing. I mean-anyone would have done the same."
Lena smirked and looked around at the other passersby who had completely ignored the plight of the splashing woman, much less a drowning hat.
"Okay, well, maybe not but it still wasn't a big deal for me."
"On the contrary, Kara, I'd still like to thank you properly. May I take you to dinner?"
"What? You want to-what?"
"It seems only fair. You are my hero, after all."
Kara stared at her, mouth gaping, totally at a loss. Lena watched her, eyebrow cocked for a second, before a nervous look came over her.
"I mean, not if you don't want to."
"No! I mean, yes! I do want to. Go to dinner with you, I mean. That sounds amazing but-"
"But-?"
Kara looked down at herself, covered in who knows what. She gave an embarrassed chuckle. "I really need to shower first."
"Oh!" Lena replied. "Of course! Would you like to get cleaned up and meet me somewhere?"
"More than anything," Kara simpered before pulling herself back with a mortified look.
Lena didn't seem to mind if the pleased expression on her face was anything to go by. "Here," she said, pulling something from her purse, a card, "it has my cell on it. Let me know what time and does Alberto's sound good to you?"
Kara's eyes bugged. That was only the nicest, most romantic restaurant in town.
"Y-Yeah, um, sure. Sounds great!" Kara had used up all of her bravado for the day and was left a stuttering mess. But who wouldn't be in the face of such perfection?
"Wonderful," Lena purred, "I'll see you later." She turned and walked away, leaving a dripping Kara to watch the sway of her hips, dumbfounded. Rushing to put her shoes back on, Kara ran from the park and spent a considerable amount of time showering the guck off of her in anticipation for her date.

"Of course." Lena gestured for her to walk ahead of her, "Shall we?"
"Lena, this is gorgeous," Kara raved as they were led to a table on the rooftop of the restaurant. Torches flickered along the edges of the rectangular building, casting shadows across the diners. The city lights spread out in all directions for a sensational view, but honestly, Kara's eyes barely left her date.
"I'm glad you think so. I was worried it might be too much."
Kara smiled at her, reaching out a hand to touch the back of Lena's with her fingertips. "Not at all. This is beyond amazing."
Lena stared down at their hands barely touching, red reaching up to the tips of her ears. "I don't know why I'm suddenly shy. I'm usually not."
"If it helps, you make me very nervous," Kara replied, with a broad grin.
"I don't know, you were doing pretty great when you came out of the water today."
"That was a fluke. I'm never that suave in real life."
"You'll still be my hero, even if your confidence wavers."
Kara felt her heart swell at those words. All she wanted from this day on was to be Lena's hero.
They ordered their food and found themselves caught up in conversation about work, family, and favorites that bordered on the abstract.
"What's your plan for the zombie apocalypse?" Kara asked, giving her a suspicious squint.
"Classified," Lena replied, flatly.
"What? You can't be serious."
Lena didn't move.
"Wow, okay."
"It's not my fault. But let's just say L-Corp is covered if zombies break loose."
"Okay, I call you as my apocalypse partner, Miss Luthor."
Lena chuckled. "That's not a bad plan, actually."
Kara looked away from her before something else came to mind. "Wait, loose? Does that mean there are already zombies?"
"Classified. Where's your dream vacation spot?"
"You can't just-" Kara stopped at Lena's level look. "Fine, but I'm sticking close to you from now on."
"I could be amenable to that," Lena murmured. Kara blushed, ducking her head. "Right, so dream vacation spot would be Ireland. I want to see the Giant's Causeway."
Lena nearly choked on the sip of wine she had just taken.
"Really?" At Kara's confused nod, she said, "I was born in Ireland."
"That's amazing. What a coincidence. Have you been back since?"
"A few times," Lena replied, quietly.
"Man, that's the dream. It looks absolutely beautiful there."
"It really is. You'll love it if you go."

Kara bit her lip, afraid to let herself free.

"Yes, but I promised."
"You've done better than most. I release you from your promise."
"Oh, thank goodness." Kara let out a laugh. "That's just too funny to hold in."
Lena scowled. "If you say so."

Lena sighed, looking away. "Sadly, no. Now I have to be an adult and bubblegum isn't really conducive to CEO behavior."

"Cinnamon."
"That's fair. I'm a wintergreen kind of toothpaste myself."

The conversation kept up along the same vein until without realizing it, Kara found their plates empty and the server bringing the check. Lena leaned over to take hold of it, but Kara tried to beat her to it.
"I've got it," she said.
"Please, it's my treat. I asked you."
Those green eyes held Kara captivated and she found herself losing the battle as Lena took hold of the bill. Of course, in a fit of romantic comedy coincidence, the air picked up and sent the paper flying off the rooftop. Kara made a grab for it, nearly toppling over the edge.
"Kara!" Lena grabbed at her to pull her back and Kara collapsed into her front, puffing with adrenaline.
"Well, it's gone."
"Geez," Lena panted, "you almost went over the edge."
"Sorry," Kara replied sheepishly.
"You have a hero complex," Lena accused.
"And you have a problem holding on to things. Is this always going to be a thing with you?" Kara asked, only just now feeling Lena's arms around her waist. They were warm and soft and sturdy, clutching her tightly. Kara could feel Lena's heart beating wildly against her back. Turning her head, she was met with the sharp edge of Lena's jaw, clenched tight against her shoulder. After a minute, Kara felt her relax and grow softer, finger splayed out across her stomach.
Lena's nose skimmed the shell of Kara's ear as she whispered, "I know when to hold on to things."
The feeling of her breath against Kara's ear left her with shivers that threatened to shake her thin frame apart. She grabbed onto Lena's hands to ground herself but it had the opposite effect, sending jolts of electricity to her limbs.
"Lena," Kara whispered, all out of air as she struggled to breathe. Since the moment she had laid eyes on this beautiful woman, she had thought of nothing else but what it might be like to be in this moment. And now it was here, Kara wasn't going to miss her chance.
The brunette continued her exploration, placing a delicate kiss to Kara's temple. "Mmm?"

"Never," Lena rasped. And with that one word, Kara turned in her arms and their lips met in a fiery collide that burst new worlds out into the cosmos. Kara's arms wound around Lena's neck as the brunette fully took her into her arms, breath mingling as one. Speaking of breath, who needed it? Certainly not Kara as she became fully enveloped in Lena's mouth, drawing life from each and every point they were connected. They were on top of the world, high above above it, looking down from the clouds from a place of pure bliss, finally finding home.

Lena's eyes softened, sparkles of light bouncing around to make them shine. Kara was enamored. She had found the only person she ever wanted to kiss again.

Kara beamed, so glad that she hadn't read this wrong. Giving a quick glance to the lips she loved she gave in, pressing against them because Lena let her. "I'm so glad I met you."

Kara barked a short laugh. "Me too," she said, leaning her forehead down against Lena's, their eyes never leaving each other.
