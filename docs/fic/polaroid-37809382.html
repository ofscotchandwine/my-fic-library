Lena was watching from the doorway of her office as Jess hung a green plant wrapped in red ribbon from the entryway of her balcony.

Mistletoe.

Lena had left her office an hour ago for a short meeting with a lab assistant that was taking over a tech while they were away on vacation. She wanted very little mishaps and this meeting was to ensure that. Jess had been informed that Lena would be away for no more than 30 minutes.

Unfortunately for her sneaky assistant, Julia Walsh was one of the most competent and conscientious lab assistants Lena had every met. So much so, that Lena was definitely promoting the woman to a lab technician.

The meeting only took 10 minutes. Which means it took Jess about 15 minutes to decide to sneak into Lena’s office and to hang that monstrosity up. Lena had another round of meetings for the rest of the day. She might not have even noticed if Jess had been quicker. She suspects that was her assistants plan.

Just as Jess finished hanging the mistletoe on the tack she’d pressed into the wall above the balcony’s door frame, Lena cleared her throat.

Jess turned, eyes immediately widening as she realized she was caught. She stepped away from the door and the mistletoe, smiling nervously.

“Hi, Ms. Luthor!” She followed Lena’s gaze to the mistletoe. “I was…I was just trying to get that. Someone must’ve tried to pull a prank while you were away and I, uh, went to the bathroom.”

Lena crossed her arms and raised a brow at Jess. Giving, “are you kidding me” vibes.

“Seriously?”

Jess gave a nervous smile. “I guess you caught me?”

“Well, I could only let you get away with it once.” Lena smirked at Jess’ eyes once again widening in shock. “I figured out it was you who hung it up at the Christmas Gala last year.”

“How?” Jess doesn’t even bother to hide her shock. “I was being really careful!”

Lena shrugs. “I have my ways.”

By ways, Lena definitely means Kara outright telling her last Christmas. Lena would have never suspected Jess.

“Why today though?”

“It’s December 15th.” Jess answers as if it’s the most obvious thing in the world.

Then it clicks for Lena. The 15th was the day of the Christmas gala last year. That would explain Kara’s comment about wanting to kiss her senseless to celebrate their beginning.

And then another thing clicks for Lena.

“Why my balcony though?”

Now, Jess shrugs a bit. States so casually that Lena almost nods along, “I figured Ms. Danvers will probably fly you home again.”

Lena is too stunned to say anything as Jess makes her quick escape.

Kara drops in to fly Lena home from work around 9pm. She lands on the balcony with a whooshing sound that Lena now finds comforting and familiar. Taps on Lena’s glass door, despite knowing that it’s already open.

Lena gathers her things and meets Kara at the door. She stands in the doorway, black briefcase slung over her shoulders, not moving into Kara’s embrace like she normally would.

Instead, Lena grins at Kara and points up. Kara’s eyes light up the moment she sees the mistletoe.

“Jess was being sneaky again. Happy Anniversary?” Lena chuckles, smiles way too much.

She feels the moment Kara finds her left hand and strokes the bracelet there. Lena’s fingers also stretch up to touch the spot above Kara’s super suit where her bracelet is hiding. In the summer, they’ll have matching rings.

“Happy Anniver-.”

Lena’s lips are on Kara’s before she can even finish the sentence.
