Author's Note:

This idea was in my drafts since last October when I wanted to do some Spooky Supercorp stuff initially. I wanted to do some kind of haunted house thing but I never could figure out how to make it happen. Then Lena got magic and a better idea came up, so let's go!

(This title was just supposed to be a placeholder until I thought of a better one but I couldn’t bring myself to change it. )

Nothing. It was nothing. 

 

Lena had been repeating that to herself for nearly two hours now. But the longer she sat in her bedroom, the less it felt like nothing. There was absolutely something wrong. 

 

Two hours prior, she’d been standing at her kitchen island with her mother’s book propped open in front of her. She’d spent the entire day trying to work through the new spells but it was difficult. Part of her was certain her mother made it much more cryptic than necessary. While she idly scanned over a page, something caught her attention. Her eyes narrowed as she slowly unraveled that this one was some sort of summoning spell. For a...familiar? Images of black cats flashed through her mind and she chuckled a little. Magic in movies was absolutely not the same as real life but she couldn't help but think about all the pop culture references to familiars that she knew of. The aspect was interesting so she gave it a shot. 

 

After quadruple checking that she had all the proper ingredients she began saying the words for the spell. The language still felt strange on her tongue. As she chanted, the little bowl in front of her started to glow. The light emitting from it got so bright she nearly had to look away as she finished the last line. There was a sudden burst of energy and then…nothing. 

 

Slow seconds ticked by as Lena waited for something. She wasn’t sure what exactly she was waiting for, even calling out a curious “Hello?” but receiving no answer. 

“And another botched spell.” She sighed and closed the book. That was enough for tonight. With a tired sigh she got up and wandered to her bedroom to start her nightly routine. When she stepped out of the shower her eyes darted around. Something felt off. Even though the room was filled with steam from her shower it still felt chilly. The thermostat said it was 62 degrees and she shivered, quickly turning it up before going to sort her hair out. As she brushed through the dark locks another involuntary shiver ran up her spine. Like hundreds of little fingers skittering up her back. The sensation made her yelp and quickly turn around. Only to find herself still alone. 

 

Strange. 

 

She had ignored it then, thinking she just needed rest. Yet now, two hours later she could not fall asleep. It felt like-...her mind tried to stop the words. It was ridiculous. There was nothing in the room. But still it felt like someone or something was watching her. Every movement she made felt like it was being tracked. 

 

"I'm just tired." She tried to calm her nerves. A good night's rest and she would be good as new and hopefully be able to actually make some progress tomorrow. Lena shut her eyes and rolled over, trying her best to distract her mind. 

 

The sound of her closet door rattling made her blood run cold. She sat up right, flicking on her bedside lamp. Lena waited and waited for something else to happen, but no further noises followed. Leaving the bedside lamp on, she slowly lowered herself back into the sheets but another rattling noise made her bolt out of the bed. She ripped the door open and...

 

Empty. Besides her clothes, the closet was empty. Lena let out a breath and turned away. 

"I'm losing my mind." As soon as she finished the sentence the closet door slammed shut behind her. 

 

She screamed. 

 

Her phone was in her hands, dialing a familiar number before she could even think about it. She power walked out of her room and into the living room as the line rang. 

"Hey, what's up?" Kara asked, sounding slightly groggy. Lena felt a bit guilty for waking her but she was afraid. 

"Ummm...I-" The words wouldn’t come out. There were more thuds and thumps coming from her room and she let out another fearful yelp.

"Are you okay?" 

"I don't know, I think someone is in my house?" Lena heard a heavy thud on her balcony. Kara landed already in her suit, looking ready to kick some serious ass. 

 

"That was fast." Lena gaped. No matter how many times she saw Kara use her powers it was still impressive and if she were honest with herself, it was hot at times too. 

"What's going on? Are you hurt?"  She asked as she scanned Lena for injuries before turning to the empty penthouse.

"I'm fine. Just a little spooked. I didn't mean for you to come all the way here." 

"It's alright. I needed to make sure you were safe. Although I don't see anyone in the place?" Kara triple checked and turned back to Lena. "What made you think someone was in here?" 

"Well…" She trailed off, feeling stupid. "It's silly and I think I just freaked myself out." Embarrassment seeped into her mind as she looked down at the ground. Maybe she’d really been over reacting. 

 

"Lena. Tell me." Kara's voice softened as she reached out to tilt Lena’s eyes up to meet her’s again. 

"It's just...I tried to do one of my mother’s spells. It was supposed to be a summoning spell for a familiar but I think I summoned something else by accident." It truly felt like the dumbest thing she'd ever said in her life but as much as her science mind wanted to fight it she knew deep down it was real.

"You-you think you summoned something else?" Kara shrank back a little, eyes darting around the room rapidly as she protectively pulled Lena closer. "Something else like what, Lena?" 

 

"I don't know! But my closet door closed by itself and I swear I felt something watching me." 

Kara cleared her throat and tried to put back on her Supergirl confidence but faltered when she tried to speak. "I'll...go check it out. Stay here.” 

"I can still come with you." She took a step after her but Kara held her hand up. 

"No, no, stay here.." Kara gave her a stiff smile before walking towards Lena's bedroom. The lamp on the side table was still on and the closet door was closed. She could see through the door and saw no one was in there but still opened it to be certain. The only out of place thing in the room was how abnormally cold it was. Even she felt a little chilly in the space which for her was practically unheard of. 

 

"You okay?" Lena shouted down the hall. 

"Yeah! I don't see anything either. Maybe you didn't summon anything and it was just your neighb-" Before she finished speaking she heard four consecutive footsteps. She nor Lena had moved. Kara turned around but saw no one else. Her heart was hammering in her chest as she walked back out into the hall. 

 

“Did you...hear that?” She asked as Lena came over to her side. 

“Those footsteps weren’t you?” Lena tried to keep her voice steady. 

“No.” 

The dim light that had been on in the hallway suddenly fizzled out and Lena gripped Kara’s forearm. Kara put her hand on top of her’s as they both stared down the hall. 

 

"Do you see anything?" Lena whispered, her voice full of panic. 

"No." Kara shook her head in disbelief as more clattering noises came from down the darkened hallway. The pair stood frozen as the noises came closer. Out of the darkness, two glowing eyes appeared. They were floating nearly 7ft off the ground. The inky black shadow seemed even darker than that of the hallway. Footsteps followed as it came down the hallway towards them.

“Lena. ” A gravelly deep voice echoed around them.  

 

The horror movie worthy scream that came out of both of them was impressive. Without much thought, Kara scooped Lena up and bolted out the window. They were back in Kara's living room in a blink of an eye. 

“What was that? What was that?” Kara repeated like a broken record as she paced around her living room. 

“I don’t know!” Lena replied in a panic. Through her overwhelming fear she registered that she was still in Kara’s arms as she paced around. Not that she minded but it was still odd to be carried like this. Kara too seemed to remember at that moment that Lena was still in her arms. She gently placed her back onto the ground. 

“Sorry. I’m just kind of freaking out.” 

 

“Me too. I need to figure out how to...son of a bitch.” Lena groaned, putting her head in her hands. “I forgot the book.”

“Oh jeez. Okay. I’ll go get it.”

“Absolutely not Kara. I don’t know what that thing is or how it could hurt you.”

“But we need the book in order to get rid of it so I’ll go get it. I’ll be quick.” Before she could lose her nerve or Lena could stop her she was gone. 

 

Lightning fast, Kara grabbed the book off of Lena’s counter and just as she took off into the air, a low voice whispered in her ear. “ Bring the new witch to me. Tell her not to fear. ” Kara nearly crashed into the side of a building before tumbling back into her apartment. 

“Are you okay?” Lena rushed over to her side.

“Yeah I’m good. I think it just umm...spoke to me.”

“What did it say?” She asked nervously. 

“It asked me to bring you to it and not to fear?” 

“Yeah good fucking luck with the no fear part. Thank you, by the way. ” She took the book from Kara’s hands before looking towards the clock. Nearly 1am. She was bone tired but they needed to fix this as soon as possible. There were too many other things to deal with right now and she didn’t want to add more problems to an already growing list.  

 

As if reading her mind, a warm hand settled on top of her own over the book. “Maybe we’ll think of a game plan in the morning? I think you need some rest before you get back into witch mode.” The term ‘witch mode’ made Lena smirk as she nodded in agreement. After bickering over sleeping arrangements, they both settled on sharing Kara’s bed. They both lay in silence in the dark for long drawn out minutes until Lena spoke. 

“I think I’m too scared to sleep.”

“Well I don’t know much about magic but I think we’re safe here. I don’t think it followed.” At least she really really hoped.

 

Another lull of silence. 

 

“Can I...get closer to you?” Lena whispered. 

“Yeah that’s fine.” Kara was thankful Lena had the courage to ask because she was still beyond freaked out too. Lena shuffled closer, until they were nearly touching. With hesitant hands Kara pulled her closer and Lena gently rested her head in the crook of her neck. “Better?” She whispered.

“Much.” Lena agreed. “God you’re warm.” Lena shuffled closer and Kara’s arm tightened around her. 

“I try.” She joked and Lena chuckled softly, letting her eyes slip shut. 

 

They both drifted off into comfortable slumber. 

 

 

“You’re joking?” Alex barked out a laugh. 

“I’m not! I saw it, I heard it. Twice.” Kara held up two fingers as Alex gave her a suspicious look. 

“You’re telling me, Lena accidentally summoned a 7ft ghoul in her apartment and now it's thumping around and playing with her lights?” 

“It sounds insane to me and I was there.” Lena grumbled as she took a sip of some much needed coffee.

 

“You think you did the spell wrong?” Nia asked and Lena ran a hand through her hair. 

“I was positive I did it all correctly but then again I truly have no idea what I’m doing.” 

“Well walk me through what you did and maybe we’ll find something?” She offered and Lena gave her a small smile.

“I’d appreciate that.” After digging deeper and with a bit more research they came closer to a possible answer. 

 

"Confirm the bond? What the hell does that mean?" Lena’s brows knit together while she and Nia sat in the lab. There was another page about familiars that was annoyingly conveniently not on the same page as the summoning spell. It spoke of confirming the bond between the witch and the familiar once it had been summoned. 

"I don't know, I'm just as new to this as you are." Nia replied. She’d been extremely helpful in trying to decipher the book and Lena was eternally grateful for not having to do this alone.  

“We do not have time for this.” Lena nearly put her head down on the table in defeat. This whole magic situation was ludicrous. If she couldn’t figure this out, how the hell was she supposed to stop a 5th dimensional imp.  

“No no come on. Maybe just maybe you did it right but you forgot the last step.” Nia bumped Lena’s shoulder with hers, trying to lighten Lena’s plummeting mood. 

“Am I just supposed to bond with that...thing in my place? Is that what I forgot while I was terrified beyond belief?” 

“I guess?” She shrugged. 

“How would I bond with it?”

“Umm...” Nia sat back in her chair, deep in thought. Lena fiddled with her fingers as the sound of it saying her name made more shivers run through her. “A gift?”

“A gift?” Lena gave her an incredulous look. 

 

“What? It's worth a shot. Maybe a gift is how you’ll bond with it.” 

“Nia this is insane.” Lena put her head in her hands but it was her only option. “Let’s go find a gift for the demon in my house then.” 

“If this doesn’t work then we’ll just have to go full ghostbusters.” She joked and Lena chuckled as they stepped into the elevator. 

 

After a couple hours of fussing about the best type of gift to bring the creature, they finally made a decision. Lena held the small cardboard box in her hands as she, Nia, Kara and Alex stood in front of her apartment door. Kelly and J’onn had absolutely no desire to tag along on this supernatural adventure and Brainy was still monitoring Nyxly. 

“One of us has to go first.” Nia whispered after a minute of them just standing there. 

“I’ll go.” Kara started but Lena held her back. 

“No I will. My magic, my problem.” She said and Kara looked ready to argue more. 

“She’s right Kara and you are not magic proof.” Alex added. Kara sighed before nodding for Lena to open the door. 

 

Chilled air hit them as if a freezer had been opened. The curtains were drawn shut, casting the space in darkness. Kara tried the lights with no luck but thankfully Alex brought a flashlight. She clicked it on and they saw there were a few things tossed around the place but the damage wasn’t too bad. The four women slowly stepped deeper inside. 

“I’m guessing it wasn’t like this before?” Alex whispered. 

“If you think I live like this on a regular basis Alex, you do not know me.” She said and Alex laughed as they scanned around the room. It died down when there was shuffling across the room. The beam of light immediately tried to point at the source but the shadow darted away. Nia felt her entire body constrict with fear when she swore it was crawling around. 

 

“D-did you see that.” Alex asked and the other three wordlessly nodded. Footsteps rushed around the room making them all shriek, bunching together even closer. The flashlight was bouncing all over the room as Alex tried to track the swift movement. 

 

“It's over there.” Kara whispered harshly. They were all still pressed closely together when the light pointed directly at a tall shadow that was now standing where Kara and Lena had seen it last. The lanky figure’s eyes shone brightly in the darkness of the hallway. 

“Holy shit. Holy shit. Holy shit. It's real.” Nia squeaked from where she was hiding behind Kara. The figure didn’t move from the spot it was in but the eyes tilted to the side as if it was looking at them in curiosity. 

“ Lena. ” The voice spoke and they all took a step back.  

 

“Should you give it the gift now?” Kara asked. 

“I guess.” She took a step forward but Kara didn’t want to let her go. Tell her not to fear. The words Kara had said last night played back in Lena’s head. “Let me go. I think I got this.” That wasn’t the full truth but she had to start trusting herself if she was going to get better at this magic bullshit. Kara reluctantly let go of her arm and Lena took a steadying breath. 



“Hi.” Her voice shook as she took a step forward. Glowing eyes followed her movement. “Sorry about last night. You kind of freaked me out.” She huffed out a nervous laugh as the void stared back at her. “So I um...I brought you a peace offering. It's one of my favorites.” Lena took a few more steps forward, much to Kara's dismay.

Slowly, she put the little box from her favorite pastry shop on the floor and slid it closer to the creature before stepping back quickly. Food was always the best peace offering in her normal life and Lena hoped it might work here too. God she really wished her life was still only devoted to science. Science made sense. This all just felt like an insane guessing game that she hoped she was playing correctly. 

 

She, Kara, Nia and Alex all watched in slight fear as the creature stepped forward. Darkness engulfed the box and the bright eyes flickered away. They all stood in silence, waiting for something to happen. Suddenly the box slid back out from the shadows and they all jumped. 

“It’s empty?” Alex peered inside. 

Nia looked over her shoulder. “Is that a good sign?” 

“I have no idea.” Lena shrugged helplessly. 

The sound of hurried little taps on the floor made them all look up. They held their breath as something moved in the darkness, slowly emerging from the shadows. It was a…

 

“Badger?” Nia gaped as it waddled out of the darkness. The lights in the apartment clicked back on as it waddled over to Lena. Its black and white striped face sniffed at her leg. 

“Hello there.” Lena said in disbelief. “I umm...I don’t really know what to say.” 

“ You don’t need to say anything. ” The fucking  badger  replied. 

“So you can still talk?”

“ When I want to. ” They said just before Kara touched her shoulder. 

 

“Are you talking to it?” She asked and Lena nodded. 

“You guys can’t  hear it speaking can you?” She asked and all three shook their heads. “Of course you can’t.” Lena turned back to the creature. 

“It’s pretty cute.” Kara noted.  

“ Cute? ”' It sounded slightly irritated at the adjective and Lena bit back a smile. 

“Agreed, but I don’t think it likes being called that. Just um-give us a sec?” She looked back at the three. 

“If you’re sure?” Alex lifted a brow, still suspicious of the creature. 

“I’m sure.” Lena nodded. Kara lingered for a few moments before following Nia and Alex back into the living room.

The badger stared up at her as Lena contemplated what the hell to ask. This was the number one strangest experience of her life and she interacted with aliens on a daily basis.  

 

“Do you have a name?” She started. 

“ No. ”

She lowered herself to the ground, sitting cross legged on the floor. “Can I call you something?”

“ If you wish. ”

“We’ll umm...work on a name later. I should have known this before I summoned you but what do familiars do exactly?” She knew they offered assistance but didn’t have the specifics. 

“ Help when needed. ” Well that was just as vague. 

“Help with?” Lena waited for them to speak again but they said nothing. “Is all this witch business going to be this cryptic?” She sighed.

“ Usually. ” They answered and Lena swore she could hear a sense of amusement in their tone. 

 

“And why did you tear up my place?”

“ When not bonded to a witch, familiars’ true forms can be temperamental. I apologize. ”

“I mean, it's fine. I should have known what I was doing before summoning you.” She rubbed the back of her neck. “Are you a badger because of me? Or?” 

“We take forms best suited for our witch. ”

“And I’m best suited for a...badger. Will you always be with me?” The idea of having this as a constant companion would not be easy or explainable in any way. 

“I will be present when you need me. I’m always by your side Lena. ” Before Lena could think of another question, the badger disappeared. 

 

“I guess we’re done talking then.” She called out into thin air, tossing her hands up. Lena got back to her feet and came over to where everyone was sitting. She plopped on the couch next to Kara with a sigh. “I think we’re good?”

“Where’d it go?” Alex leaned back, looking for the creature. 

“They disappeared but said they would always be by my side.”

“That's kind of cool.” Nia nodded. “As long as it says cute and cuddly not...whatever that was before. I’m going to have nightmares for the rest of the month.”

“Ditto.” Alex nodded. 

 

“How are you feeling?” Kara asked and Lena shrugged. 

“Strange but relieved.” She looked back to her messy apartment with a sigh. 

“We can help you clean up and maybe we can all go get dinner. I’m starving.” She offered and Lena smiled at her. 

“I didn’t sign up for cleaning duty.” Alex complained. 

“Too bad. You’re helping.” Kara nudged her as they all got off the couch to start cleaning. 



After dinner and a much needed drink with their friends, Kara and Lena landed back on her balcony. 

“Thanks for the ride.” Lena smiled. “And for helping me out.” 

“Anytime. You want to come over for breakfast tomorrow? You can even bring your new fuzzy friend.” Kara asked but winced a little. “I mean we have been together for the last 24 hours so if you needed a break at all that’s fine too.” Kara rambled and Lena smiled at her fondly. 

“I’d love to.” She gave her a sly look. “Only if you make waffles.”
“Waffles? Pancakes are so much batter.” She grinned and Lena groaned at her awful pun. “Yes I will make you waffles.”

“Good, I’ll see you in the morning.” Lena pulled Kara into a tight hug. Kara's arms wrapped around her waist and held her closer. Their hugs always seemed to last longer when they were alone, neither one of them wanting to let go. After a few more moments, Kara pulled back slightly. 

“Try not to do anything else spooky? I don’t think my heart could handle another scare like that.” She smiled softly and Lena nodded. 

“I’ll try.” They stared at one another in silence, waiting on the other to break the moment. Kara was the first to bail by clearing her throat and letting Lena go. 

“See you tomorrow.” Kara gave her one more wave before flying off. 



Lena watched as Kara flew off into the night sky. 

“ You love her. ” A voice said and Lena jumped. 

“Christ. You scared me.” Lena held a hand to her chest. “Wait...what did you just say?” 

The badger hopped up onto one of the outdoor chairs. “ The alien. The blonde one. You love her. ”

Heat creeped up her neck. “Of course I do, she’s my best friend.”

“ Not friendship. You and I both know that. ”

“I don’t know what you’re talking about.” Lena turned on her heel, walking back into her place. 

“ You do not? I have a feeling that you do. ” They followed behind her. 

“What happened to your curt sentences?” She grumbled. 

“ I have more to say now that I found something interesting. ” They replied easily and Lena rolled her eyes as she held the door open while they waddled inside her apartment. 



How was it possible her life got even weirder in a single day?
