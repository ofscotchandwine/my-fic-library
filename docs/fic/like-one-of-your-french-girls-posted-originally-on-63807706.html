    Her eyes fluttered open, and after a moment, they focused on Rio. “What are you doing?” Agatha asked, voice rough with sleep. She shuffled closer to her and rested her head against her thigh. She craned her neck to look at her sketchbook.

    Rio smiled and pressed a kiss to the side of her head. She tilted her sketchbook towards Agatha and said honestly, “Enjoying the view.”
