Death Junior Welcomes You Home

    Chapter 1

    “Shoot,” Nicky snapped his fingers. “I forgot my dagger. Hang on, I'll be right back!”

    Agatha smiled as she watched her son run off back into their small cottage. Still a home she never thought she'd see again. Now with a family to call her very own. It took time to understand, accept every change that came with it. Her eternity set in stone, damned to be Death’s wife for all of time.

    At first, it was unsettling. Rio and her weren’t on the best of terms after leaving the road behind. When Agatha’s words were left between them,

    “I don’t wanna see your face”

    The words were a nail in the coffin. A case shut closed after years of struggling to do so. Rio took it to heart, unable to force herself to bounce back and push Agatha’s words aside like she typically did.

    Instead, she made Agatha regret and choke on those words. For years, Rio did as she wished. She hid in shadows, never appearing when Agatha killed coven after coven in an attempt to summon her. Rio never spoke about Agatha Harkness when others brought her up. Always a shrug, acting clueless to who she was or her whereabouts.

    To say the least, Agatha went insane overtime. Every year that passed, she lost her mind a little bit more. Rio heard about her borderline tantrums that’d lead to her killing a giant crowd. How she shit talked Death, Rio, everywhere she went. Anyone who dared to breathe her name around the witch set her off to the point of explosion.

    Rio couldn’t deny though..it was a bit entertaining. She always wondered how the reverse card would look laid out on the table. It gave Agatha a taste of her own medicine, revenge, karma, all of the above.

    But then, it came to an end. An abrupt one, a choice Agatha made that Rio would have never placed a bet on.

    “What do you mean she’s here Nicholas?”

    Death’s child had sighed. He held the same job as Rio, more focused on wildlife over humans. Though every now and then when Rio and Hela were too busy, he’d take on a bit of extra work. This time around, it felt like he struck gold and dust at the same time. Happiness mixed with sadness. A bittersweet passing soul for him.

    “She’s on the porch..” He admitted. “I collected her this morning. Her only road to take was here. Which..she didn’t seem too surprised over?”

    Rio’s nostrils flared, her fists turned into balls as her chest tightened in her chest. She didn’t mean to appear angry how he was the one to reap her lover’s soul. After all, she was avoiding Agatha like a plague. Technically this also granted the wish of never wanting to see her face again.

    But also, it was the end of a blissful era. A time that had tasted so sweet on her tongue was over. Agatha now would face her with anger instead of just hearing through the grapevine of her meltdown without Rio in her life.

    “Was Mom always meant to be here?” He asked.

    Rio scoffed. Of course, Agatha didn’t tell him.

    “We tied ourselves to each other centuries ago,” She said. “In a way, your mother gave up her soul to Death. Don’t look too surprised”

    He slightly chuckled, not shocked in the slightest his mother would do such a thing. Plus, it brought a bit of warmth to know how shockingly in love his mother’s are. Not were, never that. He knew deep down they were meant to be together. Rio always showed how much she cared even if she never meant to do so.

    “Well..she’s here” He said. “Be easy with her though. Please? I’m still shaken up by her cause of death”

    Rio snapped her eyes back to him, standing up from her chair instantly. “What do you mean?”

    Nicky sighed. “I’ll leave it for her story to tell..”

    Rio knew back then Agatha would put up a fight. She didn’t actually know the truth for months until Agatha finally confessed. However, Nicky knew. He admitted to that, but even if he tried to lie? Rio saw it. How careful he was, the way he talked and tiptoed around the subject. Seemingly as well he’d sink into the memory, replaying it like it was an addictive song.

    “Mama..” He whispered to himself.

    Nicky’s footsteps sped up through the forest, not caring if he risked tripping over a stone or branch. He’d been called to a soul just a few minutes prior. Anxiously he awaited to know the reasoning for how this one had passed on. The closer he approached, the more vivid the scene became.

    It was a rowdy bar, dull lights with loud noise of music and yelling. Nicky figured a bar fight. Those were common. But, a woman tucked away in the back took shot after shot. She downed a beer, then two, then three and more. Slowly, it dawned on him how she was drinking herself to death.

    At first, he figured it was some kid that made a mistake. He always wished he held a power and right to reverse those deaths. Though the voices grew more coherent, spitting out insults of a witch and how they’d beat the living hell out of her. He closed his eyes, whispering a chant of hope the woman didn’t get up to fight. She did though, he shouldn’t have hoped otherwise.

    Punch after punch, a crowd circled around while multiple guys took swigs in between hits. Then a silence. The little bell echoed in the distance, ringing to let him know the soul was completely in his universe. And when the image of the woman he was looking for became crystal clear, his heart jumped and dropped at once.

    Mom.

    As he ran through the forest, jumping over hurdles of branches and bushes he could only think to himself-

    Mom.

    Like an incantation this reality stayed true. A wish for it to be granted. Later on, he’d feel guilt wishing his mother were dead. But, other kids who died before their parents did the same. Every child no matter how old they grew in the afterlife wanted their Mom back..right?

    His wish came true as he rounded a tree, finding his mother whispering harshly under her tongue. Her fingers played with the gray streak in her hair, body snug in a suit he once saw in Rio’s sketchbook. Tight leather black pants, purple boots that added an inch or more, a purple top imprinted with dark green branches and topped off with a flowing purple cape.

    It was only a few short moments Nicky was able to take in the sight of his mother. A woman he worried he’d never see again. Rio warned him years ago Agatha could choose to be a ghost for eternity instead of crossing over to him. Yet here she stood, in the flesh of the form of death herself.

    “Mom..” He whispered.

    She stopped pacing back and forth. Slowly her head lifted, eyes met his soft ones that anxiously awaited for her to greet him.

    “You’re..” Agatha stammered, finger pointing as she harshly whispered. “Don’t tell me you-”

    “Death junior?” He stepped forward with a little grin. “That’s my nickname mother has for me”

    Agatha turned to face him completely. Her look conflicted between distraught and relief at once. Rio barely crossed her mind, too busy taking in the sight of her little boy now grown into a young man. How he wore a version of his mother’s outfit. Wore a smile that still reflected the same one he used to give her. Her son stood as a form of Death. How could she accept this?

    “I..the way you passed, Mom. If I could go kill those men myself without breaking the rules..I’d do it” He said. “Not that I’m not happy to have you here. I’ve missed you dearly, just-”

    “Shut up and get over here” Agatha cut him off.

    He didn’t hesitate to rush over. Death slipped into a version of a little boy. He ran into his mother’s arms, ignoring how he stood taller than her now. His head buried in her shoulder, nearly cried when Agatha’s arms wrapped around him. She held him tight as if she feared this was a dream just like he did.

    They stayed like that longer than both realized. Stuck in the comfort of being together again. Nicky wanted to sob, thankful his mother chose him over being a ghost. Agatha was still in shock, but with it enough to register she was holding her boy again. Something she never thought her mind, heart would ever allow.

    Agatha didn’t let go, but carefully whispered, “I’m sorry it took me so long, my love”

    He only held tighter, unable to let go.

    His mother was home. She finally chose him over outrunning Death and was terrified to face him. They still had more heart to hearts to have. There was facing Rio and hoping his mother could find middle ground with her. But for now, they had each other. And that was more than enough.

    He restrained himself from snooping through the window when his mother’s first laid eyes on each other. They were on the porch when their voices began to raise. He remembered the first back and forth of,

    “I thought you would break the spell, Agatha. You told me you did” Rio said.

    Agatha laughed. “Oh, please. I just said that to shut you up”

    “You lied…AGAIN!”

    “And you can’t outrun me..AGAIN!”

    “THAT’S ALL YOU EVER DID YOU HYPOCRITE!”

    “I’M THE HYPOCRITE?!”

    Nicky wasn’t sure why they stormed across the yard to the greenhouse. Part of him wondered if it’s because they didn’t want him hearing or maybe one was trying to run away. Whatever happened that night, Nicky didn’t question or want to completely know. By sunrise, Rio was making them breakfast with Agatha at the kitchen table. There was still a slight tension, the two weren’t really speaking. Both would pay attention to him, but not each other.

    Months went by with that exact scene, energy, everything and above. Nicky silently hated it, but felt it wasn’t his place to complain. He had the wish granted. His mom’s resided under the same roof for the first time in his entire existence. Maybe they were more so co-existing, but he’d take it.

    Then creeping up onto a year since Agatha’s arrival, Nicky came home after a few days away. He remembered kicking his shoes off, hanging his coat up with a tired sigh. Before trudging off to his bedroom, voices came from the kitchen that stopped him in his tracks.

    “Out!”

    Agatha scoffed, muttering something under her breath that made Rio snicker.

    “You’ve always sucked at Rummy. I warned youuuuu” She sang.

    “You should learn to shut up by now”

    “You could always make me-”

    “EW, NO!” Nicky yelled before storming down the hallway past the kitchen. He made sure to pop his head in to add a quick, “I don’t know why you two are suddenly getting along, but for the love of god- Save the gross shit when I’m not in earshot”

    They both jumped in their chairs, looking up at him with grins that slowly grew into wide ones. Agatha was the one to unsurprisingly push against his words. She put her cards down, standing up to round the table before dropping into Rio’s lap. Her arms looped around her neck, Rio’s arms around her waist while both radiated together.

    “You mean this?” Agatha asked in a voice that made her sound clueless. “Or..this?”

    She turned, cradling her love’s cheek to lift her lips to meet her own. Nicky groaned, covering his eyes as he ran away down the hall with a loud,

    “STOP BEING GROSS!”

    The two giggled against each other’s lips, pecking softly before pulling away to rest their foreheads together.

    “I’m so happy you died..”

    Agatha rolled her eyes. “Such a romantic”

    After a year, they found their own little routine. Agatha took up a job to mentor young witches who died before their parents. Nicky and Rio continued their roles as Death with a new found sense of pride knowing they had a family to go home to. Family dinners were reserved for Friday nights. No if, ands, or buts. That was Nicky’s rule, demanding them to follow it.

    Rio made breakfast on the weekends. If she had to take off to work unexpectedly, she made sure leftovers were packed up for them both with a note left on the table. One that wished them well and she’d be home soon.

    Agatha focused back on her witchcraft studies. Always saying there was an endless amount to learn even if she wasn’t alive anymore. She also took time to focus on Rio, love her, be with her. They’d plan little dates together. Sometimes a late night picnic in the backyard. Other times, a trip to the beach or just dinner with a shitty cliche movie on the TV.

    But more than anything? Agatha focused on Nicholas. She wanted to make up for time they had missed out on. Even if he always gently reminded her they couldn’t run out of time where they were, she couldn’t shake the feeling it was only right. The two took on adventures. She’d go with him when he had to reap the soul of an animal, they’d play little tricks and pranks on strangers in the same realm. Agatha taught him her own knowledge of magic. Unlike Rio’s green witch ways, Agatha would set up bottles and cans stacked on each other or scattered in trees.

    “Almost,” Agatha said, arms folded as she slowly stepped across the grass to him. “Focus completely on your aim. And remember, shoulders raised-”

    “Do I have to really learn how to shoot someone with magic? I’m Death. I have strength”

    Agatha shrugged. “It’s important. Don’t argue with your mother, Nicholas”

    His argument only came from frustration. Otherwise, he was thrilled to take on and learn from his mother. After Rio being his only teacher for centuries, Agatha finally took her place. The second role model in his life to follow.

    Safe to say, their lives were better than ever. Especially Agatha’s. She didn’t know what true happiness, love, safety felt like. Not just the home she lived in. It wasn’t only her wife who loved her to pieces. How she was spoiled beyond words. Also how her son carried not only the same face as his mother, but traits of her. Excitement for adventures, craved to learn everything understand the sun, even carried the same dagger as she did and-

    Dagger?

    The record scratched, a freeze frame straight out of a movie happened as Agatha registered the item he had forgotten. Her eyes widened, anger flushed through her like a storm that sped at the speed of light.

    “I forgot my dagger. Hang on, I'll be right back!”

    The first few seconds were cute. Nicholas was forgetful just as Agatha was. She had slipped into bliss of realizing how fortunate, amazing, incredible her life had become. And then the realization struck her not once, but twice-

    ..A dagger?

    “Got it!” He exclaimed, flourishing the handle in a twirl just like his mother taught him. “Alright. On we go-”

    “Rio!”

    Agatha's voice screamed throughout the forest for miles. Certain to scare off all wildlife and maybe even break a few trees in its path. Her son jumped out of her way as she stormed past him. The front door of the cottage instantly opened to reveal Rio filled with concern.

    “What is it? What's wro-”

    “You gave my son A WEAPON?!”

    Rio stared, looking up at Nicky who wore an apologetic smile. Then back to her wife only a few steps away, ready to beat the living shit out of her right there. She swallowed, held the door a bit tighter with a shake of her head.

    “My love, he's already dead. There's no need to worry about his safety. I-”

    Without a missing beat, Rio felt her body pushed back into the house. Agatha had her collar tightly firm in her hold. The door slammed shut, locking their son out and leaving him dumbfounded behind. Only to hear the muffled sounds of arguing with shuffled furniture behind.

    Good luck, Mother.

    Nicky rolled his eyes, unfazed by their theatrics. Though he did wish Rio a bit of luck regardless. He fiddled with the shiny dagger, rocking back and forth on his feet as he waited for his mother’s to get through their tantrum.

    Sharon, the next door neighbour, walked up with a basket of cookies freshly baked and ready to share. Her smile was radiant, but fell when Rio was tossed through the living room window. Her body collapsed on the ground before being pulled back in by the purple magic Agatha casted.

    Nicky sighed, offering a little smile. “Morning, Mrs Davis”

    She forced a worried smile with a little nod. “Just wanted to bring some cookies over, but..seems uh, your mothers are busy?”

    He held up his dagger. “I shouldn't have shown my mother this”

    “Oh” She nodded. “Probably not, honey. No..”

    Agatha this time was the one to be tossed through the front door, rolling across the pathway until the gust of wind snapped her back inside. Sharon gripped her basket, shaking a bit as she asked,

    “Care to join me for a late breakfast?”

    Nicky shrugged with a little smile. “That'd be nice”

    The two walked off together, leaving the storm his mothers were creating behind. Nicky focused on the dagger with a little twirl, not worried he was the cause of the fight. As time went on, he learned the two would always be deeply in love. They'd more than likely be fine by nightfall and back to their usual routine.

    So, instead of worry and sadness he once felt. A little smile grew on his lips, not listening to Sharon's ramble beside him. His mind too busy thinking softly to himself,

    Yeah. Mom's home.

    Sometimes it still didn’t feel real, but as twisted as it sounded- His mother’s being their dramatic selves made it real. This wasn’t the first time he witnessed a fight like the one behind. The recent one was a few weeks ago when another woman flirted with Agatha. Rio lost her mind over that.

    “Oh and sweetie,” Sharon snapped him out of his mind. She nudged his side before stepping up onto the porch. “Maybe hold off for now about telling your mom’s about that little girlfriend of yours” She winked.

    Sharon turned with a hum as she stepped through the screen door. Nicky paused, cheeks shading a dark red while realizing his relationship had been busted.

    Guess sneaking her through the door at night wasn’t secretive..

    And the woman ahead, setting up plates on the table for them both was right. As chaos echoed in the air next door, evil laughter and glass shattering more and more-

    “I mean, you’ll have to tell them eventually, but..”

    “Maybe I should have them smoke first” He suggested.

    “Or those uh, gummies! Yes, those things. The ones your mother gave me way back when?

    Oh! I had never felt so relaxed before. Actually, can you bring me a few more?” She whispered at the end.

    Nicky chuckled as he sat down with a nod. “As long as you won’t tell them about her?”

    She grinned wide. “Only if you tell me all about her..then, we have an agreement”

    He nodded, about to start when they heard on the sidewalk out front.

    “AND YOU FLIRTED BACK!”

    “OH MY GOD, THIS WAS WEEKS AGO!”

    “FLIRTING AND GIVING OUR SON A WEAPON, RIO! YOU’RE LUCKY YOU’RE HOT!”

    Nicky snickered, shaking his head as Sharon gasped a strong,

    “Those two are so!” She shook her head. “How you are their son is beyond me. You are such a saint considering those are your parents”

    He smiled, thanking her as she put down a cup of coffee for him.

    “Yeah..” He agreed, turning to glance outside the window at the two tackling each other through their own twisted laughter. “They’re insane, but..I lucked out, I suppose”

    Sharon finally sat across from him with a sigh.

    “So..spill the deets as the young ones say!”

    Nicky waited until the two were out of sight again. His heart softened, smile gentle as he could only think to himself,

    Hopefully I have a love like those idiots..
