Once she knows that it’s not her fault that Lena’s avoiding her, that she hasn’t done anything wrong, she’ll be able to be happy again.

Lena would give anything to make Kara happy.

I’m in love with you, Kara, and I totally get it if you don’t feel the same way, but here’s my heart, so do what you will with it.

No.

Hey, sorry for avoiding you and also putting everyone in great physical danger, it’s just that you’re the love of my life and I’m pathetic.

Also no.

She has to do this. She’s going to do this.

Too quick.

She waits a lot longer than five minutes.

Kara.

Kara, 9:43pm: are you okay?

Kara, 9:43pm: i tried to give u your space and let you come in when you’re ready but you’ve been in your car outside my apartment for over an hour and i’m getting really worried. please tell me what’s wrong?

Kara, 9:44pm: lena please come in

Kara, 9:44pm: i can wait for u in the lobby if you want? tell me what i can do to make this better

Kara, 9:45pm: i know u can see me!

Kara, 9:45pm: please tell me how i can make this easier for you?

Lena shakes her head, unable to find any words and knowing full well that Kara will be able to see the action clearly even through the pouring rain and the distance between them. She doesn’t think she can do this.

Kara, 9:46pm: lena please come in

Lena looks down at her phone, reads the message, and doesn’t respond – via text message or physically. She just stares down at it, unmoving, hoping that Kara will take the hint that she can’t do this right now.

Kara, 9:46pm: ok i guess i have no choice then

“Kara…”

“Are you sick? Are you upset with me?”

“Kara –”

“Did I do something wrong? Whatever it is, Lena, I’m sorry, but if you can just tell me why you’ve been avoiding me I can fix it, okay? It’s killing me to have this distance between us and I miss you so much and I just can’t –”

Lately it feels like Kara’s been holding back when she touches her, but that is definitely not the case now.

No, now Kara is holding her flush against her body, warm capable hands wrapped around her waist to keep her right where she wants her and an eager tongue licking along the seam of her lips asking for permission to enter. Lena obliges immediately, moaning into the kiss and bringing her arms up to wrap around Kara’s neck in return, which Kara seems to enjoy based on her answering groan of approval.

Lena thinks she’d be entirely content to stay like this forever, pressed tight against Kara and showing her how much she loves her in the best way she knows. However, Kara pulls away after a bit – a minute? An eternity? Who’s to say? – and Lena groans at the loss, pushing up on her toes to chase Kara’s lips.

The Kryptonian holds firm, though. “Baby, you’re shaking,” she rasps, and Lena shakes her head.

“It’s fine, I’m fine, it’s just the rain. Kiss me.” She tugs on Kara’s shoulders again to bring her lips back down, and for the split second that she allows it, Lena feels something inside her go slack with relief. It doesn’t last long, though, because then Kara pulls away again with a start and Lena whines.

It takes Lena a moment to figure out what the hell Kara is saying, but she goes suddenly and immediately red the second she does.

Because, shit. Kara’s right. It isn’t raining anymore – clear skies and a bright sun shining down on them as if nothing had happened at all – and that means there’s only one explanation as to what happened.

“Only you could accidentally impact every single person in this city.”

“This time?”

Lena freezes, realizing just a second too late that she’s incriminated herself. “Um… no?”

“Oh my gosh – you set the Kelex on fire because of me, didn’t you?”

Lena half wants to deny it, but Kara is looking at her with amusement and something dangerously close to love in those blue eyes, and she just can’t. Instead she tucks her head into Kara’s chest and mumbles, “Guilty.”

“Lena!” Kara laughs, wrapping strong arms around her so that Lena can feel the rumble of laughter all the way down to her bones.

“It’s not my fault!” she cries again, pulling away ever so slightly so she can look Kara in the eyes. “You’re so pretty and I knew that stupid guy was going to flirt with you at Noonan’s and I just got so mad and – I’m only human, Kara!”

“You’re so cute,” Kara says simply.

Lena flushes, suddenly feeling very warm. Usually she’s much more eloquent, but all she can think to say now is, “So is that a yes on the date?”

Kara rolls her eyes playfully, like it’s ridiculous that Lena would even ask, but nods anyway.

“That’s a yes on forever.”
