Hot line, but it's a thought line!” which Agatha rolled her eyes over, but ended up calling it that regardless) had been used. Clearly Agatha had been utilizing it, but Rio forced herself not to. Somehow managed to force them out even if it was just through her mind. The words finally had been said for Agatha to hear.

Even if she had been the one ready to walk away, disappear from it, keep true to their deal to never see Agatha once again- She couldn’t.
