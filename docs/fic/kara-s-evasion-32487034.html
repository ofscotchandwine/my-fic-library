Ottis never once took his eyes off her, and it wouldn't have mattered if he had. Lena remained crouched longer, trying to draw him closer to her. 

"What, are you draining the entire Mediterranean Sea over there? What's taking so long, dammit?" 

Just as Ottis was within range, Lena striked, lashing the leather belt's buckle at his face and straight into his eye. The distraction was enough for Lena to jump him and with two powerful kicks to the groin and a punch to the throat, Ottis was down on his knees and clutching at his injured eye. Lena quickly looped the belt around his mouth and secured it behind his head, successfully gagging him. Then she took the hefty semi-automatic in one hand and pressed it into Ottis's back, the message loud and clear. 

"One wrong move and I blow your spleen out. Now move." Lena yanked her new prisoner to his feet and pushed them ahead. Ottis mumbled inaudible protests but kept moving nonetheless. 

"About damn time. I was just about to—" 

"Drop the gun and free the hostages" Lena tightened her hold on Ottis. 

"What is this?" Liberty frowned deeply. 

"I take it you're not deaf. I said for you to drop the gun and free the hostages. Quickly now." 

Liberty hesitated, and he moved to draw his gun forward but Lena was quicker than him. She lowered Ottis's gun and shot him clean in the foot without taking her eyes off Liberty. Ottis screamed under his belt gag. 

"Next one will go through what little is left of his brain. Be smarter than him and drop the gun and free the hostages." Lena waited, and she could see Liberty was fighting hard to follow her instructions. She aimed the gun at Ottis's head, who was screaming both from pain, terror and anger. Liberty finally relented. He threw his weapon to the ground and undid all the hostages' bonds. 

"Take everyone back to safety. I'll handle them," Lena said to Vela Rossa. 

"Inesha, go with everyone else now, hurry and make sure everyone is safe," Vela Rossa nodded to one of the newly-freed hostages and looked at Lena, "Lena and I will be back soon." 

Lena wanted to argue. After all, she took full responsibility for everything that had happened so far, but her time with Vela Rossa had taught her that the older woman was anything and everything but a runner. Lena nodded to her mentor in appreciation.

Vela Rossa picked up Liberty's gun and held him in position. 

"You're making a huge mistake Lena," Liberty said, now on his knees. 

"The only mistake made here was you underestimating the power of a pissed off Luthor." 

Ottis made an attempt to yank himself away from Lena, and she'd just about had enough of him, so she got a better grip on the gun in her hand and hit it at the back of his head, immediately knocking him unconscious. 

Vela Rossa went to gather some of the rope that had bound the escaped hostages and tied Ottis up. Just then, the sound of static could be heard, and Lena drew the weapon at Liberty just as a man's voice came from the radio attached to his hip. 

"Boss, the alien has just landed. Should we deploy—aaaah!" 

Lena knew very well that Kara had been summoned, she even knew that the hero would most likely already be en route to the island as soon as she'd seen the video Liberty sent her. Some part of Lena had still hoped that Kara wouldn't come. That she'd choose to send someone else. Anything else that didn't involve her being physically present.

But now as she listened to the repeated grunts, shouts and moans of pain where Kara was clearly taking down the men Liberty had posted around the island, Lena's heart sped up in anticipation. 

Kara came into view like a thunderbolt. One second Lena could hear the hero fighting her foes, the next she was staring at her face, golden strands of her hair matted to her face in sweat that wasn't supposed to be there. Fists balled tightly at her sides. Dressed in sleeping pants and a long sleeve shirt with a comical dozing cloud sown into its chest. Barefoot. 

Had she just rolled out of bed and flown here? 

"Lena." She said her name like it was a blessing, and Lena hated that she'd noticed how soft it was. 

Kara arrived looking angry, a goddess scorned by mortals way beneath her. And then her eyes found Lena, and like water draining out of cloth, her anger became many things that Lena didn't want to name. 

Lena was frozen on the spot, and even though she wanted to move her limbs, she couldn't. She just watched as Kara took slow steps towards her. 

Bang! 

It took Lena all of five seconds to assess what had just happened. A gun was fired, Liberty was armed again, Kara stopped dead in her tracks, and her white shirt, which was already dirty from her previous tussles, was now staining red. And underneath it, green veins began spreading around her abdomen. 

Lena moved fast enough to catch Kara just as she fell on her side. Vela Rossa disarmed Liberty before he could take another shot at either Kara or Lena. 

"Kara? Kara, can you hear me? Stay with me okay? Just stay with me." Even as Lena tried not to cry, she could feel her cheeks become wet. Maybe it was just the light drizzle that had begun pouring from the sky or it was just Lena's irrational fear that was shaking her to her core. She didn't know anymore, and she didn't care. 

Kara shook in her arms, moaning in pain and still somehow trying to speak. 

"Lena please. I'm…I'm sorry. Are you hurt? I—I came…as soon as…" 

"Stop it. Stop talking and save your strength, okay? You're gonna be okay. You're gonna be okay." 

Whether that reassurance was meant for Kara or for herself, Lena didn't know. She just wanted Kara to be safe, she needed her to be safe. Everything else between them, the anger, the hurt, the uncertainty. The unresolved feelings. All that was thrown to the back of her mind, monetarily forgotten. 

"Vela, please help me! Help me carry her. She needs help." 

Lena's mentor didn't hesitate, nor did she ask questions that Lena was sure she had. After securing Liberty's bonds and checking him for any other weapons, Vela Rossa came to help Lena carry Kara back to the settlement. 

"Paulo will be able to help. She is greatly skilled in gun-inflicted wounds," Vela Rossa said as she tucked Kara's arm over her shoulder and lifted her up, grunting slightly with effort. 

Lena wanted to explain that this was no ordinary gunshot wound, that it was no ordinary bullet at all. But Kara's growing cries spurred her into a rush. 

 

First Kara needed her help, long anticipated confrontations could wait. 

 

 

"I'll be damned if I let my sister spend another second in this place. I'm leaving with her right now."

"Alex, she is still injured. Let her rest."

"What she needs is proper and better care, one that she can only find back home, which is where I'm taking her right now.” 

“Can you at least let her rest for the day? She’s too weak to travel in this condition.”

“Well she wouldn’t be in this condition if it weren’t for your lunatic horde of a family.” 

 Lena was momentarily stunned. There had always been some form of contempt between her and Alex, and while Kara had been more open to seeing Lena as someone more than just a Luthor, the same luxury wasn't afforded to her by the older Danvers however. Even after their relationship had gotten somewhat better before Kara came clean in Kaznia, Lena knew that Alex still held on to some of her mistrust for her. 

And she hated how self-righteous it made Alex seem. 

"You aren't the only one who cares about her Alex." Lena stepped closer to the other woman. 

"Sure didn't seem like it for the past nine months you abandoned her."

"Lena?" 

Kara's voice, sounding small and almost inaudible, stopped Lena from giving the older Danvers a less-than-appropriate piece of her mind. Alex was quick to rush over to her sister, kneeling by her mattress and taking her hand. Lena lingered back a while, relief flooding her entire body at seeing the Kryptonian finally regain consciousness after several hours. 

"Alex? You're here. How did you…" 

"That doesn't matter now. I'm here for you and I'm going to take you back home." Alex said the last part particularly louder than the rest of her words, no doubt meant for Lena. 

"Lena, are you okay? Did they hurt you in any—ah!" Kara tried to sit up right but her still fresh wound protested painfully. Lena fought desperately against giving in to the urge to go to Kara's side.

She'd been with Kara from the moment she and Vela Rossa carried Kara into the settlement where Paulo was rushed into Lena's hut, equipment ready for use. Lena refused to leave Kara's side, both out of fear for her life and the need for protecting the woman who after all this time was still a leech on her heart. She flinched every single time at Kara's grunts and yells of pain at every poke, stab and stitch dealt by Paulo's patient hands,  who worked surprisingly fast and well despite the lack of anesthesia and all around professional medical necessities.

Just as Vela Rossa had done, Paulo didn't question Lena about why the bullet she dug out of Kara was glowing green or why it was fashioned in a way that made it look like a sharp-edged propeller. According to Paulo, Kara had been lucky to survive, as the bullet seemed specially designed to tear through her organs the more she was moving around.

She would have died from the rapid internal bleeding within hours, and it was best for her to remain in place to let her recover her strength. 

It was an instruction Lena was determined to see through, and then Alex had shown up on the island, ready to upend the recuperating hero. 

Lena actually wanted to throttle the damn woman. 

"I'm going to let you two talk," Lena said, realizing God knew how many moments later that she'd been standing frozen in place just looking at Kara. The Kryptonian called out her name again, and Alex turned around, a glare fixed on her face and one that said " well don't just stand there" 

So Lena excused herself and left, ignoring the call of her name once again and how her heart lurched at the soft sound of it. 

 

 

Outside was no better. Sure, the air was cooler, and the drizzle from before had now begun pouring down as thick heavy drops of rain. Lena didn't even bother running to some form of shelter, just let her entire body be soaked from head to toe as she walked slowly from her hut to the training arena. It was already growing muddy, and Lena's boots were no longer entirely black by the time she reached the wooden barracks and sat down in the rows of stools. 

"Come to seek some quiet? If so then I'm afraid you'll have to tolerate my smoking." 

Lena was surprised by her mentor's presence, but she didn't flinch. 

"Eh, I can handle it. I mean, it's nowhere near as bad as everything else that's happened in the past twenty four hours." Lena chuckled dryly. 

"How is she?" 

Lena sighed. 

"Better than when we brought her. I think she's only going to recover from here on out." 

"And will you ?" 

Lena was used to the older woman's cryptic speaking, and most of the time, it was to teach her something that was of help to her journey towards self-reliance. She never really questioned any of it, because she always understood what was really meant underneath it all, yet now she was purely puzzled. 

Vela Rossa seemed to sense her confusion, because she chuckled to herself and took a pull from her pipe. 

"One of the first things you told me when you first arrived here was that you were running. You didn't say from what or from whom, but there was something about the sadness that shined in your eyes whenever you were looking at the couples of our home that made me realize the answer to my unasked question. And today, seeing the way you ran to that girl, how you held on to her and refused to leave her side. I've never seen you look more afraid and alive. I understand now. Or at least I think I do."

At least someone understood her this well. 

Lena was tired of having to explain herself. Her feelings, her actions, her fears, her passions. She was tired of having to put herself on the spot and wind up paying for that mistake one way or another with the fragile pieces of her heart. She loved and trusted Kara, and Kara lied to her in return, even if she'd admitted that she loved Lena too. But how was Lena supposed to trust that when Kara had lied to her about who she really was? Was it Kara who loved her or was it Supergirl? Or did neither even love her for real? 

Asking questions would only bring Lena more confusion and heartache, and what better way to heal than to leave it all behind and teach herself to stuff every single annoying emotion into a metal box that she would never open again?

It was all going according to plan, that was until Kara showed up, threw that plan to total royal shit and reminded Lena just how little progress she'd made in her race to detach herself from Kara Danvers and Supergirl as a package. 

"I am madly in love with someone that I'm supposed to hate and it's driving me crazy, Vela." 

"And why do you say you're supposed to hate her?" 

"Because she betrayed my trust. She broke my heart with her lies and I…" 

"Can't bring yourself to forgive her?" 

Lena bit her tongue. She'd never allowed herself to entertain the thought because she couldn't simply let Kara get off so easily. She had to punish Kara somehow, and of course Lena would never hurt the Kryptonian in any physical sense, so an emotional target was her next best option. 

Kara had to feel the pain that Lena did. 

"I don't want to forgive her," Lena whispered. 

"You're afraid of something if you do. What is it?" 

Lena paused for a moment. Some small part of her resented how easily Vela Rossa was reading her like an open book. She hadn't spent all the years of her life learning to shield herself behind impenetrable walls for some wise old lady to simply walk through that wall and pick her apart piece by piece. 

Then again, the bigger part of herself could only be grateful for the woman's counsel and comfort. 

"That my love won't be enough to fix the damage dealt by all the secrecy. I mean look at me, I attract trouble everywhere I go and even when I'm trying to hide away from the world, it still manages to find me." 

Vela Rossa nodded and remained quiet for a while, contemplating. 

"The way I see it, you only have two options. A world of uncertainty with the exception of how much you love that girl, or a world of doubts and regrets about mistakes made and chances missed. There is no inbetween, and you can't grab hold of both worlds, Lena. How much time you take to decide is up to you, but you have to decide eventually." 

Lena hated how easily words could make her cry, especially when she tried to tell herself she wouldn't cry. She looked out at the empty arena, where she had won and lost countless sparring matches with some of the island's most formidable fighters. 

"You're allowed to wipe them away, you know. I'm not afraid of emotions like you are," Vela Rossa said with a humorous smile. 

"Emotions are scarier than spiders, and between the two of us, who's afraid of the latter again?," Lena teased, feeling at slight ease once again. 

"Ironic really, considering that I live on an island infested with them."

Both women laugh, and like many of their conversations before then, this one dissolved into a comfortable silence. 

And then one of the local boys ran up to them, nearly tripping on his feet as he reached Lena and Vela Rossa. 

"Mama Rossa, Miss Lena. There's an emergency. I ran with word from the other boys sent out to scout the shores of the island to check for any strange movement. We spotted another man arriving on land with some strange metal suit device that made him fly. He was touching his ear and talking to himself a lot but he was pretending to be talking to his mother. He said he's here for something called the Kryptonian." 

The boy sped through his explanation as quickly as he'd been running moments ago. 

"Where is he now?," Vela Rossa asked. 

"Passed the animal farm when I left the others. I've already warned everyone else and they're getting ready." 

Vela Rossa nodded to the boy and dismissed him. 

"Vela, you need to get yourself and everyone else to safety, now," Lena said with urgency, already having turned towards the arena's exit. 

"I'm not letting you fight this alone Lena," Vela Rossa protested. 

"I need you to try, because I know that man, and he's worse than the two we have locked up in the dungeons. I know exactly what he wants, and I'll be damned if I let him get anywhere near her. Please, go." 

Vela Rossa was tied, wanting to follow Lena and wanting to go to defend her people. In the end, she made a choice. 

"Be careful little flower." Vela Rossa hugged Lena and kissed her forehead.

"I will."

Lena didn't turn her back or slow down on her way back to the settlement, and she only stopped to arm herself with a gift that Vela Rossa had given her almost a month ago when she'd bested the older woman in practice combat. 

 

Lena passed by her hut, and she hesitated for just a little bit, wanting to go inside and look at Kara who would no doubt be sleeping by now with Alex watching over her like a hawk. She was in safe hands. 

 

Now Lena had to make sure that both sisters and everyone else on the island would be safe, for good. 

She was going to kill Lex once and for all. 

 

 

Spotting Lex wasn't hard. Lena didn't even walk for longer than ten minutes before she heard him loudly proclaim his presence for anyone and everyone to hear, making demands and spewing his typical threats.

There was a time when Lena would have frantically searched for a way to avoid confronting her brother, knowing from first hand experience just how ruthless he could be when he was challenged. She spent her entire life in his shadow, growing big but remaining small enough that she wouldn't ever hinder him in any way. 

But that was before she felt what it was like to have your heart broken by someone you love without limit and still somehow want to protect them despite the pain caused. 

It took Lena nine months of denial and less than an hour of acceptance before finally realizing that she would always love Kara. No matter how much she forced herself to stay away from the hero, she would never be able to stamp out how much Kara meant to her, especially where her safety was involved. And when that safety was threatened by anyone, come hell or high waters, Lena would defend Kara to the very bitter end. 

So as the rain beat down heavier than before, Lena tightened the grip on her sword and ran faster towards Lex, stopping only when his form finally came into view. 

"There you are little sis. Good to see you," Lex said with a feigned sincere smile. 

"You aren't welcome here Lex. Leave now, " Lena said calmly. 

"No problem. I'll be on my way as soon as I retrieve you and the Kryptonian. I see you're here now, so…" 

Lena clenched her jaw. 

"You're leaving here exactly the way you came. With nothing." 

Lex was quiet for a while, eyeing Lena like she was a speck of dust on his shiny, new and expensive shoes. And then he burst out in laughter. 

"Did you hit your head several times while you were hiding on this little island or what? What makes you think you're allowed to talk to me that way?," Lex sneered. "And anyway, where are those incompetent fools I sent to retrieve you and trap Supergirl? Don't tell me you killed them with that." 

"I'm not letting you anywhere near her."Lena was more than tired of talking. She crouched in position and raised her sword, an invitation for her brother now-turned mortal enemy. Lex didn't move, and as he continued to stare his sister down, the anger beginning to settle on his face, Lena heard rustling from behind her. 

"Neither am I." Alex came to stand by Lena's side, and it threw her off mental balance for just a moment. If Alex was here, then…

"Is she—" 

"She's safe for now. You need me here, so I've got your back. No matter what." 

Alex sparred her a curt nod and Lena quite literally wanted to throw her arms around the woman. It was almost as if Lena could see, could feel, all of the tension that was always simmering between the two of them float up and away into the sun, never to be seen again. 

Of course this was just an exaggeration of Lena's relief at seeing Alex, but then again, Kara hadn't hesitated to come for Lena even despite the fact that she'd virtually disappeared on her intentionally, without much explanation. 

Maybe there was hope that her and Alex could learn to mend things. 

"Two against one? Wow, you ladies really have it out against me. Fine then, I suppose it's only fair that I level the playing field." 

Lex, with a devious smile on his face, outstretched his arms letting the metal of his Lexo Suit materialize all over his body and caccooning him in a guard of armour, power source glowing green in the middle of his chest. 

Kryptonite. 

We have to keep him far away from Kara. 

Lena trusted that Alex was thinking the same thing, and apparently she actually was because she also geared up, unsheathing a device that Lena would have loved to inspect more where she not pressed for time, and as quickly as Lex had suited up, Alex was now donned in a black and blue full-body combat suit, most of her face masked. Lena didn't even see where the woman had withdrawn her two batons from. 

"A lot has happened while you were gone," Alex said when she sensed Lena's confusion. 

Alex was a badass within her own right, but in that moment, Lena was looking at a determined goddess hell bent on protecting her sister. 

Lena immediately knew she liked this side of Alex. 

In his true impatient nature, Lex roared before finally charging at him, Alex running as well to meet him halfway. Alex's suit must have been a source of strength and speed enhancement as well because she was able to dodge many of Lex's powerful kicks and punches, landing a few of her own as well. Lena was a shadow between the two, using the opportunity Alex provided her to find a weak spot in his suit. Her sword was mostly just a guard against his occasional swipes toward her face, because unlike with Alex, he wasn't actually trying to harm Lena physically. 

And that was already a mistake on his part. 

Alex took a rather strong kick to the midriff and yelled out in pain, allowing Lex to clamp his claws around her neck and throwing her a great distance ahead. Far beyond just furious now, Lena struck at her brother with a deafening yell. Lex turned around to face her, a look on his face like he was just now noticing her presence even though he'd been fighting her too. 

"Disarm him Lena. He's nothing without his suit," Alex said from behind Lex, having gotten back up but swaying slightly on her feet and now sporting a bleeding nose. 

"You've been stupid for all your life but I never would have guessed you'd go this far. Running away like a little child because of some alien lying to you about who she really is?" Lex chuckled, like this was the most hilarious comedy skit he'd witnessed. 

"God, Lena, when will you realize that no matter how much you give them they will always end up taking so much more from you. They're all the same, and they must be rooted out from our lives like the parasitic weeds they are. Supergirl has burrowed herself beneath every fiber of your being and it's as pathetic as it is disgusting to watch. My own sister, playing puppet to a Kryptonian. I had half a mind to ship you to the desert and leave you in the hands of bandits if it weren't for Mother's insistence for me to retrieve you. If you give me the Kryptonian I might just forgive this little resistance attempt." 

The good thing about Lex is that in his egoistic mindset, he had the nearly pathological need to berate and belittle his opponents in long, drawn out speeches. And of course because it was Lex, his words hit spots within Lena that she didn't want to address.

Before now, she would have hung her head in shame, looking down at her feet and essentially, down at her own self because of how small Lex's venomous words made her feel. 

But now Lena never felt more powerful. More sure or herself, of her steps and actions. It wasn't even just because of her need to protect the woman she loved but still somehow wanted to hate, but because she now no longer defined her strength by whether or not she pleased the Luthors. 

"I really hate whenever you try to tell me what to do," Lena said. 

Lex took this as another sign of defiance, and like the madman he was, charged at his sister. 

Lena had taken the grace period given while Lex was rambling to inspect and locate where his suit's power source could be. And when she finally found it—at his back of all places—she targeted it with energized purpose. 

Lena let herself be tackled, and when she was pinned to the ground, her sword fell from her hand. Lex in his rage-induced trance closed his hands around Lena's neck tightly, gradually slowing her breathing. 

"Backside, now!," Lena directed Alex, who didn't hesitate for even a second. Lex grunted when Alex's bullet hit its target. The suit began malfunctioning, electric sparks flying all around and some metal plates even beginning to crack. The green light from his chest began flickering like a dying light before shutting off entirely. Lex screamed and frantically pressed what was definitely the emergency retractor, fearing getting fried alive by his now damaged suit. 

Lena quickly rolled out from underneath Lex and got back on her feet, grabbing hold of her sword once again. She pressed it into her brother's back, who was now in his regular harmless suit. Alex was standing by her side once again, a hand held over her side where Lex had kicked her earlier. 

"You've lost," Lena said, pressing the tip of the weapon further. 

"Is that what you think?," Lex sighed. 

"No. It's what I know." 

He began to chuckle, and then that chuckle turned into a full blown laughing fit. This was what Lena hated most about him. Somehow he always saw himself above everything and everyone, his own family included. 

Lex got up slowly and turned to face Lena, his hands in the air. 

"Is this meant to frighten me, little sister? Are you really going to stab me? Your own brother? The brother you've grown to love as a child and hate as a man."

Lex lowered his hands and closed one over the blade poised at his chest. "Do it then." 

Lena stared him in the eye, unblinking even as the rain continued to fall from the sky. 

"You can end this right here. This is your chance Lena. Just a little push and it's over. Do it."

Lena paused, remembering things she wished she could forget. 

Lionel brings five year old Lena home and Lex gives her a kind smile. 

"Do it Lena."

Lena trips, scrapes her knee and Lex promises not only to not tell Lionel or Lillian, but he cleans the small wound and gives her candy. 

"Do it." 

"I'm always going to be your brother Lena. And I love you no matter what." Lex hugs Lena as she cries onto his shoulder. 

"I said do it!" 

Lena gave her sword one strong push and she instantly felt as the metal tore its way through flesh, slicing apart the organ beneath and protruding from the other side of her brother's body. Lex didn't so much as scream. Bloody mouth hanging open, he only had enough energy to fall to his knees and whisper his sister's name. 

Lex died, and the last look he could manage was one of surprise. Surprise at being bested, surprise at his life truly coming to an end. And surprise at his sister being the architect of his downfall after all. 

Lena lost all feeling in her knees, and she fell by his side as a fresh batch of tears fell from her eyes. 

Lena wasn't mourning the brother she knew and loved, because that brother had died a very long time ago, killed by the monster that now lay sprawled on the ground dead. She was now mourning the loss of family entirely. 

Maybe she was never even meant to have him as family to begin with. 

Lena felt arms around her shoulders. Alex held onto her tightly, whispering "it's over now, you did it" as she rocked them back and forth. 

 

Lena had just paid a heavy price. A price she was entirely willing to pay over and over again despite its demand of her, if it meant protecting Kara Zor-El.
