Your back burned in protest as you twisted it and you had to use your hands to help drag your legs across the mattress until they fell over the edge and you suddenly felt very unsure of your ability to support yourself. Once she closed the door behind her, you looked at the blonde and patted the space in the small bed behind you. It was easier to ignore the pain when it meant you were able to hold the blonde again and a small price to pay to feel her steady breathing above you.

Instead, she rested her chin on top of your head until you both fell into a peaceful slumber like you had numerous times before.
