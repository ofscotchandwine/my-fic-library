Kara sighs, not looking forward to confirming their worst suspicions, but on balance, at least they’ve foiled the plot and Edge won’t get away with it unscathed.

“We caught four men trying to dump the chromium into the inlet tonight,” Kara explains.  “Morgan Edge hired them to frame you and L-Corp.”  Kara tells Lena the rest and to her credit, the CEO listens patiently.

“Otis was head of security at Luthor Corp under Lex,” Lena informs Kara.

“Do you think Lex is involved in this somehow?  Is he part of Cadmus?  I thought that was more Lilian’s thing,” Kara thinks out loud.

“Lillian only got involved with Cadmus after Lex was arrested.  I doubt he’s involved directly.  Besides, you know his obsession centers on Kryptonians—he wouldn’t have the patience for Cadmus’ broader objectives.  But we can certainly ask my mother.”

“I knew it was only a matter of time before Lillian showed up at L-Corp,” Lena admits.  “So, with Kendra and Brainy’s help, I laid a trap.  Tonight, she walked into it,” Lena can’t help but gloat.

Kara can’t help but worry.  Lillian Luthor is dangerous, and she doesn’t want her anywhere near Lena.  She has a million questions and conflicting feelings—pride, fear, concern.  “Where is she?” she finally settles on asking.

“In a pocket dimension Brainy helped me create,” Lena answers simply.

Kara’s not sure whether to be horrified or amazed.  “She’s perfectly comfortable and safe,” Lena says flippantly, “which is more than she ever did for me.”
