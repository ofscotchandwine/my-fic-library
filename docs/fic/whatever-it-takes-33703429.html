Whatever It Takes

Author's Note:

Hi everyone, this has been rolling around in my brain for a while now and I just needed to get it out. To be honest, I watched most of season 5b, waiting for something like this to happen, and was disappointed (though not surprised) that they didn't go this route... shrug... Anyway, hope you enjoy!

It took Kara a little while to realize she wasn’t dreaming, that the KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK sound was actually pounding on her door. It was quite loud, quite persistent, and definitely someone in trouble because it was 3 in the morning. As her mind finally cast away the deeper sleep she was in and came to consciousness to register the knocking, her mind immediately began running through who could be pounding at her door at this hour in such a way, clearly in need of aid. Alex, Nia, Brainy, J’onn, Kelly. Any one of them could be injured or in trouble. Or maybe it was her neighbor, maybe something happened with their dog again.

 

She used her superspeed to rush to the door and swing it open without hesitation, never giving it a second thought, not stopping to use any other powers to see who might be at her door. She never for once, for even a millisecond, thought she would find Lena Luthor, slumped against the doorframe, one hand clutching her abdomen and the other mid-knock.

 

Kara’s eyes go wide as she gasps and stares, stunned immediately into silence as her brain seems to stop working.

 

Panting, Lena’s arm falls to her side as she winces in pain. “Thank god. I was starting to think you weren’t home.” Kara continues to stare, still in bewildered silence. “Here,” Lena says, thrusting her hand back out, “Take this.” She shoves her hand at Kara’s chest.

 

This action finally spurs Kara into action as her hand automatically reacts, reaching up, grasping Lena’s hand in her own. It’s the first time they’ve touched in six months. Since… since before Lena locked her in the Fortress of Solitude and stole Myriad. Since before Kara called her a villain.

 

When Kara looks down, her eyes widen further as she realizes Lena’s hand is tinted red in blood. This realization causing Kara to finally speak. “Lena, what happened?”

 

Lena attempts to stand up straight, but succeeds in only grimacing more, unable to hide the pain. “It’s… nothing… to concern yourself with. Just… take the drive, Kara. It has everything on it that you need to stop Lex… and Leviathan.”

 

Lex is working with Leviathan. That’s new information. But none of this makes sense. Lena chose her side. She chose to work with her brother over Kara.

 

“I don’t understand,” Kara says plainly.

 

Lena’s eye close for a few seconds as her hand grasps the doorframe harder, her knuckles turning white from the intensity of her grasp. Her eyes shoot back open, and while they have an intent look in them, they also now have a slight haze about them. She takes longer to respond to Kara, clearly growing closer and closer to unconsciousness. “Kara… please… just… just take the damn drive… I… I swear this… this isn’t a trick… or a trap…”

 

Kara finally takes it from Lena’s hand and watches as the hand falls back to Lena’s side, practically useless. Kara does a quick scan using her x-ray vision. “Lena, your shoulder…” she says, seeing the tendons barely hanging together, realizing Lena must be in intense pain. Kara then continues to use her x-ray vision to quickly scan down the rest of her body, gasping when she sees her abdomen. “Oh, my Rao! Lena, you’ve been shot!”

 

Lena chuckles once, dryly. “Yes darling… I am aware.”

 

Kara’s eyes would pop out of her head if they could. “Lena… you… you need to go to a hospital, let me take you there.”

 

Lena shakes her head once as her eyes close again. They stay closed this time, as she keeps talking. “Can’t… go… He’ll… find me…”

 

Kara shakes her head as she realizes the severity of the situation and suddenly reaches her arms out. But Lena feels her hands and pushes her back with what little strength she has left.

 

“Kara… don’t…” Lena finally finds the strength to open her eyes again to find Kara giving her a very sad and concerned look.

 

“Lena, please, let me help you.”

 

Lena shakes her head. “I… I have… to go… You… use the drive… you can stop them…” Lena tries to turn, still using the doorframe for support as she takes a step away.

 

“Lena,” Kara says more vehemently, “Lena, stop.”

 

“I got this… Kara…” Lena says, as she takes another step, and immediately slides to the ground.

 

“Dammit, Lena,” Kara says as she rushes to her side. “I’m going to pick you up and take you somewhere you can get help, ok?”

 

Lena’s head lulls to the side, her eyes half open, looking up at Kara. “You… you shouldn’t help me… after what I did.”

 

“Nope. We aren’t having that discussion right now Lena. We can have it when you are all better,” Kara says as she shakes her head, her heart racing at the thought of losing her best friend in such a permanent capacity.

 

“I… I’m sorry for pushing you away… Had to do it.” Lena speaks with each out breath, her grasp on consciousness quickly fading.

 

“What?” Kara says as her hand gently brushes some of Lena’s dark hair out of her face, tucking it behind her ear.

 

Lena’s lips turn up in an attempt to smile when she feels Kara’s gentle touch. “Had to… make Lex think…” With the last of her breath, Lena’s head falls to her chest as she blacks out.

 

Kara has no idea what that means but knows that she needs Lena to survive so they can have a conversation about this. She is gentle as she scoops Lena into her arms and makes her way back into her apartment and through her window, out into the crisp night air. She flies directly to the Tower, hoping that they have what they need to treat Lena’s wounds.

 

As she lands, she alerts J’onn, who takes only a few moments to make his way up. His eyes go wide when he sees Kara placing Lena on one of the beds in the medical room.

 

“J’onn, please,” Kara says when she sees him entering the room.

 

He nods curtly. “What do you need?”

 

“She’s been shot in the stomach area. And there is something wrong with her shoulder,” Kara says quickly.

 

J’onn moves quickly, grabbing supplies from cabinets around the room. “You should call Alex; she has extensive medical training. I’m going to need help with this.”

 

Kara’s eyes go wide at the thought of summoning Alex at this hour, for Lena. Her thoughts must be very loud, because J’onn looks at her and says plainly, “She will understand. Now go, I’ve got this.”

 

The reassurance is enough, and Kara pulls out her phone to call her sister.

 

“Kara? What’s wrong?”

 

“Please come to the Tower. It’s Lena.”

 

“On my way.”

 

Kara stares at her phone, realizing that she didn’t get to explain anything before her sister hung up. But decides that is a problem for another time, as she looks up to find J’onn cutting away Lena’s shirt around her abdomen where the bullet went in.

 

“She has lost a lot of blood, Kara. She is going to need a transfusion. And there is no exit wound, which means we are going to have to find and remove the bullet once Alex gets here.”

 

Kara can do nothing else but nod as she watches J’onn work to prep Lena for Alex’s arrival. She feels like she is watching herself, like time is moving in slow motion. Suddenly, she hears the elevator ding with her superhearing, alerting her to Alex’s presence. Kara knows she should probably exit the room, go out to explain to her sister what she is walking into. But again, she finds herself still rooted in the same spot. She finds herself unable to move, unable to leave Lena.

 

“Kara? J’onn? What the?”

 

J’onn looks from Alex to Kara, waiting for Kara to explain.

 

Kara looks from Lena to J’onn and then slowly turns to Alex who is standing in the doorway. “Alex, please. Lena has been shot.”

 

“What? How? By who? I thought she wasn’t talking to you.”

 

Kara starts to feel exhausted by the questions already. “I’m not sure. She showed up at my door like this. Just help her, please.”

 

Alex opens her mouth again, but then sees J’onn in the background, who gives her the slightest shake of his head. “… Ok.”

 

She makes her way past Kara and over to the bed where Lena lays. “J’onn, what do you need?”

 

-------------------------

 

Kara stands there, silently watching for hours, her arms wrapped around herself. A million scenarios running through her mind of how exactly Lena ended up in this position. And what exactly she could have meant by what she said before she passed out. She ends up spending part of the time deciding exactly what she is going to do to Lex and whoever else was involved in hurting Lena. She tries to stay away from any thoughts of Lena being anything more than injured.

 

Until suddenly, as Alex and J’onn are about to start closing Lena up, when Lena’s heart stops beating. In that moment, Kara’s own heart skyrockets. “No no no, Alex!”

 

“Don’t you do this to me now Lena!” Alex says as her and J’onn shock her chest.

 

It takes two more attempts before they find a rhythm again. When they finally do, Kara sinks to the floor, pulling her knees to her chest, arms wrapped around her legs as the tears free fall down her face.

 

Alex and J’onn finish working on Lena’s abdomen, and J’onn puts her arm in a brace as Alex goes to comfort Kara. Alex gets down next to her sister, wrapping her up in her arms.

 

“Hey, you’re ok, she is ok, I’ve got you.”

 

Kara starts to hyperventilate as her crying turns into outright sobbing. “I… I can’t… I can’t lose her Alex.”

 

“Sh… It’s ok… it’s ok…” Alex continues to console Kara for a while on the floor as J’onn tries to make sure Lena will be comfortable. It takes a while, but eventually Kara cries all that she can, and Alex helps her up and brings her over to a chair next to Lena’s bed. Alex and J’onn then leave the two of them alone, coming back periodically to check on Lena’s vitals, but also to check on Kara as well.

 

-------------------------

 

Kara doesn’t leave Lena’s side, despite Alex and J’onn’s attempts. She outright refuses to leave to eat. And she stays seated, her head lulling to her side or on Lena’s bed when she falls asleep. They decide to try one final time, together.

 

“Kara, Lena wouldn’t want you to do this to yourself,” J’onn said softly, Alex standing next to him.

 

Kara looked up at them, her chin resting on her hands as she laid her head next to Lena, talking to her when no one else was around. They could see the dark circles starting to form under her eyes.

 

“How would you know that? We all abandoned her.” She says flatly, all fight in her gone.

 

“You know that’s not what happened, Kara,” Alex says firmly.

 

Kara sighs and turns away from them, back to Lena. “She should be awake by now. Why isn’t she awake?”

 

Alex shrugs at J’onn, clearly frustrated at her inability to get through to her sister. J’onn nods understandingly and gives her bicep a reassuring squeeze before he walks over to the other side of Lena’s bed.

 

“Everyone is different. But think of it this way. The longer she is asleep, the more her body can focus on healing itself. This isn’t necessarily a bad thing.”

 

Kara moves her eyes to look at J’onn, searching for any hint of a lie in his words. Finding none, she lets out a tired sigh. “I… I don’t want her to wake up alone.”

 

J’onn again nods in his fatherly, understanding manner and is silent for a moment as he thinks over his next words carefully. “What… what if Alex brings you something to eat? And if you do ever want to step away, even just for a few minutes, one of us stays by her side, just in case?”

 

Kara eyes shift as they look from J’onn to Lena and then back again, the internal struggle clear on her face. “I… I suppose that would be ok.”

 

-------------------------

 

It takes Lena three days to wake up. As she starts to come to, the first thing she is aware of, is the voice, Kara’s voice. Lena keeps her eyes closed as she tries to comprehend why she is hearing Kara’s voice. As she does, that is when she realizes that Kara is reading something, out loud. As Lena sits and listens, she quickly becomes enraptured by the tale being told, something about magic fading from the land because of the ways of men, and how it is up to one woman to stop this from happening.

 

Lena listens for a few minutes, intrigued by the story, before her consciousness clears a bit more and it dawns on her that Kara Danvers is reading out loud, specifically to her. There is really no denying it when Kara pauses after reading a short description in the book and then says softly “I never knew Ireland had such a rich history steeped in mythology like this Lena. I’m… I’m sorry I never asked you more about it. I would love to go sometime.”

 

Lena’s heartrate spikes, being addressed directly like this.

 

She hears Kara stand suddenly as the monitor shows the sudden change. “Lena? Oh no, something’s wrong.”

 

Knowing the jig is up, Lena opens her eyes and says softly, voice slightly horse, “You might have to tell me what has happened so far in this book, I’m a bit lost.”

 

She watches Kara’s eyes fill with tears of happiness as she rushes to engulf Lena in a hug. “Oh, thank Rao you’re awake Lena.”

 

Lena winces, her arm still in a brace and her shoulder still very much injured.

 

Kara immediately pulls back. “Oh no! I’m so sorry!”

 

Lena half-smiles. “It’s ok.”

 

Kara falls silent after that as Lena takes in her surroundings. “Where am I?” She finally asks, realizing she most certainly isn’t in a hospital.

 

“Oh… you’re in… what we call the Tower. It’s our secret base of operations,” Kara says with a small but proud smile.

 

Lena nods. “And I take it I have you to thank for that?”

 

Kara’s face turns a light shade of red. “I wasn’t going to let you die, Lena.”

 

Lena lets out a sigh. “Thank you,” she says sincerely. “I can… um…. I can make sure I am out of your hair as soon as possible.”

 

“What?” Kara asks dumbfounded.

 

Lena sits up a bit more, testing out just how much pain she is in when she moves. “I’m sure you want me out of the way, you will be busy, with Lex and Leviathan.”

 

“You don’t want to help with that?” Kara asks, challenging.

 

Lena lets out a dry chuckle. “I mean, of course I do. But I just assumed…”

 

Kara reaches out and puts a hand on top of Lena’s not injured arm. “Lena, I want you here, back with me, with us.”

 

Lena gives a curt nod as she lets out a shaky breath. “What about everyone else? Alex?”

 

Kara shrugs. “Let me worry about them.”

 

Lena sits up slightly. “I would like to help take him down, if you’ll have me.”

 

“I do have… one more question,” Kara says cautiously. “Do you remember what you said… before you passed out in my hallway?”

 

Lena thinks back, but her memories from the other night come in flashes from the moment she was shot. “I… I remember Lex losing it on me… I remember telling him he is a monster, but that doesn’t mean that I have to be… I remember sneaking into his lab and getting what I needed… getting shot when I was trying to get away… and then shoving the drive at you… and then it all goes dark… I’m not even sure how I got to your apartment…”

 

Kara nods, having assumed as much. Over the past several days she has had a lot of time to think. She has thought so much about her falling out with Lena but also more than that, she has thought about Lex and Lena’s relationship as well. And she knows how brilliant Lena is. And what Lena has done in the past. Kara also knows what she herself has done in the past, to fool those around her, to complete a mission. “Lena… did… did you do to Lex what you did with Lillian three years ago?”

 

Lena freezes. She clears her throat. “Um… what? What do you mean, exactly?”

 

Kara half-smiles, realizing she might be onto something. “I mean, did you pretend to side with him, so you could figure out what his plan exactly was?”

 

When Lena stares at her own hands and doesn’t answer, Kara continues voicing her questions. “Did you push me and everyone else away, to convince Lex that you were really on his side? Because you knew that was the only way to know for sure what he was up to? I’m asking if, when you woke up after Crisis, when you found out he was alive again, if you decided to side with him, on purpose?”

 

Lena still doesn’t look at Kara. “I… um… I don’t…”

 

“Lena.” It isn’t said in an accusatory manner. Kara says her name the way she used to say it before everything that happened, almost in a reverent sort of way. Like she was simply amazed to be in Lena’s presence.

 

Kara reaches up slowly, giving Lena amble time to stop her or pull away, as she places her hand softly under Lena’s chin. She guides Lena’s head, so she finally looks at her, eye to eye. Lena doesn’t fight her.

 

Kara can see that her eyes are glimmering with tears. “I had to do it,” Lena says, barely a whisper.

 

“Why?” Kara asks softly, her eyes begging to undersstand.

 

Lena stares longingly into Kara’s blue eyes for a long moment before she finally speaks. “I was still so angry with you when I woke up in this new world. But I will be damned if I was going to let Lex or anyone else hurt you… or worse. I knew the only way I could stop him was to work with him… or, at least, seem like I was working with him… to everyone… but especially you… which meant, I had to actually commit to working with him.”

 

Kara gazes back, taking in Lena’s words fully. This entire time, she thought they were enemies. It turns out, they never were, not really. Lena was still protecting Supergirl.

 

“So… what now?” Kara asks.

 

“I think I need to make you another kryptonite suite. And we take down Leviathan and Lex, once and for all,” Lena says with a smile.

 

“And… us?” Kara asks, the hopefulness clear in her voice.

 

A small laugh escapes Lena with her breath. “I think… we have some things to talk through still… but I’m willing to work through them, if you are.”

 

The beaming smile on Kara’s face was more than enough of an answer.

Notes for the Chapter:

For those interested, Kara read to Lena the Sevenwaters trilogy by Juliet Marillier while she was unconscious and recuperating. She had just gotten a little ways into book 3 when Lena had come to. :)
