1. "Kara Danvers is Supergirl"

Summary for the Chapter:

In the aftermath of her brother’s death by her hand, Lena finds a child that is genetically half her brother and half his Kryptonian puppet. What is she supposed to do with a mini lex with superpowers since all of her friends have been lying to her for years about Supergirl, her former best friend?

Notes for the Chapter:

The kid in this really only serves to bring Kara and Lena together mostly because I know nothing about children. If you are looking for an in depth Supercorp baby as a character, this is not the fic for you.

The dying words of her brother ring in her ears as she tries to distract herself.

The video of Kara using her heat vision, freeze breath, and stopping bullets all while dressed in pastel cardigans plays in the background taunting her.

With every new clip, her heart sinks further into her chest as the weight of what she is witnessing crushes her.

Desperate to get Kara off of her mind, she screams and slams her fist down on the computer effectively turning the monitors off.

Then she begins combing through Lex’s files desperate at first to find a way to prove it farther even though in the back of her mind she knows that the closest friend she has ever had has been lying to her since the day they met.

Unfortunately, the vile acts her brother committed are documented in detail.

Lena reads how her brother and Eve tortured innocent people to death.

The largest file is on red daughter, Supergirl- Kara’s clone.

Eve’s notes were rife with jealousy while Lex’s were detached and scientific.

Under extra security, Lena finds the most horrific files she thinks could ever exist, Eve’s photoshopped pictures of her and Lex.

Barely restraining herself from throwing the computer across the room, Lena gags and gets out of the file as fast as humanly possible.

A file catches her eye, project rebirth.

She clicks on it and has to step away.

In an attempt to produce a version of Lex that loves her back, Eve produced an embryo that is a combination of Lex and red daughter.

After the initial shock, Lena learns that the embryo is now a month old living child and rushes to find out where the boy is.

Kara pops into the back of her mind.

The kid technically has Kara’s DNA so it’s technically her child as well as Lena’s nephew.

She is torn between the betrayal she is feeling and the responsibility she has to tell Kara about the boy.

With a sigh, she pulls out her phone but pauses with her finger hovering over Kara’s contact.

How do you tell someone that they have a child that is half already a clone and the other half, a mass murderer? She decides to worry about that later when she actually finds him. If he is still alive.

Lena places the phone back into her pocket and continues searching the file, more frantic this time. Within the first few minutes of her search, Lena stumbles upon footage of a room she recognizes as her childhood bedroom, but in the center of the room is a containment unit that is glowing green. Lena’s heart sinks. However, she observed that the child is alive, and all of his vitals appear to be normal for his age.

She pulls out her phone again and takes a deep breath.

“Lena?” Kara’s innocent voice laced with concern after hearing Lena’s deep exhale.

“Kara. I need you to get to the Luther mansion as soon as possible.”

“Okay. Lena, I’m on my way, can you tell me what is going on?”

“I...you won’t believe me until you see for yourself.”

“Sure Lena. Whatever you need. I’ll be there as soon as I can.”

“Please hurry. Thank you.” Lena hangs up and makes her way to her bedroom not waiting for her lying friend to fly over.

The door is just as she remembers, no extra security like she had been expecting. As soon as she opens the door, she can see the baby lying in the containment unit in a suspension gel that is keeping him alive. The Kryptonite is at a low enough level that it is not harming the baby, just weakening him down to normal human level. Lena’s heart breaks for the second time today. Her brother let this happen to this poor child. His child. A wave of relief surprises her as she now celebrates the fact that she killed her brother.

Lena studies the pod, wanting to learn everything about it so she doesn’t accidentally hurt the poor baby any farther. Deep in thought, she doesn’t register the footsteps behind her. Until Kara places her hand on Lena’s shoulder.

“Lena.”

“Kara!” Lena startles. “You should wait outside of the room until I can get him out safely.”

“Whose is he?”

Lena hands her the file on project rebirth.

“Lex and Red Daughter,” Kara whispers incredulously.

“Yes. Which is why I called you.”

“Wha...” Kara’s eyes widen.

“Don’t continue to lie to my face, Kara. Or should I say Supergirl?” Lena cuts, bitterly.

“Lena,” Kara begins pleading before Lena cuts her off.

“Red daughter is your clone so the baby has your Kryptonian DNA and unlike you, I can’t keep something this big hidden from my best friend.”

“Lena, please let me explain.”

“I’m not an idiot, I don’t need you to explain it to me. I understand that you didn’t trust me enough to know who you really are.”

“I wanted to tell you for so long...”

“But you didn’t,” Lena interrupts. “You lied to my face every single day. But I didn’t bring you here to talk about this.”

“Lena,”

“The boy is more important than anything you have to say.” Lena interrupts again.

“I’d like to stay if that’s okay with you.”

“There’s Kryptonite being used in there.”

“If it isn’t harming him, it shouldn’t harm me, but thank you for your concern.” Kara smiles at Lena, but she turns her attention back to the pod.

Lena presses several buttons on the exterior of the pod which lower the amount of the gel. Once it has gone down enough that the baby is on his own breathing again, she opens it and pulls him out. In turning to Kara, she can see her weaken slightly especially when Lena hands her the child. Turning back around, Lena closes the pod.

“Don’t worry, little one, you’re in good hands now.” Kara coos softly, cradling the baby in her arms of steel. “He is so small, are you sure he is healthy?”

“All the notes indicate that he is, but he should be checked out by a doctor right away.”

“Can... I don’t mean to overstep, but Alex is a trained medical doctor.”

“But that would mean the DEO. I won’t let you subject him to endless tests and confinement.”

“No, Lena I would never let that happen. With your permission, she can do it at L-Corp.”

“Fine. We can meet her there. I can take him while you call her.”

“Yeah. Sure.” Kara reluctantly hands over the baby. “Um, Lena, we should probably, like, give him a name.”

Lena’s face visibly whitens. She hadn’t thought about that. What do you name the biologically engineered superpowered son of two murderers?

“I hadn’t given it much thought.”

“Would it be okay with you if, um, he has a Kryptonian name?” Kara asks nervously. “It can be his middle name if you want.” She adds quickly.

Of course she would want the baby to have a Kryptonian name, her whole planet is gone, she probably never thought she would have a kid. But despite all that, Kara, being Kara, still leaves it up to Lena.

“Of course,” Lena finally says aloud. “I know how much this must mean to you.”

“El-Et. It was my grandfather's name. We can call him Eliot if you want, a more human sounding name.” Kara shrinks, awaiting Lena’s reaction. “Do you like it?”

Lena smiles glancing between Kara and the tiny bundle she has curled in her arms. “It sounds perfect.”

Kara’s eyes light up at the approval and she nods before pulling out her phone to call Alex. She puts it on speaker so Lena can hear.

“Alex? It’s me.”

“Kara? Is everything alright with Lena?”

Kara glances at Lena sadly. “We can talk about that later, right now we need you to get over to L-Corp.”

“Kara, you are being cryptic. What is going on?”

“Be at L-Corp in 10 minutes and we will explain everything.”

Alex sighs. “Fine.”

Kara hangs up.

“I’ll pack up all of the research while you hold him.” Lena says handing back Eliot.

“Are we flying there?” Kara asks hesitantly.

“It is the safest mode of transportation.”

“I should change.”

“Yes you should, Supergirl.” Lena digs.

“Lena...”

“Go get changed.”

Kara looks like she wants to say something else but holds her tongue, storming off to change, not able to just speed away due to the residual kryptonite.

Once she is out of the room, she speeds into her suit but overhears Lena talking to her new nephew.

“I won’t let anything bad happen to you,” she whispers.

After all of the research is packed, it only takes a minute, Lena, holding Eliot and the research, allows Kara to pick them up. She flies straight to the L-Corp balcony and lowers them as soon as they land. They arrive just as Lena gets a call from her building security downstairs informing her that Alex is there. Lena tells security to show her to the medical labs, even though she already knows where they are. At the same time, Lena hands off a sleeping Eliot to Kara as they head down to meet her.

2. Checkup

Summary for the Chapter:

“Alex, Lena knows I’m Kara and that I called you here. We need your medical expertise. To examine,” Kara lowers the bundle in her arms so Alex can see better, “Eliot.”

Alex stands before them with her face contorted in surprise. She covers her open mouth with her left hand and reaches for the small child with her right.

“Whose is he, Kara?” Alex asks in a whisper as she lightly strokes the baby’s cheek.

“Mine,” Kara replies even softer than her sister.

Alex takes a step back at that. “What? No. How?”

“Eve.” Lena intercedes. “Eve made him with Lex and his Kara clone’s DNA.”

“I’ll try not to be offended. Kara, set him down on the table please.” Alex peruses the medical equipment, finding everything she needs, and returns to them. “I assume you used your x-ray vision already.”

Kara’s eyes widen and she intakes a sharp breath before squinting and scanning Eliot. “He looks fine.”

“Okay. He’s going to need to be awake for the next part.” Alex winces. “He’s not going to like it.”

“It won’t hurt right?” Lena warns more than asks.

“No pain, just discomfort.”

After a little less than half an hour, the crying subsides, and Alex declares him healthy and begins to process his DNA to confirm everything.

He falls asleep again in Kara’s arms as she whispers a Kryptonian lullaby.

“He has your eyes,” Lena whispers.

“Yeah.” Kara smiles, but it fades quickly. “You know, she’s dead. My...um clone. She sacrificed herself to save me, but something… strange happened after.”

“What?”

“Well, she sort of,” Kara gestures attempting to find the right words. “Dissolved? Vaporized? I don’t know, but she turned into a little purple cloud. Then…”

“Then?” Lena prods after Kara trails off.

“I absorbed her?”

Lena’s Luthor training, years of being ridiculed and judged without batting an eye, it all goes out the window as she stands there with a look of pure surprise on her face. Trying her level best not to yell at Kara and wake Eliot, Lena whisper-shouts, “You what?!”

“Alex,” Kara moans, but allows herself to be dragged to the table. Lena follows, amused and curious. “It’s not that bad, My heat vision is purple now though.”

Alex says nothing but stares down her sister. Kara burns a small hole in the floor at her feet, demonstrating her new ability and annoying her sister.

“Damn it, Kara!” Alex shouts, too loud and Eliot wakes up and starts crying.

Kara stands up to take care of him, but Lena stops her. “I’ll get him. You get yourself checked out.”

Lena smirks picking up her nephew and bouncing him gently up and down in her arms. Alex pushes Kara back down on the table.

By the time Alex is through with her tests, Eliot is back asleep in the crib.

“What’s the word, doc?” Kara teases.

“Everything seems in order,” Alex grits out, clearly frustrated at her sister’s antics, but perks up, “You’re telling J’onn about all of this.”

Kara’s eyes open comedically wide which causes Alex’s smile to grow. “Alex, please.”

“I’ll call him right now.”

“No! Alex!” Kara reaches for her phone, but Alex moves to throw it to Lena.

“Lena, catch.” Alex launches her phone at Lena, but Kara uses her superspeed to intercept it. “No fair!”

“I’ll tell papa bear in the morning unless you shout it with your mind so loud he hears it all the way across the city, aunty.”

Alex gasps. The reality hits her that her sister has a son, her nephew. She squeals, “I’m going to be the coolest aunt in the universe.”

“Wrong,” Lena says confidently joining in on their banter. “I’m already the coolest aunt in the universe.”

“We’ll just have to see about that. I’m going to teach him how to punch and ride a motorcycle. We are going to talk about girls together. You can’t top that, Luthor.”

“Oh yeah,” Lena pulls out her phone, tapping it a few times before showing Alex the screen. “I just set up a several million dollar trust fund for him.”

“Oh that’s just what every kid wants.”

“Okay! That’s enough. You will both be excellent aunts, but we should probably discuss what we are going to do next.”

Lena nods, throwing a playful look to Alex. Alex in turn sticks her tongue out at her. While Kara shakes her head at them.

“Lena,” Kara prompts.

“I know he is technically your son biologically, so I really don’t have a say.”

“No, Lena, he is just as much a part of my family as he is a part of yours. I want you to be involved in his life as much as you want.”

“I…I don’t know what I want. I’m still mad at you and especially at Lex, but when I look into his sweet innocent face, I want to hold him and give him all the love in the world.”

Kara smiles at her friend. “I can’t do this alone, especially not with having two jobs.” Her face falls. “Oh Rao, I’m going to be a terrible mother.”

 

“Kara, calm down.” Alex grabs her sister by the shoulders and shakes her. “You were sent here to take care of Kal, so I know for a fact that you are going to be amazing taking care of Eliot.”

Kara nods, finally taking a deep breath, but then remembers, “The last time I babysat, I lost Carter and he got on a train with a bomber on it that exploded.”

“Yeah,” Alex chuckles. “Cat was so pissed.”
“I should call her for advice. Oh no.” Kara’s hand flies over her mouth as her eyes widen once again.

“What is it Kara?” Lena asks.

“I have to tell Eliza,” Kara answers in a small voice.

Alex bursts into laughter, “Please let me be there!”

Her outburst wakes Eliot once again. Kara walks over to soothe him.

“You know…” Kara says with a smirk, “Eliza is probably going to be coming around more often to see her new grandson.”

“Shit, I didn’t think about that.” Alex’s face falls causing Lena to smirk.

After Eliot is once again asleep in Kara’s arms, she walks back over.

“Maybe he will sleep better at home.”

“You don’t have anything that is suitable for a baby.”
