Lena was sitting on a chair when Kara finally found her. 

 
The rest of the room looked like it had been hit by a hurricane. Papers overturned, glass shattered. Kara was overwhelmed by the smell of rust and iron before she even saw it. The body was lying in what looked like an impossible pool of blood. It was almost unrecognisable. So mangled. She'd seen her share before, but this… this. 

 
If not for the Rolex with embedded emeralds and bald head, Kara wouldn't have known who lay dead on that floor. 

 
And Lex was dead. The only heartbeats in the bunkered room were hers and Lena's. 

 
Lena, sitting as rigid as a queen, blood-drenched, the knife still in her hand, unseeing even as Kara called it in. Unhearing as Kara took the blade from her fingers. Unknowing as the DEO swarmed.

 
She was still all that, Kara thought, watching her from the other side of the infirmary glass. Bandaged and changed into a DEO t-shirt, with the worst of the blood cleaned from her skin. She'd been photographed. Her clothes had been taken for evidence. She'd been interviewed, answering monosyllabically. How she'd been kidnapped. What Lex had wanted her for. 

 
That she'd killed him. 

 
There was the question of jurisdiction. Human on human violence didn't fall under the DEO's purview, after all. Kara knew there was a shitstorm that Lena was the centre of waiting in the wings, that only Alex was holding back. Between the FBI, NCPD and the White House, nobody seemed to quite know what to do.

 
Lex Luthor was dead, by his sister's hand. 

 

Kara had stuck to her like glue the entire time, nobody daring to question her presence as she emanated the unspoken certainty that if anyone so much as breathed the word 'arrest', they'd be thrown through the roof and a great deal further. She saw the looks the other agents were exchanging. She heard the murmurs. Rumours had already spread. They'd seen how Lena had looked coming in and what that meant for how Lex had died. Kara had no idea how Alex had managed to swing it, disappearing for at least two hours to her office to argue with people on the phone. Still, there wasn't even one guard outside the infirmary door, and no charges had been laid.

 
Self-defence. And tomorrow's headlines would read that Lex Luthor was dead, with no explanation as to how. One story Kara would not be reporting on, no matter how much Andrea would want her to. How Lena's non nocere program was going to be used by Lex to brainwash the world. How he'd taken Lena to make sure it did just that.

 

 
There'd been so much blood.

 

A laboured breath escaped her, and Kara tried to contain herself. She focused intently on Lena, worry eating at her as she watched her just sit on the edge of the mattress, as pale as a ghost. Alex finished stitching a cut on her arm, not saying a word. It was at times like this when Kara was so grateful for having Alex as a sister. For all her faults, at her heart, she was the fiercest protector of those she loved. And even after everything Lena had done and all the mistrust she created and been dealt, she was still the only one with a badge who treated Lena with gentleness today. And it definitely wasn't all for Kara's benefit.

 
Alex cleaned up and left the room, the doors closing with a hiss behind her.

 
"How is she?" Kara's voice was cracked from underuse, and her words felt hollow to her ears, but she didn't take her eyes off of Lena when she asked.

 
Alex sighed, taking her place beside Kara.
 


"Physically?" She answered. "She's been hurt, but it's nothing that won't heal. Lacerations, stitches, bruises… A cracked rib."

 
Kara's visage darkened. Her fists clenched so tight her nails nearly cut through her skin.

 
"I've fought so many monsters," Kara growled, hate flooding through her veins. "I knew what he was. What he'd done to the world, Clark, me… But… how can you do this to your own sister? How can you think that this is coming from love?"

 
It was fury mixed with guilt. Guilt that she hadn't gotten there sooner. Shame that she hadn't… 

 
If you're going to be a villain… I'm going to treat you like one.

 
"It's reprehensible."

 
"It's insanity." Kara snarled back at the words, her jaw clenched tight, and her eyes burned hot. "I said I'd never kill, not on purpose, not if there was another way… But him?"

 
There was a burnt vision of Lex dead on the floor, covered in blood. It played over and over again in her head, but in the depths of her soul, Kara only saw herself holding the knife.

 
"Him I'd kill." The words oozed out of her darkly. "I'd bring him back to life just to kill him again, and I wouldn't lose a second of sleep over it."

Something Red Daughter knew. Something that Red K could unleash. It wasn't all of who she was, but it was a part of her. Because it would mean that Lena wouldn't have had to.

"Lena-"

"Let's go."

The potted plants were where they always were, the knick-knacks in their spots too. The unwashed dishes remained in the sink. "Is there anything… do you need anything?" She asked.

"A change of clothes would be nice. And a shower."

Twenty minutes.

But…

"Lena?"

"Lena?"

 
Still no answer, a spike of fear stabbed in her mind. Disastrous scenarios ran amok.

"He wasn't going to kill me."

"Lena… he doesn't-"

 
"I know he doesn't deserve my kindness!" Lena shouted, furious and unhinged in Kara's face. "I know that, ok? I know that he was a cruel, vindictive man who's done nothing but hurt me again and again, but I can't… I can't…."

 
She was shuddering again now, with unshed tears.

 
Kara let her fingers reach for her now.

 
"Whatever you're feeling, Lena, it's ok. It's ok."

 
Lena flinched away from Kara's touch, pulling her hands back towards her chest.

 
"No! It'll never be ok!" She shouted, eyes wide and wild. "I'm a monster! Don't you see? Just like him. Worse. And I can forgive what he's done to me, but I couldn't forgive what he'd do to everyone else. If I'd stopped him sooner… I could have... I could have stopped... Don't you get it? I deserve everything that happened to me."

 
The pit in Kara's stomach dropped further. She'd never understood before meeting Lena just how much watching someone hurt, hurt. To see someone who hated themselves so fundamentally, Kara's only instinct was to engulf Lena in everything good and kind. She couldn't find her voice to refute Lena quickly. Wordless in the face of it. She remembered every look of happiness, every gift, every extravagance. All the hardheadedness, but desperation to reach out. All that warmth from Lena. The need she had to earn love. Deserve it. Be worthy of it. Prove she was good enough for it. Unable to see that people loved her without trade. That anyone could. 

 

That Kara could. 

 
Kara stared at her, tracing the injuries on Lena's body. Every bruise, every cut. Without direction, she reached her fingertips and lightly brushed them. Her eyes found Lena's then as she did. For permission, and more. Lena shivered but didn't move as Kara continued, finding them all, from her hand to her neck. It was only when she was nearly cradling her cheek that Kara finally spoke.

 
"All this, Lena?" She whispered. "You think you deserved this?"

 
Kara took Lena's hands in hers, gentle, breathing in sync together before she kissed the back of them. Lena was watching her the entire time, lips trembling with tears in her eyes.

 
"You're not broken," Kara said, firm and gentle, breathing the words against Lena's skin. "You're not unforgivable. You're not just some thing. That can be packaged and processed and molded. You're a human being."

 
Lena's hands squeezed Kara's, ever so slightly.

 
"But you were right… not to trust me," she answered, pained.

 
Kara's heart stuttered.

 
"I've always trusted you." Kara's pain leaked out now, mixing with Lena's. "I just… I couldn't lose you. But I lost you anyway."

 

Kara looked down, unable to face her.

 

"You have so much to be angry at me for," she whispered. "You get to be angry. But the entire world would be under Lex's thumb if it weren't for-"

 
Lena stood, the movement pushing Kara back on her heels. 

 
"Don't you get it?" Lena's words spilled over hers, viciousness and self-loathing pouring out of her like murky water. "All those people never would have been in that position if it wasn't for me. I screwed up. I thought I was doing something right, but…. I just…"

 
Lena turned away from her, the pain on her face so visceral Kara actually felt it in her soul. The trench between them, the deep abyss that fed on everything hateful, hurt, and anger-filled thing they'd felt or done to each other, swallowed the words and held them hostage. 

 
"Forget it." Lena clutched at her head. "Just forget it."

All the versions of Lena pressed together like pages of a book. Young and old and innocence stolen, along with hope. It stained her, tried to drown her. Somewhere in the middle of this, Kara had let herself become so lost in trying to reach Lena that she shored up all the parts of herself that were exactly like her. Two grown women stood in this room, sure. But there were also two girls, cut adrift into an unknown world, full of expectation, to meet the fear and pain inside their hearts. 

 
Kara reached up and touched the skin of Lena's cheek, softly tracing it with a smile. Lena shivered under her touch, leaning into it ever so slightly in the seconds it took, blinking when Kara let her hand fall. The space between them didn't feel that scary anymore.

 
"Now you listen to me." Kara gentled, trying to memorise every expression Lena made. "You've got the biggest heart of anyone I know. Feeling this... This hurt, it makes you human." Rao, she felt that seep into her soul. 

 
"So human." The repeated words were even softer. 

 
So much had shifted between them. It had broken the barriers of their friendship, plunging them into the raging storm that they been sheltered from on their own tiny island. But maybe now Kara finally realised that what they were, both of them had never been alone. There was a world more fantastic that the storm couldn't hide forever. 

 
"And I wish I could take it and make it all better… but I can't. And I can't make you believe something about yourself you don't see. But I've seen it, Lena." 

 
Kara was pouring all of herself out. All the bits and pieces and jumbled parts were forming into some beautiful, imperfect thing that was the way she saw Lena. 

 
"I've seen you do things that no one else could dream of doing." A small smile curved across Kara's face as she shook her head in wonder. "Achieve things that no one else could dream of achieving."

 
She shook her head in memory, in awe. Kara refocused on Lena's face, and all the longing in her heart wanted to soothe the pained desperation in Lena's eyes.

 
"I see you," she whispered. "A brilliant, smart, determined woman who would have risen in life no matter what, and no matter what didn't deserve what happened to her."

 
Kara took a deep breath and swallowed, her brows creasing together. 

 
"You've made mistakes," she nodded, not wanting to pretend that she'd forgotten or that it wasn't important. "Big ones."

 
Lena looked down, Kara catching the flicker of shame in them, and her fingers caught Lena's chin at the same time. Holding Lena's gaze to her's, needing some tactile understanding, needing the whole truth out before Lena shut it down.

 
"But so have I. So many." It was resolute, and it hurt to admit to them. It always did. But she needed to. To cut away at the rot that had spread between them. It was more than just her lie to Lena because if Kara was honest when she held up a mirror to herself, she didn't like all that she saw. There was vanity in her. A self-righteousness that she was quick to see in others, but not herself. Hypocrisy. And she needed to cut it out and finally allow herself to breathe without the crushing weight of denial and blame. Show it to Lena and say, 'you see? I see it too. This part of me that I don't like. I see how it's me too, and I see what I've done.' 

 
Kara swallowed past the lump in her throat, tears at the back of her eyes. Mourning all the hurt, she caused towards the most precious person in the world to her.

 
"And if you don't know what to do or where to start, then do the hardest thing," she said, voice hoarse and gritty. 

 
Mournful. 

 
"Forgive yourself," Kara finished softly. 

 
Lena's eyes were full of tears and fear and coiled hurt, and Kara saw it all. She knew it. The conviction was a fault in her and a blessing because she felt it electrify her bones and skin, and she channeled all of it into her words. 

 
"I know that one day you'll have a chance again to show the world what you're made of, I know you'll shine brighter then the sun, because you are not broken."

 
Kara loved her. She loved her more than if the matrix had engraved it into her DNA that she was coded to love this woman. More than if the stars spelled it out in the sky. It defied sense to those at a glance. How could it not? But on closer inspection, it made the most sense in the world. A compliment of who they were, what they were, and what they wanted. It'd been messed up with confusion and fear and pain, but Kara knew she wasn't crazy for thinking that there'd been more than one unspoken thing between them for as long they'd been friends. 

 
Kara let her fingers drop, nearly on fire. Lena was just staring at her, no words on her lips, but her eyes were welling with a river of tears, and barely a few seconds passed before she was reaching for Kara and hugging her hard. Shaking, crying and trembling like a leaf. Kara hugged her back, feeling it all. Love and loss and relief mixed, and then she was crying too, and as close as they were pressed together, she felt closer still. Like Lena's and her tears had filled that canyon between them, that wasn't really unending after all, the water rising and lifting all that had been and all that could have been.

 

All the regret and hurt pulling them further along and apart and weaving the tapestry of their entire relationship.

 
Lena had killed more than just her brother tonight. Kara could hear it in her cries, her sobs. The sound of a lifetime of fear and violence, digging and clawing, bloody from her throat. The ghosts of trauma, of abuse. A theft of everything that she was and then had made herself be. It was the cry of a lost soul trying to find just one person who cared. And Kara held her, hugged her as tightly and as warmly as she could and tried to convey everything with it. Protection and safety and understanding. She let all of it wash through her, all the injury and pain and corroded, raw spirit. Because it wasn't something, she could fix.

 

Not in a single conversation. Not in a night. Maybe not in a lifetime. But she could be there. Be here in all this feeling. 

 
For Lena. 

 
"I just wanted him to stop and I killed him," Lena wept, the words hyperventilated out of her. "I couldn't stop. Oh god, I killed him. I'm so sorry. God, I'm so sorry."

 
"It's alright," Kara's lips whispered as they near-kissed the side of Lena's head. "It's alright."
 

Lena cried harder and hugged her tighter. 
 

Kara couldn't take it all away. She wished she could. She'd fight the world to be able to, and she knew she'd win. But she couldn't, and that hurt so damn much.

 

"It's alright," was what she said again, with everything she had.
 

Lena's face turned into the crook of Kara's neck.
 

"Stay with me," she begged unflinchingly. "Don't leave me."
 

Kara closed her eyes, tears leaking out past and down her cheeks.
 

"I'm not going anywhere," she whispered as they held each other in the dark.
