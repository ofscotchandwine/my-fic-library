whisper from the heart

"My good opinion once lost is lost forever." - Fitzwilliam Darcy. 

“Till this moment I never knew myself.” - Elizabeth Bennett.

Pride and Prejudice.

1813; Jane Austen

 

 

 

 

"Protect yours as I protected mine," Red Daughter had said as she died. Reabsorbed by Kara's own body, once again.

Kara hadn't had time to mourn her, or to process really, but she felt her senses being sharper, whole, complete once again.

Not only that, she had now... Conflicted feelings about Lex.

She figured it that, as she had him dangling from her hand, hundreds of feet in the air.

"It's over," Kara pleaded. "Let me save you."

"Saved?" Lex asked, only to laugh. "By a Kryptonian? I'd rather die."

And then he was gone.

Dead.

 

 

Or so she thought at first

She didn't know when she'd memorized his heartbeat— She probably didn't. Red Daughter did.

But she'd pick up on it almost immediately after he had supposedly died at the explosion at her feet.

East.

And he wasn't alone. The fast heartbeat that accompanied him, she also knew in hers. She flew.

As fast as she could.

The sonic boom that followed her until she located the heartbeats, seemed insufficient.

Were insufficient.

She caught the bullet just before it hit Lex.

The second bullet. But she'd been too slow to catch the first one. She couldn't save Alex.

She wasn't even conscious of where she was… except somewhere close to The Rockies, a bunker of some sort.

"Lena?" She questioned, surprised. She'd never thought Lena would kill in cold blood. To defend herself? Others? She would not expect less, Lena is a protector too. But premeditated, and her own brother? Hard to believe, if she hadn't seen it herself.

"The world will never be a safe place with him in it," Lena said, her voice somewhat lacking. Letting her hand, still holding the revolver, fall to her side.

"Oh, brava!" Lex let out, slight surprise coloring his words. "You did it, Lena. You killed me. You finally proved that I've been underestimating you all this time." He slowly crawled the floor, toward a desk, leaving a faint trail of blood.

Kara was still dumbly standing between the siblings, the bullet still pinched in between her fingers.

"But, when I'm gone, who will be left to be proud of you?" Lex continued talking. Somehow Kara recognized the tone. Bitter, resentful words said with the sole desire to hurt. She'd never been the target of it. But at the same time she'd been, she had heard the tone before.

"What, your friends?" Lex continued, his gaze glued to Lena's form.

"Stop," Kara heard herself say. Low, between her teeth. Both halfheartedly and in warning.

"The joke's on you. It's always been on you." Lex spat, cruel and with barred teeth. "Your friends have been lying to you from the start. Your boyfriend Jimmy, Alex, J'onn, that little alien runt Brainy. Even your own mother."

Kara saw Lena take a step back. Unconsciously even.

"They've mocked you, humiliated you, betrayed you."

"That's not true," Kara said louder this time, turning completely to Lex. "You know it's not true, Alex."

"Every last one." He said, emphasizing every single word. "Denial's a very powerful thing, isn't it? It's been standing right in front of you all this time. And you chose not to see it."

"Lena, I promise you, that's not true," Kara said, turning around to look at her best friend.

Lena had tears running down her cheeks, a light illuminated her face and her eyes fixed not on Lex, her eye-line a bit higher, by Kara's estimation.

"Kara Danvers is Supergirl."

She heard him say, she wiped her head around to see video evidence of herself, Kara Danvers having superpowers. Supergirl appearing just as Kara Danvers disappeared, still stressed and windswept.

"I'm about to die, but at least I lived without ever being a fool." Lex spat, cruelly. "You're left with no one and nothing:"

"That's not true." Kara vigorously said. "Lena, I swear it's not true."

"It's not true that you are not Kara Danvers?" Lena quietly asked. "Or that you lied to me and used me?"

"It's not…. it wasn't like that, Lena," Kara said. "I was… I wanted to tell you so many times—"

"But you didn't," Lena cut her off. "Everyone else in our life knew but me."

"He's taking it out of context. Lena, it wasn't like that."

"How was it, then?" Lena asked. "Did you and your sister laugh about me with my boyfriend when you sent him to break into my vault?"

"I never laughed about you! Lena, I promise you."

"Excuse me, if at the moment, your word means literally nothing to me," Lena said, opening the face of the watch at her wrist.

"Lena, please, hear me out—-"

"No! I killed my brother for you, for our friends!" Lena screamed, a purple light swirling behind her, as soon as she pressed a button in her watch. It reminded Kara of the portal communicating the D.E.O. to Argo.

"Don't you understand what you've done?" Lena continued. "I thought I was protecting my innocent, helpless best friend from my homicidal brother, but it turns out you were never my friend. You were just making sure that I wasn't like him".

"Lena—"

"Save it, Kara." Lena said, as if her name was poison, walking towards the purple portal.

It closed as soon as she crossed it.

Lena was gone.

And it was all her fault. Kara's legs almost gave out.

She turned around trying to escape, and she's confronted by Alex's corpse. Getting colder and bluer by the second. Blood coloring his shirt.

Lex's body; a voice at the back of her mind, corrected.

Kara was trapped in her pod again.

She couldn't breathe. She couldn't move, her limbs seemed locked in place.

Kara could see Krypton exploding again.

And again.

The darkness, the cold. The floating aimlessly in the vacuum of space.

Alone.

Lost.

"—pergirl? Please report back." Alex's voice somehow managed to break her downward spiral. "Kara just let me know you are okay."

She took her hand to her ear, focusing on the cadent of her sister's voice, "Alex, I need your help."

The response was instant, "I'm here, what do you need?"

 

 

This time, Alex Danvers' pep talk … was less of a pep talk, and more of an order to fix it.

Kara wasn't sure she could, not anytime soon, at least. But for now, she had a plan to at least, say her piece.

Alex, after hearing what had happened promised to take care of Lex's body. Sticking to the idea that he preferred to fall to his dead than be saved by an alien.

"I know Lena too," her sister said. "At this point, she needs saving from herself. She's no damsel in distress, but she has a tendency to go to the worse possible option available. You need to make her listen to your truth… And then, let her be."

"Alex—"

"I know," Alex nodded. "But you need to let her process. Her instinct is telling her something, neither her head nor her heart would agree for a while. But you need to get to her before it's too late."

Kara simply nodded. She could do that.

"She hates lies," the voice at the back of her own head said, with it's heavy Slavic accent. 'You lied, but you did it for a reason. She doesn't know it."

And that gave her the idea.

 

 

Lena was at her penthouse. Kara could see her fleeting around her room when she landed at her balcony. She wasn't wearing her supersuit. She wanted to face Lena as herself, not as what she could do.

Kara's breath hitched when she realized her best friend was packing.

She inhaled some air pretending it was courage, and knocked on the open glass door with her free hand.

Lena walked to her field of view, her eyes still a bit red, and with a scathing look. As if Kara disgusted her, as if Kara was offending her by her mere presence.

She probably was, to be fair.

"Fuck off," Lena said. Loud enough for a Super to hear, but not enough to cross the distance if Kara was human.

"I never wanted to hurt you," Kara said. "I know I'm already asking for too much, but Lex wanted to hurt you, to inflict the most pain. What he told you, is not the truth." She lifted the container with the answer to all of Lena's questions. Hopefully. "I just want you to hear it from me, one time. The actual truth. Not your brother's cruel facade of it. I will tell you everything. Anything you ask."

Kara could almost see the gears in Lena's head, pondering if she should repeat herself, repeating Kara's words in her head. If she should listen to Lex.

"I'm leaving National City," Lena announced.

"I understand you'd need to process, if you think you need to leave for that, I will respect it," Kara said. It wasn't a lie…

But she was willing to beg for Lena to stay.

Or at least for her to come back.

"My plane leaves in 2 hours," Lena warned. "Clock is ticking."

Kara doesn't need much more. She walked close to Lena. She didn't move, but her gaze was cold, scrutinizing, challenging.

"I'm sorry again," Kara whispered, as she left the container over the table. "I wanted to tell you so many times, I almost told you when we were in Kaznia, but then…" She shook her head. It was better if Lena knew she was being sincere. Factually.

She opened the container, the Vertullarian, latching onto her hand as soon as it was close enough. She showed the truth seeker to Lena, along with the very small shard of Kryptonite that made it possible for it to pierce her skin.

"I'm sorry," she repeated. "I'm Supergirl, and I should've told you so long ago, I know that. But I just kept making excuses because you've been hurt so many times and I convinced myself that I was protecting you. And then one day, you were so angry with me, with Supergirl. But you still loved Kara. I just kept thinking if I could be Kara, just Kara that I could keep you as a friend." She said, sighing. Her shoulders dropping. She hadn't realized how heavy the secret was, how she'd been waiting for that perfect moment to finally say it. A moment that never came, and now it was too late, she was probably going to lose Lena.

"I was selfish and scared, and I didn't wanna lose you, so I kept pretending, and I never stopped," Kara continued, her eyes full of tears. "Every time I kept my secret from you, I wasn't protecting you, I was hurting you, just like everyone else, and I am so, so sorry. I am so sorry. I should have been braver. But the idea of losing you… I've never felt more scared of anything in my life."

Lena was not saying anything, she kept looking at Kara with big eyes that betrayed none of her thoughts.

Kara panicked, and her mouth keep talking, quite without her permission.

"And I go against monsters, meta-humans and aliens on a daily basis," she nervously laughed. "But losing you it's like the nightmare of losing Krypton again. Of being trapped in that stupid pod for over two decades, an endless void in front of me for eternity. I didn't want to lose you, because I knew your mom would be right."

Kara blinked surprised with herself, and yet her mouth continued moving.

"She got a hold of Jeremiah, she knew who I was pretty much as soon as I started being Supergirl, and I asked, when we were going to rescue you from Rhea, why she hadn't told you. And she said: 'because she will figure out on her own, and she will hate you for it'. In that moment, I didn't think much of it, and then… you know, I kinda lost myself, because I thought I'd sent my then boyfriend to his death. But then you brought me back to myself, and then… then you got poisoned. Remember?"

Kara started, unconsciously, pacing through Lena's living room.

"At the time, I didn't really think about why I didn't tell you when you told me you'd dreamt that I flew with you. I swear it was at the tip of my tongue. But I didn't… because you almost died! And I thought… If you knew, you'd be in even more danger. And then… And then you figured out how to make Kryptonite, the one thing that could… And I was terrified of losing you, in another way. Because, I got scared. And maybe your mom was right, and you'd hate me enough to work with Lex.

And I could not lose you. Everything but you, the first best friend since losing Kenny. And that's the thing, Lena," Kara said, with a sort of desperation that she wasn't used to hear in her own voice. "I'm so sick of losing people. They took Jeremiah Danvers from his own home, because of me. My first best friend, here on Earth wasn't Alex. His name was Kenny Li, he was kind, and so smart. And he died, because of me. My uncle, Non, killed a Catco co-worker. Because of me, to teach me a lesson. Fucking Rick Malverne kidnapped Alex and almost killed her, because of me, so he could manipulate me into getting his father out of jail, because he had figured out I was Supergirl. You called me that day… And I couldn't even tell my best friend that I was scared out of my mind, that I was about to lose my sister.

Rhea decided she wanted Earth to be her new kingdom because Mon-El wouldn't leave Earth, because of me. My sister had to literally erase her memories, to forget I was Supergirl, because that knowledge put her in danger. Basically stopping being my sister, because she couldn't remember why we connected as kids. Your brother sent Red Daughter, to threaten my adoptive mother so he could defeat me. They are all in danger because of who I am. But, out of everyone, I couldn't lose you, Lena"

"Maybe you should've told me the truth, then," Lena let out, lifting her eyebrow, like a challenge.

"Maybe… Probably," Kara conceded. "But I did say I'd been selfish. You were the only person I've ever known, that prefers simple, non-special, very much human Kara Danvers to Supergirl. You'd be surprised of how addicting that is. How addictive you are. I didn't want to lose that. I don't get many opportunities to tell important people the truth… without someone being in danger, at least."

"Do you really expect me to believe that? That you told everyone in our circle of supposed friends because they were in danger, but I happen to not enter in that circle, when I get almost assassinated at least once every three months?" Lena asked, seemingly offended by the insinuation that the she wasn't in danger enough. "Don't treat me like and idiot, Supergirl…"

Kara shook her head, "I don't think I explained myself well…" She mumbled. "Alex… Alex knows, she remembers now because… well, I was dumped on her when we were barely teenagers. Superman simply left me with Eliza and Jeremiah, as if I was— disposable. And she had to deal with a sister she didn't ask for, nor wanted."

Kara continued after a brief pause, "Winn… I do admit he was the first person I ever told I was Supergirl… And mostly because I'd just flown for the first time in like ten years, to save the plane my sister was on from crashing, and let me tell you she did not appreciate it. I had no one else to tell, no one to share the excitement… I wasn't even really Supergirl when I told him. He made the suit, with a lot of trial and error.

James… He knew before he even met me," Kara explained. "Superman told him, so he could… I don't know, check on me. As always, is something we never addressed, but that's the usual routine with my cousin. And J'onn— back then he was going by Hank Henshaw. I never told him. He was, supposedly, who took Jeremiah from home because I was recklessly flying around, so Hank was supposed to know about me.

I told Lucy Lane, because Cadmus had taken J'onn as prisoner to experiment with, when he revealed himself as alien, because of me, and Alex too. I told her who I was so she could help me rescue them." Kara frowned. Debating if she should even mention Barry, Oliver, Sara and the rest, given that they were from another universe and they never really met Kara, only Supergirl.

At the end, she apparently had wondered about that out-loud, given that Lena's eyebrow was about to lift from her face, from how up it was sitting on her forehead.

"For another time, then," Kara said. "Mon-El… He also never met Kara Danvers, I didn't… so much tell him I was Supergirl, more than just started treating him more like Kara Zor-El.— That's my birth name— my alien name, I guess. He was from Daxam, so close to my home, he was somewhat from my culture. I didn't need to go over the sad story, he just knew it. He had one of his own. I do wonder if maybe that's why I thought we should've been together." The last part, surprised her. Not even that she said, but that she even thought about it.

"Maggie figured out because of Alex. But neither of us told her," Kara clarified. "Brainy, he's from the future. I never told him, he just showed up and knew who I was. They said, than in the future, everyone knew my name, that the identity of every hero in their time, was public knowledge." Kara shrugged. "I don't know how they do it, when in my experience everyone who wants to cause me harm, use my loved ones to do so."

Kara had a hard time reading Lena's expression, probably intentionally. She was still standing in front of her, with her arms crossed protectively over her chest. Probably for the best… Since she couldn't really concentrate every time her best friends' bosom was at a watchable distance.

The thought smacked so hard into Kara that she involuntarily took a step back. Thankfully, she hadn't said that particular thought aloud, and Lena remained in silence, just looking at Kara.

"I guess Lois Lane also knows, but I didn't tell her, either." Kara wobbly continued. "She knows because Superman, of course. But that one I mind less than with James. At least with her, he told me about it, and I didn't have to find about it because Lois accidentally let it out, or something," she shrugged again. "I did tell Nia. She'd been in a fight with her sister, right after their mother died. She told her, and I quote: ' she was not a real woman'," Lena visibly reacted to that. Kara needed to pick her words a bit to not reveal Nia's identity as Dreamer. That was not her place. "I had just had a fight with Alex as Supergirl, because she didn't know Supergirl was her sister. So I—selfishly don't get me wrong— I told Nia. Because I was missing that sisterly energy, I was mentoring her, I thought it made sense, I guess."

Lena observed her, and then the truth seeker still attached to Kara's arm. "I'm not diminishing your relationship with Nia, but it does sting when you told her, when you just met her. But couldn't tell me. Your supposed best friend."

Kara shook her head.

"It's different," she said.

"Yes, it's different. Because I'm a Luthor."

"No! Of course not," Kara defended. "It has absolutely nothing to do with your family. It has everything to do with you, and how I feel about you—"

"That you think I'm just like my family?"

"No. That I'm stupidly in love with you!"

Silence stretched between them. Kara blinked surprised about what she said. She had never thought that, so she looked at the Vertullarian attached to her arm. Everything she said must be true. Even if she didn't know it herself.

"I—" she started to say. "It truly started with wanting to protect you. At least, after the… 'don't tell anyone, ever' part of it. I didn't— I didn't want to know that I was in love with you because, if I told you and then you hated me…" Kara stopped speaking. There wasn't much to say.

She loved Lena. In a very different way she loved the rest of their friends.

"It was never just friendship with you," Kara mumbled. "We are friends. It's just that you were always more special to me than the rest of my other friends. You were… more."

Lena's eyes fixed on the Vertullarian too. Probably thinking if it was real, if it was still attached to Kara to make her tell the truth.

"I—" Kara started again. "I didn't want to disentangle my feelings about you, because you didn't know who I was, I think," she said. "But I didn't tell you who I was because I was afraid to lose you. And I think that was always the problem. I never gave you the chance to make your own decision."

"No, you didn't," Lena replied, with the coldness of ice. "You didn't even give me the dignity of telling me yourself. You didn't have the bravery of facing me. Instead I had to learn it from my brother. Who told me, to twist the knife in my back. And now you expect me to just accept that, you love me."

They both had tears in their eyes. But every word said by Lena was said with such vitriol that Kara almost shrunk into herself.

But she resisted. Because she knew, that was Lena lashing out. She knew her enough to know that that was her first instinct, to lash out, strike where it hurt the most. And as much as the words did hurt, they weren't any less true. Kara had betrayed Lena. She deserved that response.

"I'm sorry I was such a coward," Kara whispered. "I'm sorry you had to learn it from Lex."

"You need to leave." Lena replied. "I have to go to the airport."

Kara subconsciously clenched her hands in fists, "will you come back?" Then, realizing that could be too presumptuous, she sighed. She wasn't sure she deserved Lena to come back. "You don't need to answer that. I do hope you come back. I don't think I know how to be Kara without you. But I want you to know, that not telling you, waiting so long for it, might have been the biggest mistake of my life. Even if at the moment I thought I was justified in that decision. I know you don't owe me anything," she whispered. "But if you find it in yourself to forgive me, someday. Please, come back. And if you don't… Well, I hope you find a way to let me know you are well. I wish you happiness, Lena."

Lena said nothing, her eyes were still full of tears. As were Kara's.

Kara nodded, and then managed to take the Vertullarian from her arm, closing the canister with it in it.

"Have a safe trip," Kara said, as she walked towards the balcony again, holding the Truth Seeker.

She wished for the perfect hopeful speech that would make Lena forgive her, and make Kara feel better. She knew it would never come. She knew she didn't deserve Lena's forgiveness just because of who she was.

She hoped Lena would return to her. Didn't even need to be as anything more than her best friend. She would be content even with just having her as her social friend. But she would accept her judgment.

It was the least she could do.

"If you ever need me," Kara let out, just as she floated over the balcony. "All you have to do is call. No expectations." She smiled.

And then she flew away.

Before she started to cry again.

She only wanted to bury her sorrows in a gallon of ice cream. Because she knew… she knew she very likely, just lost Lena forever.
