"Kara-"

"You can't kiss me one second and then say you are mad at me the next."

"Yes, I can." Kara nods her head once resolutely. "Now, you made me go into the sunlight to heal. You need to rest. I'll be here when you wake."

Kara genuinely can't think about being apart from the CEO. She doesn't want to be, especially at first. She doesn't think she can be alone, and she doesn't want to leave Lena alone either. Or have her stay over at the loft for the foreseeable future. They both need to heal, and she firmly believes they can heal better together, be stronger together.
