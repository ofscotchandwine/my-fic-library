In some ways, this was my “never” fic. I never thought I’d be able to write a red k Kara. I had these thoughts for processing Kara’s relationship with her parents and the issues with Kryptonian culture, but couldn’t come up with a standalone fic based on that idea. But the puzzle pieces fell into place, and I think (hope?) they flowed together.
