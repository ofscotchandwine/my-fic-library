CatCo Worldwide Media was a media conglomerate known for its mostly objective reporting of the truth, its fashion tips and its affiliation with Supergirl. It was a household name in National City, therefore it was no surprise when a forwarded invitation to a fundraising gala landed in Kara Danvers's mailbox. Still, the reporter frowned at the invite. It wasn't unusual for CatCo to receive invitations to this sort of events, however her boss didn't send a reporter in unless there was an actual story to report - God forbid an employee should have too much fun doing their job. The short sentence summoning her to her boss's office was as friendly as it was long - bordering on rude and four words long. Kara had gotten so used to the lack of 'please, thank you and sorry's her brain didn't even register the incivility. She quickly grabbed her notepad and a pen before heading to Snapper Carr's office, where she knew he would be counting the seconds she had made him wait and considering deducting that number from her salary. 

She knocked on the glass door and stepped inside before the man even looked up from his desk. 'You wanted to see me?'

'You're three minutes late,' he notified flatly. 'Do you know Lena Luthor?' He didn't wait for her reply before carrying on. 'I just heard she'll be going to that gala tonight. I need you to make contact so CatCo will be her go-to media outlet in the future.'

'The gala's tonight?' 

Kara didn't know it was even possible, but Snapper's frown deepened. 'Did you not read the invite? Or did you perhaps lose your sense of time?'

'But I have-'

'Ponytail,' the man interrupted with a bored tone. 'Does it look like I care about what you did or didn't have planned tonight?'

The blonde shut her mouth. The answer to that question was obvious. 'Lena Luthor? What is she doing in National City?' She asked instead.

'She moved here a couple of days ago,' Snapper informed, his attention already moving on to the case file in front of him. 

The nonverbal cues indicating his disinterest were hard to miss, and Kara knew this was the furthest he was willing to discuss the topic. It seemed that she was in for an afternoon of Internet-stalking. 'What about the gala?' She questioned.

'What about it?'

'Did you want me to write an article about that?'

'I did hire a reporter, did I not?' The editor-in-chief checked. At the blonde's silence, he looked up and sighed. 'Write about those rich people's outfits, write a fluff piece, I don't care, but you can't attend an event like this as the media and not write anything about it. Now, go back to work and leave me alone, unless you also need my help to pick out a dress for tonight?'

Although she was curious about what a shopping spree with her boss would look like, she answered, 'no. I think I've got that covered.' She stood there awkwardly for a minute and when Snapper didn't offer a response, she took the dismissal for what it was and walked back to her own office. She notified her cousin about her meeting with the Luthor to which she received a 'be careful' and 'good luck' before texting her sister to stop by her apartment later in the day to help her with her outfit. She opened her laptop and her fingers hovered over the keyboard before she typed in 'Lena Luthor' and began her research.

Lena Luthor should've known a gala wasn't the best place to make friends. To her defence, most people wouldn't touch her with a ten-foot pole after her brother went mad, so she was a little rusty. Still, she had hoped at least one of the white, self-righteous millionaires would prove to be an ally but so far, she had no such luck. In fact, considering the night she was having, it would be more accurate to say she had absolutely no luck at all. While polite, the other guests had quickly shown their wariness towards the Luthor and even though the host was grateful for her money, he wasn't feeling thankful enough to have a conversation with her that went further than 'thank you for the donation' and 'with pleasure, thank you for the invitation'. So, Lena wasn't having a good time and she would've already gone home to her cold, empty apartment if it hadn't been for the fact that it was way too early to leave. However, her current conversation partner was making her rethink her decision. Neal Davis was a young businessman; good-looking and slightly drunk, Lena might've even considered him a noteworthy, potential candidate if he had kept his mouth shut. Now, she was trapped in the company of a man she didn't know who was making overt sexual advances and misinterpreting her hard 'no' as a playing-hard-to-get 'yes'. If he noticed her growing unease, he didn't show it although the CEO had a feeling his narcissism and inebriated state were clouding his judgement.

'Come on, Lena. This is a charity event, so let me show you how much pleasure I can give you,' he flirted and a chill ran down Lena's spine at the implications of his words.

'Mr. Davis,' the Luthor addressed, hoping her voice wasn't shaking with fear like the rest of the body was. 'As I've said before, I'm not interested in having sex with you. If you would be willing to discuss your work on the other hand, then I'm sure we would both enjoy the conversation.'

'Don't play coy. I know you want me.'

Lena wasn't a violent person, but it took her a large amount of effortful control not to punch the man in the face. She was about to express her dissent once more but somebody else beat her to it. 'I believe she said no.'

The L-Corp CEO turned her gaze to the stranger and her eyes were met with a cascade of blond hair, a light pink embellished dress and a glare that could kill if it grew any more intense. 

Neal grinned at the addition. 'You're welcome to join us,' he spoke suggestively.

'I know this is hard for you to understand given that you didn't have the intellect to pass your classes without cheating and your parents had to pay for your diploma, but when a woman says 'no', it means she's rejecting you and she wants you to leave her the fuck alone, alright?' The blonde taught without an ounce of mercy. 

The young man faltered and the boyish smirk that had been present on his lips disappeared for a split second as he seemed to realise that part of his flawless plan to seduce the Luthor had gone wrong but he shook it off before his brain could process the social cues and smirked again. 'We were having fun,' he denied. 'Go bother someone else.' He made a move to grab Lena's arm but even his hurried movement couldn't compare to the Super's speedy reflexes. 

'What the hell?' He winced and looked down at the hand firmly latching onto his wrist.

Kara's voice was low and dangerous when she spoke. 'You better leave now.' 

Neal huffed angrily. 'Alright, fine. You crazy bitch.'

The blonde twisted his wrist. 'What was that?' 

'Nothing. I'm leaving.' 

Kara released her grip and the pair watched him walk away with his tail between his legs.

'Thank you,' Lena told the other woman gratefully as she faced her. 'I was running out of ways to tell him to fuck off politely. My next method would've involved less talking and more punching,' she admitted.

'Don't worry about it, he was being a jackass. I'm glad I stepped in before anything could happen. Did he hurt you?' Kara asked with concern.

'No,' the CEO reassured. 'Looks like you're - literally - my knight in shining armour,' she joked, referring to the sparkling sequin on her outfit. 

Kara chuckled and slightly dipped her head. 'My Lady.'

Lena laughed out loud for the first time in a long time and she hoped the dynamic with this beautiful stranger wasn't going to change after she revealed her name, but she knew not to have any expectations. 'Was it true what you said about him not actually getting his degree?' She wondered suddenly. 

The reporter nodded thoughtfully. 'It was a whole thing about how his parents had bribed the college into changing his grades so he could graduate with his friends without retaking some of his classes. I was still Cat's assistant at the time but I remember it being a big story for the magazine.'

'Cat?' The Luthor recognised. 'As in Cat Grant?'

Kara smiled brightly. 'Yes. Kara Danvers, CatCo Worldwide Media,' she introduced herself.

Lena hesitated. Moment of truth, she thought to herself. 'Lena Luthor, L-Corp.'

The Super's eyes widened. 'Luthor?' She didn't know why it hadn't occurred to her to look at a picture of the woman other than her Twitter profile picture during her extensive research a few hours earlier because the small image clearly didn't do her justice. With green eyes resembling Kryptonite, Kara knew the woman was going to have a similar effect on her.

The CEO pursed her lips. 'Sister of the crazy Superman hater, Lex Luthor? Yes.'

'Well, obviously,' Kara agreed. 'You look nothing like him, by the way. You're much more beautiful, it's hard to believe you share the same genes.'

Lena wasn't sure what threw her off more: how casually the reporter just complimented her or how easily she accepted that she was a Luthor without the pity and judgement it usually evoked. 'Um, thank you. He's my half-brother,' she offered as an explanation. 'Same father, different mother.' She frowned slightly. 'Do you know my brother?'

'Peripherally,' the blonde answered sheepishly. 'I followed the Superman v. Lex Luthor quite closely. His bald head appeared on my TV screen a lot.'

The businesswoman chuckled. 'Let's hope he inherited the baldness from Lillian and not our father.' 

'While I'm certain you can pull off a lot of looks, I can't quite picture you being hairless,' Kara teased.

Lena smiled at her. 'So, CatCo, huh? Are you collecting data for an article about the best and worst outfits of the night or is there something juicy going here?'

The reporter winced at the reminder of the article she was expected to write. 'The former, but my main task was to socialise with you,' she admitted. 'That's why your name sounded familiar.'

A tight-lipped smile graced the Luthor's red lips as her hopes came tumbling down. 'Looking for an exclusive, are we?' She asked with a raised eyebrow, it was meant as a tease but her heart wasn't in it. 

'No!' Kara almost yelled when she realised what must be going through the other woman's head. 'Well, that might be what he wants but I just wanted to get to know you. Everything I said, I meant it. I wasn't trying to butter you up or anything and I don't care about the exclusives and whatnots. Besides, reporting is about human connection and truth, not how well a story sells.' She knew she was rambling but she didn't know how to stop. 'I'll admit I did a bit of Internet-stalking before coming here, and I've read all these incredible things about you. I was really curious to meet you.'

Lena couldn't help the amusement on her face. 'Internet-stalking?'

The Super looked horrified. 'Did I say that? I meant research of course. I did some research. About you. On the internet.'

'Are you always this wordy?' The green eyed woman sounded almost endeared so Kara took it as a good sign. 

'Only when I'm nervous,' she promised.

Lena's polite manners were telling her to change the subject and ease the blonde's nerves but she had never met anyone as honest as her and she couldn't bring herself to do it. 'You're nervous?'

'Well, yeah,' Kara answered as if that was a given. 

'Why?'

She furrowed her brows and tried to rearrange her thoughts in a more orderly manner. 'Because you're Lena Luthor, a CEO, an engineer, a philanthropist, and a billionaire. You're a brilliant person with a bigger heart than anyone I've met tonight and you're incredibly attractive.'

Lena tilted her head curiously. 'Am I intimidating you?' 

'I'm intimidated,' Kara corrected. 'But in a good way.'

Sensing her desire to talk about something - anything - else, the Luthor mercifully returned to their previous topic. 'Did you find anything on the Internet that piqued your interest?'

Blue eyes lit up with excitement. 'Actually, I did. I read your paper about the utilisation of nanotechnology in cancer treatments and I had some questions if you don't mind talking about it?'

Lena looked positively impressed. 'I thought you were a reporter?'

'I am,' Kara assured. 'Both my biological and adoptive father were scientists, and my adoptive mother and sister are bio-engineers,' she explained. 'I always enjoyed science and I was lucky enough to grow up in an environment that fostered that desire for knowledge so I like delving into the nerdy stuff from time to time.'

'You're adopted?'

The blonde grinned at her selective attention. 'Yes, but that's a story for another time.'

It warmed Lena's heart that Kara believed there would be another time. Maybe her quest for friendship hadn't been fruitless after all. She returned the enthusiastic expression and raised an eyebrow. 'Alright, Miss Danvers. Show me your nerdy side.'

'Of course. I'm sure the damsel in distress air I seem to be emanating will win her over, never mind that my brother tried to kill her cousin,' Lena kidded.

'You'd be surprised. I heard she has quite the god complex. Letting her save you might be the best way to start a friendship with Supergirl,' Kara replied with a grin, poking fun at her alter ego in the process.

'She will,' the reporter assured certainly, her words almost sounding like a promise. 'What kind of superhero would she be if she didn't protect everyone in this city?'

She shook her head in mild disbelief. 'You're something else, Miss Danvers.'

Spending hours socialising with people you had nothing in common with and pretending to be interested in what they were saying, was mentally draining. By the time Kara's head hit her pillow, the reporter should have been ready to fall asleep, except the opposite was true. Meeting Lena was like a shot of caffeine and Kara was wide awake as the memories of the evening replayed in her mind like highlight reels: the grateful smile on the Luthor's face when she first laid her eyes on her, the laugh escaping her red lips when she managed to crack a funny joke, the goddamn smirk and raised eyebrow that still turned the blonde's insides into jelly despite them being blocks away in their own respective homes.

The more Kara thought about it, the more awake she became. She counted one thousand sheep before concluding that the supposedly sleep-inducing activity was unproductive. Jumping animals turned to pushups and burpees in the cramped space of her living room as the Kryptonian tried to tire herself out - her plan didn't work. Feeling more energised than before and fresh out of ideas, Kara crawled back into her bed, wishing that managing her sleep-wake cycle fell under one her superhuman abilities.

Whether it was the spontaneous workout or pure boredom that put her to sleep, Kara wasn't sure, but after four short hours, the morning sunlight was seeping through the curtain edges, awakening the Kryptonian. She felt far from rested but her drowsiness quickly dissipated as the events of the previous night trickled back into her conscious mind. With a smile on her face, Kara changed into her suit and took off for the clear orange-blue sky. Flying always provided her with a sense of peace and this morning was no different from the others. She watched with wonder as National City was slowly drawn out of its slumber and its inhabitants started their daily routines. Before long, the Super returned to her living quarters and changed into her Kara Danvers-outfit. The fresh dose of solar energy and oxygen was revitalising but there was one more thing Kara needed before she could start her workday - or well, two more things: food and good old sisterly advice.

'I needed to talk to you and you weren't home so I let myself in,' Kara replied defensively.

Winn glanced at his best friend before revealing, 'it's Jeremiah. I saw him trying to break into the DEO mainframe. I tried to stop him and there are a couple of agents down there trying to get to him, but he closed the door and he's practically unbeatable.'

That was all J'onn needed to hear before he rushed to the scene of the crime. Considering his Martian physiology, closed doors weren't an issue for him.

The crinkle between the Super's brows was ever present as she noticed her friend's pained posture. 'Are you hurt?'

'Just a couple of bruises,' Winn reassured. 'And I might have a light concussion, but I'll be fine. I don't know what he's looking for, but you have to stop your dad, Kara. If he's stealing intel for CADMUS...,' he trailed off, realising he was talking about his best friend's long lost parent. 'I'm sorry, Kara.'

Jaw clenched, Kara shook her head. 'No, you're right. Jeremiah's dangerous and he needs to be stopped. Can you open the doors from up here? I'm pretty sure the DEO alien-proofed their doors and I'll be useless if I can't get inside.'

Winn grimaced at the question. 'Jeremiah probably fried the system with his high-tech robot arm, but I can try.'

'Thanks, Winn.'

*

'What have you done?'

Jeremiah turned to the familiar voice and warned the Martian, 'J'onn, please, stand down. I don't want to hurt you.'

'I asked you a question,' J'onn demanded angrily. 'What have you done?'

'What I had to,' the Danvers parent replied, feeling his own temper rising.

J'onn's eyes landed on his cybernetic limb and he shook his head. 'Maggie was right to doubt you. You are a CADMUS man.'

'J'onn, please, it's not the whole story,' Jeremiah begged.

'I will not let you leave,' the Martian promised as he shapeshifted into his true form.

'You don't have a choice,' Jeremiah told him before reaching for the gun on the console. J'onn grabbed the weapon, preventing him from firing it, and used the moment of inattention to throw a punch and make the other man stumble backwards. Jeremiah recovered quickly and started fighting back until he was holding the alien down, his cybernetic arm raised, ready to strike the Martian down hard. Using his powers, J'onn got out of the way and threw the former DEO agent headfirst into the monitors. Ducking and punching followed as whatever data Jeremiah was looking for was being transferred to his flash drive. The CADMUS agent finally got the upper hand and pinned the DEO director against the wall before driving his enhanced fist into the Martian's face and knocking him out cold. Once the data transfer was done, he grabbed the flash drive and the gun, and aimed the bullets at the computers to slow down any of the DEO's efforts to track his movements. As he exited the room, he heard the metal door slide open and came face-to-face with a familiar face.

'Kara.' He smiled despite himself at the sight of the strong superhero before him.

Safe to say, the joyous expression was not returned. 'Jeremiah. I'm not letting you walk out of here.'

A groan resonated from the mainframe and Kara reflexively turned her head towards the sound. 'It's him or me, Kara,' Jeremiah told her. 'And he took quite a beating if I do say so myself. J'onn needs you, Kara.'

Kara knew he was right. If Jeremiah had somehow managed to overpower J'onn and leave him lying on the ground, then something must be extremely wrong. Despite the flurry of emotions she was experiencing at the moment, there was one feeling that stood out the most and that was how much she hated the fact that her father was knowingly using her compassion against her and treating it like a weakness. Without waiting any longer, she rushed towards the DEO director, giving her father the chance he needed to make his escape.

'J'onn?' The Super called out. She could see his wounds slowly closing up of their own accord. Another groan escaped the Martian's lips before he opened his eyes and tried to push himself up. Kara quickly offered her support and with most of his weight being effortlessly carried by the Kryptonian, J'onn managed to stand on his feet. The pair made their way to the med-bay where the agents were busy treating a conscious Alex and J'onn stumbled onto one of the beds.

A look of relief crossed Kara's face when she saw her sister - hurt, but for the most part, alive and well. 'Thank Rao, you're okay.'

'Couple of cracked ribs and too many bruises to count, but nothing time won't heal,' Alex replied with a tired smile. She motioned towards her boss and asked with concern, 'is he okay?'

'I will be,' the Martian assured, although the pained look on his face offered no comfort.

'Jeremiah got the jump on him,' Supergirl explained.

'He couldn't have,' Alex retorted immediately, wincing as she tried to sit up straighter. 'Hey, look, even if my dad has turned, there is no way he could have overpowered J'onn.'

'Yeah, that's what I would have thought,' J'onn agreed, his voice hoarse. 'Jeremiah's arm has been enhanced. Cybernetically.'

'Like Hank Henshaw?' The Super asked.

The Martian gave a solemn nod before turning his gaze to the eldest Danvers sibling. 'Jeremiah isn't who he was. I'm sorry.'

Alex felt her heart harden at the thought of her father and the mess they were in. 'Not as sorry as CADMUS is going to be.' A soft hand on her leg brought her back to reality and she felt the corners of her lips curl upwards in an attempted smile. 'How did you find me anyway?'

Kara retracted her hand and crossed her arms. 'Your subdermal tracker went online for a couple of minutes, but they had re-routed the IP address and sent us somewhere else. That's actually how we found Jeremiah,' she recounted. 'After that, Winn had the genius idea to track the weapons CADMUS was using and we got a signal when they moved you.'

'Actually, it was Jeremiah's idea to track the radiation signatures of the CADMUS weapons,' Winn corrected, his right hand holding up an ice pack against his head.

An uneasy silence fell upon the members of the Super team as the pieces of the puzzle started to fall into place.

'It was a set-up,' Maggie stated finally, voicing what the others were too afraid to say.

'Starting with the attack,' Alex concurred. Her breath caught in her chest as she recalled the previous night's ambush at Al's bar and realised her father had been using her as his pawn the whole time. Looking up, she was met with concerned eyes and genuine care, reminding her that while her father could not be trusted, these people had her back. 'My dad was there. We talked a little and he told me to meet him at the park at night.'

'Why didn't you tell me?' Kara asked without missing a beat and Alex knew her well enough that it was worry driving her question.

'He told me not to. He said it was to protect you,' Alex answered honestly and as the words left her mouth, she felt foolish for even believing her father for a moment. 'I'm such an idiot,' she muttered.

But Maggie was quick to disagree. 'Hey, don't say that. He had us all fooled.'

Kara couldn't count on both hands how many 'I told you so's Maggie had earned the right to utter thanks to her impeccable gut feeling. Nevertheless, she appreciated the fact that the cop was willing to put her pride aside to comfort her sister.

'And he's your dad, Alex. No one can blame you for wanting to believe he had good intentions,' the detective continued, her voice soft and understanding.

'What happened at the park?' J'onn prompted.

Alex gritted her teeth before spitting out, 'he never showed up. I thought CADMUS had gotten to him, so I headed home to grab my stuff and, I don't know.' She sighed, 'look for him? Or come to the DEO? Either way, I didn't get far. A black van pulled up and picked me right off the streets. I fought back and I tried to escape their custody a couple of times, hence my injuries.'

'He won't get away with this,' Kara vowed, betrayal and hurt fuelling her anger. 'We'll find him. We'll find all of them.'

Maggie admired the superhero's ambition, but she couldn't help but wonder, 'how?'

'Oh, okay,' Winn piped up suddenly. 'Um, don't be mad, but, I may have hid a tracker on Jeremiah.'

Kara locked eyes with her sister and she knew cracked bones and bodily trauma would not be enough to keep Alex Danvers from going after Jeremiah.

As if to prove her point, Alex sat up and slid off the medical bed, biting through the pain as she did so and nodded towards the tech guy. 'Let's go get him.'

'Alex, you need to rest,' J'onn reprimanded.

'You can barely keep your eyes open,' Alex noted, 'and someone has to go with Kara.'

'I'll go,' Maggie proposed, stepping forward to help the other woman stay steady on her feet. 'You are in no shape to face down your dad or any other CADMUS agents.'

The DEO agent disentangled herself from the detective. 'I can stand on my own and I can hold my gun, I'll be fine,' she dismissed their concerns, conveniently leaving out the part where it physically hurt to breathe.

'Alex-'

'Look, this is personal, okay?' Alex confessed, her voice cracking as she spoke. 'I'll stay behind until the coast is clear, but I need to face my dad. I need him to look me in the eye and admit-' She raised her hand and let it fall halfway, palm open as if to keep any painful emotions at bay. 'Admit what he's done.'

'We'll take a team with us,' the Super compromised. 'You can follow us from a distance until it's safe enough for you to come out and talk to him.'

Alex nodded. 'Thank you.'

* * *

'You're late, Danvers.'

Jeremiah slowed his pace as he approached the obscure meeting place. He spotted Hank Henshaw sharpening a piece of wood and instead of offering a response, he turned his attention to the mastermind behind the operation.

'Jeremiah, it's been too long,' Lillian Luthor greeted.

'There was a little more resistance from the DEO than I anticipated,' Jeremiah reported. 'Nothing I couldn't handle.' He raised his cybernetic fist to make his point.

'Did you get the files?' The CADMUS leader asked, getting straight to business.

Jeremiah fished out the flash drive from his pocket and handed it over, earning a satisfied smile from the Luthor.

Shots fired interrupted their reunion and the trio faced the culprit.

'It's over,' Supergirl announced as she floated a few feet above them.

'Not yet, but it will be. Soon,' Lillian assured.

'Jeremiah, you're coming with us,' Kara told him, leaving no room for debate.

'That's unlikely, dear,' the Luthor returned without hesitation.

A train blew its whistle in the distance, deafening in the quiet of the dark forest.

'Ka-boom,' Hank Henshaw vocalised and using her X-ray vision, Supergirl watched as two explosives sent pieces of the tracks flying.

'No!' She turned to Maggie. 'Train.'

Without lowering her guard or her weapon, Maggie nodded. 'Go.'

Hank used the split second of inattentiveness to knock down a tree to block their path.

'Damn it,' the cop cursed before motioning to the two DEO agents to go after the runners. 'I've got Jeremiah,' she told them as she broke into a run.

'Freeze!' She yelled once she close enough to take a shot at the former DEO agent. Maggie could see the man falter, but she couldn't tell if he would actually stop running. It wasn't until Alex emerged from the shadows with a steady 'Dad,' falling off her lips that Jeremiah came to a standstill.

Weapon and flashlight raised, cheeks stained with tears, Alex demanded, 'look at me!'

Maggie kept her gun aimed at the Danvers parent. It didn't look like he was armed, but she wasn't willing to bet Alex's life on that.

'Look at me,' Alex repeated more forcefully.

Jeremiah held his arms out and slowly turned around, as if a part of him had also been dreading this moment.

The DEO agent let out a sob as she stepped towards her father. 'How could you? How could you?'

'Would you believe me if I said I did it for you?' Jeremiah asked.

Confusion flashed across Alex's face. 'You betrayed everyone at the DEO. Your friends. Our family, everyone that I love.' The emotion in her words, in her voice was so raw, Maggie couldn't understand how unaffected her father seemed by it. 'You did that for me?' Alex questioned with disbelief.

'Family's complicated, honey,' was her father's reply.

'I'm bringing you in,' Alex spoke up.

As Jeremiah uttered his next words, Maggie knew they would leave a scar on the DEO agent. 'Not alive, you're not.'

Alex let out a pained gasp and gently shook her head. 'If you want to bring me in,' Jeremiah continued, 'you're going to have to shoot me.' The Danvers sister took a deep breath as she applied some pressure on the trigger. 'If you do it, I'll understand. You are always the best part of me.'

It was cruel how a parent could abuse their child's love for them for their own gain. Maggie found it especially cruel how Alex's father, the man she looked up to and adored, could give her an ultimatum to shoot him or let him escape, and claim she was the best part of him in the same breath.

Hands shaking and heart shattered, Alex lowered her gun and watched as her father turned his back on her. She exhaled heavily before crumbling to the cold ground, as if all her resilience and strength had left her body along with the air in her lungs. Maggie rushed towards her and held her trembling frame in her arms.

*

Winn was on his way to the DEO mainframe when he ran into the Danvers sisters. 'Hey, hey, hey,' he greeted, speeding up his pace to reach them. 'Any luck?'

'We lost,' Supergirl updated.

'I'm sorry, guys,' he spoke genuinely.

'On your feet already?' Kara asked an approaching figure.

A look over his shoulder revealed the DEO director walking towards them. 'No time to waste,' J'onn claimed. 'Any idea what he stole from us? What he gave to CADMUS?'

Winn rubbed his neck. 'Not yet. No, I have to go check down on the mainframe and then once we get the systems back online, I can run a diagnostic...'

J'onn nodded. 'Quick as you can. I want to know what we're up against.'

'Yes, sir.' The DEO agent took that as his cue to return to his mission.

'How are you both?' The Martian asked the siblings.

'It's been hard,' Kara answered honestly.

Her sister nodded in agreement, but didn't elaborate further.

'Alex?' J'onn prompted.

The elevator bell sounded and out stepped Eliza Danvers. 'Excuse me,' Alex told them before walking to her mother.

Kara watched the scene unfold, arms crossed, a troubled look on her face.

'What are you thinking?' J'onn wondered.

The blonde sighed, unsure how to answer that question. 'I'm... just worried. I'm worried about what this means for Alex, for me, for Jeremiah and Eliza, for our fight against CADMUS. I'm just worried it's going to change everything.'

'It will,' the Martian assured before placing a comforting hand on her shoulder. 'It will make us stronger.'

While Kara was usually a firm believer in hopeful outcomes, she didn't know how the betrayal and anguish she felt at the moment could ever be a source of strength. It would certainly make her furious, but that wasn't the kind of energy she wanted to feed on. Nevertheless, she nodded and offered a small smile.

*

Maggie took Alex home and despite their disagreements that morning, Kara was immensely grateful for the detective's presence in her sister's life. To her surprise, Winn was waiting for her outside of the changing rooms.

She smiled tiredly at her best friend. 'You should go home.'

'I will,' Winn assured as they made their way to the exit. 'After I walk you home.'

Despite the emotionally and physically draining day she had had, Kara found it in her to snort at the man's offer. 'You do know I can take care of myself should I get jumped on my way home, right? In fact, I'm not really sure how you could protect Supergirl from a mugger.'

'Oh, trust me. In the event of a mugging, I'm leaving all the heroics to you,' Winn told her certainly. 'I just thought you could use the company.'

To be honest, Kara hadn't put much thought into how she would spend anything that exceeded the following two minutes. It suddenly dawned on her that she would come home to a dark, empty apartment and she appreciated her friend's thoughtfulness. 'Thanks, Winn.'

'Listen, I know what it's like to have a villain for a parent,' Winn started as they stepped into the dimly lit streets of National City. 'It sucks as hell and you keep asking yourself over and over again why they do the things that they do. Chances are you will never really understand their behaviour or their motives, or how they could tear your family apart like that. It uh, it hurts, and you may never get the closure you're looking for. You'll want to hate them for all the pain they caused, but a part of you will always love them because you're still a kid and they're still your parent.' He paused. 'Those are a lot of big emotions to process, even for Supergirl.'

Kara felt her heart warm up at the concern Winn was showing her and it reminded her of their CatCo days when their schedules were less hectic and they were each other's confidantes. 'You don't have to worry about me, Winn. I'll be okay.'

'I do, though,' Winn insisted. 'These past few days, today especially, have been rough and you shouldn't have to carry it on your own. I know Alex is usually your emotional support human and I would gladly offer my 'best friend' services, but I have a feeling there's a certain someone you would rather call.'

The blonde let out a shaky breath into the cool air and pushed her glasses up her nose, a nervous habit. 'It's really late, I don't want to bother Lena. Besides, she was really upset when I left her today.'

'Understandingly, you had just told her her mother was the leader of an illegal, not to mention immoral anti-alien organisation,' Winn pointed out.

'I know, I was there,' Kara reminded dryly. 'I just... I don't know if she'll want to hear from me.'

'You'll never know unless you try,' the former CatCo employee stated simply.

Kara stayed quiet for a while. As soon as the words had left her mouth, she knew it was her own insecurity talking. Previous experiences had taught her to be wary of relationships, still, a part of her trusted that Lena would be there for her, unconditionally. It was almost unsettling how comfortable and easy it was to be herself around the other woman.

Kara was too deep in thought to notice that her feet had automatically led them to her apartment until they stopped in front of her door. She dug out her keys from her purse and unlocked the door before turning to her friend. 'Do you want to come inside? You can crash at my place if you'd like.'

Winn gave her a grateful smile and shook his head. 'I'm going to head home.'

Kara nodded. 'Thanks for walking me home, I really appreciate it,' she said and she meant it.

'You're not too bad company yourself,' Winn teased.

The blonde hugged the man as humanely tight as possible before pulling away and leaning against the open door. 'I'll see you tomorrow.'

'Technically, you'll see me later,' Winn corrected before slowly retreating back to the stairwell. 'Call her.'

'It's 2 in the morning.'

'Call her.'

Kara huffed and smiled. 'Alright, alright. I'll call Lena.'

*

Winn was right. Kara really should not be alone with her thoughts right now and while reaching out to Lena scared her, the idea of spending the next couple of hours drowning in her fears, her doubts and her pain scared her even more. So, she picked up her phone... and put it back down on her coffee table. She repeated this a number of times until she finally built up the courage to actually unlock her phone and call the Luthor.

The rational part of her was not expecting Lena to answer, however, her emotional self was really hoping she would. To her surprise and delight, Lena picked up the call after three rings.

'Lena Luthor,' a groggy voice greeted Kara and just the sound of Lena waking up was enough to render the blonde speechless for a solid five seconds.

'Hello?'

'Lena, hi,' Kara breathed out. 'It's Kara.'

'Are you okay? Are you in danger?' Lena questioned worriedly, suddenly sounding more alert than she had a few moments before.

'I'm safe,' Kara replied and it was the truth. Perceptive as ever, Lena didn't miss how she failed to answer her first question. 'I'm sorry, did I wake you?'

Lena shook her head even though the blonde couldn't see her. 'I hadn't reached my deep sleep yet, I was just resting my eyes.'

Kara chuckled at Lena's roundabout way of saying 'yes.' 'I'm sorry, I should let you get back to sleep.'

Lena was wide awake now. 'Why did you call, Kara?' She prompted gently. Going by the late hour and Kara's uncharacteristically unsteady tone, Lena had a feeling the blonde was in dire need of emotional support, but she still needed a confirmation before showing up unannounced at the other woman's apartment at 2:30 AM.

The breath Kara let out trembled from the unshed tears and the lump in her throat. 'Today has been hard and I really don't want to be alone right now,' she confessed, too exhausted to tell anything but the truth.

With her phone trapped between her head and her shoulder, the Luthor nodded as she changed into some casual daytime clothing. 'I'm on my way. I'll be there in twenty.'

*

With a blanket covering her body, Kara lay curled up on her couch in the foetal position. A knock announced Lena's arrival and Kara invited her in with a hoarse, 'it's unlocked.'

'Hey,' Lena greeted softly as she closed the door behind her and approached the resting figure. 'Are you okay?'

'No.' Lena appreciated the honesty.

'What's wrong? What happened?' She asked as she stood near the edge of the couch, wanting to offer closeness and at the same time, hesitant to cross any emotional boundaries.

Kara looked up upon hearing how close the other woman actually was, the lack of clicking heels taking her off guard for some reason. Sure enough, Lena was wearing white sneakers, having opted for comfort over fashion that night.

The vulnerable look in the blue eyes she had grown to adore spurred Lena on to take her shoes off and take a seat on the couch. It was unnerving to see Kara this defeated and disheartened, and she knew whatever happened that day must've been harrowing at best and traumatic at worst. Lifting the blanket, she said, 'hey, come here.'

Kara didn't need to be told twice. She sat up and sunk into Lena's body, finding comfort there even in the darkest of times. Lena held her tightly, pouring all the care and affection she felt for the woman in the embrace. Despite their argument earlier that day, Lena knew she couldn't turn the blonde away, and while they still had to talk about their previous interaction, Kara's current needs took precedence over that conversation. It was clumsy how they had stumbled into each other and into their relationship, but Lena wanted the other woman to know that she would show up for her no matter what.

'We found Alex,' Kara's quiet voice brought her back to the living room in apartment 4A. Sadness hung in the air despite the objectively positive news and Lena patiently waited for Kara to share whatever was weighing on her mind. 'You were right. I shouldn't have trusted my father so easily. He's working for CADMUS.' While those four words hardly covered the depth of the betrayal by her adoptive parent, it was enough for Lena to understand the pain she was going through.

Words couldn't fill the space left behind by the loss of a loved one, still, Lena gently pointed out, 'you couldn't have known that he had switched sides, Kara.'

'I should've trusted Maggie. I should've trusted you,' the blonde regretted.

The Luthor brought her hand under Kara's chin and lifted her head to meet her gaze. 'Hey, don't be so hard on yourself. It's perfectly normal to want to believe that your parents care for you, it's human nature. Babies literally depend on their caregivers to keep them alive and facing a reality wherein your parents fail to protect you can be so overwhelming and... painful, that you'll do anything to keep seeing them in a good light, including disregarding what others or your gut is telling you.' Lena would know, she had gone through a similar process upon hearing the news that her mother was the leader of CADMUS.

Kara stayed silent as the words drifted around in her head before her brain stored them for future purposes. The wound was too fresh for her to actually process what Lena was saying, but she had a feeling her words would mean a lot when she was in the right headspace to hear them.

'Your heart and your compassion are what makes you the strongest person I've ever met. Don't let anyone convince you otherwise,' Lena asserted firmly, picking up on the Super's insecurities like a fine tuned antenna.

Hearing Lena's heartbeat, feeling her body pressed against hers, and witnessing Lena's unwavering belief in who Kara was, was enough to stop the world from crumbling under Kara's feet. The blonde gazed at the Luthor before reaching up and brushing her lips against Lena's, catching her in an unexpected, but tender kiss.

Lena chuckled softly once they had pulled away and pushed the blonde's hair out of her eyes.

'Thank you for coming,' Kara expressed genuinely.

'I'm glad you called me,' Lena returned honestly. She wasn't going to ask how she was feeling since she suspected it would take a while before the other woman would truly feel okay again. Instead, she wondered, 'what do you need?'

'Just be here with me,' Kara requested simply. 'And wake up with me.'

Although it had come out as a statement, Lena could hear the question in her voice. 

'Hey,' she said, causing the Kryptonian to look up. She delicately wiped a stray tear on Kara's cheek with her thumb and told her, 'I'd love to,' before placing a soft kiss on her forehead.
