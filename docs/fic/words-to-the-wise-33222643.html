Words to the wise

“I’m sorry, you want me to do what ?”

 

Kara pouted, resting her chin on the back of the couch as she watched Lena pour the popcorn into two bowls in her kitchen.  “Oh come on, it’ll be fun.  Like working together on a sneaky spy mission.”

 

Lena laughed, walking over to join Kara on the couch and handing her a bowl.  “Kara, I’m not going to couples counselling with you so you can spy on your sister’s girlfriend.”

 

“It’s not spying …”

 

“You literally just called it a ‘sneaky spy mission.’”

 

Kara huffed, slumping back into the corner of the couch and shoving a handful of popcorn in her mouth.  “I don’t understand why Alex is being so secretive about it.”

 

Lena rolled her eyes.  “It’s a new relationship, Kara.  Maybe she just wants to have it to herself for a while.”

 

“But she knows how much I love knowing things,” Kara whined.  “I just want to meet the woman that has my sister all giddy.”

 

Lena sighed, knowing she never stood a chance of saying no to Kara.  “Just one session, right?”

 

Kara lit up, putting her already empty popcorn bowl on the coffee table and scooting along the couch to excitedly press herself as close to Lena as possible.  “You’ll do it?”

 

Lena raised an eyebrow.  “You do realise that when Alex eventually does introduce the two of you she’ll realise what you did?”

 

Kara grinned smugly, taking a piece of popcorn from Lena’s bowl.  “Nope.  We’re going to be in disguise.”

 

———

 

Lena looked around the sparse waiting room, trying to curb her rising anxiety.  The pale blue painted walls, the soft leather chairs, the smell of cleaning products and air freshener were all too familiar to achieve their intended sense of false security.  The quiet clacking of the receptionist’s keyboard to the out of time metronome of the clock on the wall did nothing to help.

 

Kara was sitting beside her, reading a magazine from the side table, blissfully unaware of all the catastrophic potential scenarios Lena’s brain was coming up with.  She was wearing a tweed jacket with elbow patches that she had apparently found at a thrift store for seven dollars, along with a pair of circular glasses with thin wire frames.  The dark wash jeans and brown oxfords were from her usual collection of work attire but she had assured Lena it was a foolproof disguise.  

 

Kara had persuaded Lena to come in a knitted cream sweater and soft jeans.  She had also somehow found Lena’s reading glasses (that she had seen her wear one time and never stopped asking about) and made her wear those.  Lena was unconvinced but when had she ever been able to say no to Kara?

 

“Kara and Lena?”  

 

Kara practically leapt out her seat to go and excitedly introduce herself to the woman who had just appeared in the doorway.  “Hi!  That’s me.  Kara D- ...Kieran.  Kara D. Kieran.  Yep.  That’s my name.  The D is for my middle name, which is… Dave.”

 

The woman slowly nodded and shook her hand.  “Dr Kelly Olsen.  Nice to meet you.”

 

Kelly led them down a hallway and into a room that was clearly another attempt to seem cozy.  The carpeted floor was a little worn but nice and there was a red Moroccan rug under the coffee table.  The walls were a warm off-white and held shelves of self help books and succulents.  At least it wasn’t the dark reds and expensive abstract paintings of therapists Lillian used to find.

 

“Please, have a seat.”  Kelly gestured to an old couch with a blanket thrown over the back as she closed the door and sat across the coffee table from them in a cozy brown leather armchair.  Maybe it wasn’t so bad.

 

“So what brings you two here today?”

 

There was a moment of silence where it occurred to both of them that they probably should have come up with a story, before Kara blurted out.  “Lena has lots of unresolved childhood trauma and unhealthy coping mechanisms.”

 

Lena turned to her indignantly.  “ I have childhood trauma?  You can talk!”

 

Kelly watched in mild horror as the two of them argued over who’s trauma was worse, revealing more and more concerning things that definitely should have been addressed before now.  

 

She cleared her throat loudly enough for the two of them to stop, turning back to her with wide eyes as they remembered she was in the room too.  “While those are all very important issues that you should definitely talk to a therapist about - please , dear lord, see a therapist - they are actually more… individual issues, and this is a couples therapy session.  So why don’t we just start with-”

 

“Are those for eating?”

 

Kelly stopped, staring at Kara who was pointing at a bowl of sweets on the coffee table hopefully.  “Yes.”

 

Kara lit up, taking two candies from the bowl and offering one to Lena.  Lena shook her head but caved at Kara’s pout and took it.  The candy was halfway to her mouth when she caught sight of Kelly’s curious look.  She tried to eat it as inconspicuously as possible. 

 

Kelly looked between them in thought.  “What do you two know about boundaries?”

 

Kara grinned, throwing her arms around Lena and resting her head on Lena’s shoulder.  “We don’t have any boundaries because we’re best fr- wives!  The best wives!”

 

Her grin lessened gradually the longer the ensuing silence went on and dread pooled in Lena’s stomach as Kelly wrote something in her little black notebook.

 

She smiled up at them in that way that only therapists who are about to gently tell you how messed up you are do.  “Why don’t we start there.”

 

———

 

Kara drummed her fingers slowly on the steering wheel.  

 

They were sitting in the parking lot, staring blankly out at the road in front of the therapy center.

 

It was quiet apart from the occasional woosh of a passing car.

 

“Maybe we should come back again next week to find out more.”

 

Lena nodded, looking over at her.  “...About Kelly.”

 

A red car drove by.

 

Kara hummed.  “Yeah.  About Kelly.”

 

———

 

“So then I said ‘sorry, but it looks like your time is of- fish -ially up.’  Get it?  Because we were at a fishmongers!”  Kara drummed on the DEO mission table as she dissolved into a fit of giggles at her own story.

 

Lena just shook her head, trying not to let her smile grow too wide as she scribbled numbers into the margins of an open case file.

 

Kara sighed as her giggles died down, picking up her milkshake from the pile of food she’d brought in, and turned around to lean back against the table with a grin.  “And then I punched him really hard.”

 

Lena couldn’t tell if the flex of a bicep very close in her peripheral vision was intentional or not but she cleared her throat, brushing crumbs off the case file to distract from her rising blush.

 

“KARA!”

 

Kara froze mid-slurp of her milkshake as her name echoed forebodingly through the halls of the DEO, accompanied by the angry stomping of boots.

 

Every DEO agent in the vicinity fell silent, quickly pretending to go back to work.

 

Alex stormed into the main mission room, stopping in front of Kara to glare at her with a look that rivaled Kara’s laservision in lethality.  Lena’s attempt to subtly sneak away was stopped by Alex pointing at her the moment she moved, never taking her eyes off Kara.  “You.  Stay.”

 

Lena abandoned her attempts to escape and opted instead for standing as still as possible.

 

Alex crossed her arms.  “Kara.  Care to tell me why I just showed a picture of the two of us to my girlfriend to be met with the response ‘oh I didn’t know your sister was married to Lena Luthor’?”

 

The milkshake straw made a pathetic sputtering sound and Kara let it flop out her mouth back into the cup.  “Aw shucks.  I can’t believe she saw through our disguises.”

 

———

 

Alex paced in front of the one-way mirror, Kara and Lena sitting at the metal table and watching like scolded children.  The harsh light and grey walls of the interrogation room were doing nothing to help the situation.

 

“So let me get this straight-”

 

She stopped and glared at Kara when she giggled.  Kara cleared her throat awkwardly, looking down.

 

Alex took a deep breath and started pacing again.  “So the two of you tracked down my girlfriend, pretended to be a couple, and have been going to therapy for the past month to spy on her.”

 

Neither of them said anything, looking guiltily down at the table.

 

“I might have expected this from Kara but really, Lena?”

 

Kara grasped Lena’s hand.  “Hey don’t be mean to my wife!”

 

She deflated when Alex didn’t laugh.

 

Alex sighed.  “I just wanted to enjoy having something that was just about me for a little while.  Was that too much to ask?”

 

Kara bit her lip, twisting her fingers together.  “Hey, Lena, would you give us a moment please?”

 

Lena nodded and stood quietly.  “I’ll just wait outside.”

 

Kara waited until the door clicked closed to gently pat the chair beside her.  Alex sighed and sat down.

 

“I’m sorry, Kara, I know I’m overreacting-”

 

“No, no you’re not.”  Kara sighed, turning to face Alex and taking her hands.  “I’m so sorry for what I did.  I crossed a boundary and that’s not okay.  I- I’ve been learning things in therapy.  And I know I can’t keep seeing Kelly but Alex…”  She swallowed, holding Alex’s hands a little tighter.  “Alex, I think I want to see a therapist.”

 

Alex pulled her into a tight hug and Kara released a deep breath.  “I’m really glad, Kara.  I will support you all the way.”

 

She smiled into Alex’s shoulder.  “And I will respect that you don’t have to share everything with me.”

 

Alex laughed, letting go of Kara.  “I can’t believe you managed to trick yourself into going to therapy.”

 

Kara groaned and shook her head.  “I know.”  

 

Their giggles lapsed into a comfortable silence.

 

Kara bumped their shoulders together.  “So… Kelly, huh?”

 

A blush rose on Alex’s cheeks as she smiled dopily.  “She’s the best.”

 

Kara grinned and pulled her feet up onto the chair to stare at Alex excitedly over her knees while she gushed about her girlfriend.

 

“… and when she smiles her eyes go all crinkly and cute and- oh!  And yesterday when we were making pancakes she-”  Alex cut off as she noticed Kara staring happily at her as she rambled on.  “What?”

 

Kara shrugged.  “I’m just happy for you.”

 

“Well maybe I’ll let you meet her properly if you promise to behave.”

 

Kara laughed and halfheartedly dodged Alex’s swats at her.  “I’m always well behaved.”

 

Alex rolled her eyes.  “Sure.”

 

Kara smiled, glad that Alex had seemingly forgiven her.  She bit her lip.  “Hey, Alex?  Did Kelly really think Lena and I were a couple?”

 

Alex grinned.  “She said you were an adorable couple and she could tell how happy you made each other.”

 

Kara smiled shyly at the idea.  “Do you think there’s a chance Lena might want that kind of relationship?  With me?”

 

Alex smacked her over the head.

 

“Ow!  What was that for?”

 

Alex rolled her eyes.  “That was for being such a doofus.  Of course she does!  We’ve all been complaining about it behind your backs for the past four years.”

 

Kara wasn’t sure whether to be offended or excited.

 

Alex’s smile softened.  She stood up and gestured to the door.  “Now go ask the one person that really matters.”

 

Kara hesitated.  “But-”

 

“No buts.  I’m leaving and sending her in so you better be ready.”  

 

Kara leapt up and supersped in front of Alex as she went to leave.  “Whoa wait no!  I’m not ready!”

 

Alex tried to side step her but Kara moved with her.  “Tough.  Now move.”

 

“No!  Kelly would not approve of this!”

 

“Well Kelly isn’t here right now.  And you did a dumb thing about my girlfriend so now I get to do this as payback.”

 

“That’s not how this is supposed to work!”

 

They both froze as the handle of the soundproof door to the interrogation room turned.

 

“Alex?  J’onn said I’d find you in-”  Kelly cut off as she took in the scene before her.

 

“Oh.  Hey Kelly.”  Kara smiled awkwardly from where Alex had her in a headlock as she grappled Alex’s waist.  “Nice to meet you… again.”

 

Kelly looked halfway between shocked and amused.  “Nice to meet you again too, Kara Dave Danvers.”

 

Alex frowned confusedly down at Kara as Kara laughed sheepishly.  They both seemed to realise at the same moment that they were still locked in the middle of their wrestling match and let go, straightening up as if nothing had happened.

 

A mischievous smile that Kara did not like spread across Alex’s face.  She stepped forwards, kissing Kelly’s cheek and interlacing their hands in an adorable display that devastatingly made Kara drop her defenses for a moment.

 

“We should go - Kara wants to talk to Lena.  I’ll explain on the way out.”  Before Kara’s brain could catch up, Alex turned and yelled down the hall, “hey Lena!  Get in here - Kara wants to ask you something important!”

 

She smiled smugly as she stepped out into the hallway with Kelly.  “Bye, Kara.  Have fun.”

 

Kara gulped as Lena entered, greeting Kelly a little sheepishly as she passed.  Alex closed the door behind them with a click.

 

“Is everything okay?”

 

Kara gulped.  “Mhm.”

 

Lena stepped closer, frowning in concern.

 

“I’m sorry I pressured you into going to see Kelly with me,” Kara blurted.

 

Lena smiled, giving a nod in acknowledgement.  “It’s okay.  I need to learn to say no sometimes too.  But Alex said you had a question?”

 

Kara swallowed thickly and took a deep breath.  “I’m in love with you.”

 

Lena’s breath hitched, her heart beating a little faster, and she slowly started to step closer again until they were almost pressed up against each other, her eyes on Kara’s lips.  “Still not a question.”

 

Kara brought a hand up to gently cup Lena’s jaw and she leaned into the touch.  “Can I kiss you?”

 

In answer, Lena pushed up on her toes and connected their lips, arms coming up to cling around Kara’s neck.  Kara’s arms wound around her waist as she melted into it.

 

Alex’s voice crackled over the speakers.  “You two are disgustingly adorable.”

 

Lena huffed a laugh against Kara’s lips and flopped back down onto her heels, burying her face in the space between Kara’s neck and shoulder as Kara glared at the one-way mirror.

 

“Now go get a room because we actually need this one for an interrogation.”

 

Kara poked her tongue out at Alex, using her x-ray vision to see where she was, but it was difficult to stay mad with Lena wrapped up in her arms.

 

She vaguely heard Kelly pleadingly murmur something about hiring a DEO therapist.
