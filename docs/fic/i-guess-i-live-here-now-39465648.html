Lena moves into Kara's apartment while Kara is in the Phantom Zone, and never moves back out.

Moving In

The Super Friends all noticed she was around the Tower a lot, but none of them put it together right away.  It had been two weeks since Lex had ousted Lena from LuthorCorp. Two weeks since Lena felt safe going home to her penthouse. Two weeks since she assumed she’d be evicted soon anyway, and had just packed three bags of clothes and necessities, tucked them into the truck of one of her cars, the sensible bulletproof Audi that was generic enough to blend in, but also safe. As safe as a car can be when you have a homicidal genius for a brother. Two weeks of being at the Tower before anyone else, working on gadgets to help fight the phantoms, to help find Kara, and to help fight whatever else Lex might come up with. Two weeks of being the last one to leave, promising to go home soon, once she finishes this one last thing. 

Its Kelly who finally notices that Lena’s car has been parked in the same spot for as long as she can remember now, and that there is a small blanket, neatly folded and tucked down low in one of the bookshelves in the Tower’s living room area close to the couches. And its Kelly who suggests to Alex that Lena might appreciate being asked to look after Kara’s apartment, to help heal the wounds of past distrust and animosities, and because without her LuthorCorp job, and Luthor trust fund, she’s likely going to have to start looking for housing soon. It’s an olive branch. She doesn’t mention that she suspects Lena is currently homeless, she knows Lena isn’t ready to talk about it, and Alex, as much as Kelly loves her, is as subtle as a sledgehammer.

“Hey, I know you’re about to join me in the world of unemployment, and probably are going to have to give up your fancy penthouse sooner rather than later, so Kelly suggested that you might want to take care of Kara’s place while she’s, you know, gone.” As subtle as a sledgehammer. 

Alex offers, and Lena accepts the olive branch, and the housing, and begins leaving the Tower every night. She’s still there before everyone else, but her car moves from its permanent parking spot. Lena takes some comfort, as well as plenty of torture, from sleeping in Kara’s bed, washing Kara’s dishes, and watering her plants each day. It’s a constant reminder of her just what she’s been missing, for over a year now. 

The first night in Kara’s apartment, she made it inside, bringing her three bags of belongings up from her car, and immediately broke down once the door was closed behind her. Without anyone to see her, knowing Kara couldn’t and wouldn’t hear her, she cried herself to sleep on the couch, still in her clothes from that day. 

When she wakes up on Kara’s couch, still in her clothes, face pushed into the corner that Kara should be sitting in, she pulls herself up, treks over to the small kitchenette, opening the top left cupboard and finds her tea, she had brought a box to Kara’s a few years back, and she’s relieved its still there. It’s been 13 months since she’s been here drinking tea with Kara. She flips the box over and checks the expiration date, preparing herself to drink some sad, weak tea that’s out of date and has lost its potency, but the box is new. The best by date is another 12 months out. With this knowledge, that Kara kept buying her tea, even though they were barely talking, she finally lets herself acknowledge the thing she’s been actively avoiding for years. Lena Keiran Luthor is in love with her best friend, former best friend, whatever you can call her now, and that is why all this hurts so much. 

Somehow, knowing that Kara is in the Phantom Zone, sent there by her own shit brother, right after she and Kara were finally able to start making their peace, it’s somehow worse than the year they spent estranged and “hating” each other. It’s worse, she admits to herself, finally, because that entire time, she knew that if her stupid pride broke, she could call Kara, and Kara would pick up. Even if they were still fighting, Kara would answer her signal watch. Even if she pointed her shit brother’s kryptonite canons at her, Kara would still be there. 

But now, now she is here in Kara’s homey apartment, mostly invited, and she can’t talk to Kara. She’s in the place that felt the most like home to her, since she was four years old and whisked off to Luthor Manor. And now she’s finally admitted to herself just how hurt she was.
