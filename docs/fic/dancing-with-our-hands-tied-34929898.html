“No, I'm enjoying myself here, actually.”

 

“Lena.”

 

“Kara.” Lena gave her a knowing look that Kara almost failed to decipher if it wasn't for the subtle blush on Lena's cheeks.

 

The two of them stared at each other for almost a minute, gaze acting like a tennis ball as it went back and forth, sending signals that were very much reciprocated.

 

“Oh.”

 

“Yes, oh.”

 

Kara knew exactly what Lena was suggesting, and who was she to say no?

 

 

And somehow, Kara was thankful for two things that night. Her job that was completely illegal, and Lena's strangely attractive (+sexy) accent that started this whole kidnapping fiasco thing in the first place.
