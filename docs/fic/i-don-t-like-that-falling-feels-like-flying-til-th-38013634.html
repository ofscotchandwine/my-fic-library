I don't like that falling feels like flying 'til the bone crush

"I see time has only made you stronger," Lena hears the new voice coming from Kara's earpiece. It's low, and sultry, and completely unknown.

"Wonder Girl," Kara answers easily. Lena can hear the smile in her voice, and she feels like ice water is dripping on her back, for some reason, "I didn't know you were in National City."

"I saw certain announcement on TV, I figured it was a good excuse to come back and visit an old friend." The voice says, still in the same tone, and from her spot, in front of the monitors at The Tower Lena has the urge to intervene.

"Supergirl, status?"

"Ah, Lena. Yes, the dominator is contained, easy-peasy. You know they're quite weak, well, I don't think you know. I didn't really battle them here, or with you in the know," Kara sounds... nervous. More Kara Danvers than Kara Zor-El, very rambly and there is no other possible reason but the company she has over there. "I'm gonna drop it at the DEO. Brunch still standing for tomorrow?" 

"Yes, of course," She answers without any doubt, and waits for Kara's usual reminder of no kale, but she gets something else. A hurried,

"Okay, cool, see ya," and Kara's earpiece goes completely silent, clearly disconnected, and Lena feels like she's been dunked on ice.

Sleep doesn't come easy that night, and Lena can't quite put her finger on why she's so rattled about Kara's company in the field. Maybe it was not knowing; the mystery. Or maybe it was Kara's reaction to it, nervous, yet smiley. Flustered, perhaps. She slept, but her dreams were plagued by faceless women simply taking Kara away, which was ridiculous because there was no reason for that to be a nightmare, right?

Lena arrives for brunch, at their selected restaurant, 5 minutes late. She expects Kara to be looking forlornly at the menu. Instead, she finds her sitting at a table, laughing loudly with a beautiful brunette sitting next to her.  She feels frozen, and it's only her upbringing with Lillian and her years as a woman in a male dominating world, that she can keep walking and smile as if nothing. "Kara." She says as a greeting, standing next to her best friend.

"Lena!" Kara stood, and so did her companion with a smile plastered on her lips. Kara hugged her, still smiling as if she hadn't invited a stranger to their weekly brunch. "Hi! Let me introduce you to Donna Troy. Donna, this is Lena Luthor.”

“Pleasure to meet you, Miss Luthor,” the brunette says, extending her hand.

Lena, of course took it, (and if she squeezed her hand a little too hard, and the lights of the place flickered a little, it was because she was stressed at meeting new people who would quickly judge her for her last name, nothing else).

“Likewise,” She says, letting Donna’s hand go. She was seemingly more interested in the lights than her hand. All three took their seats, and commented nothing about what was clearly her magic acting out, (at least it should be as clear to Kara, as it was to herself). “So, how did you two meet?” The question was out of her mouth almost without her noticing, but the truth is that she recognized Donna’s voice as the one from the night before. The voice of Wonder Girl.

“Ah, Donna was my college roommate.” Kara quickly says, opening the menu in front of her. Too quick. Too short and cutting.

“Ouch, KD. Is that how you remember me? Just the college roommate?” Donna’s voice was teasing, and Kara was a shade closer to her cape than usual. Lena’s hair at the nape of her neck was standing up, she cleared her throat, a smidge uncomfortable at the tension.

“N-you–It’s– Donna.” Her best friend spluttered for a second, before just glaring at her former roommate, who simply laughed.

“Not like you left me heartbroken or anything,” Donna teases. Lena hopes it is just a tease. Because if the last year taught her anything, was that losing her best friend was worse than losing anything else. And whatever Kara and Donna had going on, it felt suspiciously close to that. Losing Kara.

“Rao. You’re being ridiculous.” Kara turns to Lena and as if in confidence simply says, “Just ignore her, she likes to see me blush. Do you guys want to order? I’m starving.”

“When are you not?” Like in a fucking nightmare, Donna and herself ask in unison.

Yes, this is definitely a nightmare.  

Brunch is a tense affair. At least it was to her. Kara seemed oblivious to anything of the sort, and Donna was classy, and charming. Looking at Kara like she understands her better than anyone. And maybe she did, what does Lena know, anyways? If she was correct, and Donna was Wonder Girl, maybe she understood Kara way better than her. Living a double life, under the shadow of a very well known superhero. Not that Kara was still hiding her identity, but hiding her true self during her formative years, was still such a defining trait. Half an hour past noon, Lena excuses herself, saying that she has a meeting with a possible investor for the foundation. (It’s not a lie, but the meeting it’s at 4:00 PM, so…) She insists on paying, and leaves Kara and Donna alone.

She shows up at the Danvers-Olsen’s doorstep 15 minutes to 7:00 PM, to help Kelly with Esme, while Alex had her sister’s night with Kara. Or, at least that was the plan, because Alex is the one who opens the door. Her confusion must be clear in her face because Alex simply just moves out of the way and shrugs.

“She canceled on me. Some friend is visiting or something. Wine?” 

“Sure. Why didn’t Kelly call to cancel? I’m sure you guys want to enjoy your family time.”

Alex looks at her disapprovingly, after getting three glasses from the cupboard.

“When will that big head of yours understand that you are part of this family?” Alex says serving the wine. “Besides, unless there was a world ending catastrophe, Esme likes to spend time with you. She's been waiting for you all day. She loved the fireworks you showed her the last time. Very annoying not having a magical miniature fireworks show when she’s being stubborn and doesn’t wanna nap.”

Lena accepts the wine when Alex passes her the glass and sits on the comfortable sofa.

“Yeah, well, sucks to suck…”

“Language. That girl is like a sponge,” Alex comments in a murmur looking at the bathroom door. Lena nods. 

“Maybe I can build her a projector or something like that. Would make nap time easier.”

“Or…” Kelly’s voice floats to them, emerging with Esme from the bathroom, “We stop spoiling her rotten and set some rules and boundaries. I know, crazy idea.”

Lena’s would be witty retort, ends when the 6-years-old flings herself to her with an excited “Aunt Lena!” and starts with a fast paced recount of her entire day and all the stuff she did at school that week. 

Alex put Esme to bed after dinner (and her magical fireworks show), a bottle and a half of wine had been consumed between all three adults, with easy conversation, and Lena felt warm, with her chosen family.

“It’s very unlike Kara to cancel so last minute,” Kelly comments, taking a sip of her wine “And is really weird she hasn’t shown up yet,” Lena involuntarily tenses up. Something Alex notices immediately, if her eyes focusing on her are any indication. Lena hasn’t even finished the thought when Alex simply says, 

“Talk.”

Lena taps nervously at her glass, looking for the appropriate words to explain whatever she’s feeling about it. Alex and Kelly don’t push, simply wait for her to order her thoughts.

“I simply don’t know what to make of Donna,” She says, and whatever else she would have said dies on her throat when Alex pretty much spits the wine she had just put on her mouth. Kelly rubs her wife back trying to calm down the fit of coughs she had devolved into, and Lena is a little lost at the reaction.

“Donna? As in Donna Troy?” Alex splutters.

“Yeah. I’m not crazy and she’s Wonder Girl, right? Like, it’s no coincidence that Wonder Woman's sidekick shows up to help Supergirl, out of nowhere by the way, and the next day Donna waltzes to brunch with Kara.” Lena rambles to Alex, ignoring the weird look both women share.

“I mean, yeah, that’s no coincidence. I guess Kara canceling now makes a whole lot of sense,” Alex comments, cleaning the wine stains from her hands with a napkin.

“What do you mean?” Her wife asks, just as curious as Lena herself.

“Well, they have history together. Donna is sort of Kara’s ex,” Alex says, and for unknown reasons, the air in Lena’s lungs simply evaporates, and she’s incapable of just breathing for a few seconds.

“No,” She says with simplicity and ignores again the shared look that Alex and Kelly exchange. “Kara's straight. If she weren’t I would know, I’m her best friend. I mean, Kenny, James, Mon-El, even William. That’s a pattern…”

Alex and Kelly share a look, again, and this time she’s incapable of ignoring it.

“Okay what’s with the looks? Am I missing something?”

“Lena…” Alex starts, but Kelly interrupts her by just saying her wife’s name, in warning. Alex purses her lips and rubs her forehead. “I don’t know, I can only tell you what she told me after I came out to her. That she understood what it was like to hide a part of yourself. Then, after Maggie she told me that while to her the idea of romantic love was a very earthly concept, she was hopeful about it, and that she understood what it felt to be attracted to someone of the same gender. And then told me about Donna, her NCU roommate, a constant flirt, apparently… And when you take in consideration that she’s almost as strong as Kara… The physicality of a relationship just gets easier too, I guess. No broken noses.” 

Yeah, Lena didn’t want to think about the practical aspects of Donna and Kara’s entanglements. In any case, a part of her was hurt that Kara had never told her about her sexuality, or something along those lines. Like if she was reading her mind Alex continued talking.

“I don’t think she wanted to hide her… preferences. I don’t even think she has them, or has a label for it.” She looks at her wife like searching for support, and as always Kelly came through.

“If there is anything Kara has to tell us, she will do it when she’s ready,” Kelly smiles at Lena, before saying something that will tilt Lena’s world forever, “As will you.”

There is a fire in a factory in the outskirts of the city the next morning, of course Kara is helping. Lena sees the news from her office in the foundation, and while Wonder Girl doesn’t make an appearance, Lena can clearly see Donna in between the onlookers, behind the reporter in the scene. She’s looking at the sky like she’s watching the sun shine, and taking pictures with a big camera. Lena, of course, knew that in the sky was also Kara, who could shine just as much as Sol . 

She hadn’t slept at all, when she left the Danvers-Olsen household, she went back to her office at the foundation to immerse herself in work and not think of whatever thing Kelly wants her to think about. It had been fruitless of course, because if Lena Luthor is anything, she’s a genius. But if Lena Luthor is any other thing, is an expert in denial, so she dives into her work, and keeps diving until there is no more immediate work. Dives until her body is tired from the lack of sleep. Dives until she can’t ignore what she knows like it’s an universal truth. She’s completely, utterly, undeniably, in love with Kara Zor-El Danvers.

Of course, now that she figured that out, Lena avoids her family as much as she can. Kara actually cancels movie night, thank God (not that she actually believes in any), because Lena wouldn’t be able to be in front of her and just act normal. Especially, she would not be able to just be a supportive best friend to Kara and her old… new… girlfriend? And Lena herself cancels dinner with Brainy, because he could actually read her like a book and that thought alone was unnerving.

Evidently her avoidance wouldn’t last long, Nia kept texting her about Kara behaviour at Catco, (“I’m telling you, Lena, she’s a mess. She almost walked through the glass door today, because of something Donna said to her.”) Alex keeps calling her and texting her (“Are you okay?”) Full caps, sometimes, threatening to not let her take Esme to the zoo next week like they had planned, if she doesn’t answer. And Kelly tried to schedule a meeting with her through her secretary, (the only reason that didn’t pan out was because she tried to do it for the next day and Lena had the full day booked). 

Kara haunts her, in the form of the news, now that’s she biggest celebrity in National City, (only rivaled by herself and Cat Grant, as not so close seconds) her every move is documented. (Lena doesn’t go looking for it, but she’s bombarded with it as soon as she turns on the TV, or opens up her tablet). When she rescues a cat from a tree, because of course she does. When she gets out of Noonan’s with so much food she could feed an army (Lena knows is just her mid morning snack), and her, laughing and walking around with a tall brunette with a Red Sox baseball cap, is the biggest gossip in town, apparently.

She got away with the avoidance for three days, so of course, Kelly showed up at her apartment door, a little past 10 PM on the fourth day.

“I had to talk Alex out of coming with a DEO team,” Kelly says as a greeting, showing her a wine bottle, once Lena opens the door.

“Thank you for that, I guess.”

“If you just answered the damn phone I wouldn’t have to blackmail my wife.”

“Blackmail? Do I even want to know?” Lena asks, frowning while serving the wine Kelly had bought. It was cheap, but it was sweet and fruity, and made her feel warm and loved because it was the one that she shared with her family most often.

“No,” Kelly answered easily with a smile. “Now, why are you avoiding everyone?”

Lena remains silent for minutes, Kelly doesn’t push, simply drinks her wine, sitting on Lena’s expensive and uncomfortable white leather couch.

“I’m in love with Kara,” she says finally, her eyes fixed on the blank space in front of her, “I’ve been for a long time, I think. But now that I know that, I can’t… I can’t keep it in a box in my head” She pauses, takes a breath. “I don’t know how not to love her, and I think, seeing her with someone else would break me. But not seeing her, would also mean not seeing the rest of you, and that would be even worse.”

When she finally has the guts to look at Kelly, she finds her friend already looking at her, no judgement or pity on her brown eyes, but understanding.

"Lena, you could never lose us. Even in the absolute worst case scenario, you'll never lose us. Not me, or Esme. Pretty sure Brainy would rather chew on his own arm." Kelly smiled, just as warm as always, no teasing, no mocking, but pure and unadulterated honesty. Lena returns the smile, and takes a steadying breath before saying aloud what she’s been probably hiding from everyone, even herself for half her life.

“I think… for now at least, I think that bisexual is a good label for me.”

“You don’t need to define yourself right now. Or tell me anything.”

“I know, but I feel comfortable with it.”

“Okay, then. Thank you for telling me, for trusting me with it.” 

The conversation flows to lighter topics, such as Esme's new obsession with some cartoon princess she’s been watching with Alex, and how they had an appointment with an optometrist for some headaches she’s been having if she’s reading too long for school. And Lena tells Kelly about one ridiculous man that tried to tell her what one of her own plastic recycling projects was about.

The wine was a little over halfway done, when Kelly stood up and hugged her.

“I have to go, or Alex will let Esme sleep in our bed and she really thinks it is her bed, because she takes up half the mattress and all the blankets.”

“I believe you. Hey, I’ll open up a portal, you’ve been drinking. It would be safer and quicker,” Lena lets Kelly ponder for a second and when she nods, she does as promised.

“Lena, I… We want you at game night, tomorrow. But I have to warn you, I think Kara is gonna bring Donna.”

Lena pauses, (more like freezes on the spot), the air trapped in her lungs, but nods after a few seconds. “I’ll be there. I’ll have to get used to it eventually.”

Kelly hugs her once more and disappears into the purple portal without saying anything else.

She sleeps that night, or more like she slips out of consciousness and when she opens her eyes again, the sun is already peaking out in the horizon. She tried to follow her routine, but she was nervous. She had always prided herself on her fantastic skill to hide her true feelings, an eternal mask over her features, but since her trip to Newfoundland her emotions are all over the place, and they manifest in her magic outbursts. Lights flickering, the water in the pipes inside the walls start rumbling so loud that even her human ears pick it up, one time when she was first trying to establish the foundation, this one man was making her life impossible and she was so pissed that she caused a thunderstorm in a whole block. And if there is something that can catch on fire, it will catch on fire, no match needed. So, yes, she was nervous because she didn’t know how her magic was going to react to Kara’s presence after figuring out her feelings, how her magic was going to react to jealousy, to seeing Kara with someone else, to seeing Kara and Donna together, knowing they had history together.

She was trying to not think of it, and dreading the time she had to be at the Danvers-Olsen’s to game night, so of course the time flew by. She was so stressed about the whole situation that she felt she needed a little of her former L-Corp CEO power, so even if she was dressed really casual, in jeans, tennis and a long sleeved red sweater, she puts her hair in a high ponytail and throws in one of her favourites red lipsticks, like some sort of armor. On her way out she also grabbed the comparatively expensive single malt, Estate Macallan bottle, to try and calm down. She was sure that she could share it with Alex and she would love it, (Maybe she would even offer her second child for how good the whisky was, wouldn't be the first time). 

As it was, she had called the town car to not drive herself or show up unannounced in someone else's home, and tried to steel herself to whatever the night throws at her.

When she arrives, only Kara is missing, since J'onn was on Mars with M'gann. She's immediately tackled by Esme, who assigns herself as her game partner for the night. Nia furrows her brows as that.

"You know your aunt Kara will fight you for that, right?"

"Well, the night is still young, and if you snooze you lose, so… Looks like Esme will be my partner tonight," Lena smiles at her goddaughter and then proceeds to actually greet everyone else.

She had downed three tumblers of scotch by the time Kara and Donna arrived. Kara introduced Donna to Esme, who apparently was the only one who hadn't met her. The kid looked at the newcomer with curious eyes cocking her head.

"You're not an alien…"

"No, I'm not." Donna answers with an easy smile.

"But I can feel your powers…" Esme says, her head still tilted.

"Really? Well, I think you could say that the Gods that gave me my powers are not from around here. Maybe that's why you can feel them." 

"Where are they from?" Esme was a curious child, and Lena felt proud about the very pointed questions, and part of her really wanted to know the answers too.

"A place called Olympus. Is not quite in space, but it's something like it, another dimension." 

Esme perks up at that. "Mom, like the portal Hordak tried to build."

Alex takes a moment to respond, (if she was trying to understand or if the alcohol was slowing her down, it was a mystery) 

"That's right, kiddo, just like in She-Ra."

Esme then turns to her, "The portals look like yours, Aunt Lena."

"That's cool, honey."

"Well, now that that's out of the way," Kara says with a blinding smile that has Lena both smitten and heartbroken. "What are we playing first?"

There was an odd energy in the room. It wasn't uncomfortable, per se, but odd. She could feel Kara's eyes on her several times during the night. Of course, she didn't do really well on charades or pictionary (having Esme as partner has its limitations after all) but they excelled in Monopoly and Uno. There had been no magic incidents either, not even when Donna got really close to whisper something in Kara's ear that made her as red as her boots. Or when Kara complimented Donna's steady hands at jenga. Her heart, however, was a little traitor. Hammering hard in her chest whenever Kara was a little too close to Donna or that moment when Lena was refilling her drink and Kara went to the kitchen too to find more food.

Her heart is acting so erratically that Esme picks up on it, as she's somewhat drifting to sleep, her head on Lena's chest, hugging Lovey to them, when she looks at her with curious eyes, and opens her mouth as to ask something, but is interrupted by Kara's loud laugh at some comment Nia made. 

"Do you want to go to bed?" Lena asks Esme in a whisper.

"I think Lovey is sleepy."

"Well, Lovey needs her rest. Say goodnight to everyone." 

She signals Kelly and Alex that she will take care of it, as her goddaughter does her goodbyes.

Esme takes her hand and they walk together to the bathroom to brush her teeth. Then go to her bedroom, Esme climbs her bed after putting on pajamas, and Lena covers her with the blanket.

"Do you like Donna?" Esme asks in a whisper, hugging her toy zebra and looking at her expectantly. Lena feels a little exposed by the question, but answers with honesty.

"She's a good ally to our group. I don't know her very well, but she's okay, I think. Do you not like her?"

Esme takes her time to answer. Lena doesn't push, part of her is curious about the 6-year-old input. Another part of her simply didn't want to go back to the living room and be a witness of the budding love life of the person she just figured out she's in love with.

"I think I just don't like her with Aunt Kara." Esme finally says.

Lena laughs at that, (feeling a little vindication maybe).

"That is simply Aunt Kara's decision, honey. If she so desires, we'll have to learn to live with it."

Lena does a little magic to entertain Esme for a little while before she lets her sleep (as she tries to delay her return outside), but she couldn't keep a child awake just so she could avoid seeing Kara with someone else. So she kissed Esme's forehead once she closed her eyes and got out of the room. She immediately found herself in front of Kara, the one person she was trying to avoid.

"Hey," she says, looking a little out of her comfort zone, which is weird being in her sister's home and all. And Lena's traitor heart starts pounding in her chest. "Did she fall asleep easily?"

"I– Yes, she wasn't completely out, but she was really sleepy, so…"

Kara hummed, looking at her… weirdly. Part of Lena will always miss the glasses, and it seems like part of Kara will too because she almost pokes her eye out trying to fix them, even though they weren't on her face. "She had a lot of questions earlier."

"Well, you know her, she's a very precocious child and wants to know everything." Lena smiles, looking at Esme's bedroom door, like she could actually see her goddaughter through it.

"She would be a great reporter, someday." Kara comments with a blinding smile.

"Or a scientist," Lena's mouth speaks without her permission, gaining a chuckle from Kara. "Or, you know, a detective of sorts."

"She would be a great scientist, I think. She has a really great role model." Kara smiles at her and it feels like too much. "I missed you this week." Kara says in a whisper, and if the smile was too much the admission was a killer blow. "I know I've been busy too, but I– I just wanted to let you know that I missed you."

"Kara, you don't need to feel guilty about not spending time with me," she says, sincerely. "We both lead busy lives, something more important than a friendship would eventually come up. I knew that."

Immediately, adorably, a crinkle formed in the middle of Kara's forehead.

"Lena, there is nothing more important than our friendship."

Lena smiles, and while not the happiest of smiles, is honest nonetheless, she puts a hand in Kara's bicep and squeezes it affectionately. "Yes, there is. There should be. You deserve it."

"Lena, I–"

"I have to go," Lena interrupts because she feels like she can't breathe. "I have to meet tomorrow morning with Lillian's state lawyers." She moves past Kara and goes to the living room to say goodbye. It’s not a complete lie, she does have to deal with Lillian's state, just not the lawyers. She catches both Kelly and Alex’s worried looks, but they say nothing even when she says a tense goodbye to Donna and Kara.

She's so bloody hangover the next day, after her quite brilliant idea to consume almost an entire bottle of whisky when she got home, that she considers not leaving her apartment for a grand total of 15 minutes, but as has been the case in the last week, the idea of being alone with nothing to do, terrifies her. So she dresses and goes to Noonan's to get something that could resemble a bucket of coffee and to change her surroundings, while she looks over Lillian's documents and thinks about what to do with the almost literal mountain of properties and possessions her mother had.

She's been tucked in a discreet corner of the restaurant for close to an hour, when a childlike voice catches her attention, asking for a picture with Supergirl. Her head whips so fast that it actually hurts, and of course they are there, because Lena Luthor has actually never been able to avoid Kara Danvers, not even when she was so fervently trying to hate her. 

She's there, beaming like the sun at a cellphone camera, with two kids with light brown hair, that could not be even a year older than Esme, smiling like Kara had given them the stars. Not that Lena blames them, Kara has that effect on people. But also there, next to Kara, looking fondly at her is Donna, and Lena feels like she’s been stabbed, like her heart is being destroyed in her chest. And she can’t stop looking at them, from the safety of the booth she’s sitting in. Donna keeps looking adoringly and somewhat amused at Kara while she makes small conversation with the girls, and signs paper napkins. She can’t actually hear her, but knowing Kara, she can hear her asking their names, and how are they doing in school, and what do they want to do when they grow up, if they play any sport, their favorite pastry, and she would relate to them or recommend the sticky buns. 

Once the kids move on, Kara puts on her glasses, which were on her hair while she was taking pictures. That was weird, Kara had not used her glasses since she announced her identity. Maybe she was trying to not stand out so much, to keep some privacy with her girlfriend. 

But now that the kids weren't next to her, her entire demeanor changed. Her shoulders dropped, her smile didn't get to her eyes, she grabbed her paper cup and drank some of her coffee (something ridiculously sweet, with too many syrups, probably) and talked quietly with the brunette next to her. She was tense, somewhat… sad. It was not something she was used to seeing on Kara, even at their worst. Donna was talking quietly, one of her hands on her coffee, the other on Kara's arm like offering some kind of support. Kara shakes her head, and looks directly at her former roommate in the eyes, like she's pleading and Lena's heart might as well have been squashed in Kara's hands. 

It's too much, so Lena gathers all her stuff, and looks for a way out of the restaurant without being detected, but before she even moves, Donna's hand is now cupping Kara's face, and like in slow motion she kisses her. (If the lights immediately flicker, and the earth shakes, well, National City is over the San Andreas fault. Completely coincidental stuff). She stands up and leaves, without looking back.

Apparently the quake was enough to keep Kara busy, and seismologists in the city baffled at it, since they couldn't pinpoint the epicenter of the seismic event, (Lena, since her excuse of the San Andreas fault wasn't gonna fly now). There was no damage, it was such a small quake, but people were freaking out. And Lena felt guilty enough that she called Alex and explained everything, as best she could.
