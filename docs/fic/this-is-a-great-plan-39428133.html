Summary

Being the heir to Bishop Security, a loving dog mom, and the world’s best archer have kept Kate too busy to have a relationship.

But when an invite to an important charity event and some unfortunate paparazzi attention coincide, Kate finds herself in need of a date who can stay cool under pressure. It sounds like the perfect job for an ex-assassin who also happens to be extremely hot and charming.

Or: The fluffy, fake relationship one full of pining, flirting, and Yelena smirking at Kate a lot. ;)

Notes

This story is set in the universe established in the Hawkeye TV series and begins about a year and a half after that series ends.

Chapter titles are all titles of Screaming Females songs. Really for no other reason than I saw them in concert around the time that I started writing this fic and I love Marissa Paternoster.

    Chapter Notes See the end of the chapter for notes This is a Great Plan Epilogue There’s a small box sitting on the table when she finally makes it out of bed and into the kitchen. “What’s this?”

    Yelena looks up from where she’s perched on the kitchen counter, idly scrolling through her phone. A blonde eyebrow raises.

    “Only one way to find out.”

    Kate picks up the box, holds it for a moment, and looks back to her girlfriend suspiciously. “Is this a ring? Are you trying to make an honest woman out of me?”

    Yelena sighs, deeply, in a way that can only be triggered by Kate’s behavior. She sets her phone down and pins Kate with a gaze.

    “Darling. I thought we agreed long ago that you would be the one buying the ring.” At that moment, Kate knows that if she did have a ring, she would drop down on one knee, right there on the kitchen floor.

    Anything to make sure she gets to wake up and see Yelena Belova every day for the rest of her life. Pulling away from those hazel eyes with effort, she returns her gaze to the small box in her hand, resisting the effort to shake it.

    As she slowly undoes the ribbon tied neatly around it, Yelena asks, “Do you know what day it is?” At this point, the non-sequitur shouldn’t be surprising.

    Yelena’s mind works in mysterious ways.

    “Uh, no?”

    “Today is the two year anniversary of the day that we met on that rooftop.” “And you promptly threw me off that rooftop.”

    She gets a dramatic eye roll in response.

    “One day you’ll have to get over that.”

    They smile at each other like lovesick fools for a moment before Yelena makes an impatient gesture with her hand.

    “Are you going to open it?”

    Kate wastes no time in tearing off the ribbon and opening the box.

    Inside is a small bow and arrow keychain. Her brow furrows in confusion.

    She picks it up and lets it dangle from her finger.

    “I mean, this is really cute babe, but I’m not sure why it required so much build up.” Yelena hops off the counter and saunters over, her smirk and the sway of her hips distracting Kate so that she doesn’t notice the blonde woman pulling something out of her pocket. Then Yelena is leaning in and Kate’s eyes are fluttering closed and she feels her new keychain being plucked from her hand.

    But that doesn’t matter, because her girlfriend is pressing light kisses to the side of her neck. When she abruptly pulls away, Kate whines in response, eyes still closed.

    “No. Come back.”

    “Don’t you want to see your present?”

    Deep blue eyes pop open and quickly focus on the keychain dangling from Yelena’s hand, now with the addition of a shiny purple key.

    Her mouth drops open.

    “Is that?”

    Yelena waves the key slowly back and forth, but Kate is focused on her beautiful hazel eyes. “This is a key to my apartment building, yes. There are other inside doors, but I have already set them to open to your fingerprint. This is the last piece you need to get in.” Kate snatches the key away and stuffs it in her pocket as if she’s afraid it might disappear if she hesitates for another second.

    Yelena is biting her lip now and Kate realizes that she is nervously awaiting a response. As the brunette continues to stay uncharacteristically silent, Yelena continues, now rambling slightly.

    “I know I have always been able to come and go in your apartment as I please, and I want you to be able to do the same. My space is your space. Plus Fanny will be very happy to see you whenever you stop by. And you are not saying anything which is really weird for you…so I hope this is an okay gesture…”

    As adorable as Kate finds this nervous version of Yelena, she can’t hold back anymore. She steps forward and pulls Yelena into a deep kiss, followed by several more, until the blonde is relaxing against her body.

    Then she pulls away just far enough to rest her forehead against Yelena’s. “This is a very okay and sweet and thoughtful gesture. I love you so much. And I love that you remember the anniversary of when we met and almost killed each other.”

    “I put a safety line on you.”

    “True. You were sweet from the very beginning.”

    “I was not sweet. I was very intimidating and ferocious.”

    Yelena’s affronted response might have been taken more seriously if she wasn’t also burrowing against Kate’s body like a sleepy kitten.

    Now Kate kissed a slow line from Yelena’s ear to the crook of her neck.

    “So intimidating. So ferocious. So hot.”

    The blonde head popped up again so Yelena could see Kate’s face.

    “You thought I was hot?”

    “Um, duh. Because you are. Our eyes met and I was lost. You were trying to kill my childhood hero and all you had to do was give me one look and I couldn’t take that shot.” “I’m glad you didn’t take that shot. I would have dodged, obviously. But still.” “I’m going to ignore that second part because we’re having a moment right now.” Now the teasing, cocky edge that got Kate every time was back in Yelena’s voice. “And what kind of moment is that?”

    “A romantic moment. You seem to be very good at creating romantic moments Ms. Belova.” “So you finally admit that I am the true Prince Charming?”

    A smile starts winding across Kate’s face and as it gets wider and wider, Yelena knows that her girlfriend is about to do something impulsive. She loves this smile.

    Suddenly, Kate is dipping down, wrapping her arms around Yelena, and scooping her up into a bridal carry.

    “Kate Bishop!!”

    In response to her shriek, Kate cradles her in closer and then says, “I have a present for you. It’s also in a box.”

    Yelena considers struggling, but she doesn't really want to get away and though she’s never admitted it, she finds these displays of strength when Kate picks her up or tosses her around to be pretty hot.

    “Is this box you are referring to the special box in your nightstand?”

    “It very well might be.”

    “This is an acceptable gift exchange.”

    Kate pivots and starts carrying Yelena towards the bedroom.

    “Then let’s go princess.”

    Yelena’s response is in the smoky, sultry tone that she can pull off better than anyone Kate has ever met.

    “Baby, I’m a queen.”

    Before her attention is fully distracted by the gorgeous blonde in her arms who just started nibbling on her neck, Kate has a thought.

    That tabloid article was right. Kate Bishop really did find her queen.

    Rolling her eyes at her own internal monologue, she carries Yelena over the threshold and kicks the bedroom door shut.
