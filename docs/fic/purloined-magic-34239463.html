Newfoundland 1693

"Do you hear a children's choir?" Lex stood up straight and tilted his head to the side as he spoke.

Eve frantically looked around the cabin they used as their base of operations. It wasn't far from the Luthor Mansion. "No, but that sounds fun, what do you they sound like?!" Eve leaned forward and rested her head in her hands.

"A bit spooky...Now it's starting to get intense."

Lillian slammed the vial she was holding onto the table. It shattered into pieces, the green substance leaking out onto the table, burning a hole into the wood. She barely spared it a glance. "You two must focus on the task at hand! The townspeople have started to notice their fellow villagers have gone missing. Eternal youth is not something I am willing to wait for."

"I am working as fast as I can, Mother. And Eve has, well, she has been here the whole time. If we can get one more Kryptonian..."

"I will worry about securing our power source. You worry about finishing the potion before your father returns, Lex. Eve, just...stay out of the way."

Eve squeaked, happy to be addressed by name, "Yes, Mrs. Luthor."

Cat Grant knocked on the door of a small white house on top of a hill overlooking a cemetery. When no one immediately answered the door, she peered into a small window to the side. She spotted a young man seated at a desk writing furiously. As someone who never liked to be kept waiting, she knocked louder and continuously. She had come to the town to investigate and report on the rumors that people were disappearing. The dreaded Luthors were supposedly behind it. However the whispers that they were witches made Cat skeptical of any 'proof'. No one dared go near their mansion or the small cabin on their property. No one, except Clark Kent.

The door swung open, revealing the man Cat had seen through the glass. "You must be Cat Grant."

"And you must be Clark Kent."

"Please, come in. We have much to discuss. I hope your - " Clark's sentence was cut off as he suddenly stood very tall and had a glassy look come over his eyes.

"Clark?"

He started to walk out the door, bumping Cat and ignoring her completely as he continued down the hill in the direction of Luthor mansion.

Cat scanned the interior of the home quickly. It seemed like a normal house with candles and writing parchment strewn haphazardly around. Finding nothing to explain the odd behavior, Cat hurried after Clark.

"Clark! Mr. Kent!" Cat tried to keep up, but fell further behind as Clark briskly walked through the trees and fallen leaves.

Cat cursed herself for wearing a dress rather than trousers, as she finally crested the hill to see Clark enter a small cabin.

"What is happening in this town?" She muttered to herself as she silently approached and tried to find an inconspicuous window where she could see what was going on.

Lex grinned as the cabin door slowly opened. His smile widened as he saw that it was Clark who stood in the entryway.

"Well, if it is not Mr. Kent." Lex turned toward Lillian. "I approve, Mother. Of all the Kryptonians to choose, this one has been a thorn in our side since the beginning. Accusing us! Us! Of being the cause of the disappearances."

"But Lex, we are the cause of the disappearances," Eve unhelpfully chimed in from where she was seated out of the way.

Clark stiffly walked toward a chair in front of a large boiling cauldron. He turned and sat, staring straight ahead.

"Yes, but he did not have to go blabbing about it to half the town."

"Silence, both of you," Lillian interjected. "It's time to start the extraction."

Cat had experienced quite a bit of the world throughout her travels. She prided herself on learning about people and places. As someone who was trying to become a world-famous reporter, she had a driving need to find the truth and tell the truth to all who were willing to listen.

Cat had no idea how she was going to convince anyone what she was looking at had actually happened. She presumed the three figures surrounding Clark were performing some sort of ritual. The green glow emitting from the boiling pot was otherworldly.

She pulled the letter she had received from Mr. Kent over a month ago from her pocket. She quickly re-read it, and based on the cabin and the trio, she realized that the two standing figures must be Lillian and Lex Luthor. That left the woman seated to be Eve Teschmacher. She thought most of Clark's letter was fantastical and couldn't possibly be true. What had originally brought her to the town was that Mr. Kent had a reputation for being one of the most honest and upstanding reporters the east coast had ever seen. For him to send her a letter, filled with mythical and alien fables, she assumed her story would be how a once great reporter had lost touch with reality.

Cat witnessed the Luthors begin to drink the potion. As green mist seemingly entered Clark's body, Cat realized that reality was no longer an option. She gasped as Clark's veins turned green while the three witches glowed ethereally.

"They will surely kill him." Cat whispered before leaping through the window. She felt her dress tear on a nail on the windowsill as she landed. "Stop! All of you!"

Lex was the first to turn, his bald head reflecting in the candle light. "I do not know who you are, but I suggest you leave."

"You are hurting him."

Lex smirked, "Well, that is the point." Lightning shot from his fingertips and knocked Cat off her feet. She managed to stay conscious but couldn't convince her limbs to move. She watched on in horror as Lex redirected his attention to Clark. Cat heard spells being muttered by all three witches, but was unable to see the effect of the enchantment.

The chanting stopped and Eve, appearing far younger than earlier, jumped around in celebration. With Eve no longer blocking her view, Cat could see Clark's now lifeless body in the chair.

Lillian walked up to the window and basked in the orange glow from the setting sun. Lex skipped over to where Cat was crumpled on the floor. Cat gasped as she saw Lex, also far younger, had grown a full head of flowing red hair with bangs hanging down the front of his face.

"What...what is going on? What did you do to Clark Kent?!"

"Oh, we took his life force. What do you think of the hair? Too much? I think it really brings out the color of my eyes." He spun around while whipping his new head of hair back and forth.

"I do not think there's enough life force in this universe to make you look good." Cat spat out.

"Enough!" Lillian had strutted over to stand by Lex. "And what is your name?"

Cat tried to sit up straighter from her spot on the floor. "Cat Grant. I am a reporter. You will not get away with this."

"Hmm, I disagree, Cat. Hm, Cat." Lillian spun around and shot a beam of green light from her hands while incanting a spell from the book she was holding. Cat's yell turned into a 'meow' to her own ears.

Lex threw his head back and laughed when he realized what his mother had just done.

"Well done, Mother. Turning Cat into a cat. Precious."

Suddenly the door flew open, and one of the larger townspeople stood in the door. Lex could see almost the entire town had arrived bearing torches and pitchforks.

"Lex and Lillian Luthor. You are under arrest for the disappearances of over 20 townsfolk."

"I do not see how we'll possibly escape this, Mother."

Lillian, Lex and Eve were all lined up to be executed, Eve was fidgeting much to Lillian's chagrin.

Lillian smiled and nodded toward a town elder whose necklace glinted green sparks in the setting sun.

Lex perked up, "Eve, get ready. Mother, lead the way."

Lillian's voice echoed throughout the clearing, "On All Hallow's Eve, upon the first step of the last Kryptonian descendant on Newfoundland soil, Cadmus will return."

"Silence them!" The leader of the town's people shouted. The silence following the deaths of the Luthors was interrupted by a quiet 'meow'.

"That shall be the last we hear from them. Tell me, who is Cadmus and what is a Kryptonian?"

"I do not know. Clark mentioned those words a few months ago, when he dropped off a letter to mail to a Cat Grant. Speaking of whom, Cat should be arriving in town any day now. We will have to inform her of Clark's death. She will have traveled all this way for nothing."

National City September 2021

"But Cat Grant never arrived. And thus, the Lore of the Luthors was born." Kara Danvers hit the spacebar on her laptop, halting the presentation she was giving to her sister, Alex.

"I can't believe you made a Powerpoint to try to convince me to go on vacation with you. I don't see why we have to fly six hours to go to a town with a ghost story. We could just go to the beach here for a weekend."

"Because we will be the only ones there. Ugh, I can't believe you're talking me into this. Okay, fine. Book the inn and our flights, but this counts as your birthday present."

Kara threw her arms up and squealed. "You are not going to regret this, Alex."

Newfoundland Oct 30th 2021

"I've regretted every moment of this so far, Kara."

Kara rolled her eyes as she opened the door to the inn they would call home for the next few days. "Oh, come on, Alex. It was just bad luck. It's not like I meant to book you a seat between two people who had that much garlic and onion for lunch."

Kara, seeing no one at the desk, rang the bell. A woman popped up into view with a sharp squeak.

"Oh gosh, I'm so sorry. I didn't see you. I'm Kara Danvers. Checking-in."

The woman lowered her hand from where it landed on her chest. The bell must have startled her. Kara was rendered speechless at the woman's beauty.

"Oh, I'm not - " The woman paused mid-sentence as she made eye contact with Kara. She dropped the stack of papers in her hand and cleared her throat roughly. "Hi, I'm Lena. Kara Danvers, was it?"

Alex's eyes dodged back and forth between Kara and Lena, "Yes, one room with two beds, please."

Kara felt her arm being nudged by Alex. She shook herself out of her daze and blurted, "Yes, Kara Danvers! I am she."

Alex rolled her eyes and Kara thought she heard her mutter, "Useless gay."

"Hi, Kara Danvers. Welcome to my inn. Your room number is written on the cards. I hope you enjoy your stay here in Newfoundland. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

"Do you have any recommendations for lunch?"

"Oh, um, there's an Italian place within walking distance. It's fairly romantic."

Kara scrunched up her nose, "Do you have anything less romantic and more spooky? My sister and I aren't really interested in watching a bunch of couples eat their food, but I am definitely interested in anything Halloween themed."

"Sister?" Lena's eyebrow raised slightly.

Alex stopped her assessment of the lobby, "That's me. Kara's been raving about the Luthors and the history of this town the entire flight out here. And don't worry about the spooky, I have a feeling she's going to drag me to multiple haunted houses while we're here. The least she can do is let me eat my meals in peace without any vampires or witches lurking about." Kara pouted at Alex. "Is there a bar around here where we can just grab a bite?"

"Oh, yes, of course. Noonan's is a couple of blocks away. They have decent food."

Alex nodded in thanks and dragged her luggage behind her heading to their room.

"Oh, and Kara." Lena called out as Kara followed Alex. "They do have Halloween themed drinks, I hope that holds you over until the haunted houses."

Kara grinned in response, "Thank you so much, Lena! Hopefully I'll see you again?"

Lena nodded with a small smile on her face, "I'd like that."

Lena was still standing watching the Danvers sisters walk away, when her friend Sam walked up behind her. "Who was that?"

Sam crossed her arms, "Guests who got checked in by the owner of the inn, rather than the front desk?" Sam pointed to herself when she said front desk.

Alex sighed, "We could just drive around."

Alex groaned and adjusted her too-short skirt as she saw Kara spot a little girl, no more than five years old also wearing a Supergirl outfit, "Here we go again."

Alex worried for their safety when Kara simply replied with a squeal and ran into the strange house with a 'Thank you' tossed out on the way by.

"Oh, it was just an expression."

"I...I know, Kara. Hold still. And please, stop humming along."

"But Alex remembers ever step from when we practiced. I'm so proud of her!"

Lena rolled her eyes and began the incantation under her breath, her hands outstretched towards the magical ropes surrounding Kara. Lena exhaled in relief when she saw the ropes loosen and fall to the ground.

"I can't believe that worked!" Kara whisper-yelled at Lena.

Lena looked up incredulously. "You said you believed in me!"

"That was completely independent from your magic working. I believe in you."

"I'm not fucking Santa Claus, Kara."

"That's good. You definitely don't want to end up on Mrs. Claus' naughty list. Are you harboring any romantic feelings for the Tooth Fairy?"

"Oh, my god. We're going. Now."

Kara had the audacity to wave at a dancing Alex while Lena dragged her out the door. Lena whispered an incantation on her way out causing a small fireball to appear near the sprinklers, immediately setting them off. The loud shriek of the alarms echoed as they rushed to the car.

Lena turned off the car after parking as close to the cemetery's entrance as she could. Alex and Cat had clambered into the car at the last moment before the Luthors had realized what happened. Cat was slightly damp from the sprinklers and had a permanent frown on her face.

"It's almost sunrise." Kara commented as she refastened the cape to her suit that Lena had brought with her.

"Finally. Kara, promise me you will do everything Lena tells you to do."

Lena smirked at Alex and wiggled her eyebrows, "Everything?"

"I promise I will do anything Lena tells me. Anything."

Alex took in a deep breath through her nose, and grumbled something about 'putting up with this shit' as she opened the car door and climbed out. Kara and Lena exchanged a grin and followed suit with Cat trailing behind.

"Alright, Lena. How do you want me?"

Lena chuckled, "I was thinking you could use this hole - "

"Nope!" Alex yelled and turned to see Lena pointing at the hole in the ground that Brainy had crawled out of earlier. "Oh."

Alex glared at Lena's smirk while Kara climbed into the empty grave.

Lena suddenly felt arms wrap around her pulling the car keys from her hands. She spun around to be met with Brainy, who was using the key to pry open his sewed shut mouth. A puff of dust was released when he was successful. Lena gagged and immediately fell into a coughing fit.

"I must apologize, Lena. The smell must be quite horrid."

"You can talk!" Kara exclaimed.

"Yes. I have been following you trying to return these to you. You dropped them earlier when you ran away." Brainy was holding out a pair of sunglasses.

"Oh my gosh, thank you! I thought I lost them forever."

"My pleasure."

Alex and Lena shared a look of disbelief.

"I would like to fight alongside you when the Luthors arrive."

"That would be great, Brainy! Join me in my hole!"

Lena whispered at Alex, "She's your sister."

"She's your future girlfriend."

Lena barely contained her smile as she finished emptying the salt in a circle around the grave.

The Luthors arrived as the sun was just beginning to glow over the horizon. Lillian held a glowing green potion in her hands.

"Kryptonian! You will drink this potion! This Kryptonite!"

"Where's Kara?" Alex asked.

"What do you mean where - " Lena looked at the grave where only Brainy sat.

Kara was standing with her hands on her hips, cape billowing in the wind on top of the small hill nearby.

"Idiot." Lena ran over and shoved Kara back into the hole.

Kara squeaked out a "Lena!" as she tumbled over.

Lillian and Lex started to throw small bolts of lightning at Lena. Eve cheered them on from the side.

Lena and Alex dodged behind a rock. "Ideas?"

"Keep them busy, the sun will rise any second. We need to get that potion out of their hands."

Alex picked up a rock. "I've got a rock?"

Lena smiled and whispered a quick spell. "Throw it."

Alex looked suspiciously down at the rock before shrugging and hopping to her feet with her arm notched back in position. She launched the rock which appeared to change course mid flight at hit the vial straight on, shattering it.

"The kryptonite!"

"Nice throw, Alex!" Kara yelled from her prone position in the grave.

"I will end - " Lillian's shout cut off as the sun fully rose behind her. All three witches let out a shriek as they disappeared into puffs of smoke when the sun shone on them.

An eerie silence followed.

Kara popped her head up, "Are they gone? That seemed too easy."

Everyone shot Kara an incredulous look.

"Yes, they are gone. Please move, you are in my spot." Brainy was trying to maneuver around Kara to lie back down in his grave.

Kara scrambled out of her hiding spot to joined the rest of the group.

"Thanks, Brainy."

"Any time, friends. And Kara, please never step foot here on All Hallows' Eve ever again."

"Yup, learned that lesson."

They helped Brainy pile the dirt back over himself.

Once they were done, Lena turned to Cat. "Do you want be human again? I think I can turn you back."

Cat looked down at herself, "I was never able to finish writing that article about the small town full of disappearances."

"It's probably easier to type with human hands than cat paws."

Lena smiled as she opened the spell book and flipped through the pages. She eventually found the reversal spell to use on Cat. She took a deep breath and whispered, "Here goes nothing." Her smile widened as Cat morphed into a young woman wearing the same clothes she had on the day she was transformed.

"Yes, this is much better. Thank you, Lena. Now, I have work to do. Stay out of trouble, Kara. Alex, try to keep Kara out of trouble."

"Please don't give me an impossible task, Cat."

"Stay in touch." Cat wandered off.

"Where do you think she's going?"

"I'm not sure, but I don't think that's the last we'll see of Cat Grant."

"You know, this is the part where the damsel in distress gets a kiss from her rescuer."

Kara and Lena walked side by side as they trailed behind Alex on their way to the Inn.

"Hmm, you were hardly in distress. In fact, it's worrisome the complete lack of distress you were in during the last 24 hours. Unless you're implying I should kiss Alex? She seemed to be in quite a bit of distress..." Lena laughed at the look on Kara's face. "Now you look like you're in distress."

Lena stepped up to Kara and placed her hand gently on the side of her face before rising up on her tiptoes to bring their lips together. Kara's eyes stayed closed after Lena pulled away. After a few seconds, she opened them to look at Lena.

"That was... well, this Halloween just secured itself a spot in the top two holidays of all time."

"Make sure you update your spreadsheet." A sad look crossed Lena's face, but she didn't back out of Kara's arms. "Now what?"

"I guess I get on a plane and Alex and I head back to National City."

"You live in National City?" Lena shook her head slightly. "I'm going out there next week. I was looking to expand there."

"It's fate! Wait, expand what?"

"My hotel chain." At Kara's blank look, Lena clarified her statement, "L-Corp."

"You own L-Corp?"

"Yes, I said I owned the Inn."

"Yeah, but I didn't know L-Corp owned the Inn, too! Or that you owned L-Corp!"

Lena tilted her head to the side, "Does that change anything?"

"It changes everything! It means I can take you out on a date next week! We can go to the science museum!"

"I meant me owning a hotel chain, does that change anything?"

"Oh, no shouldn't change anything." A thoughtful look passed over Kara's face. "I don't have any vacations planned so I don't need to rearrange to stay in your hotels. But now I do have to think of you as both hot and rich. A rich witch!"

"Doing two spells doesn't make me a witch."

"It's tragic how few words rhyme with magic, Lena."

Kara clicked to the last slide with a sense of finality. "And that is why we should go on vacation as a family to Metropolis for Christmas. Trust me, you won't regret it."

Lena and Alex exchanged a look and a sigh. "She's your sister."
