“For I would have lived a thousand lives to reach this one,” she says, “I would have fought with every ounce of my being to meet you here, at this moment. To feel this free, how the air intoxicates in a way I never experienced prior, is an honour. You, Lena Luthor, have lead me from the darkness and taught me I can defy the cosmos above. You gave me purpose. Together, we will reach the apex of our destiny. Follow me, my darling. Follow me to apex.”
