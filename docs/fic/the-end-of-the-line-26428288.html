It’s not all perfect. Some people are creeps, but if Kara’s being honest with herself, the ratio of creeps to respectful people is about the same across any job she has ever had, so it’s not the phone operator job that’s at fault. She doesn’t want to give it up, so she keeps the job even after getting promoted to cub reporter.
