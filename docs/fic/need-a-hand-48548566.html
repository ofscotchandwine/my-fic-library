Chapter 1

It was 7:30AM when Lena stepped into her office. She was one of the first in the building. Instead, she found it in a bigger disarray than her assassins had left it before she went on her business trip. When she was discharged and got back to her office, it was very clear that she wouldn’t be able to work in it anymore. On top of the trauma they had inflicted on her body, they had demolished the rest of the room too. And so Lena tasked Jess to hire a company that could repair and renovate her office and do a background check this time while she was out on a business trip.  

 

Now, three days later, a day earlier than scheduled, she had arrived at her office. Or well, something that was supposed to be her office.

There were loose planks leaning against the wall, the windows had not yet been cleaned, there were paint cans lying around, and most of her new furniture was still covered in a sheet. Clearly, the new maintenance crew hadn’t finished renovating just yet. She should have expected this, but a part of her had just hoped that it would have been fixed by now.

 

But apparently hope was wasted.

And if Kara says no, Lena will just have to never break anything ever again- Or just hire a different maintenance company. Though, that would be unprofessional of Lena. She couldn’t punish Kara for her own stupid feelings.

 

Before she can come to a conclusion, Kara is done and moving towards Lena, ready to take the headphones from her. (check)

 

“It’s all fixed up again.” She smiles, putting the headphones back around her own neck.

 

“Thank you, Kara, again. I know it must be an inconvenience to be called here constantly.” Lena admits, feeling that she has to apologise in some way for her behaviour in the last couple of weeks. “I’m sorry.”

 

Kara just waves it off, adjusting her glasses as she looks down at her shoes. “It’s fine, truly. Otherwise I’d be spending time on other boring projects anyways. You keep me on my toes, every week something different.” She jokes but it feels a bit forced and Lena just wants to disappear under the desk. Kara knows, that’s the only reason that can explain why Kara has been acting off today. The shorter talks, too polite comments and her even turning down lunch. Kara has figured it out and is done with Lena’s bullshit.

 

“Uhm, but about that..” Kara looks back up at Lena, who just looks back at her with a small frown, pretending not to know what that means.

 

“About what?” She tries to sound oblivious and she hopes that for once all the training she’s had from the Luthors about lying could finally come in handy.

 

“The reparations, the uhm,” the blonde pockets her hands, leaning backwards on her heels as she looks back at her own feet. “The frequentness of them… I uh gotta ask, Lena.”

 

There she goes. Kara knows, Lena can never see her again. She might as well give Kara one good look because after this Lena will have died of embarrassment. But instead of interrupting, to find some excuse (there isn’t one really) she stays silent, letting Kara speak. Which doesn’t go very well as she stammers. “I know that the things hadn’t just broken down on themselves. Like, in the beginning it was normal things, like the AC and the door but uhm,” she looks back at the new coat hanger, “it doesn’t make sense, always. They seem to be, and I’m- I’m not accusing you of anything just bringing it to light uhm- They seem deliberately broken. Like someone broke them.” 

 

“Ah.” There it is. And Lena has two options now. One, the dangerous one, tell the truth, let Kara know of her actions and desires, with risk of this little safe place they’ve built. Or two, the cowards way, pretend not to know and continue as they always do. Lena chooses the latter. “That’s… interesting.” 

 

“Yeah. It is, uhm…” Kara looks a bit at a loss and Lena almost feels guilty for not giving her a proper answer. But she just can’t, not with Kara looking so genuinely worried. “Okay then, do let me know when somethings broken again. But before I go I do want to ask you something.”

 

“Sure, go ahead.” Lena tries to keep her voice even, not wanting to show her inner turmoil. 

 

It’s then that she notices how nervous Kara looks. Her neck is red from scratching it so much, her cheeks are red and she keeps fiddling with her glasses even though they sit there perfectly fine. She’s not looking at Lena, she is just looking at her desk, the same she had replaced not all too long ago. 

 

“Can I-“ She clears her throat, “Or well, do you want to- it’s okay if you don’t- go somewhere tomorrow. With me. That is. If you got work that’s understandable, I just wanted to ask, we can also do it another day. And if you don’t want to-“

 

Lena quickly jumps in before Kara can dig her own grave, “That would be nice, Kara.” She tries not to smile too widely, not to let the heat in her belly redden her cheeks. She isn’t sure what Kara exactly is asking but her mind seems to solely focus on how much this sounds like Kara asking her out on a date.

 

No. No. That would be inappropriate. Kara has never shown interest in Lena, so that would not make sense. Kara is just being nice and wants to do something outside of work. That’s it. Not a date. Surely not a date. Right? 

 

The blonde chuckles and relief is clear all over her face. “Okay, okay good, uhm.” She lets go of her glasses and instead pockets her hands. “Okay, what time are you free?”

 

“I’ve got the day off tomorrow, so just let me know the time and location and I’ll be there.” Lena lies (she won’t have a day off in the next year, but she’ll free it up for Kara) with a warm smile. It’s a genuine one too, the fact that Kara is asking her to do something outside of work is well… something Lena never dared to do. Date or not, Lena would love to not have to break things in order to see Kara. Definitely not now that she is running out of things to break. 

 

“Okay, I’ll text you the time and place, alright? I’ll need to arrange some stuff!” Kara smiles brightly at her. She doesn’t turn as she walks over to her toolbox and picks it up, her eyes focused back on Lena. “Thank you.” 

 

“What for?” Lena frowns. Why is it so hard to just act normal? Her hands feel clammy and her cheeks hot. She needs to call Sam, would Sam classify this as a date? No, it's just meeting up with a friend. But what if-

 

“Saying yes.” Kara grins and walks over to the door. She opens it with ease, the hinges still working perfectly fine. “I’ll see you tomorrow!” And then she is gone. 

 

Lena has never dialled Sam’s number as quick as she is doing now.

 

—

 

“I think I see her. I got to go.” She sighs into her phone.

 

Sam lets out an excited sound. “Yeah yeah, go. Have fun, be safe, wear protection. Do you have your mints-“ 

 

“I can still fire you.” Lena threatens and she nears the corner where Kara is standing with her back turned towards Lena. 

 

Her friend just laughs, “But you won’t, because I am covering for you so that you can ogle Kara’s muscles some more.”

 

“I’m not going to ogle-“ She hisses into the phone, slowing her step.

 

“Have fun on your date, Lena.” Sam says and Lena can just hear the mischievous smile of her friend as she does so.

 

With a scoff she moves her mouth closer to the microphone of her phone, just to make sure Sam understands her. “It’s not a da-“ 

 

“Oh, Lena!” The familiar voice of Kara Danvers calls out to her. She quickly presses the red button, successfully cutting off the laughter emanating from her phone. 

 

“Kara.” Lena says as she straightens herself, glueing a smile to her face as to hide her embarrassment. She straightens out her dress. “Sorry, I’m late. Heels are apparently not the right attire for a light stroll through the city.” 

 

“You look-“ Kara looks Lena up and down shamelessly, taking in her outfit. And seeing the surprised look on Kara’s face she has a feeling that this is not what she is supposed to wear. It’s too much. She should have gone with her original idea of just pants and a blouse but no she had let Sam talk her into her white and black dress, the one that matches with her high heels. It is a dress that is definitely not meant for meet ups with friends, which this clearly is. 

 

Lena opens her mouth to apologise for overdressing but Kara finishes her sentence as her gaze meets Lena’s again, “-good. You look really beautiful.”

 

“Oh.” Lena smiles, blaming the blossoming blush on the warm summer weather. 

“I feel so incredibly underdressed now.” Kara mumbles, looking down at her own clothes. She’s wearing a white T-shirt and jeans that fit her perfectly. It’s a simple but good look, fit for a friend-date. 

 

“You don’t. You look-“ hot, way too good for somebody who is just wearing a white shirt, handsome, beautiful, did she mention hot? “perfect.” 

 

Kara laughs, dropping her head and adjusting her glasses. “Well, thanks. Uhm, ready to go?”

 

“Yeah.” Lena smiles easily. When had she become such a smiley person?

 

They talk about Kara’s hobby as a painter, how Sam and Lena met and some more. It’s calm, relaxing and they fall into an easy rhythm of walking, talking and sometimes Kara stopping to take a picture of a cute dog she sees walking past. It almost feels like they’ve been doing this for a long time.

 

After finishing their walk through the park, Kara buys ice cream for her (even though Lena insists she shouldn’t, seeing as she is the billionaire here and not Kara) they head back into the city.

 

“I want to take you to this place, but I don’t want you to uh, be offended?”

 

“Why would I be offended?” Lena asks, her voice wary as she eyes Kara’s nervous fidgeting.

 

“Well uhm, remember the whole breaking stuff thing?”

 

 Ah, there it is again. Lena can feel the warmth in her chest grow cold within seconds. She slows her pace ever so slightly but Kara immediately catches on to it and slows down too. The silence speaks volumes and so the blonde continues.

 

“Well, I talked to my friend about it. She’s a psychologist and-” 

 

“Ah great, psychologists.” Lena mutters as she shakes her head. The people she’s tried to avoid her whole life. They always manage to ask just the right questions, causing her to spill all the secrets she has spent years trying to hide away. It’s their job, Lena knows that, but that does not mean she likes it. At all.

 

“It’s fine, she’s nice. But she suggested you might have some issues…” Kara adjusts her glasses as she avoids Lena’s gaze.

 

A bark of laughter escapes Lena, it’s loud and it turns the heads of some passers-by and Kara. Some issues. That’s a good one. But as she sees Kara’s confused expression, her laughter dissipates. “Sorry,” Lena looks away, into the distance, “continue.” 

 

“-with anger.” Kara finishes her sentence in a soft voice, almost scared.

 

Anger? Anger issues?! Oh no. Lena stops dead in her tracks as she looks at Kara with widened eyes. “Anger issues? Your friend thinks I have anger issues-” Kara looks at Lena sympathetically but still a tad bit uncomfortable. It’s then that Lena notices her agitated voice and tense stance, almost confirming Kara’s assumptions. 

 

“I don’t judge you, my sister has a lot of them too. I just don’t want you to hurt yourself while you, well, break stuff.” Kara continues, her voice soft as she puts a hand on Lena’s shoulder, guiding her forward again. “So well, I thought we’d just break stuff in a safe way.” 

 

“How-” Lena starts but stops as they turn the corner and she finds her answer.

 

Smash and bash . The sign reads and Lena can’t stop her mouth from falling open slightly. A rage room. Kara has taken her to a rage room. Because she thinks Lena has anger issues and wants her to break stuff in a safe way.

 

Oh, she was so wrong. This is no date, not even a friend date. This is Kara thinking she has anger issues and being sweet. This is one person trying to help another, nothing more nothing less. God, she can’t do this.

 

“Oh.” Lena sighs softly, trying to hide her discomfort but clearly not well enough as Kara speaks up again. 

 

“I’m sorry if I’m overstepping, I just, well- I was just worried and I thought this might help. You don’t have to, I just-” 

 

“No, no. It’s sweet.” Lena quickly interrupts before Kara goes on rambling. “It’s just that I-” She sighs, pinching the bridge of her nose. “I don’t have any anger issues.”

 

“What?” 

 

Lena drops her hand and looks up to face Kara, turning to her. “I don’t have any anger issues.”

 

“But the broken stuff?” 

 

“I did break them, but not because I was angry.” Is she really going to do this? Should she even? What if Kara doesn’t think the same of her? She probably doesn’t. She could get over Kara not liking her back, she has already prepared herself for that, but not letting her think that she has anger issues. For that is the one Luthor trait she refuses to have inherited. 

 

Kara furrows her brows as she tilts her head, “Then why?” 

 

It takes everything in Lena not to turn on her heels and just make a run for it. Instead she takes a deep breath and tries to gather all the courage she can possibly find in between her doubts. “Because I wanted to see you. I broke stuff to see you.” She says quickly and Kara’s still confused look makes her think that perhaps the woman hasn’t heard her. So she opens her mouth to repeat it only for Kara to cut in.

 

“Wait, what? Why? That doesn’t make sense.” Kara looks so puzzled that Lena almost takes pity on the obliviousness of her.

 

“Because,” here goes nothing, “I like you. Your visits brighten up my day. So when I felt particularly stressed I would break something, knowing that once you came in, everything would seem less stressful. But then after a little while I just did it whenever I wanted to see you. So, there you have it, no anger issues.” Lena breathes out deeply, avoiding Kara’s eyes.

 

It takes a couple of seconds for Kara to respond and Lena fears the worst. She should have just kept it all professional. That’s what normal CEO’s do, they keep emotions far away from business. And seeing as they have had a business arrangement, she should have kept it professional. 

 

“Why didn’t you just ask me to come by? Without breaking stuff?” Kara speaks softly, careful almost, as if she senses Lena’s urge to run away the moment this goes awry.

 

“That would have been weird, wouldn’t it? We barely knew each other and it would have been, well, unprofessional of me.” 

 

“It wouldn’t have been. Otherwise it would have been weird of me to ask you here too, right? Did you think it was weird when I asked you to do something today?”

 

Lena’s heels look particularly interesting right now. “No, no. I did not.” 

 

“See,” Kara laughs softly, “it would have been fine. Though I must say I was nervous too. You seem like a very professional person and so I didn’t think you’d, well, feel the same.” 

 

This causes Lena to snap her gaze right back up to Kara’s. She finds warm eyes and an even warmer smile. “The same?” 

 

“Well, why do you think it was always me showing up and never Alex? You made my day a little bit better too.” Seeing Lena’s baffled face, Kara rubs the back of her neck awkwardly. “I’m saying I like you too. Unprofessionally so.”

 

“You-” Lena starts but she doesn’t have to finish her question to find her answer. It’s clear in Kara’s sudden shy behaviour and the complementary blush reddening her cheeks, the same blush that reddens Lena’s. “Oh.” 

 

“Yeah.” Kara chuckles softly. “So, uhm, yeah. Would you be interested in a date? With me? That does not involve breaking stuff?” 

 

Wow. The warmth from earlier returns in earnest as she breathes in, her chest swelling with this light feeling. “Yes. Yes, I’d like that. Though this seems fitting too.” Lena smiles, her cheeks almost hurting from how wide it is. 

 

Kara laughs, warm and from the belly. “Yeah, that it does.” She nods and holds out a hand for Lena. “So, Miss Luthor, would you be so kind as to show me how to properly break something?” 

 

Lena grins as she takes Kara’s hand. Big, calloused and warm. It causes a jolt of energy through her body, exciting her stomach and heart. “I would love to, Miss Danvers.” 

 

As Kara leads them over to the entrance of the rage room, she turns her head to look at Lena. “I do have one question.”

 

“Shoot.” 

 

“How did you break that coat rack?”
