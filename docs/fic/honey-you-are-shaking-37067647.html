    Again.

    They were bruised.

    Again.

    Blood.

    Fucking cold.

    Again.

    Her wrist did not look normal.

    Again.

    Lila was slipping away.

    'Lila stay with me'

    Lucky was still under there. She had to get Lucky. She can't let Lucky die.

    And before someone could grab her she was swimming back towards the place Lila and Lucky had crashed in searching for her dog.

    Fucking lake. She should have been more careful. Kept a better look on Lila. On Cooper.

    The ever coming darkness - that stung her eyes - didn't help. The needles were unbearable. She could barely move in the water. Freezing water.

    Everything was a fuzz. A couple of short memories she won't be able to remember all too well in the future. Like ellipses in a poem. Unfinished sentences in a big old book.

    Lucky.

    He was there in the water. Slowly swimming closer. And after grabbing him and turning around she saw that she has in fact moved less over 10 meters underwater.

    He followed as much as he could. Good boy Lucky. Such a good boy.

    Everything was a fuzz again. Confusing but clear. A weird feeling. She couldn't explain it. But paradox could meet it pretty well if she had to lie.

    The hole she broke was there. And she pushed Lucky up. Or rather just held him where there was no ice hoping someone would take him from her.

    Thanks to her fucking amazing breath management she survived a minute - maybe three -

    longer than a normie would. Those gold medals did pay off.

    But by the time she realized she was still alive she couldn't feel anything. She was floating.

    The only thing she could see was the endless blue that stung her eyes and feet and arms - sending millions of needles into her body as it was slowly turning into ice.

    Even the sudden grab of her jacket didn't snap her back to reality.

    For a second it was heaven and then it was like hell rained on earth.

    She could breathe but the pain was worse. And she wanted to go back into the water. It was warmer. More welcoming.

    But she couldn't. She couldn't even really open her eyes.

    Someone was talking in the distance but she couldn't hear them. Not clearly. The rustling in her ears was too loud.

    She knew she was throwing up all the water she gulped down. And she knew she was probably in no position to move or do something hentched over the ice. Because she was probably going to be dead in a couple of minutes. Brain-dead or just frozen for the rest of her now miserable life.

    She heard something with yes. And of course. And thanks to you - before there was some kind of new warmth around her.

    'Well this is it then Kate Bishop. If arresting your own mother on Christmas was extreme then dying on Christmas is just pathetic. At least you died saving a child and a dog from drowning.' she taught before the oh so long awaited darkness fully took over her fuzzy mind.

    By the time she slowly started to regain her consciousness she was in a warm blanket on something soft with someone talking to her.

    Unlike the scared voice she heard back when she was in the freezing lake this one was soft, it made her feel something warm.

    Slowly cracking her eyes open she cursed the gods above for the unbearable headache awaiting her.

    "Oh god." she started pushing herself up suddenly feeling every single cut and wound covering her body as she leaned her forehead on her palms rubbing her eyes.

    "How's Lila?" she asked as the fuzzy feeling in her ears started settling. Finally.

    There was a shrug or a huff. She couldn't make it out.
