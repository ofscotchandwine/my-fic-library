But I had no idea it would make you…” Lena searches for the words to explain it. “I didn’t know that what we did would happen! I’m sorry, Kara. Regardless of my intentions, I shouldn’t have done it. I’ll understand if you never forgive me.”

 

Kara is silent for a moment, chewing at her bottom lip as she stares at the opposite wall. 

 

“What spell?”

 

“It’s called Hex of Passion. It was supposed to make you act on your passions. Toward me.”

 

Kara nods. “Well, I guess now you know it worked,” she jokes. “But I should be the one apologizing. No matter my… passions , I shouldn’t have forced myself on you so aggressively.”

 

Lena shakes her head. “Kara, you didn’t have control. I took that away from you when I cast the spell.”

 

The blonde takes Lena’s hand in her own. “Lena, I knew what I was doing every single moment. I wanted to do everything I did. Even now, I know what I wanted and still want.” Kara’s eyes travel along Lena’s neck, to her exposed shoulder. Clearing her throat, she continues. “The only thing I didn’t expect was the extra, uhm, growth .”

 

Lena blushes and looks away. “Yeah… I didn't expect that either.” She’s quiet for a moment, reflecting. “For what it’s worth, I liked how aggressive you were.”

 

It’s Kara’s turn to blush with a nervous laugh. “Me too.”

 

“Did you mean what you said, when you, you know…”

 

“I meant every word I said to you, Lena.” Kara says earnestly. 

 

Tears threaten to fall from Lena’s eyes. “You love me?”

 

“More than anything.”

 

Kara’s face becomes blurry as Lena tries to blink away the tears. “I love you too.”

 

Lena drops the comforter to wrap her arms around Kara instead. Their lips come together like magnets. Somehow, it’s even better than the night before. Their kiss is soft now, not so desperate. 

 

“I love you.”

 

“I love you.”

 

They whisper it to each other over and over again, even when they eventually stop kissing and curl up together under the covers. They’re staring peacefully into each other’s eyes when Kara finally says something else. 

 

“Hey, Lena?”

 

“Mhm?”

 

“Maybe you could cast a spell like that again sometime? Since you liked me fucking you however I wanted…”

 

Lena scoffs, playfully shoving Kara’s shoulder before rolling over. 

 

“Maybe.”
