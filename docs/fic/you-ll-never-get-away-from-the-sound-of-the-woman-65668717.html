There was crying, screaming, bargaining, sure, but drinking in silence was high up there. Losing yourself at the bottom of a bottle after all your support is exhausted, if it was even there to begin with.

    Rio swears she can hear Agatha’s laughter even now, and she sighs, already exhausted. “Sometimes,” she relented, fully aware of the people now watching them, humans who would need to wake up with a headache and no recollection of this, “People, though. Not robots or spells.”

    Wanda’s eye doesn’t twitch, but her fingers tighten on her jacket.

    “I have someone, someone who needs saving,” she tells her, and moves past, settling in the stool next to Death. Rio pulls herself to the right, leaning her chin on her fist with her elbow on the counter, an insufferable smile on her face.

    “... Who?”

    She is curious, who could be so important to the Scarlet Witch that she would come to another entity for help? Wanda leaned closer, unblinking eyes hollow, with a spark of something she dared not word. Hope.

    “Take a look.”

    Rio allowed herself to wonder how long Agatha spent with Wanda to explain the ins and outs of her threads, but doesn’t allow the jealousy to surface fully. The world is engulfed in blue once more, and she barely looks at the spirits hovering around the woman. Scorned people who want revenge, begging Rio for it before she silences them all. She didn’t have time for the restless dead.

    The string of pain is bright, not as blinding as Yelena’s was, but just as harrowing to experience. Rio follows it, dragging her fingers along the string, until she finds herself opposite a familiar sight.

    Natasha Romanov Daughter - Sister - Avenger She hums in amusement, looking at the pink cherry blossoms above and the piles of gifts, available even now. In this version, Wanda stood, staring at the headstone with a numb expression and tears down her face.

    Rio steps back into the bar, out of the spiritual realm and opposite her again. “I was expecting your parents, your brother maybe,” she sighs, leaning back and into the warmth of the bar, letting her glamour slip off for a second, just to hear the humans gasp in fear.

    “But Natasha Romanov?”

    Wanda did not flinch, but she also reclined, holding out her hand and receiving a bottle from the fridge, guided by red as the bartender gasped and ducked. She uncapped the bottle with a thumb and took a deep drink.

    “Yes,” she ground out, “Natasha Romanov.”

    Rio stared for a moment, tilting her head out of curiosity.

    “... Why?”

    Wanda rolled the bottle on the table, the grooves on the bottom scraping against the wood, and she stared at Death.

    “I have plans for Pietro and my parents,” she admits, “but her death is harder. Her soul isn't where it should be. I cannot get it back.”

    Rio knows such an admission should be bad; she should want to stop her, but why? What’s four souls in a world that loses them so often? A bottle appears in her hand, too, guided by green, and she flicks the cap off.

    “So you come to me?”

    “Yes,” Wanda admits, “I come to you.”

    Rio held her bottle out to her.

    “Well, Scarlet Witch,” she taps their beers together, and brings it to her mouth with an amused grin, Agatha’s laughter in her ears, “Let's talk.”
