1. new world, old scars

Author's Note:

title is from Anson Seabra's Kerosene

The night is silent, curfew enacted by the Hope robots patrolling the city. The moon is full and the stars sparkle in the sky, starkly bright against the dark backdrop. The eerie glow of red radiates from CatCo as Kara glides through the air towards the top floor.

She touches down softly, her boots tapping against the floor as she flies through the open window.

The bullpen is a maze of desks and cubicles, but Kara doesn't dare use her x-ray vision. She wanders through the aisles formed by the desks and calls out as bravely as she can.

"Miss Luthor? Lena? Lena, it's me."

The bullpen is silent, save for her voice echoing in the large space.

"It's Kara."

She turns a corner and hears Lena's voice. Her familiar tenor echoes through the space between them and when Kara's eyes land on Lena, she feels aching similarity, but simultaneously, she has never felt further away from Lena.

"Who's Kara?"

Kara feels the words strike her between the ribs, knocking into her lungs and forcing her breath out in a sharp exhale. She forces herself to find the words she needs to answer.

"Just a friend."

Lena tilts her head curiously, her eyebrow arching suspiciously as she pushes herself up from the spinning chair.

"Supergirl. What are you doing here?"

Her tone is surprised, almost cheerful, but Kara can see it in Lena's eyes that she was probably expecting this visit.

"I'm so sorry, Lena. I didn't... I thought... I didn't mean for this to happen."

Kara watches in real-time as Lena shuts down, sees the shutter sliding down over her face, squashing her expressions until all that's left are hard eyes and a twisted smile.

"Well then, what did you mean? What did you want? What did you do for this to happen to me?"

Lena's face contorts, the corners of her mouths turning down and her lips twisting into a sneer as she spits the words at Kara, beginning to circle her.

Kara follows her footsteps, tensing as they circle each other. Two women, one tired, one vengeful; A Super and a Luthor, fated to be enemies.

Kara's head is bowed as she begins to speak.

"I... I wished for something. It was a stupid wish and I never should've made it, but I just wanted you to be happy. You have to understand that." Kara is pleading now, her head raised again as she begs Lena with her eyes.

Lena scoffs and levels Kara with one of her CEO glares.

"Happy? Do I look happy to you? Do you think I was happy when my helicopter crashed onto the concrete helipad? When Lillian dragged me to a lab and experimented on me instead of fixing me? Do you think I was happy then?!"

Kara closes her mouth around a gasp, forcing it down as she looks back at her toes.

"I'm sorry. I didn't... I didn't mean for this to happen. I thought... I thought you would be happier if you had never met me. I didn't... I didn't think it through."

"Didn't think is right."

Lena seems to lose some of her fire, her defenses easing down, the barbs no longer aimed at Kara.

"So, what? I'm just some silly imagination that won't exist anymore?"

Kara realizes then that Lena is tired, too. She's hurt and it's Kara's fault.

"I'm sorry," Kara says in lieu of an answer, hoping she can somehow convey her regret in those two words.

"Sorry doesn't fix this."

Lena sounds colder now and it's too late when Kara realizes Lena has pulled away, has drawn into herself.

By the time she looks back up, Lena is standing directly in front of her, the red glow of the building eerie behind her, but Lena still looks beautiful. Lena has always looked beautiful.

Even as Kara sees the glow of kryptonite seep past Lena's coat, even as the beam of green shoots towards her, pummeling her in the abdomen and forcing her to the ground; even as Lena tips her chin up, her vision blurring, Lena still looks beautiful. 

"I won't fight you, Lena. I... I really am sorry," Kara chokes through the pain.

"I'm sorry too."

The world fades into blue and Kara sits up on her apartment couch, gasping for breath. The feeling of kryptonite lingers, phantom pains she can't seem to brush away. She can still feel where it hit her, can still feel Lena's fingers on her chin, can still feel the cold stare Lena had directed at her.

She sends Mxy away, his parting words bouncing around in her head.

"That kind of magic can't be forced... it has to be found."

She stews in her thoughts up on the roof of her apartment. The sky reminds her of the one she had seen in that other world and she closes her eyes. In her mind's eye she sees the eerie red emanating off of CatCo, Lena, framed in black and green, and her eyes snap back open.

She keeps casting glances at CatCo, searching for the red glow that isn't there.

2. scars, old and new

Kara flies through the night sky, sliding through the currents smoothly.

She sees the tall building in the distance, the single office lit up a stark contrast to the rest of the building. The L-shaped logo glints in the moonlight as she touches down on the balcony. 

She can almost imagine the cold of the concrete creeping past her boots and up her legs as she grounds herself there.

The deja vu hits her full force as she takes her first step forward and she almost keels over with the force of it.

The soft glow from inside the office is an indication that there's someone inside it.

Lena. Lena's inside the office.

Kara has to keep reminding herself that it's her Lena, not the Lena she had seen just an hour ago.

It's Lena. It's Lena. It's Lena.

She breathes in and out, steeling herself as she takes another step forward.

Her long strides make her feel better, make her feel purposeful, and by the time she's walked the length of the balcony to the door (thank Rao she had landed on the other side of the balcony), she's confident enough to talk to Lena.

She pushes open the glass door and steps down into the office. Lena is poring over her desk, rubbing her temples and muttering as she scribbles something on a document.

"Lena."

She sees Lena jolt in her seat, sees the way she looks up frantically, eyes snapping to Kara. The coldness sets in and Kara feels her heart sink.

"Supergirl."

Lena's tone is even, cold, just like her expression.

"What can I do for National City's superhero?"

"Lena. I just... I know that you don't believe me, but I didn't want to hurt you. I never wanted to hurt you. I just... I was selfish and I wanted to keep you and... and I lost you." Kara laughs at the irony, a watery sound that chokes up her throat and bubbles past her lips.

"The one time. The one time I was selfish, I lost you. I've never been more selfish in my life. I'm always the selfless one. I'm always the one who has to sacrifice everything, but when it came to you I just... I couldn't. I couldn't leave you. I've never wanted anything more than I've wanted to just stay with you. I... I even tried to fix it."

Kara buries her fingers into her hair, carding them through as if she can rake the memories away, head tipped down.

"I tried to fix it and you died. You died every time until you didn't, and then we all died. And then... and then I... I wondered what it would be like if we'd never met."

She hears Lena's muffled gasp, trapped in her throat but still audible to her, knows Lena will never want to see her after this, but she pushes through regardless. She can't stop now that she's started, and telling Lena everything is the only way she can tamp down the rising hysteria.

"You... you hated me. You ruled over National City because I wasn't there when your helicopter crashed, because Lillian gave you a kryptonite heart and I wasn't there to save you, and I'm sorry. I'm so so sorry. I didn't... I didn't mean to hurt you like that."

Kara isn't even sure who she's apologizing to anymore. She's talking to Lena, sure, but she can feel the other Lena's presence, too.

"I never meant it. I just... I just wanted to keep you. I just wanted you to love me, because..."

And this is it. This is really why she had done everything. This is why she had hurt Lena beyond repair, why she had gone back in time to try and fix it.

"Because you looked at me with hate in your eyes, and I never wanted to see that again. I didn't... I'm in love with you Lena. I'm irrevocably in love with you... and it tears me apart that I'm also the one who hurt you the most."

Kara is sobbing now, her arms wrapped around her midsection as if she can hold herself together, keep herself from falling apart and shattering into pieces on the tiled floor of Lena's office.

The office is silent save for her quiet sobs and the sniffles that bounce between them, and Kara is so scared to look up and have to see the same hatred directed at her again, but she has to. She has to know how Lena feels before she's kicked out of her life forever because if there's just a chance that Lena might not hate her, she has to hope.

So, she looks up.

Lena's eyes are red-rimmed, and her hand is clasped over her mouth, her other also wrapped around her midsection.

Lena looks horrified and surprised, but there's no hatred.

There's no love either.

Kara feels her heart sink even further, feels the second wave of tears stinging her eyes. She blinks them away and turns around, bending over, curling into herself, trying to stave off the nausea.

She takes a step forward, and then another, and then another until she's at the balcony door. Lena makes no move to stop her.

She pushes open the glass door, staggering onto the balcony. Just as she's ready to leap off the balcony, probably unsteadily, she hears a sound behind her. It's almost silent, but she hears it. It's the unmistakable sound of Lena's voice and she's saying no.

The wind snatches it away from her, though, and she entertains the idea for a moment before brushing it off. The frenzied ice-cold wind whips past her, snapping her cape behind her.

She crouches down, ready to push off the cold concrete, feeling her boots dig into the concrete as she grounds herself to get as much power as she can, to fly as far away from this battleground full of burned bridges.

Before she can leap off, she feels it.

She feels Lena's hand on her wrist, a whispered no echoing in the space between them, and then the grip tightens.

"Don't go. Please. Stay."

Kara goes.

She turns around, her eyes shining with hope as she takes in Lena's desperate expression, and then feels her heart drop as her comms crackle to life.

She wants nothing more than to crush them in her fist and throw the remnants over the edge of the balcony, but Alex's voice comes over them before she gets a chance too.

"Supergirl, there's an incident downtown. There are two aliens ripping through stores and buildings. They're headed for a hospital."

Her face falls and she wants to scream because this is the worst timing possible for an emergency to happen. She looks at Lena helplessly, pointing at her ear, but instead of Lena turning back into herself, retreating and withdrawing, she only smiles and nods her head.

The unspoken invitation to come back later hangs in the air between them as Kara takes off, fighting against the currents as she reaches the scene of the crime.

Alex is right. The two aliens really are ripping through buildings. Their claws sink into the brick and wood easily, pulling off chunks of concrete and floor.

Kara has to dart in to save several handfuls of people before she can get a chance to face off against the aliens.

One of them has purple skin and the other has dark red skin.

She launches herself towards them with the help of her superspeed and the rest of the fight is a blur.

She doesn't go easy on them. She has to fight fast and hard. Lena is waiting for her.

She remembers feeling the force of several punches and the agony of one of their claws scraping over her torso, but she makes it through the fight mostly unscathed.

She throws the unconscious aliens to the DEO and flies off, promising Alex that she'll come back.

She throws a meaningful look over her shoulder and sees the realization dawn on Alex.

She sees the look Alex returns, the one that says 'you better tell me about this later', and nods before taking off again, feeling the ground cave and crack just a little beneath the force of her push.

She makes it to L-Corp in record time, breaking the sound barrier and touching down as quickly as she can without breaking the balcony.

Lena is right where she left her and Kara's shoulders sag with relief.

Lena is sitting on the couch, a tumbler of scotch in her hand. She isn't looking towards Kara and doesn't notice her, but Kara can see the noticeable tracks of tears and the glisten of them. She clears her throat nervously as she holds onto the edge of the door, afraid she might be intruding yet again.

Lena looks up and wipes away her tears quickly before inviting Kara in with a gesture to the couch.

Kara tries not to let the distance she puts between them sting too much. Lena needs time to heal and process what Kara has confessed. What's now sitting between them.

She almost wishes she could take it back. But then her memories cast her back and she remembers a Lena shrouded in shadows and pain and she knows it wouldn't be any better if she waited.

The moon is bright and the silver light casts longer and longer shadows into Lena's office, the floor more dark than light.

"I... I meant what I said." Kara breaks the silence first, trying to find a way to start to talk, to somehow be able to heal what they've been through.

"I know."

Lena is quiet, her voice soft and barely discernible, even in the otherwise silent room.

"I'm... I'm really sorry, Lena. I should've... I just... I just wanted you to know, but I... I just wanted you to know the truth. I understand if you hate me or if you never want to see me again. I get it. I'm... I would be more than honored if you would let me even just be your friend again." Kara fidgets with her hands, trying to find something to say, to fill the silence that pushes them further and further apart from one another.

"No, no. It's... it's not that, Kara. I'm just... I didn't expect any of this and you have to understand that I... I'm still hurt. It doesn't just go away overnight."

Kara nods, her eyes sad and understanding.

"I know. I completely know that. I just miss us. I just... all I ever wanted was to be your friend and you... you didn't know, so you didn't treat me differently. Everyone else, no matter what they say, as soon as I tell them, they treat me with an extra layer of something because I'm suddenly not just lowly reporter Kara Danvers, I'm also National City's hero, Supergirl. It's ironic, really. I should've known that you would be the only person who would still see me as just Kara. And because I didn't tell you, I ruined that, too."

Kara barks a laugh into the quiet of the room and feels like crying again.

"I just wanted one thing. I just wanted one thing and I couldn't even get it right."

Kara bows her head again and feels the tears stinging her eyes again, welling up and threatening to spill over. She blinks them away again and stands at Lena's continued silence.

"I'm sorry. I shouldn't... I'm just being sorry for myself. I'm... I should go."

Lena doesn't make a move to stop her, still mechanically swirling her scotch in the tumbler. Kara sighs heavily, curses herself for hoping, and stands up. She wonders if there will ever be a day where she isn't crushed by her hopes, where she manages to see the dark side of things, enough so that she doesn't get beaten back down when her hopes turn out to be too high.

Only when she reaches Lena's desk, peering down at the cracked glass of their framed picture, does she hear Lena move again.

"I'm sorry too, Kara. I'm... when I heard Lex tell me the truth, I already knew it. I was in denial for so long because I refused to believe that Supergirl, the very same hero who had pushed me away for making kryptonite, was Kara, the friendly reporter that acted more like a human golden retriever than anything else." Lena chuckles and the corners of Kara's mouth quirk up into a smile.

"More than that, I didn't want to have to put Supergirl, the hero that was beaten into a coma and has had several near-death incidents, with Kara, the cheery reporter that I've only ever seen smile."

Kara's mouth twists down and she sighs again, but she doesn't make a move to walk back towards the couch.

"It's not just near-death either."

Lena's head snaps up and Kara finally gets a good look at her eyes for the first time since she stepped into her office.

"What?"

"I've died before, Lena. I heard my family when Red Daughter fought me until my heart stopped. I could hear Alura when I almost died in that alternate universe. She was calling to me, telling me to join her and our family. I wanted so badly to just step into the light and make the pain go away, but you know why I pulled through? The first time was because of Alex's voice, but the second time... the second time was because of you, Lena."

Kara smiles, and it's wry but it's more than genuine.

"I knew I couldn't give up because you were still hurting and I had to find a way to help because it was my fault that you were hurt and I couldn't... I couldn't live with that."

Kara keeps going, her thoughts crashing into each other, stretching on and on into an even longer train of thought.

"I thought that if I could save enough people on Earth, then maybe I could make up for all the people who died on Krypton, for all the people I was supposed to die with. I thought I could make up for the people I couldn't save by sacrificing myself time and time again. I still think that maybe if I can be selfless enough, if I can find it in myself to give myself up even when I have you, then maybe I'll have made all this pain worth it."

Kara feels the tears flow over, tracking down her face.

"But I can't and it won't. I can't give you up because you're everything I wanted and I've never been happier before I met you. If I sacrifice myself for the good of the many and lose out on a chance of a life with you, that's not worth it. So, I was selfish. I was selfish and I kept my secret from you because I just wanted you to love me and you did. You did."

Kara chokes back the sob rising in her throat.

"You loved me as Kara and you respected me as Supergirl and I wasn't allowed to tell you. And then, and then I was... I was thinking of telling you, and everything just fell apart. I... I got scared and I lashed out. I was scared not because you're a Luthor, but because you're Lena. You mean everything to me and to even entertain the idea that you could make kryptonite was terrifying for me. So, I lashed out and I hurt you and then you turned around and still loved Kara."

Kara heaves silent sobs, panting for breath as she spews more and more confessions.

"I hated myself for lying to you, for making you believe in something that wasn't true, but I just kept telling myself that if I just waited one more day, maybe it would be better, maybe the timing would be better or you would hate Supergirl less, and then one day we were fighting Lex and I told myself I had to tell you. I told myself I had to. And then Lex got there first and there were no more days to wait."

Kara leans heavily against Lena's desk, hoping it will support her enough so she can still stand under the burden of everything she holds.

Lena is silent.

"If it makes a difference, I really am sorry and I... I really do love you. I love you more than anything or anyone I've ever loved, but I ruined that, too."

Lena looks shocked, like she's still trying to process everything Kara has told her, all the secrets she's given away in a single night.

"I understand if you need time to process this. I just... I just wanted you to know."

Lena's tentative nod is all the answer Kara needs and she tells herself again that Lena needs more time, that she can just wait one more day for Lena to be ready to talk, for Lena to be able to process this.

(She knows she would wait forever and a day for Lena.)

The moon is bright and the stars are still sparkling in the sky when she makes it back to her apartment. She doesn't sleep.

She stays up and names the constellations one by one, trying to figure out what she would've seen in Krypton's night sky. She tries to remember names, tastes, smells, things, people she's lost. Some of the memories are lost to time, but she revels in what she has left until the faintest light peeks out from behind the horizon.

She listens as the city comes alive, the bustling and honking of cars and buses and trains filling the air. The street below her apartment balcony comes alive, the pattering and shuffling of vendors and civilians overlapping and the sound of doors opening and closing resonating through her building. 

She watches the sun rise for another moment before walking back into her apartment and getting ready for work.

3. looking past the scars to something better

After weeks of unanswered texts of invitations and offers and jokes, Lena finally replies.

It's random and something exactly like they would do before and Kara wonders if Lena's already forgotten that Kara confessed her love to her before the elation kicks in. It's an inconspicuous picture of a restaurant somewhere in National City, but Kara knows exactly what it means. 

Sometimes, just for fun, Lena and Kara would send pictures back and forth of places in National City that they had hidden a message in and it was an inconspicuous thing that no one else would understand.

Kara is practically vibrating in her seat by the time lunch break starts and she almost bowls over several other employees rushing out of CatCo.

She finally makes it outside of the building, almost flying out of the front door as she breathes in the fresh air and soaks in the yellow sun.

She peers around the surrounding buildings as cars honk in the streets and birds chirp in the downtown park and the search begins.

Kara can see some of the surrounding restaurants in the picture and she can see a hint of green on the side. She tries to remember where she's seen the familiar shingle on the side of one of the restaurants and then it hits her. 

That's the other Noonan's in downtown National City! She doesn't go to it as much because it's farther away, but sometimes when she's coming back into the city, she'll stop by and grab a sticky bun or two... or ten.

Too impatient to wait for a taxi or bus, she jumps into her suit and flies over the city. She sees the familiar shingle and then the restaurant next to it that featured in Lena's picture. A corner of a white piece of yardstick is peeking out one of the decorative bushes and she plucks it out before flying off again.

She's sure media coverage will be curious about her visit, but she can't find it in herself to even try to be subtle. Lena has left her a message and it's been weeks since they've last talked; Kara can't help it.

She flies up to the roof of CatCo and glances at the time. She has about 10 minutes left of her lunch break. She quickly turns the card around and reads the elegant, flowing script on the other side.

the bench. 7.

Kara smiles again. Another inside joke. Since Lena was always targeted by assassins, Kara joked that maybe they should develop code names and vague ways to be able to communicate with each other but not have anyone else know.

The bench is referring to their bench, the one in National City Park. It's a bench they've used for lunches, coffee, or just simple catch-ups when Lena doesn't have enough time for anything else.

Now, they're using it again, and Kara's heart wells over with love. The bench is a place that they throw away everything except for each other and now Lena is inviting her there.

She tucks the card away into a pocket and flies back down, rushing back into CatCo just in time to hear Andrea yelling for her.

She's never felt happier; the sun has nothing on Lena.

That evening, she's in her apartment. It's already 6:12, but she's too nervous to do anything except call Alex over.

Alex arrives five minutes later, looking concerned.

"Hey, you okay?"

"I'm... I'm nervous. And scared. What if... what if this is goodbye?"

Alex frowns confused, and only then does Kara remember that she still hasn't told Alex anything yet.

"Okay, wait, back up. What's happening?"

Kara breathes out and starts figuring out how she should start.

"You should sit down first."

Alex sits down on the couch next to Kara cautiously, instinctively reaching to hold Kara's hands, grounding her.

It's something they always do. When Kara struggled with a panic attack, Alex would always hold onto her, telling her to breathe with her, to listen for her heartbeat. It always worked, and ever since, Alex has always used it to try and ground Kara, to try and reassure her, to make sure Kara knows she's there for her.

"Take your time. I'm here."

"It's about Lena. She, um... she..."

She gets choked up and the tears well up again, but Alex just murmurs reassurances and lets her cry.

"She... we talked about me being Supergirl and I... I told her. I told her and she said she needed time. She invited me to our place and I'm scared. What if she's saying goodbye? What if... what if she hates me?"

Kara bows her head and cries into Alex's shoulder.

"I'm so scared, Alex. I'm so scared."

Alex doesn't say anything. She just holds her and lets her cry and cry and cry.

When the sobbing turns into sniffles and the hand of the clock reaches 6:30, Alex starts talking.

"I haven't seen anyone look at you the way Lena does. If she doesn't love you, I don't know who does. She definitely won't hate you, that's for sure."

Kara looks up at Alex, hopeful. If anyone would know, it would be Alex.

"Really? Are you sure?"

"I'm sure."

Kara can't help but believe that Alex is telling the truth. The earnestness of her tone and the exasperated affection is all Kara needs to know how sure Alex is.

"Okay."

Kara wipes away her tears and gets up.

"Okay," Alex responds, still smiling at Kara.

"Okay."

Kara smiles back tentatively and she feels hope sparking in her chest, a burning ember that warms her from the inside out.

The sky is darkening by the time she makes it to the bench, the oranges and pinks blending with the darker blues and purples as the black starts to creep in from the East.

She doesn't have to wait long. Lena shows up only a few minutes later, and Kara gets up before awkwardly sitting back down.

"Um, hi."

Lena seems to startle at being directly addressed and fidgets with her hands.

"Hi."

They sit in silence for another moment before Kara realizes Lena isn't going to say anything.

"Have I ever told you that you can still see Krypton from here?"

She sees Lena perk up in the corner of her eye but she keeps talking, looking up at the sky.

"You can see it. It's a little bit red and you probably can't see it with your eyes, but it's there. If you fly up just past the clouds you can see it. It's just a tiny red spot, but it's there. Sometimes, I wonder when it would've been on Krypton."

Kara squints, seeing past the clouds until she can pinpoint the little dot of light.

"Do you miss them?"

Lena's voice startles Kara out of her reverie and she blinks back to the present.

"What?"

"Do you miss them? Krypton? Your parents?"

Kara wants to retreat, to draw back into herself, but she keeps telling herself that Lena isn't trying to mock her, that Lena doesn't want to take her memories away from her.

"I've never talked to anyone about this before."

Lena immediately opens her mouth to apologize but Kara continues.

"I was always afraid that I would lose my memories by sharing them with other people."

She looks over at Lena and smiles.

"I trust you with them, though."

Kara looks back up at the now dark-blue sky and sighs.

"I do. I miss them so much sometimes. Sometimes, I just want to be me, Kara, and now that's gone. I can't just be normal anymore because I have superpowers. I can't be normal anymore because I don't even remember what it felt like to be normal on Krypton."

Kara closes her eyes and sifts through her memories again. It's been weeks since the last time and already one of the colors is fading from her. She opens her eyes again, greeted with the alien colors of Earth, bright and vivid, burning into her eyes. Krypton's colors were softened by the red of the sun, dimmer but warmer.

"You were the only one I could be normal with. I could just be Kara with you. That's all I wanted, to just be Kara, and I finally found someone I could do that with. I should've known that you would be the only one to treat me normally no matter what. It's only fair that the universe decides that the one person I keep my secret from is the one person who would've accepted it."

Lena hums then, shaking her head.

"Kara, I do accept you. I never really hated you. It was never... I never hated you per se. I just hated the idea that you and Supergirl were one person, that two people I knew so well and who interacted with me so differently were the exact same person. You have to understand that it's hard for me to reconcile the two of you together."

Lena tilts her head and turns to look at Kara more directly.

"But I think I see it now. I... it took me too long and I hurt you so many times, but I think I get it now. It's just you and me. Kara and Lena."

Kara hadn't even registered when she had started crying but she feels the tears and she leans forward, hoping that maybe Lena will hold her, that she'll hold her in turn.

Lena's arms open and she settles into them and she can't imagine a time when she didn't have this warmth.

"I'm so sorry, Lena."

Lena places a kiss on her forehead and wipes away her tears when Kara looks up at her.

"I'm sorry too."

4. new beginnings

Kara pulls down on the bottom of her shirt, smoothing her hands carefully down the front as she inspects her appearance in the mirror. Alex is on the couch, feet thrown lazily over the armrest as she scrolls through her phone.

"Kara, you look great. Don't worry about it! Lena's going to love you no matter what."

Alex taps on her phone some more before announcing, "Plus, you're supposed to leave in 5 minutes."

Kara startles, glancing at her wristwatch quickly before beginning to panic.

"Alex! I told you to warn me!!"

Alex finally looks away from the phone screen, disgruntled.

"Didn't I just do that?"

Kara doesn't hear her, too busy gathering all her things as she rushes out of the door.

"Glasses," Alex murmurs under her breath, watching as Kara zips back in for them before the door slams shut behind her.

Kara wrings her hands nervously as she approaches the casual Italian restaurant.

Breathing in and out, she pushes open the door, hearing the jangle of the bell above the door as she steps onto tiled floor. The smell of yeast and dough and tomato sauce immediately fills the air and she sighs happily. Pizza isn't nearly as good as potstickers, but boy is she craving some right now.

She steps forward a little more confidently, looking around the room until she spots a hand waving in her direction. Lowering her gaze, the hand connects to none other than Lena and she hurries in her direction, smiling.

"Hi," she breathes, standing at the edge of the table, unsure of what to do.

"Hi," Lena answers, equally breathy and with an edge of nervousness.

"Um, I'll just, uh," Kara waves her hands in the direction of the opposite side of the booth and slides in, barely putting her weight on the leather seat 

They both sit there, stiff and unsure, until Kara finally settles her weight onto the seat, breathing in and out quietly as she reaches for the menu. She pops it open but doesn't look at it, instead moving to ask Lena a question.

"I, uh, I'm... what do you recommend?"

Lena relaxes back against her seat, smiling broadly but biting her lip in an effort to conceal it.

"What do I recommend? Kara, you know that I haven't been to this restaurant before." Lena arches an eyebrow, bringing her glass of water up to take a sip.

"Right! I, uh, yeah... uh... I'm— I'm kinda of nervous," Kara admits, smiling sheepishly.

Lena chortles delightedly.

"It's okay, I got you what you normally like to order. Two pepperoni pizzas with extra cheese and extra pepperoni, right?" Lena tilts her head questioningly, gaze locked on Kara.

Kara blushes under the intensity of her stare and ducks her head as her hand comes up unconsciously to rub the back of her neck nervously.

She licks her lips before speaking, stuttering nervously, "uh, yeah. That's right."

Lena, unseen by Kara, smiles softly at Kara's nervous behavior before hiding it with another sip of water.

"So..." Kara begins, trailing off into silence as she regains her composure.

"So," Lena drawls, arching an eyebrow in Kara's direction.

"So, uh, how are you?" Kara asks, hand reaching unconsciously forward on the table, her palm upturned for Lena's hand to take.

Lena smiles, opening her mouth to speak but being interrupted by the pizza arriving.

"Here you go, miss," the waiter says, placing the pizza down on the table before leaving them alone again.

Lena chuckles a bit to herself before looking back at Kara.

"I'm doing fine," she bites her lip, pausing a moment before she decides to correct herself. "No I'm... I'm doing better."

She places her hand in Kara's twining their fingers together as she smiles at her.

Kara smiles back, eyes shining with a little more than happiness.

"Good. I'm glad."

"...Now, which pizza is yours again?"

Lena can't stop the laugh that bursts past her lips. It sounds like forgiveness, like something blooming, new and bright.
