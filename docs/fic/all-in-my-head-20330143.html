The Job

Alex and Kara Danvers were known as the dynamic duo at their police station.  These two had the highest arrest rates out of everyone in their precinct and they teamwork was legendary.  All of the rookies worshipped them and all of the veterans respected them and loved them.  It was always “good job Damnvers sisters!” when they solved another case and a happy slap on the backs for a job well done and a case closed.  The two were always so in sync and used their strengths to cover for the other’s weaknesses to solve cases.  Where Alex was more logical and pragmatic, Kara was compassionate and emotional.  They were both equally good with their guns, but Alex had more finesse when it came to hand to hand combat while Kara was brute strength.  Kara also had an edge with being taller and faster to tackle any escaping criminals, much to their chagrin and Kara’s delight.  Alex was serious and broody whereas Kara was the sun incarnate, a human puppy full of sunshine and an always joyful disposition.  The two sisters had never failed in bringing criminals to justice and their flawless record showed.  And their police captain, John Jones, adored them and was the father they both lost, Kara twice and Alex once.  He was their rock after Jeremiah died in a lab accident where he worked.

This is precisely why the last few months had been utterly frustrating.  No one had been able to do anything about it for nearly a year.  The World Killers case.  A group of three vigilante women.  So far, they had done no harm, but they were also vigilantes.  There was very little information on the table about the three women so far.  Of the three women, Supreme was the tallest and had an S marking her outfit, Roulette was the next tallest and marked herself with an R, and Leader was the shortest but not by much and marked herself with an L on her chest.  All three women always seemed to show up when a crime alert had come through on the police scanners or crimes in progress that hadn’t even been reported yet and it was stumping the police how they knew.  Any one of them could have resources and unimaginable wealth because of how well designed their suits were and how they always seemed to be one step ahead of the police force.

Countless cases of the women getting away without even being engaged by the police.  And if there was even any contact it was minimal at best.  Like, a shadow, a mere glimpse of a retreating figure already too far gone to give proper chase.  It was making the police look incompetent in the media’s eyes and they had a following and sympathy because they were doing what the police supposedly couldn’t do.  Many people in the media and social media supported them and were angry the police were actively trying to catch them when the Police Commissioner, Maxwell Lord, made appearances and had press conferences about them, people booed at him an made nasty comments while he spoke.  They already disliked him long before that because of rumors about him being corrupt, but this just added to the fuel of public hatred for him.

While no one at the police station outrightly hated them or wanted to persecute them that badly other than Morgan Edge, who was in charge of evidence, there was some frustration and unease about how efficient they were.  There was never a strand of hair, any form of DNA, they could trace.  All of them kept their hair under their helmets so anyone who had caught a glimpse of them couldn’t even say what hair color they had.  Even those lucky enough in the media to catch some image of them only caught the most defining features of their suits, the single letter on their suit.  They didn’t even know what any of the women looked like because they wore helmets that were equipped with voice modifiers making them nearly impossible to catch as it were.  It was a small miracle that someone, at some point, had posted on social media that all the letters on their suits were L, S, and R and had dubbed them The World Killers and given them names based on these letters: Leader, Supreme, and Roulette.

The media ran wild with the monikers and started referring to them as such.  One of them used knives and daggers exclusively while one had some kind of high-tech powered suit with many unknown capabilities and one of them was the strong.  Despite the fact that the people they were victimizing were criminals, many of them were quick to confess what they knew of the women.  Supreme was strong, could pick up a man who looked twice her size by the throat, Roulette was good with knives and her accuracy and precision was scary, and Leader had so many capabilities with her suit there always seemed to be something more.  Not a single attempt to capture them had gone well.  There was never anything more than a glimpse of dark suits retreating if they even saw that much of them.  Partners Hartmann and Vasquez had gotten nowhere when trying to figure out how to lure them out of hiding to capture them.  Every sting they seemed one step ahead and had somehow managed to outsmart every attempt and plan to capture them so far.  The funniest thing was seeing how angry Edge got while Hartmann and Vasquez seemed okay with it, to a certain extent anyways.  Edge was openly sexist and made comments about them being women outsmarting dumb men.  He almost got a fist in the face from various superiors in the police task force for his sharp words.

One day Hartmann had explained, tongue in cheek, “well, even if vigilantism isn’t legal, they are doing the work for us and I get to see my kids and wife more than five minutes at night.”  Vasquez had slapped him on the back and smiled in agreement while winking at the Danvers sisters at the next desk over.  They had winked back.

So, after a few months of failures the case was reassigned to the Danvers sisters in the hopes of better results from the previous failures coming straight from Lord’s command.

John had everyone in the group meeting to pass on the news.  “Okay, so The World Killers have been on the move for nearly a year now and we have nothing on them since they surfaced.  Let’s be realistic here.  We don’t have a lot of information on them, they are very prepared and armed, and all attempts at capture have been stonewalled by their intellect.  These three women are always ten steps ahead of us and have garnered media support and the public are rallying for them.  Hartmann and Vasquez, did you get anything useful out of what you did to try and catch them?”

“Not much, really, sir.”  Hartmann responded.

“I mean, the most we can tell you is that they are very well organized and they can definitely listen in on our radios.  They might know police code because they could always tell when we tried to set something up to catch them.  I think out of anything, that is our biggest obstacle.  Whatever it is they do, they know everything we have planned from what we say down to what we’re planning.  If they don’t know what we were planning, we might be able catch them off guard, maybe once.”  Vasquez added.

“Yeah, something else is that they are all really smart and skilled.  The one guy said that he couldn’t get out of the place he was robbing fast enough because Roulette threw daggers that had him pinned to a wall.  He was afraid of moving and cutting off his … erm, privates.  Another person who was caught by them said that he’s worked out all of his life and is 188 pounds of muscle (85.3 kilograms) and Supreme lifted him like he weighed nothing by the scruff of his neck.  He doesn’t think she’s even human for being able to do that.  And that Leader person.  She’s fast and really smart, captain.  Like, I think she’s behind all of their escape routes being so flawless because one woman we arrested observed that she seems to give out the orders for retreats and if one way might be blocked, she figures out all the other ways to get out.  More than one criminal arrested has said that she always has an exit strategy for them.”  Hartmann continued, recalling what he could of prior criminal statements.

“Out of all of them, the moniker Leader might be the most accurate because she is obviously their ring leader.  She might not be the strongest, but she is definitely the one in charge because of the way they seem to look to her for cues.  The one time we had one of our own,” she nodded over to Price, “said that she was the one who called them off, realizing that Price is one of ours within minutes of being there.”

“Took one look at me and told them to retreat.”  Price laughed a little, “not sure how since my hair was blue and I looked like a punk.”  Everyone else in the precinct chuckled because Price definitely looked like a rebel with her blue hair and lip piercing.  “Maybe I should’ve worn a beanie and a leather jacket!”  Kara’s laughter was contagious.  She was guffawing like it was the funniest joke she’d ever heard.

“Maybe you should have tattooed ‘gangster’ on your arm to really pull it off Chlobear,” Kara said, still cracking up.

“Whatever, Kara!  Like you can do better with the way you look!” she said, smiling at her nickname.  No one else was allowed to call her that other than her girlfriend Max.

“I probably could actually!  You tried being a punk but it didn’t work and they ran off again!”

John, though he was the most serious, had a small smile on his face.  “All right, all right.  Though it’s true that Price’s punk looks didn’t fool them, we need to come up with something better.  Hartmann and Vasquez don’t take this to heart, but you’re off the case and the Danvers sisters are to take over the case now.  This is coming straight from the commissioner himself.  If we work as more of a team and pool all of our resources maybe we can get somewhere with this case.  Seeing as you have the best arresting record, what do you ladies think we should do?”

Kara spoke up first, “well, I’ve had some ideas about how we can lure them in!  See, we all know that they are doing things to prevent crimes and aid in our arrest record.”  A few grumbles of agreement as well as Edge muttering under his breath, more than likely something unsavory.  “I think the best way to trick them is by setting up something that is going to be a long haul they won’t anticipate.  And another thing is that all the details have to between only a select few which we will pick later.  I think my plan will only work if we choose just a group of four at most and someone who hasn’t tried anything yet.  There are two people I would like to consult as the other half because Alex and I will, of course, be one half of the sting.”

Alex nodded in agreement, “as we talked about, I think this is best way to get things done.  We have to keep it as quiet as possible so we have less of a chance of it being leaked.”

Sawyer spoke up next, “well, seeing as you two almost always seem to know what you’re doing, I think that’s pretty reasonable.  Just wish I was part of it too.”  Maggie winked, causing Alex to look away and pink to creep up her neck.  Kara kept insisting that Alex just talk to her at least if she wasn’t going to ask her out too.  Alex insisted she had no idea what Kara was talking about because she was professional above everything else.

“Okay, everyone, back to your posts.  Danvers, both of you in my office now!”  With that, everyone went back to what they were doing, though Edge decided he was going to comment on how he didn’t get the case instead.

“Those two aren’t even that good.  I could do a better job,” he announced loudly to another officer.

Maggie laughed.  “You’re part of evidence.  Not only are you not a detective, it takes being smart to solve a case, Edge.”  He turned red in the face but knew better than to argue with his superior with so many people around.

“Watch yourself, Sawyer,” he said instead.

“If I didn’t know any better, I’d say you’re threatening me, Edge.  But seeing as it’s just you, Edge, I don’t have anything to scared of either way.”  She smirked as she walked away, making his face turn almost purple in anger.

“Women like that need to be put in their place,” he said, being overheard by Vasquez.

“You better watch your mouth, Edge.  Men like you don’t know when you’re beaten.  You don’t have any talent, that’s why you’re in evidence.  If you were smart enough and had morals, you’d be a detective, of you were faster with response times, you’d be on patrol, and if you knew how to use a computer, you’d be gathering information and be one of our tech support officers.  There’s nothing you really contribute other than making smart comments because you’re not good enough to do anything else here.”  He brought his hand up like he was going to punch her.

Before he could Hartmann came up, “you better not think about putting your hands on my partner, Edge.”  There was a hard edge to the normally easy-going man.  Edge gave Hartmann a look back.

“Of course not, Hartmann,” he said, as he brought his arm down to his sides, fists clenched as he gave them both a hard look before leaving finally.

“Bastard,” Vasquez muttered and Hartmann nodded in agreement.  “I wish he’d give me a reason to get him suspended or fired, man.”

“Don’t we all, Vasquez?”  She laughed.  “Come on, let’s see what cases we have now that we’re off of the big one."

“We’re still arguing about who because Kara wants to get kidnapped and I object to that.  Yeah, I know that it’s all pretend to catch The World Killers, but I’m still a big sister.  I mean, I know you’re easier to want to protect since you have that cute face everyone wants to protect, but I’m better at fighting Kara.”  Kara rolled her eyes.

“I know that, but if I seem more … frail, they’ll underestimate me.  Probably.”  They stared each other down to see if either would cave in.  John gave them a minute before intervening with a sigh.

“Maybe you can have someone else be the deciding factor since neither of you can decide?  I might just ask Sawyer to be the one who gets kidnapped because she is our shortest detective and she also has a very … sweet disposition that makes others want to protect her.”  John never said anything was cute.  Not even his daughters.

“He has a point and she’s one of the good ones for sure,” Kara said, avoiding calling any cops dirty.  “I vote for her too then!”

Alex sighed.  She didn’t want either of them in any danger but she supposed this was the best they could ask for.  “Fine, but we shouldn’t tell her until it’s closer to the time we want this to go down.  Less likely to be leaked or overheard by any prying ears since I’m still not convinced that they didn’t bug some part of the precinct somehow.”

John nodded, “it is highly unlikely that they didn’t somehow bug us.  They could have moles, bugs, taps, wires, and even might have hacked our cameras.  It isn’t out of the realm of possibility.  Whoever these women are, they are highly intelligent.  I’m starting to suspect that their Leader is in fact a genius.  She must have genius intellect because she is far too organized and has enabled them to evade arrest for nearly six months.  While we didn’t actively try to capture them when they first appeared nearly a year ago, the last six months have seen no progress.  Personally, I don’t mind that they are out there, but this is coming from the commissioner.  He’s too high up for me to ignore.”

“I don’t even get why he’s so interested in them getting arrested.  He let a bunch of criminals go when they’re rich and the bad ones are let go when they do something that we’re supposed to arrest them for.  Badge or no, everyone should be held accountable.”  Kara was obviously angry about that.  Their precinct didn’t have many dirty cops, but other people under the commissioner had gotten away with murder, assault, and grand larceny.  She absolutely hated him.

“John, I’ll have you know I also eat burgers and ice cream!” she gasped.

“You’re being mean, Alex.”

“Crinkle, Kara, crinkle,” Alex taunted and Kara pouted harder at her sister.  It was like a grumpy puppy looking at her so Alex laughed at her.  “Just a grumpy little puppy in Kara form.”

Both James and Winn were on board with the idea when they all met up for game night.  Winn helped come up with their code words and meanings to keep their text messages from being easily intercepted and possibly interpreted.  Pot stickers would be the code for having an idea to discuss over dinner.  Game night was going to be a group meeting to brain storm ideas and for their weekly meet up to discuss how things were going with the plan so far.  Pizza was for starting an operation.  Watching new movies would be for new gadgets to be introduced since they wouldn’t do that very often.  Phone emergency would be for failed plans and to abort a mission if needed.  If someone said they forgot to grab their keys that meant that they were on their way for a mission.  The most important one was Code Red Mountain Dew for a big emergency on the field.  Everyone was anxious going into the first mission.  James was geared up in a suit that was a sleek black with some gray accents.

This first mission he planned on robbing a random police detective’s home to make it seem like he was targeting the police and also to control the damage.  Of course, Vasquez was told ahead of time that she should be prepared for something happening at her place without being given any details to make her reaction more believable.

He had gone in, sneaking in through a basement door to her home and setting off an alarm.  It took all of ten minutes for them to show up while he was making his way up to Vasquez’s bedroom on the second floor and he made sure to escape their clutches while fighting them off with some difficulty.  James barely managed to jump on his motorcycle before they caught up to him.  Vasquez had come out of her room during the commotion, gun in hand, as the women chased after James and barely got more than a glimpse of them.

Back at their base of operations, Winn’s place because he had all of the tech set up there, James was explaining how good they were because he wasn’t there for very long before they came in.  Winn made James’ suit with a hidden camera on one of his chest buckles, another on his helmet, and one on his back just in case.  The footage was good and clear, but there was nothing easily distinguishable other than what was already known.  L, S, and R as initials, the one known as Supreme was the tallest, Roulette just a bit shorter, and Leader just behind both women in height, Supreme was definitely strong from having body slammed James before he jumped down the whole staircase and running down the basement where he came from and barely making it on to his motorcycle as they came up to him.  They were more synchronized than a synchronized swim team was.

“Okay, so this is definitely going to be harder than we thought.  What should we do?”

Kara smiled encouragingly, “well, we have something to base our next attempt on now!  This time we weren’t as prepared, but now we know their response time is really good.  Just a little less than ten minutes and they were already on the scene.  And they must know each other really well because they worked really well together.  Hm.  They were also quick to chase you out, like trying to keep damage on the bare minimum.”

“You almost sound like you admire them, Kara.”  Alex was smirking at her sister.

“Well, you have to admit that they haven’t really done anything bad.  Yeah, they aren’t the police, but they are helping us arrest people and I feel like they help with current murder investigations or other crimes we have as our case.  Not just us, but other detectives.  You know that if it wasn’t because of the commissioner’s insistence I wouldn’t even care about arresting them.  Do you honestly want to arrest them, Alex?”  Kara asked, giving her sister a pointed look.

“In all honesty?  No.  They are faster than we are with a lot of crimes happening.  I’m sure they’ve even stopped other crimes that aren’t even reported because our incident reports have dropped about eight percent since they showed up and that was only eleven months ago going on one year soon.  There’s a lot more positives than negatives from what I can see.”  Alex shook her head.  “But we have our orders, Kara.”

“Yeah, yeah, we do.  If I could though, I’d just let them go free.  The power hasn’t gone to their heads and they’re still helping us even though we’ve tried to capture them since the commissioner put Hartmann and Vasquez on the case six months ago.”  Kara’s expression turned dark.  “If I didn’t think he was corrupt before, I definitely know he is now.”

“Well, we have a job to do so let’s just analyze this footage now.”  Alex nodded to the screens Winn had pulled up with all three camera angles.

Chapter 2

They’re Good

It had been three weeks since they started on the case and they hadn’t gotten very far either.  James was usually stopped within twenty minutes of any attempt at crime and it was getting a bit tedious to plan enough to get even that much time before they were stopped.  From the first mission until now, they had only been able to improve how long James had against them from just a few minutes to maybe twenty minutes.  It did not help that there was a significant advantage to three against one.  Kara had suggested having both Alex and her join in the fight and become The Legion of Doom instead of just The Lonely Destroyer.  Obviously, he wasn’t making much progress, if any.  There were too many of them and only one of him but Alex vehemently refused the idea because then it would make it harder for them to set up for Kara to get kidnapped.  Kara had slyly suggested that Maggie might get hurt and roughed up if she went instead because she was so much shorter and frailer, not that Kara believed any of that, but it had been enough to convince Alex that Kara was the better choice to get kidnapped.  Not only did Kara have half a foot on Maggie, she was also built stronger from working out.

Underneath her sweaters and cardigans, she was a freaking tank!  Alex was not behind the idea of including two more and dubbing them The Legion of Doom because then they would become more aggressive and might possibly hurt James in the process.  There was no need for major injuries or casualties if the women started becoming more dogged in their pursuit of James as The Destroyer.

So far, James had broken into Hartmann, Vasquez, and Edges apartments and homes, though he did enjoy giving Edge a black eye when he had realized James had intruded upon his home and tried to punch him only to get a black eye instead.  He also took pleasure in breaking down Edge’s front door instead of sneaking in from picking a basement door lock.  Edge was rude and racist too on top of being openly sexist.  He had shot at and killed a young black boy for playing around in his grandmother’s backyard in an affluent neighborhood when he wasn’t even on duty and had been visiting his wealthy cousin.  Unfortunately, he did not get more than a slap on the wrist when the commissioner had a say in it.  Suspended with pay for all of a week was a fucking vacation.

James was more than happy to get in some petty revenge in breaking his door, busting his window on his way out, and giving him a black eye for good measure, even if Kara expressed mild disapproval over the violence.  She snickered at the door and window though.  That she could get behind for petty revenge.  Whatever he made on his stupid vacation suspension he could spend on repairs instead.

Winn was spinning around in his chair, deep in thought.  He let out a big sigh and announced, “they’re good.  Like, way too good.  I got nothing, nothing for three attempts at getting something from our encounters with them.  Like, are we even doing anything at this point?  Everything’s a bit pointless.  We botch an attempt at information gathering and they get away easily.”

“That’s not all true.  We know that they’re really quick to respond to James breaking into cop homes even without radio chatter.  They must have some kind of system alert for certain kinds of noises like gunshot radars.  Whatever alert system they have in place tells them where it was heard and that’s something.  Maybe we’re trying too hard with a single angle and we need to do something different.  Like, I know we’re trying to keep damage to a minimum, Edge aside, but maybe we can up the ante?  Just a little bit?”

Alex rolled her eyes.  “And how are we going to do that?”

Kara narrowed her eyes at her sister’s skepticism.  “Gee, I’m glad you asked in your most supportive voice.  Anyways, I’m thinking that we can have James do something more dangerous.  While I know this is a bit much, but I don’t see how we can get their attention better than doing this.”  Kara took a deep breath, “what if we have him go to a known crack house?  He can try to steal money from one of the dealers and this way The World Killers will be distracted by both James and the crack dealers at the same time instead of going against him all by his lonesome!  This gives us an opportunity to actually see their dynamic while making sure James isn’t their sole focus this time!”

Everyone took in a sharp breath.  “This is a lot riskier, but it’s also a smart plan to draw them out and give them something to focus on instead of The Destroyer.  If it works out, we’ll be able to get more out of what they are capable of and try to suss out a weakness.  For now, they have been invincible.  Maybe this will give us something more to work with than ‘they’re really good!’” Alex said, imitating Kara, much to Kara’s displeasure if her scowl and crinkle appearing were any indication of her discontent.

“I do not sound like that and it is a good plan!  Also, if we get to take down crack dealers, all the better!”  Kara said all bubbly again like she wasn’t just scowling at her sister a moment ago.

“All right.  Let’s find an active crack house.”  Winn typed into his computer and got them ready for their next mission and set everything up for the end of the week because they changed up the days so The World Killers didn’t anticipate when James would strike as The Destroyer.  It was hard balancing their jobs and finding the time to set up every mission, but they made it work.
