Lena was comfortable in the mansion's living room, reading a good book on her couch with a warm blanket draped loosely on her lap when she heard the front door open.

-"Erin ?" she inquired and saw that it was indeed her maid making her way in.

-"You're back already ? Lena asked confused. "I thought you were going to the grocery store ?"

-"I was Miss Lena but then I found something, or rather, someone on the road who wants to see you" Erin explained. "And since it was raining a lot and the poor thing was drenched from head to toes I thought it best to end her misery and bring her here already."

-"Someone ?" Lena wondered out loud. Who could it be ?

-"Tall, blonde, American ?" Erin provided.

Kara ? Lena deduced immediately but chastised herself as soon as the thought entered her mind. That's not possible. They haven't talked since the award ceremony and nobody beside Jess and Veronica knew that she was staying here.

-Name's Kara ?" Erin added and smiled when she saw Lena jump out of the couch like she's been electrocuted.

-"She's here ?" Lena asked, staring at the front door as if she could see through it.

-"Yes, that's what I just said Miss Lena" Erin replied patiently, smiling. "The poor girl is freezing outside in my car Miss, should I let her in ?"

-"Yes, of course" Lena said, finally snapping out of her stupor but Erin made no move toward the front door. She just eyed Lena with an eyebrow raised questioningly.

-"What ?" Lena asked.

-"I just thought that maybe you would want to change before I let the Miss in" Erin informed Lena, eyeing her again.

Lena took a quick look at her fluffy socks, her over-used sweatpants and her birth mother's old wool jacket. She didn't have to look at a mirror to know that her hair were a mess since she didn't brush them this morning after she woke up.

-"I'm just..." Lena said to her maid, pointing at the staircase before darting to her room on the first floor of the mansion to change. Erin just laughed lightly.

A minute later, Lena was back in jeans and a sweater, her hair tied in a messy bun.

-"I'm ready" she told Erin, glancing at the front door and playing anxiously with the hem of her sleeves.

Erin opened the front door and motioned at Kara to join her. Kara exited the red pick-up, put her sports bag back on her shoulder and walked the short distance between the car and the front porch.

-"Sorry for the wait" Erin apologized. "Miss Lena will see you now."

-"Thanks" Kara answered, hugging herself to protect herself from the cold and to prevent her hands to fidgeting with her glasses.

Erin opened the door and kept it open to let Kara in. Lena couldn't believe her own eyes. Kara was here.

-"Hi" Kara said shyly to... the coat stand. With the temperature difference between the outside and the warm mansion, Kara's glasses had misted up for a few seconds and she had of course, picked the wrong shadow she could perceive, to greet.

-"I'm here" Lena clarified and couldn't stop the amused smile that graced her lips.

Erin wasn't so considerate of their guest feelings and snorted loudly, then pressed her hand to her mouth to suppress her laughter and failed.

But Kara didn't seem to mind. As soon as the fog had dissipated from her glasses, her focus lay solely on Lena.

-"Hi" Kara said again, looking directly at Lena this time.

-"Hi" Lena replied, still trying to wrap her mind around the fact that Kara was really here, standing in front of her.

Erin considered staying a little while to see how all of this would play out, the conversation promised to be quite interesting. However, if Erin didn't go to the grocery store soon, there wouldn't be much to eat for dinner and she had the feeling that there would be one more person at the table this evening.

The front door closing behind Erin brook the two CEO back to reality. They didn't know how long they've been standing there, staring at each other not knowing what to say.

-"Maybe you want to dry yourself" Lena finally spoke.

-"Yes, that would be great" Kara replied hastily.

While Kara dried herself and changed in the bathroom on the first floor, Lena fixed them two cups of tea that she set on the coffee table, in front of the couch, and waited for Kara.

A few minutes later, Kara was back in clean clothes. They were still too light for the current weather but the mansion was warm enough.

-"Thanks for the tea" Kara said and sat down on the velvet reading chair, next to the couch Lena was already seated on.

-"You didn't bring anything warmer than cardigans ?" Lena asked, noticing that Kara had gotten back in the same outfit, only replacing the wet clothes with similar items in style but of different color : a shirt, kakis and a cardigan.

-"No" Kara admitted sheepishly. "It isn't supposed to be spring here too anyway ?"

-"Yes, but here spring means chilly and rainy mostly."

Silence fell over the room again. Kara fidgeted with her glasses, trying to find the best way to explain herself to Lena and apologize. Kara spent the whole trip thinking about this moment and now that she was finally here, in front of Lena, she didn't know where to begin.

Lena brought her cup of tea to her lips and inhaled it. It was too hot to drink yet so she put the cup down again. Kara didn't seem like she was going to speak soon and Lena couldn't stand the quiet tension anymore.

-"I can't believe you travelled all the way here" Lena suddenly broke the silence.

-"I tried to call you but since you didn't respond and I couldn't wait to speak to you, I decided to come" Kara answered, relieved that Lena broke the ice first and was apparently willing to talk to her.

Lena remembered Kara's call. When her phone had rang that day and she had seen it was Kara calling, Lena had almost answered immediately. Then she had thought that maybe Kara was calling her to tell her that she was done with her professionally too, that she was the worst or something like that so she had decided to wait and listen to the voicemail instead but Kara never left one.

-"I was in the shower" Lena lied. "And since you didn't left any voicemail I thought it wasn't that important."

-"You don't have to explain yourself" Kara replied quickly. "I didn't answer you for days before the award ceremony and after... Let's say I deserved it and I'm not mad."

-"And now you're here" Lena stated. She picked her cup of tea again to keep her hands busy. "Why ?"

-"Because now I know Lillian had set me up" Kara told the truth. "She came to me that night and she made me believe that everything we had was a lie" Kara continued. She glanced at Lena. She didn't seem surprised or angry, just weary.

-"She told me that you pretended to be with me to win the press and the public favor so as to save your company after your brother's trial" Kara kept going. "That you offered to work on a joint project with me to benefit from my ideas and my company growing popularity to restore L Corp image."

-"And you believed her ?" Lena asked even if she already knew the answer.

-"I'm sorry but she played perfectly on my insecurities" Kara confessed. "She just had to instil the doubt in my mind and then I did all of the work by myself like the idiot I am."

-"The more I thought about Lillian's theory, the more things added up" Kara admitted. "How you answered to my job advertisement, how it was you that came up with the fake relationship idea in the first place and of course how someone like you would fall for someone who can't handle herself at a damn photoshoot ?" Kara finished angrily, leaning forward on her seat and hiding her face in her hands in embarrassment. She was still so mad at herself for falling right into Lillian's trap.

-"You should have come to me" Lena said but there wasn't one shred of reproach in her voice.

-"That was my first thought too" Kara agreed. "To what end though ?" she then asked rhetorically. "I would have come to you and you would have told me the truth, that all of what Lillian said was made up but doing that you would have accidentally strengthened your mother's story because that's what people that are caught do. They deny and they lie" Kara explained the reasoning she had at the time.

-"I was lost so I did the only logical thing that came to mind" Kara continued after a pause. "I asked for advice to my family and friends and they..."

-"All turned their backs on me because that's what you do to a Luthor" Lena finished for her. "Isn't it funny that my mother and I share the same last name but people always believe her over me ?" Lena added, laughing hollowly.

-"I'm sorry I believed Lillian and that not once it occurred to me that your mother could have invented all of it" Kara quickly apologized again. "Because what kind of mother would do that to their own child ? I knew your relationship with her was strained but that was basically all I knew. You never speak about her. I couldn't have known she would be capable of such cruelty towards you."

-"What made you change your mind then ?" Lena genuinely asked. Usually, when she was involved in anything and things didn't turn out as expected, people didn't bother to look for the truth, it was easier for them to blame everything on the Luthor. Of course Kara was different. Kara had always been different.

-"You" Kara admitted in a heartbeat, looking straight at Lena. "I was wrong before, speaking to you was the right thing to do. Well, speaking isn't the right word because I didn't exactly said anything back and it wasn't really what you said but more how you said it" Kara ranted but judging by the creasing brow on Lena's face, what Kara was saying wasn't making much sense.

-"That night at the gala" Kara clarified, "I knew you weren't lying. I don't know how, but I knew. And since my logical mind couldn't trust a simple hunch, intuition or whatever people call that, I had to find proof, but he moment I found it I came here" Kara explained, smiling uncomfortably, still ashamed at herself for taking so much time to discover the truth and apologize to Lena.

-"Once I found out, I had to came here as soon as possible because..." Kara exhaled loudly. "Lena, you're the best thing that's ever happened to me and if you'll have me, I want to give us another try."
In that moment, Lena could have asked Kara what was the proof that she had found exactly, how Kara retrieved it, but Lena realized she couldn't care less. For the first time in her life, someone was choosing her. Kara had believed her, had searched for the truth and crossed an entire ocean to tell Lena in person that she was sorry and she still wanted her.

It was a weird feeling for Lena, to finally hear everything she had always wanted someone to tell her, especially Kara, and still feel uneasy. Lena had the sensation that she was dragging Kara down with her somehow. Love shouldn't be that hard. Kara shouldn't have to work so much to justify her relationship with Lena to everybody else. So before giving her answer to Kara, Lena had to make sure Kara was aware of what exactly she was getting herself into.

-"Kara, if we give ourselves a second chance you have to know that this incident with my mother won't be the last" Lena warned Kara.

-"Yeah I know" Kara replied, smiling brightly. "Never believe anything Lillian says, trust you always, I learned my lesson well."

-"I'm serious Kara" Lena said, her face growing very serious. "My mother won't stop there. The media won't stop either. Remember what you said the first time we met ? That I wasn't responsible for my family's mistakes ?

-"I still think that."

-"But you're the only one" Lena added, sighting. "My mother will always make me pay for my father infidelity and the media will always make me pay for my father and my brother mistakes with the Law. All of them decided to take me down since I took over the family company and they will stop at nothing until they manage to destroy me." Lena took a deep breath and continued. "You have to understand that you can't pick me without my baggage and all the mess that comes with it. We're a package deal, sorry."

-"I can take it" Kara replied instantly.

-"Really ?" Lena inquired, raising an eyebrow at Kara.

-"Yes. All of it" Kara assured Lena, her eyes never leaving Lena's.

-"You, who panics every time someone ask a question too private for your liking in an interview ? You, who hates mediatic events and run away as soon as things get too hard ?" Lena reminded Kara and then quickly sipped at her tea to avoid looking at Kara anymore. Lena knew her words would hurt Kara but Lena needed to say them. She needed to know if Kara really understood what it meant to date a Luthor, to date her, and if Kara was ready to face it all.

Kara took each of Lena's words like punches in her stomach. She clenches her jaw as she feels her heart start to race and her breath become labored. This is exactly what Lena wants Kara reasoned. She wants Kara to get overwhelmed, to panic and run away, proving therefore that Lena is right about her.

So Kara remained seated, hiding her clenched fists in her pockets to not show Lena her internal struggle and said nothing. She started counting to five in her head to calm herself and fight the urge to get up and leave.

Not so long ago, Kara would have left. But this time was different. This time, she had a reason to stay and fight. Someone worth it and, as always since Kara met Lena, the latter didn't realize that she was that person. That she deserved to be fought for so she was trying to scare Kara away. That's okay Kara thought internally. She had enough faith for both of them.

-"If someone had told me two years ago that I would invent a revolutionary app that would be used by billions of people, I wouldn't have believed them" Kara said, voice unwavering. To Lena, she had never looked so calm and collected.

-"If I had been told that I would be in a fake relationship then a real one with the most beautiful and smart woman of this planet, I wouldn't have believed it" Kara went on, smiling absentmindedly and gazing into space like she was recalling some fond memory.

-"And yet, I did it" Kara stated, chuckling as if she still didn't believe it. "Because you see, learning, it's what I love most. I'm good at it. The thing is, there's no book or science to teach you life. The better way to learn about life, is to live it" Kara continued then paused to look at Lena again.

-"So yes, I hate mediatic outings, I abhor that I have to do small talk with old snobs because I need their money to produce my ideas and I loathe above it all that the press keep saying those nasty things about you" Kara admitted.

-"But you know what ?" Kara said as she went to seat next to Lena on the couch. "I've done all that and I survived. I learned. So now that I know what your mother is capable of, I will be more careful. I won't believe anything until you explain to me what's going on" Kara continued, taking Lena's hands in hers.

-"And I won't buy champagne to celebrate anything anymore because turns out, I don't like it" Kara added, earning a laugh from Lena.

-"You learned quite a lot indeed" Lena observed, smiling. She stroked Kara's knuckles gently with her thumb.

-"And I don't forget anyth..." Kara started to brag but was cut short by Lena's lips on her.

One Year Later.

It was a beautiful Sunday morning. Lena was enjoying her breakfast on the balcony of her penthouse, letting the sunrays and the fresh morning air graze her skin.

As she was scrolling the news on her phone, she reflected on the past year. So much had happened. Now LCorp numbers were more than good, Kara had moved in with her a few months ago and Lillian was in jail where she belongs, after Lena had finally summoned up the courage to leak anonymously her mother's tax files in the press.

This is how it feels when one has it all ? Lena thoughts smiling to herself. She was truly happy for the first time in a long time and it felt really good.

As she continued to scroll absentmindedly on her phone, Lena stumbled upon a picture of her and Kara from last night, when they were dining at one of their favorite restaurant. The article was titled "The proposal !" and in the picture, Kara was showing Lena a little black objet and Lena was beaming, her hands joined in front of her face in a overjoyed gesture.

The thing was, Lena recalled this scene perfectly and there wasn't any marriage proposal involved, she was sure of it. What Kara had showed her last night and which looked like a black jewellery box, was instead a finished new prototype. Lena had been very moved by the sight of it at the time because Kara and she had been struggling with its design and mechanism for weeks and Kara had finally found how to make it work.

It was this moment that Kara chose to wake up at last and join Lena on the balcony.

-"Mornin' " Kara said, bowl in hand and mouth already full of cereals.

-"Good morning darling" Lena replied, taking a sip of her coffee. "Have you seen this ?" she then asked Kara, showing her the article.

-"Oh, that's interesting" Kara observed.

-"We could be eating a sandwich on a bench and they would find it interesting enough to put it on page six" Lena complained.

-"What's your answer then ?" Kara inquired, stacking up toasts on her plate like she was famished.

-"To what ?" Lena asked confused.

-"The article" Kara clarified. "They don't say what your answer was in there" she continued, motioning to Lena's phone with a half eaten toast.

-"Are you... serious ?"

-"It's just, the last time we played along with the medias little fantasy it ended pretty well I think, so... why not try my luck again ? Kara said, smiling broadly at Lena.

Lena, totally dumbstruck, glanced at Kara still in her pyjamas, then at herself with her robe. It wasn't the most romantic scenario ever but looking back at how their relationship started, Lena agreed with Kara's leap of faith. It's wasn't perfect or utterly romantic but it was them. So much like them.

-"Yes !" Lena exclaimed as she threw herself at Kara.
