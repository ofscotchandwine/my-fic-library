I’m head over heels in love with you, Lena, and it would have been really awkward if we hadn’t cleared this up right now, because I was planning to ask you to move in with me at the end of May.”


The thought of moving in with Kara didn’t quite set off Lena’s phobia of doing anything her mother would enthusiastically support.


“We would see each other every day,” Lena considered, too practical to pour all of her hope into a hypothetical just yet.


She wondered how that possibility would go in hand with her life goals, and whether she would continue her work as a scientist, or if her famous alpha would just parade her around to parties. At one point in time, Lena scoffed at omegas who strived to become trophy wives, but she could see a future for herself on Kara’s arm and she didn’t hate it.


“I thought maybe you could fly with me to Amsterdam for a tech conference in June. I’ll be presenting the products I’ve been working on for two years and it would mean a lot to me.” Kara smiled and her eyes shined with a secretive gleam of happiness that Lena attributed to all of the many gadgets she was planning to reveal.


“I would love that, but please don’t utter a word to my mother,” Lena begged, oblivious to the fact that Lillian herself was standing in the doorway and overheard their plans.


“A little late for that,” Lillian intoned strictly, her lips taut with a smile and her arms folded over her chest as she leaned into the doorway. “I came to call you both down for dinner.”


Lena eyed her mother, who crossed the room to take Kara’s hand and personally escorted her to the stairs.


“Kara, I think it’s wonderful you’ve invited Lena to accompany you,” Lillian praised, leaving her daughter to trail behind them and dropping her voice to a conspiratorial whisper. “I also couldn’t help but catch your discussion about moving in together. I’ll start sending you boxes of her things.”


Lena forced her chin out as far as it would go and pictured her mother meticulously packing up all of her possessions to marry her off.


“Calm down, mother,” Lena hissed and rescued Kara before their conversation turned to more serious topics, or Lillian rushed her girlfriend into proposing.


They were dating, and Lena was more than content with that for now. She looked forward to a long spring of mating and getting to know Kara on a deeper level with the security of love and commitment between them.
