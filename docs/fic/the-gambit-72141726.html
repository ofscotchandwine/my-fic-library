    the gambit

     

     

    "I have a surprise for you," Lex said.

    Lena doubted that the surprise would be bigger than him being alive after all the tribulations she went through to kill him. She doubted that the surprise would be bigger than waking up in a world where, not only the Luthors reigned supreme but were ruling with an iron fist, and Lex seemingly delighted in openly torturing aliens.

    She doubted the surprise would be bigger than waking up in an universe where apparently she was okay with that.

    Still she knew Lex, she knew that to thwart whatever plan he had, she first had to study him. See how he moved the pieces on the board.

    For now, his first mistake was actually thinking she did not have her memories. His second was thinking she was exactly like him.

    "Who knew you could be so generous," Lena replied, turning around in her chair to look directly at Lex.

    "Come on, Ace," Lex smiled, and it reminded Lena so much of their younger selves, that she almost believed that version of her brother. But she knew too much now. She had learned her lesson. "I'm not so bad a brother…"

    Lena didn't answer, instead she lifted her eyebrow in what she hoped was something that could pass as a bantering gesture.

    Lex seemingly bought it as he laughed, "come on, you are going to love it." He opened he office's door, waiting for her.

    Lena following, dreading whatever it was that had him so giddy.

     

     

    Lena was absolutely not ready for what was waiting for her.

    Supergirl chained with kryptonite, slumped forward in the middle of the room, swollen, bleeding and bruising, only halfway up because of the chains.

    "She tried to get into one of the vaults, like a fool." Lex informed her. "As if I didn't have safeguards against her type in my property."

    Lena did her best not to react with disgust at his tone, or with worry at Kara's state.

    Her ragged breathing, the green veins coursing over her face and hands.

    "Lena…" She said, as if merely saying her name was giving her hope.

    Maybe it was Lena's wishful thinking. She knew she'd only been a stepping stone to Supergirl. Someone to keep close so she could keep her controlled.

    Lena hummed, as detached as she could, "and why pray tell, dear brother, do you see this as a surprise for me?"

    "Oh, come on, ace!" Lex said, almost giddy. Lena could see the trap from miles away. He was just waiting for a reaction. "You've been dying to test that little virus you found. You think I don't know you have been modifying it to work on Kryptonians."

    "I think you are confusing me with mommy dearest," Lena replied, quickly connecting the commentary to the medusa virus. "But I'm assuming you want me to test it, for some unholy reason."

    "Well… you are the one with the formula to the isotope that makes it work," Lex continued. "Mother said you refuse to work with her, so I thought I'd give you first dibs on your test subject."

    "Well, mother is always comparing me to you," Lena said. "I'm too old to be listening silently to that very annoying comparison." She deflected, or at least tried.

    Lex's smile told her she hadn't entirely succeeded.

    He was waiting for her, to do something to prove her alliance to him. So, she deeply sighed, like when she did as a teenager, tired of his bullshit.

    "I'm not working at this stupid warehouse," she declared. "Bring her to my lab. We can dissect her first, and then test the virus."

    "Speaking like a true scientist," Lex laughed.

    And the kid at the back of her heart, that hung at every word her big brother said, once upon a time, almost preened.

    "Lena, you are… not him," Kara said. Pleadingly, always hopeful. "You are kind, and brilliant.."

    "Of course I'm brilliant," Lena interrupted her. Not wanting her to lose her breath begging when she was on her side. She just needed time to come up with a plan. "So brilliant in fact, that I'll cut you open a figure out exactly how you work. How you do all you do, and figure out a way to make it better. To make it human."

    Lex clapped, clearly having fun. Feeling vindicated, somehow. Victorious.

    Because he thought he broke her. Molded her to his will, in his image.

    The thought came unbidden, then. She was going to enjoy killing him again.

     

     

    Lex didn't waste much time. By the time Lena arrived at her lab early the next morning, he was already there, Supergirl still swollen and shallow breathing, handcuffed with kryptonite but positively murdering Lex with her one good, open eye.

    "Morning Ace," her brother greeted her as soon as she got in his field of view. "Ready to work?"

    "Do you practice your lines in front of the mirror everyday, Lex?" Lena asked, trying to act apathetic about his presence, and not extremely worried about Supergirl's well being.

    "Just excited for the day ahead, sis," Lex cryptically smiled, offering a paper cup of coffee from Noonan's.

    Lena took the coffee, examining the lid with her eyes, "I'm not giving the formula of the isotope to you, either…"

    Lex seemed disappointed for a few seconds but then simply shrugged, walking backwards. "As long as she's dead by the end of it, and we get enough of the virus to permanently eliminate all of them, I simply don't care if you share your formulas with me."

    Lena heard it loud and clear, he would still them from her at the first opportunity. It wouldn't be the first time, either. Lena had the advantage of truly knowing Lex now, something he wasn't counting on.

    "Nice work with the kryptonite cuff, though," she compliments, as a hook. She was sure it was natural kryptonite, and not her own synthetic. Which meant, his stockpile of the mineral was limited. But the kryptonite in cuff was substantial enough to avoid the Super building resistance and escaping them.

    Lex smirked, "they were pricey, but all to crush the Maiden of Might."

    The way he said it, Lena had to suppress the sneer, that almost manifested in her face.

    "Lena…" Supergirl managed to enunciate, wispy and low. Raspy.

    She tried not to react to the clear plea too obviously. Instead she cocked her head, looking at the blonde.

    "I guess it's better to beg in those pants than with the skirt," she goaded.

    Kara wasn't in a state to even react, but Lex, who was the real target of her word, laughed.

    "I should leave you to work," he said. "Do you think we could have results this afternoon?"

    "I'm good… I don't know if you should expect miracles, Lex…"

    "Tomorrow morning, then?" he laughed, as he left her lab.

    Lena waited, for him to pop back in. For someone else to invade her space. For Kara to not die right in front of her.

    "Hope," she called for her own AI, in the lowest voice she could, as she walked towards her high table. "Check for hidden cameras or mics."

    "No external signals detected," Hope's detached voice replied after a few seconds.

    Lena still was suspicious.

    "Disconnect every RF frequency signal," she ordered.

    "Disconnecting all RF frequency signals will result on my own disconnection, until you manually power me up again, Miss Luthor," Hope advised.

    "Do it," Lena replied, picking up a writing pad.

    She waited for a minute. Maybe a few more seconds than she should have. She looked at the camera she knew for a fact was at the corner of her lab.

    "We don't have much time," she told Supergirl. "How long has he have you cuffed?" She asked.

    "I— I don't." Kara rasped.

    Lena retrieved the small sun grenade. She didn't know how it had survived even a new universe, but she was glad about its existence, when just the week before she was just about to throw it out the window.

    She also picked a small laser cutter gun, she knew in any moment Lex could barge in again.

    "It's okay. We just need you to get you out of here," she continued. Quickly putting on protective eye-wear, to cut off the handcuffs from the Super's wrists. "I'm sure he will do something that will cause you the most pain. But for now you need to survive."

    "What…about you?" Kara questioned with difficulty. Every word ragged, the green veins more and more prominent.

    "I'll figure something out," she dismissed, more preoccupied with taking the cuffs away from the Kryptonian, so she could go.

    "You don't understand," Kara raggedly replied. "He will hurt you."

    Lena snorted, one side was done, she quickly moved to the other. "Of course he will, I'm letting you go, what's worse, I'm betraying him."

    "He will do something to cause me the most pain, Lena." The Kryptonian unevenly stated.

    Lena almost stopped cutting the cuffs, but she knew she was pressed for time. She didn't want to unpack, whatever the Super meant by that, so she just kept cutting the kryptonite cuffs.

    The blonde kept loudly breathing, almost as loud as the cutters, Lena wasn't sure how much longer Kara would last without lasting consequences.

    When the cuffs finally dropped from the kryptonian's wrists, Lena quickly dropped them inside a lead lined container. "How are you feeling?"

    Kara took a deep breath, and then another, and another.

    "Much better," she finally replied. "Lena, please. Come with me. He will… he will hurt you."

    Lena kept the protective eye-wear, as she detonated the sun grenade. She had to close her eyes anyway.

    "Lena," the blonde mumbled, her voice stronger, and yet still so gentle. Coaxing almost. "I know you think you can handle him, I'm sure you can, actually," she slowly, almost expecting Lena to recoil, touched her cheek, caressing her with her thumb. "I'm just not sure of what I would do to him… to the world if he hurts you."

    "That's big talk for someone who betrayed me," Lena let out.

    "You remember…"

    Lena refused to answer that.

    Supergirl instead, nodded. "I know you won't believe me. But that doesn't mean I want you to be tortured, or to die at the hands of your brother." The blonde stated. "Just because you helped me, or because he knows…"

    Lena took a step back, "knows what?"

    Kara didn't answer.

    What's worse. Lena didn't need her to.

    She knew.

    "Come with me," Kara asked again, extending her hand. Offering. "We can think of something, but let me get you out of here."

    Lena almost took it.

    "What would that fix?"

    "Nothing. Everything. I don't really care…" Kara stood up, walked closer. "I just care that you are safe."

    "Supergirl, I will be fine."

    Kara dropped her hand, "you hate me so much that you'd risk your life, just to not come to safety with me?"

    "I don't care about you enough to hate you," Lena lied through her teeth.

    Kara kept quiet for another second, "I can hear your heart, I know you are lying."

    Lena turned around, walked towards her computer. "You need to leave."

    The words were not even fully out of her mouth, when everything went red.

    And then, everything went black.

     

     

    When she regained consciousness again, the first signal was the voices she heard. They were hushed, foreign. Worried.

    Lena opened her eyes, the place was bright, and white… and blue, somehow.

    She recognized it immediately.

    The Fortress of Solitude.

    She wasn't cold. Which was surprising. But the very human, cozy blankets over her, explained that.

    "Your guest is awake, Kara Zor-El," a robotic voice announced.

    Kara was immediately next to her, "how are you feeling?" She asked, worried.

    "Vitals are within human range," the robot replied.

    "Kelex, go take a walk," Kara snapped.

    Lena saw as the, very much floating robot, went away.

    "What happened?"

    "A bomb or something. I'm sorry I didn't hear it," the blonde apologized. "I guess he figured out you were helping me."

    Lena tried to sit, Kara stopped her.

    "Please rest."

    Lena obeyed. "What now, then?" She asked.

    "You rest… And then we figure out a way to expose your brother for who he truly is." Kara replied.

    "And then?"

    "And then we fix our relationship?"

    Lena almost scoffed. But then she thought about how miserable she was without Kara around. She was sure they had much to talk, much to clear up. But maybe… maybe they could. She decided it, then and there.

    "And then we fix our relationship."
