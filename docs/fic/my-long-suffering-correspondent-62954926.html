    Chapter 1

    Rio Vidal Tromsø, Norway Mar. 19 1892

    Dearest Agatha, The northern borealis proves to be a better scam artist than even the likes of you. I toss and turn in the ice all night only for a glimmer of putrid green, only for it to appear and vanish in seconds, like a ship sailing through thick fog.

    It is all so very disappointing I was forced to end the life of my dear tour guide, who lies, as I write to you, at the feet of several starving wolves. Fortunately the wolves are excellent company. They remind me of you.

    Greedy, with great, sharp teeth. A devilish smile I’d follow into the dark. I hope prison is treating you well.

    Do let me know when you’d like me to break you out.

    Fond Wishes, Rio Vidal

    Agatha Harkness Bucharest, Romania April. 04 1892

    My Long Suffering Friend, How dreadful. If I was the type of vampire to possess the gift of flight, I’d soar up to the Heavens and punish the borealis for the sin of their disappointment.

    But unfortunately I am confined to my body and even more confined to this foul-smelling cell. Yet I would be lying to say I am unhappy here. Just today I have started three separate fights amongst the guards.

    I Suggested to one that the other was plowing the third’s wife, and oh, how delicious the mayhem. There was a stabbing with a fork, then a retaliatory attempt with a spoon. A spoon!

    Have you ever seen one man attempt to end the life of another with an instrument meant for soup?

    Oh Rio, it is delightful. How I wish you were here to witness it.

    Still, I beg that you please do not break me out, not yet, I have so many experiments still to conduct on these little sheeplings I refer to as prison guards. Tomorrow I am going to see if I can make them kiss. I’m sure it will end disastrously.

    The political climate of Bucharest does not look well upon homosexuals.

    Well, you know that – for that’s how I ended up here in the first place.

    If only I had ravaged you, or at least pretended to, so we could play puppet master with these men together.

    Forever Your Favorite, Agatha Rio Vidal Tromsø, Norway May. 15 1892 My Dear Compulsive Liar, We all know you’d never deign to ravage me. My claws are much too pointy.

    It is summer now, and the wolves have left. The reindeer have replaced them. They are too soft and sweet. Not like you at all.

    I bore of the cold. Maybe it is time I return south. Your cell sounds cozy, and your antics cozier. Save me a body or two to play with before the witch hunts begin, won’t you?

    See you, Rio Vidal Agatha Harkness Bucharest, Romania May. 20 1892 I do adore that you always sign your full name, as if I might confuse the many Rios in which I correspond.

    Rio Vidal Tromsø, Norway May. 30 1892 It abhors me that you’d waste paper on a single sentence when I wait all week to hear from you.

    Agatha Harkness Bucharest, Romania June. 1 1892 It abhors me that you haven’t come to steal me away yet.

    Rio Vidal Bucharest, Romania June. 18 1892 My train arrived this morning. I saw a newsletter in the station about three prison guards kissing naked in the field.

    How I’ve missed you.
