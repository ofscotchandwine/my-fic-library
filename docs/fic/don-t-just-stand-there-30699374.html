Don’t Just Stand There


Going to a dinosaur theme park for a little date was quite possibly one of the most unexpected things Lena Luthor could have done for her girlfriend. In fact, a few months after Lena met Kara, when she found out the reporter held some fondness over dinosaurs, she made a decision in investing and becoming one of the biggest shareholders of Jurassic World without even flinching.

Now, nearly a year into their relationship, Lena has finally surprised Kara with a plan that involved the Kryptonian accompanying her to her business trip at the aforementioned park.

Due to the creation of the newest attraction, all shareholders and investors were invited to be the first batch of people to catch sight of the Indominus Rex.

With how bright Karaʼs face was when Lena presented her the VIP passes, the Luthor thought she would’ve gone blind for a moment. She was immediately scooped into the arms of the Super, who was quick to twirl them both around in the air.

“Lena, you’re the best!”

The ecstatic compliment was one of many things she had heard, but such line coming from Kara felt warmer and more meaningful. With a light chortle, Lena gave her lover a kiss on the cheek before murmuring, “I know I am, darling.”

For the past couple of days before their trip to Isla Nublar, Kara was in the same beaming mood as she was when Lena said yes to being her girlfriend. Over and over again, she told Jʼonn and Alex that she and Lena had a special trip to Jurassic World and that she was pleading them both to not call her in unless it was absolutely necessary.

Of course when the momentous day finally came, it felt as if the universe was cackling cruelly at the couple. Especially at Kara, who was unfortunately called in by none other than the DEO when they received various reports concerning alien technology being smuggled throughout the city.

Apparently, someone out there thought it was the best time to illegally distribute extraterrestrial equipment and due to the list they acquired of what was already shipped, Jʼonn was quite confident that whoever their enemy is, they wanted to make a weapon of mass destruction.

A weapon that would surely be a great threat to National City, and quite possibly, the entire world.

And so, Kara had to begrudgingly cancel with Lena, who offered a sympathetic smile and a promise to still take her to the theme park once they had a vacancy in both their schedules again.

This, of course, leaves Lena with her trusty assistant, Jess, to make their way through the park to attend the whole presentation they came for in the first place.

Much to their disappointment though, the gathering was postponed for tomorrow after they were told that there might be a few technical difficulties. Jess glanced at Lena and pursed her lips in amusement when she saw the CEO curl her lips in a displeased frown.

Yes, Lena may be more than a tad bit interested in the research and work that goes on in the labs here, but she was only looking forward in this particular trip because she was supposed to be accompanied by a certain bright, blue-eyed reporter. Any other problems or reschedules were just a waste of time for her at this point.

“Is there anything else I need to do before I can hide away in my hotel room?” Lena asked as she and Jess entered the Innovation Center.

The people around them were a bit loud as they all chattered in awe towards the displays and holographic figures. Lena couldn’t stop herself from taking in a deep breath when she got her right foot stepped on by some running child for what seemed to be the third time ever since she and her secretary entered the pyramid-like building.

“Well, you could try a couple of the amusement rides, Miss Luthor.” Jess suggested, eyes flickering towards the Brontosaurus that was abruptly projected at the center of the premises.

“No.” Was Lenaʼs immediate response, the disgruntled curl returning. “I’m not exactly fond of… thrill-seeking activities.”

“Miss Luthor, may I ask?”

“What is it, Jess?”

“If you’re not really interested in amusement rides in the first place, then… why invest here? Is it the dinosaurs? The research about them, I mean.”

“Partially, yes.” Lena then stopped and turned to her secretary. “But mainly, it was all for the sake of impressing Kara.”

Jess smiled at the mention of the reporter. “Miss Luthor, I may not be an expert on relationships, but I’m quite certain you’ve already swept Miss Danvers off her feet when you bought CatCo for her.”

Lena gave a light but somewhat anxious laugh, her face uncharacteristically reddening. “That was… an entirely professional and profitable move, Miss Huang.”

“Of course. Sorry for assuming, Miss Luthor.” Jess returned, clearly not apologetic at all. Lena didn’t mind it in the slightest. It was always nice to be talked to without someone worrying that she’ll bite their head off just because they're afraid of offending a Luthor.

They were just about to head towards the main doors of the Innovation Center when both of their attention seemed to have been caught by the operations manager of the theme park and her two nephews.

The woman clearly struggled with conversing with the two young boys, the eldest between them looking entirely unsurprised that their aunt couldn’t make the time for them and was more focused on whatever it was that was pinging her through her phone.

“Let me just call one of my assistants to help you out.” The woman, Claire Dearing, said as she started scrolling through her contacts in her phone.

The older nephew, Zach, shook his head and said, “No, it’s fine. I’ll handle Gray. He and I are…-”

“Not old enough to around the park alone.” Claire finished differently for him. “It’s a bit crowded, you could get separated and a lot of things can happen. I don’t want your mother and father to chew me out if anything happens to you two.”

From the side, Jess seemed to be just waiting for Lena to move on ahead, while the CEO herself was eyeing the two kids. The younger oneʼs enthusiasm reminded her so much of Kara whenever she told her she had potstickers delivered. Compared to the agitated brother though, Lena could easily envision them both as her and Lex, especially if she were to look at their dynamic when they were younger.

Regardless, Lena still wasn’t entirely sure what possessed her to walk up to the group, wave at Claire when the woman noticed her approach and say, “How ‘bout my secretary and I accompany them?”

Claire looked like a deer caught in headlights. “M-Miss Luthor!”

Lena flashed an assuring smile, while Jess looked amused beside her once again. “Jess and I merely went here for the presentation of the new attraction, but since that was obviously postponed, we have all the free time we have.”

“I couldn’t possibly…-” Before Claire could decline, Gray was quick to latch onto the sleeve of Lenaʼs blazer, nodding eagerly at his aunt.

“She says she can take us around! Can we go now? Please? Please? Please?”

The conflict was clear on Claire’s face, but when her phone rang for what felt like the fourth or fifth time ever since she spoke to her nephews, she felt like she didn’t have much of a choice but to trust the multi-billion dollar CEO volunteering to watch over the children.

“Alright, okay, you can go with Miss Luthor. But please, behave.” Claire said, shooting the two boys a bit of a pleading look before she refocused her gaze back to Lena. “Thank you, Miss Luthor, for all of this.”

“No problem.”

Once Claire turned and said goodbye, promising to see her nephews later on, Lena immediately felt Gray release her sleeve and drag his brother towards one of the exits. Her eyes widened at the quick pace, immediately moving her own legs to chase after the duo.

“What have I just gotten myself into, Jess?” Lena asked a bit breathlessly as she jogged after the pair with her high heels clicking noisily on the floor.

“I think the correct term would be ‘babysitting’, Miss Luthor.”

“Oh God.”

Meanwhile, back at National City…

It took all of Supergirlʼs strength not to chuck the unconscious alien in and through the DEO truck, grumbling under her breath about how she and Lena would probably be petting and feeding a Brontosaurus now or maybe even riding some Triceratops if it was a thing at Jurassic World and if she was actually there in the first place.

“Supergirl, focus.” Came Alexʼs voice through the earpiece.

“I know, I know.” Kara huffed, shooting back up to the sky once the alien was securely restrained. “Where do you want me next?”

“There’s a bit of trouble going on two blocks away from L-Corp. Reports of laser beams being shot around at that area came in just a minute ago, so try to hurry.”

“On my way.”

Kara certainly wished she could say the same to Lena.

Speaking of Lena, maybe she could give her a call after intervening with the ruckus she’s about to break off. Her lover did give her permission to do that anytime, so she’s pretty confident Lena wouldn’t mind answering a simple phone call.

At Jurassic World…

Lena had her arms crossed as she watched Zach lazily meander around the crowd, while Gray climbed one surface to another just to take pictures of practically everything that captured even a sliver of his interest.

Currently, Jess was a few spaces away, buying the children some snacks after being asked by Lena herself. It was a bit of a habit of hers. Whenever Kara said anything remotely close to the message of her being hungry, Lena was always quick to order food, so it wasn’t unexpected she’d ask Jess to do the same for Gray when the boy said he wanted a hotdog.

Buzz! Buzz!

Lena uncrossed one arm and withdrew her phone from inside her blazer, smiling before swiping her finger across the screen to answer the call from none other than Supergirl herself.

“Kara.” She greeted, lips immediately shaping into a smile. Her gaze distractedly wandered to the baby Triceratops in the pen barely in her view as she listened to Karaʼs voice through the tiny speaker of her phone.

“Lena! Hey!”

“Are you flying right now? I can hear a lot of wind.”

“I’m at the top of one of the cell towers in the city.” Kara replied, the sheepish smile she wore echoing through the phone. “How’s Jurassic World?”

“Boring without you here.” Lena admitted, glancing over to where the two kids were still idling at before letting her eyes follow one of the running Triceratops.

“I can hear all the excitement around you though.” Kara said before sighing. “I wish I was there.”

Lena bit her lip, her smile not at all faltering. “Well, can’t you spare at least fifteen to twenty minutes? Fly over here maybe?”

“I wish. Right now, Alex literally just gave me a five minute break, and I only have three more minutes left.”

A click of her tongue. “That’s disappointing.”

“Maybe tonight I’ll be able to fly there. Meet you at the hotel maybe? Er, well, that is, if I’m not tied down here by another alien who thought today was the perfect day to wreak havoc in the city.” Kara practically grumbled into the phone, and the thought of her pout on her lips was just too much of a distraction for Lena, who failed to notice that the two kids she was supposed to be watching over had taken advantage of her preoccupied state.

Zach and Gray ran off, ditching the CEO, who looked towards their direction a minute and a half too late.

Any further banter Lena wanted to exchange with Kara died down like a flame being doused by ice cold water. Her eyes widened and darted around in search of the two boys, her heart quickening in panic when she failed to catch sight of the duo.

“Um, Kara, I think I need to call you back.” Came her mildly frantic say, cutting off the little story Kara was telling her. 

Worry immediately cloaked over the Kryptonian. “Lena, is everything alright?”

“Everything’s fine, it’s all fine.”

“Then, why do you sound so nervous?”

“Listen, Kara, I need to go. I’ll call again later, bye!”

Lena barely heard Karaʼs own goodbye before she spun around to face Jess, who was holding two hotdogs in her clutches. The secretary glanced around before returning her focus on her bossʼ visibly panicked expression.

“The boys ditched you, didn’t they?” Jess asked in conclusion, easily piecing out the unsaid puzzle.

“They did!” Lena groaned out, running a hand over her face. “I cannot believe I even volunteered…”

“It’s going to be alright! I asked for the contact details of the main security personnel of the park because of obvious reasons when we arrived, so I can just give them a call and ask them for their help in tracking the boys down.” Jess said, handing the food she had in hand to a couple who was just about to pass them and head to one of the food stands.

The strangers didn’t even have the chance to question the sudden edible gift from Jess when the woman placed a hand behind Lena and started guiding her out of the current area they’re in. Once Jess was sure Lena would continue walking alongside her, she withdrew her phone and started making the necessary calls that would hopefully calm the CEOʼs growing anxiety.

As if losing the boys wasn’t bad enough for Lenaʼs list of concerns, it seems that she couldn't even afford at least a small taste of a break.

One second she and Jess were hastily moving through tourists, the next they were weaving through agitated people wondering why they were being cleared away from the attractions. Lena knew that once all of this was over, her feet were going to suffer blisters from running around so callously in heels.

“The boys were spotted at the West Gate!” Jess informed her through the loud chatter of the crowd situated around them. “I told Miss Dearing we’re headed there now. They're on their way.”

Lena nodded. “Alright, quickly then.”

Yet, when they did reach the boys and finally got them back, Lena had half a mind to give them a lecture about behaving. Before she could do so, the PA system of the park started with a little feedback, followed by the announcement of, “Ladies and Gentlemen, due to a containment anomaly all guests must take shelter immediately.”

The message repeated over and over again like a broken record, and the turn of events shifted into something incredibly drastic. People started screaming, running, practically stampeding throughout the park as they hurriedly sought for any form of shelter.

Lena ushered Jess to hold onto the boysʼ shoulders to keep them from running, but all it did was prompt them to somewhat leave her behind. Unlike Jess, who had the luxury of wearing flat shoes, Lenaʼs heels were unforgiving and caused her to fall back a couple of steps away from her secretary and the kids.

Their small group had just ran out of the ‘Gentle Giants Petting Zoo’ area and the boys didn’t seem to have any intention in reigning down their panic. The two continued skittishly running like the people around them, which prompted Jess to grab onto their arms in an attempt not to lose them from the crowd.

“Stop running!” Lena called out, wanting to groan and slap a hand on her forehead when her words didn’t seem to reach Zach and Gray. It would've been best to separate from the crowd. She's more than sure that someone with her status and the boysʼ connection to their aunt would be more than enough for them to find an actual shelter from the commotion.

Once they stepped into the main area of the park, Lenaʼs eyes widened upon the sight of Pterodactyls flying around and picking people up. Some carelessly threw them aside like ragdolls, some took nibbles from their bodies upon tackling them down the ground while some just hoisted up one unlucky victim and flew them off to God knows where.

The terrifying sight succeeded in stilling the kids, causing Jess to try and drag them to one of the abandoned stalls at the side, which would be able to provide them at least temporary coverage from the creatures flying all over the sky.

Seeing the chaos around them, Lena knew she needed to call Kara. Now. 

Using the signal watch around her wrist, Lena flipped it open and pressed the button underneath, the sigil of House El glowing bright red before she latched it close again.

“Come on! Get over here!” Came Jessʼ nearly pleading voice.

When Lena spun around, she found Zach and Gray staring up at the Pterodactyls with absolute fear written on their faces, their shock rooting them in place and giving Jess the dilemma of not being able to tug them towards the closest space they deemed safe.

“Don’t just stand there!” Lena shouted at them, her frustration showing not only from her voice and face but with the motion of her arms as well.

And suddenly, Lena wasn’t on the ground anymore.

“Miss Luthor!”

Whatʼs even more horrifying here was that it wasn’t Supergirl who took her up in the air.

It was a Pterodactyl.

Its hooked claws were clasped over her shoulders and if it weren’t for the expensively thick fabric of her blazer, Lena was sure it would have already pierced through skin like a searing blade.

Lena screamed.

She screamed, and screamed, and screamed, especially when another Pterodactyl tried stealing her from the one who captured her in its grasp. The flying reptilian thief caught her by her ankles, its claws painfully scratching against her skin and digging in.

Lena wasn’t sure what happened. With her own screams, the rushing wind, her unsteady line of vision and the flapping of wings deafening her, she wasn’t certain if the Pterodactylʼs grasp on her had slipped, or if it just decided to let her fall to a different version of her doom.

Once she was dropped and she found herself free-falling so quickly below that all she could do was call out, “KARA!” before she plunged into the water.

Despite the panic that's threatening to engulf her like the frigid water she’s in, Lena still managed to give an attempt to swimming up to break through the surface with a desperate need for oxygen.

Unfortunately, someone out there didn’t seem to have any desire in giving her even a glimpse of a break.

Three Pterodactyls dove into the water with her, all of them rushing towards her and only one of them was fast enough to zoom towards her and take her by the waist with its long beak.

It dashed upward, bursting out of the water and furiously flapping to get some traction and return them both back to the sky. Lena felt herself slip away from its mouth, causing her to fall back into the water, her screams and cries for Kara becoming garbled once more.

For another time, the Pterodactyl plucked her from the water and Lena struggled relentlessly and desperately called out, “KARA!”

Splashing back into the water, hope didn’t even flicker in the slightest bit within her. Lena felt herself be hauled out of the water once more, this time by the Pterodactylʼs claws yet again. It dug its hooked nails against her sides and unlike earlier, Lena wasn’t even sure if it pierced her skin or not.

All Lena knew was that she survived countless attempts on her life, only to meet her end by the literal claws of a Pterodactyl.

Whatever being that existed above was cruel though. Things in her current predicament could apparently still get much, much worse than it already was. 

At this point, Lena was barely conscious enough to be aware of what was happening. But, when she managed to open her eyes, she found herself still clutched by the desperately flapping Pterodactyl… and in the huge, death-dealing jaws of a Mosasaurus that burst through from underneath them.

Lena clawed at the slippery surface of the dinosaurʼs mouth, but when she looked up, she knew this was definitely going to be her end. Digested alive by a large aquatic reptile that's supposed to be extinct.

Clenching her eyes close, all Lena could think of was Karaʼs face and how she should have probably took more leaves from work just to spend time with Kara.

All of a sudden, Lena heard an audible grunt, the lack of jaw snapping and last and definitely not the least was the disgusting splatter of limbs.

When Lena opened her eyes, the innards of the Pterodactyl rained down on her, its body, which was split into two, falling past her and sliding down the Mosasaurusʼ throat, its guts staining most of the top of her head and back.

“Lena!”

Snapping her head upward, she found the one and only Supergirl holding the Mosasaurusʼ jaw open with her bare hands. The aquatic reptile was falling back into the water, giving them the shortest window of escape. Using its momentum, Lena exerted the last of her fill of energy and dug her nails into the mouth of the dinosaur in order to reel herself up.

The second she managed to grab Supergirlʼs cape and used it to pull herself up until she had an arm wound around the heroʼs shoulders, everything became a blur for a brief second.

Next thing Lena knew, she was in Karaʼs arms, whose own eyes were wide as she watched the Mosasaurus retreat back into the water. Her attention immediately snapped down to Lena, who shuddered and clung to Kara like a kitten that was rescued from a raging river.

In this case, she was a Luthor rescued from a raging Pterodactyl and a hungry Mosasaurus.

“Oh Rao, Lena…” Supergirlʼs eyes landed on either side of her torso, the white blouse she wore underneath the black blazer staining red with the blood that oozed out of the wounds the Pterodactylʼs claws inflicted on her. “Lena, that was…-”

Lena exhaustedly shook her head, not even noticing how Supergirl flew them up much higher than they were to prevent the other Pterodactyls from noticing them or encouraging the Mosasaurus to give another attempt in chomping at her and Lena.

“The people, Kara… Jess… She’s still there… With… with the kids…” Lena could barely speak, voice hoarse from all the screaming while she exhaustedly gestured with her hand.

Only when Gray called out for the superhero did Kara realize she still had a lot of things to do and people to help rather than just hover in the air with a bleeding Luthor in her arms.

“Right! Yeah, okay, umm…” Kara took a quick scan of the theme park, her expression clearly showing she didn’t find any suitable places nearby where she could have Lena acquire medical attention from. “I’m going to take you back somewhere safe first.”

Eventually, Kara settled in bringing Lena to a storage warehouse at the edge of the park. There weren’t any chaos there and only park personnel and a couple of people dressed in tactical gear were present in the vicinity. There, she asked one of the people to patch Lena up and once Kara got the response she needed, she immediately flew away and back to the chaos happening at the heart of it all.

Lena felt absolutely drained and the moment she was crowded by medical personnel and laid onto a gurney, the darkness of oblivion swallowed her whole.

 

 

Bright lights, a thin but soft blanket and a comfortable mattress and pillow underneath were the first few things Lena noticed when she ultimately acquired a grasp on her consciousness once again.

Lena stared up at the plain ceiling of the room she’s in, her eyes blinking at it in a daze while she absently listened to the heart monitor quietly but steadily beeping beside her.

It was silent save for the mentioned medical equipment, and it gave Lena the time to recall everything that had happened to her. Not only was her throat feeling dry and sore, but her lungs were also experiencing this dull burn within her due to the drowning and flailing she had went through. Arms, legs, her entire body aching painfully, Lena felt like she had just fallen from a building and unfortunately survived.

With a bit of a struggle, Lena reached over to one side of her torso, her fingers tracing over the outline of the bandages underneath the light blue hospital gown she was clad in. Just moving the smallest bit, it hurt her.

Dying didn’t seem such a bad idea all of a sudden.

“Oh thank God, you’re awake.”

Lena instantly turned to whoever it was that spoke, only to find herself regretting the motion once she felt a jolt of pain surge through her neck. She groaned and closed her eyes, forcing herself to relax back on the bed.

“Hey, no sudden movements.” It was Alex. The older Danvers walked up to her and started checking her vitals before physically checking if she was suffering any other illnesses as a side effect from the traumatizing events she had just went through.

“Water…” Lena hoarsely requested, prompting Alex to grab the bottle of water apparently prepared for Lena at the bedside table. She unscrewed it open before helping Lena take a couple of sips, stopping when the CEO gave a nod and a murmured thanks.

“I saw everything that happened through a the surveillance cameras at the park.” Alex said and just by the expression on her face, it looked as if it pained her just to remember the footage she and the other agents watched through the theme park’s security cameras.

Lena kept her voice above whisper, still feeling the soreness of her throat with every word she spoke out, which came out huskier than usual. Alex probably would have teased that Kara would be a little happy at the sound of it, but the words that left Lena quickly diminished her intention in mentioning it.

“What a way for a Luthor to end, wasn’t it?”

Alex frowned disapprovingly. “Don’t joke about that, Lena. Kara was worried about you. Heck, I was worried. Everyone was.”

Lena pursed her lips, knowing this was probably not the best time to joke about her near-death experience. Instead, she chose to shift the topic to, “Where’s Kara?”

“Still helping with the containment at Jurassic World.” Alex said, offering Lena sympathetic look. She knew the Luthor was more than half hoping the Kryptonian would’ve been there beside there once she woke up. “And trust me, it’s taking Kara a whole lot of effort not to laser every single Pterodactyl she sees out there. Or just fly through the Mosasaurus and string it up after as if it’s your typical catch in the sea.”

Lena softly sighed, a quiet chortle leaving her lips when she was told about how Kara seemed to want to throw a tantrum at the reptilian creatures that nearly killed her girlfriend.

“You know, if what you went through wasn’t so serious, I would say that it was a bit ironic.”

Lena raised an eyebrow, curious at the thought running through Alexʼs mind. “Ironic how?”

“Ironic because through surveillance, I think I heard you tell those kids ‘Don’t just stand there’, only for you to, well... do what you said not to do before you got caught.” Alex then eyed Lena with a knowing look in her eyes.

“Those boys were obviously scared.” Lena said, sighing again. “And I was honestly on the verge of panicking, so maybe I did just do what I literally told them not to do. You can’t prove anything, Director Danvers.”

“The footage says otherwise.”

Lena playfully swatted Alexʼs arm, causing the latter to snicker at her little snapback.

A couple more minutes passed with Lena asking Alex about how everything went, which also led the other to assure her that Jess and the two boys they were watching over was completely alright. The thought of seeing your boss be grabbed by a flying reptile and being left in the midst of the chaos was likely traumatizing as well, so Lena was absolutely set on giving her secretary a raise once she gets back to L-Corp.

That is, if Jess doesn’t turn in her resignation when they see one another again.

Lena was left to sigh to herself at the thought of it. It would be such a hassle to find a secretary as good, capable and loyal as Jess.

The thought of stealing Kara from CatCo and turning her into her personal secretary was quite tempting, especially since she does have the qualifications. She was Cat Grantʼs longest secretary, after all. But, then again, she knew she didn’t have the heart to convince the hero to take up the role. 

Though it was certainly nice to think about having Kara at her beck and call in another literal sense.

‘Keep those thoughts down, Luthor.’ She scolded herself, but nonetheless smiled at the concept.

The following hours passed by rather quickly, especially since Lena fell asleep again after eating the meal Alex personally brought in for her. Ever since the salad she had the same morning she went to Isla Nublar, she hadn't eaten anything yet, so it was no surprise she finished everything on the tray.

The next time she woke up, she found Supergirl herself situated on a chair closely situated at her bedside, hands loosely clasped together on her lap while her head was pretty much lolled back. It looks like she fell asleep in the middle of watching over Lena, and the position she was in was definitely not comfortable.

“Kara?”

The reaction wasn’t entirely immediate, but there was a response nonetheless. Kara stirred awake from her sleep, looking out of it for a couple of seconds before she realized who called.

Practically shooting off her seat, Kara immediately enveloped Lena into a hug. The CEO smiled and nuzzled her nose into Karaʼs hair as she returned the embrace as much as she could without straining her still aching arms and shoulders.

“Rao, Lena, I was so worried.” Kara murmured, giving her one light squeeze before releasing her and settling right beside her on the mattress. “Once I heard the signal, I immediately threw this guy I was handling through a window before flying to you.”

Lenaʼs eyes widened in amusement. “You threw someone out of the window?”

Karaʼs worried look morphed into a sheepish expression as she evaded Lenaʼs gaze and started playing with her fingers. “I told him I needed to go somewhere more important and he kept threatening to shoot the hostages he had. I had to think fast.”

“And throwing him out the window was the key?”

“He’s fine, Lena! Just a tiny, little concussion, but nothing serious! Alex even checked herself!”

Lena feigned a scoff and a disappointed shake of her head. “And they say Iʼm the evil one.”

Kara huffed. “I just wanted to save you as quickly as I could.”

“You did.” Lena dropped the look she wore and replaced it with a delicate smile that she knew causes Karaʼs heart to skip a beat. She sat up, with the assistance of the Super, of course, before inching forward and giving the latter a sweet kiss on the cheek. “My knight in blue and red spandex.”

Karaʼs dopey smile didn’t stay long. She made a face and said, “It’s not spandex! It’s a type of Kryptonian fabric similar to eurojersey.”

Lena flashed a small teasing grin. “Could’ve fooled me.”

“Don’t do that. Don’t say that.”

“What’s wrong with what I said?”

“The way you said it and when you said that the first time…” Karaʼs pout appeared and when she fixed her wandering gaze back to Lena, she found the Luthor smiling in return with a wistful look in her breathtaking eyes.

“You, Kara Danvers, are absolutely adorable.”

“And you, Miss Luthor, are gorgeously insufferable.”

“You like it.”

“I love it.”

The moment was perfect. The moment was set. Lena was closing in again, and Kara was moving towards her lover in order to meet her halfway. While Kara could clearly hear how Lenaʼs heartbeat started to race thanks to her super hearing and the heart monitor beeping at the side, Lena could already feel the natural warmth from her blue-eyed saviour.

“Oh, and Lena?”

Lena, whose eyes had slid close a second ago, opened her eyes again, raising an eyebrow. “Yes, Kara?”

“I think I hate dinosaurs now.”

“Kara, I think we’d both very much appreciate it if we’re kissing now rather than discussing the creatures that tried eating me for lunch.”

“Oh, right, yeah, yeah, sorry.”

“No apologies needed, darling.”

And with that, Lena finally captured Karaʼs lips and tasted what felt like the embodiment of the sun itself…

Only for Kara to pull away again a few moments too soon.

“Not to put a damper on the mood, but I think those Pterodactyls would make a really good target practice for my heat vision.”

“Kara...” Lena groaned, flopping back on her pillow and wincing when she felt a dull ache course through her back for a second.

“I’m just saying!”

“You know what, talk to me when you’re ready to just kiss me and forget about the fact that I was nearly eaten by a couple of reptiles.”

“They’re not just reptiles, Lena! The thing that carried you around and practically drowned you was a, okay, wait, you already know what they are and now I just realized you’re probably just pissed that you and I aren’t making-out yet.”

Lena offered a sweet smile. “Oh, was I not too obvious?”

“We can’t make-out now! You’re still recovering.”

“Make-out or get out.” Lena said, finality ringing crystal clear in her voice. “Your choice, Supergirl.”

“That’s… That’s unfair.” Kara replied with yet another pout. “Cuddles, maybe?”

“No.” After some slight difficulties, Lena managed to pull the blanket over her shoulder and turn herself away from the Kryptonian, who started lightly poking at her arm.

“Lena. Lena, come on. Leeeenaaaa.”

Lena merely huffed quietly, pulling the blanket tighter against her. Doing this was her punishment for Kara for ruining the mood between them, but once she felt the bed dip low behind her and the firm wall of warmth that went by the name Kara Danvers pressed behind her, she knew her resolve was going to cave in any moment now.

Like a little puppy, Kara snuggled behind her, carefully wrapping an arm around Lenaʼs waist and pulling her much closer than she already was. She could feel the Kryptonian press her face between her shoulder blades, nuzzling against her for a few times before she murmured against the material of her hospital gown.

“Love you, Lena.”

The moment those words were softly and warmly uttered by the love of her life, Lena had no chance in resisting any more. She reached down and intertwined one of her hands with Karaʼs, scooting closer and vanquishing any remaining space between her and the hero.

“I love you, too, Kara.”

From the claws of a Pterodactyl and the jaws of a Mosasaurus to the arms of the most tender-hearted Kryptonian the world knew as Supergirl, who would’ve thought a Luthor would find herself in these predicaments? 

Certainly not Lena.
