Author's Note:

I haven’t watched any of season 6 so I have no idea what’s happening in the show. Consider this a love letter to everyone harmed by the showrunners and writers.

The entire final season could have just been all of the ladies on the show standing around looking incredible and it would have been better than whatever they came up with I'm sure.

Also, this is a sort of re-write of my other fic where I meant for it to be fluffy and it turned into a therapy session somehow. None of that here, just Kara falling in love with her best friend as a cat. Inspired by two works of art on tumblr by papurrcat and comickergirl. Go support them.

“Ok, I have pizza, potstickers, and when did you get a cat?”

Alex lowers her raised arms filled with food and movies and stands in the open doorway of Kara’s studio. Kara watches her from where she sits curled into the corner of the sofa with a black fur ball resting on her chest.

“Yeessss, I’m so hungry,” she says and extends her arms and makes grabby hands. Alex drops the food on the kitchen island and throws a wagging finger at Kara.

“Uh, uh, don’t avoid the question. Where’d the cat come from, and if you say the dumpster I swear-”

“The Tower,” Kara interrupts and adjusts her glasses. “I found it in the lab under one of the shelves. Don’t worry, it’s clean.” At Alex’s accusatory glare, she clarifies, “I...checked.”

“It’s Lena, isn’t it?”

“What? No.” Kara bursts into a giddy laughter, her face and shoulders scrunched to contain herself. The cat jumps from Kara and meanders to where Alex stands in the kitchen. It weaves through Alex’s legs, arching its back to scratch against the rough fabric of her jeans. Alex follows the shadowy creature’s movements as it leaps onto the island and plops onto its haunches in front of her.

“I take it this,” she waves a hand up and down in front of the cat, “was not the intended result.”

“Not exactly.”

Alex jumps at the words and pulls a hand to her chest. “Oh, geez, you talk,” she says, “what happened?”

“I thought it was a protection spell,” Lena says and pulls a paw up to the back of her ear and wraps it around to her jaw a few times. She eyes the pink pads of her paw before placing her foot back on the table.

“Quick reflexes, multiple lives, makes sense,” Alex says and goes to the fridge. “Who wants a drink? What’ll you have, Lena, milk?” Lena tips her head sideways and Alex thinks she almost does a good job of pulling off her sassy look, except the adorableness of her cat face counteracts whatever shade Lena is throwing at her.

Kara steps to the table, scoops Lena up, cradles her into the nook of her elbow and scratches her stomach.

“You know,” she says with her face dipping down towards Lena, “milk isn’t really good for cats’ bellies. But scratches are, aren’t they? Who loves getting her belly scratched?”

“Oh, my god. Nope.” Alex sets her drink in the sink and walks towards the door. “Raincheck on sister night.”

“What? Alex, come on, don’t leave.” Kara’s plea loses whatever effectiveness it might have held when she draws Lena’s paw to her lips and kisses each toe pad.

“I’m going home to my wife and my kid.”

“She’s just jealous because she’s not as cute as you,” Kara says without registering Alex’s departure. “Let’s see what movies Alex brought us.”

***

Kara wakes with a nose full of hair. The sleeping cat draped over her neck lets out a raspy breath on each exhale, and Kara’s lips pull into a lopsided smile. She manages to get one eye open before the pitter patter of little feet storm across her apartment and burst into her bedroom.

“Aunt Kara!” Esme says loud enough to wake her neighbors. “It’s true, it’s true! Aunt Lena’s a cat. Mama said I could play with her, can I?”

Alex calls from behind the room divider, “please tell me you two are decent.”

“It’s safe,” Kara says with laughter in her voice. Lena rumbles with purrs against her skin.

“Esme, sweetie, what did we talk about you going into people’s bedrooms without knocking first?”

Lena drops a paw on Kara’s lips and swats her with her tail.

“But there’s no door.”

“An oversight on our part,” Kelly says and nudges her shoulder into Alex. “You’re right, honey, but we still need to respect people’s privacy and ask before we go into their space.”

“You didn’t ask before we came in the front door.”

“Wow,” Alex says, “we are really failing at this, aren’t we?”

Kelly chuckles. “I think we just need to re-evaluate,” she says with nothing but love in her eyes. She turns to Kara and Lena. “We thought we’d make breakfast since we dropped by unannounced. Are pancakes ok?”

“Pancakes and your company are always ok,” Kara says, a grin splitting her face.

“Let’s let Aunt Kara and Aunt Lena get ready,” Alex says and reaches her hand out for Esme. “How would you like to help me make pancakes while we wait?”

When they have the room to themselves again, Lena tucks herself back under Kara’s chin.

“She’s adorable, but I’d rather stay here.” Her voice is muffled against Kara’s neck. Kara scratches Lena’s head and rubs one of her ears between her fingers.

“Come on, Aunt Lena, I’ll make it up to you later.”

At that, Lena adjusts herself so she can see Kara’s eyes.

“We’ll go to the store and pick you up some catnip. Maybe one of those little mouse toys. Hey!” Lena’s already trotting to the bathroom where the litter box sits when Kara digs her finger into her ear to wipe where Lena licked her. The bathroom door slams shut behind Lena’s swishing hips and Kara freeze-breaths her hands to apply cool fingers to her overheated cheeks.

***

“No. No, absolutely not. I’m not getting in there.”

“Lena, come on!”

Kara flails her arms around and behind herself, chasing after Lena who is clawing and scrambling to climb through the neckhole of Kara’s shirt.

“You’re covered in syrup, you need a bath.”
“No.”

Kara gives up the fight when Lena crawls down the sleeve of her shirt. She sits on the floor of the bathroom in front of the tub filled with two inches of water and smiles at her lumpy arm.

“Ok, why don’t you want to get in the tub?”

A silence falls over them.

“The soap,” Lena starts, “is bad for my natural oils that keep me soft.” It sounds every bit like the excuse it is.

Kara worries her lips and debates if she should voice her concerns. When Lena doesn’t say anything more, it jumps out of her mouth before she can stop it.

“Is this because you don’t want me to see you bathe? I can call Alex or Kelly or, or Nia! I’m sure Nia wouldn’t mind. I just don’t think you’re going to be able to get all of the syrup out by yourself, it’s kind of a lot. Esme did a good job of getting you sticky.”

Before she has a chance to regret the words or feel bad about her niece covering Lena in maple syrup, a black paw pokes out of the end of her sleeve and settles into Kara’s palm. She hears a little sigh and bites back the tears forming in her eyes.

“I don’t like water. I mean, cats don’t, I think.”

“But you’re a human in a cat body.”

“I chased your laser vision around the living room for 20 minutes, Kara.” And, oh, Kara smiles at the thought and can’t wait to rewatch the videos Alex recorded. The paw retracts into Kara’s sleeve. “Cat instinct is apparently included in this spell.”

“Oh,” Kara says and nods her head, “that makes sense. But I’ll be right here and I won’t let anything happen. Once you’re all clean again, we can cuddle on the couch and watch whatever movie you want.”

At that, Lena pops her head out of the bottom of Kara’s sleeve, the fabric stretched well beyond recovery. “No complaining.”

Kara draws an x over her heart with her other hand and Lena attempts to roll her eyes.

“And no pictures,” she says. Kara tries not to look too bummed. She decides seeing a soaking wet and unamused cat Lena in her bath is worth the promise.

***

“Why didn’t we ever do this before?” Kara speaks the words like a prayer, soft against Lena’s fur while she runs her fingers along Lena’s body. Curled against Kara’s chest, Lena’s response vibrates against her.

“I’ve never been a cat before.”

Kara lets out a reverent hum.

“As much as I’m enjoying you as a cat,” she says and wraps her hand around one of Lena’s paws and brings it to her lips for a kiss, “I think I'd prefer you in human form.”

Kara once again wakes with a nose full of hair and she thinks this might be the best way to wake up every day, with her senses full of Lena. She keeps her eyes closed to focus on the scent of Lena’s freshly shampooed hair, the steady drum of Lena’s purr, the weight of Lena’s body on her chest. It’s then Kara realizes the weight of Lena’s body also sits on her stomach and her hip and one of her legs. She doesn’t risk opening her eyes and, instead, brings a hand up to Lena’s head and drags her fingers through the strands to the ends of Lena’s very long, very human hair. Kara scrunches her eyes and her breath quickens along with her racing heart. At the featherlight press of a thumb over the crinkle in her brow, she inhales a sharp breath and tears leak from the corners of her eyes.

“I think I prefer this in human form as well.” Lena’s fingertips trace around Kara’s brow to cup her cheek in her palm. As if by instinct, Kara nuzzles into the skin and turns enough to place a kiss at the base of Lena’s thumb.

“I’m glad you still like doing that.”

Kara nods her head in Lena’s hand, too afraid to open her eyes in case this is a dream that will end the moment she opens them. When the bed dips and Lena’s no longer on top of her, Kara swallows the lump in her throat and gathers her courage to look.

“I wonder what else you’d still like to do now that I’m human again.”

It doesn’t take super senses for Kara to know which direction Lena’s voice is coming from. The sound of water hammering against the floor of the bathtub is enough to snap Kara’s eyes open.

***

“So,” Alex says and leans against the counter next to Lena, “how long have you known how to turn yourself back into a human?”

Lena’s lip curls and Alex gives her a pat on the shoulder.

“Good for you.”
