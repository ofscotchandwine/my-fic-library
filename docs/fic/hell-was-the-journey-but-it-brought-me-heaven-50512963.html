Lena’s head was resting on Kara’s shoulder, with her eyes closed. Kara could tell she was still awake, just by the sound of her heart, but she looked and felt… serene.

    “Did it bother you that I went to see Kiley without you?” Kara asked in a whisper, her thumb caressing Lena’s skin at her hip, where her hand rested.

    “No.” Lena succinctly replied. “I am okay with not watching his ugly mug ever again.”

    “So you don’t think it was dumb?”

    “It was dumb.” Lena shrugged. “But you are right, he won’t remember anything about your identity and he wanted to kill me anyways so, it has no relevance. If you wanted and needed to tell him something, that’s okay.”

    “I could have killed him. I was angry enough for it.” Kara whispered, almost in confession. “Maybe if I had visited any other day but today, I might have done it.”

    Lena sat down properly, to look at her eyes, “No, you wouldn’t have. Because that’s not who you are. If you were to kill anyone it would not be something calculated and premeditated. You are not an executioner.”

    “For some reasons I might, Lena.” She argued. “If he harmed you, in any way, I would.” Kara looked in Lena’s eyes, just so she could see how truthful her words were.

    Lena didn’t say anything for a few seconds. Long enough for Alex and J’onn to return with Brainy and Nia, boxes of Chinese food in tow. At the end, Lena sighed, putting a hand on Kara's cheek.

    “I know you are telling the truth,” She voiced. “But I wouldn’t want you to do anything you might regret later on.” Lena leaned in, kissing her lips. Short, chaste, just a blip in the grand scheme of things, but Kara felt it in her very soul, like forgiveness, for some reason. “Hopefully you won’t need to do anything that drastic ever.”

    “Hey, lovebirds!” Nia called, “If you don’t come eat, Esme might eat all the potstickers.”

    Kara whipped her head towards them, “I love you kiddo, but don’t you dare.”

    Lena’s melodious laugh made Kara’s heart melt a little, but it wasn’t distracting enough for Kara to not pick a big box with potstickers just for her… And maybe Lena.

     

    After dinner, they all watched a movie, though after the halfway point, the silent, nervous tension between the superfriends was palpable. When the movie was finished, Kara gave away the rest of the satchels to the family, Esme last.

    “I need you to hold onto this very, very hard for the next few days.” Kara told her niece, crouching down to be at the same eye level as her.

    The child took the tiny bag and held it under her jaw, “It smells funny.”

    Kara smiled, “Yes, it does. But it’s important to me that you keep it close to you. I would hate for my favorite niece to forget who I am.”

    Esme giggled, probably unaware of how dire the situation was to the rest of them. “Silly Aunt Kara. I am your only niece.”

    “Still my favorite, though.” Kara said, before kissing the child's forehead. She shared a look with her sister, before turning around. “Whenever you guys are ready, just let me know what you need.”

    Lena, J’onn and Brainy marched to the elevator back to the control room. Kara followed them in a sedated step, a few minutes later after hugging her sister, who seemed more nervous than the rest of them. When she entered the control room, J’onn was donning the robe left by his father, his face stoic, clearly trying to remain calm. Brainy put on his crown, to amplify Lena’s spell and J’onn’s connection to the world. And Lena was burning some incense, again. She had cleared the space, close to a mortar in the middle of the room.

    Lena closed the space between them, a kind smile in her face and her heart beating fast in her chest.

    “I don’t really need you here for the spell,” she confessed quietly. “But I’m always better when you are around. You are my strength, Kara.”

    She held Lena’s face, kissing her lips for a brief second. “I will do whatever you need me to do. If you want me to stand around and look handsome for you, I will.” Kara smiled. 

    “Dork,” Lena mumbled with a smile. She kissed Kara’s cheek, longer than usual, and then took a step back, returning to the mortar, with the ingredients laid around it.

    Kara walked towards Brainy and J’onn. “Thank you for doing this.”

    Brainy nodded, and J’onn put one hand on her shoulder.

    “You and your sister are like my daughters, Kara.” He said. “Anything I could do to help you, I would.”

    Kara hugged him, and before walking towards the door, she clasped Brainy’s shoulder in support.

    Kara looked on in silence. 

    Lena nodded towards J’onn and Brainy, confirming they were ready. The latter, touched the tip of his fingers to each other.

    Lena picked up the ingredients one by one, the fresh laurel leave with Kara’s name written in it, the verbena, the star anise, the vanilla herb and seeds she picked with some tweezers, and added everything to the mortar, “Diis veteribus ac novis, auferendum vestigium tuae identitatis ac pro omnium salute audi; Aperto corpore, apertis mentis.” She said, loud and clear, with her hands over the mortar. 

    It started to sputter a light and some lavender smoke. Lena nodded towards J’onn, who put his fingers on his temple, his eyes glowing red. 

    Kara was tense, her shoulders rigid, sorely focused on Lena, whose hair was flowing almost wildly, like the wind was blowing in The Tower, although it wasn’t. It was simply the magic flowing through her. 

    Alex showed up, using the stairs, which was weird enough, but the crazy look on her sister’s face told her something was wrong.

    Kara got close to her, “What is it? What’s going on? Did you forget?” She asked, grabbing her forearms.

    Her sister shook her head, “Not me. No, no one. Listen,” she started looking towards the center of the room where the others were. “I just have a bad feeling. When you were out, she gave me this, to give you after she did the spell. She told me not to read it. I haven’t but… something felt wrong, Kara.”

    Alex extended an envelope towards her, Lena’s loopy calligraphy on the side of it, only spelling Kara’s name.

    As soon as she saw it, Kara got the bad feeling too. She opened the envelope and read the letter as quickly as she could. She couldn’t believe her eyes.

    When she turned towards her girlfriend, the smoke and the light shoot back towards the mortar. J’onn had stopped touching his temple, and Brainy had taken his crown off his head.

    Lena smiled sadly at her, registering the letter in her hands, and then, her eyes rolled back and she fainted. Kara caught her before she could really get to the floor. 

    Kara’s eyes were flooded with tears. “Don’t do this to me, Lena Luthor.” She said, forgetting the presence of anyone else in the room. “Don’t you fucking dare do this now that we are finally together, after the secret identity, villains, the end of the freaking world, the secrets and the family feud. Not now that we are happy, Lena.” She put her forehead on Lena’s, even with the awkward angle. “Don’t you fucking dare forget about us, zhao.” She kissed her, begging, pleading. She felt a force, just exploding around them, not that she cared about it. “Please, zhao.”

    “You keep saying that word…” Lena's groggy voice made Kara open her eyes. “Am I supposed to know what it means? There is not a Kryptonese-English dictionary I could really check out.”

    Kara’s laugh just bubbled out of her, relieved, tears still streaming down her face. “Don’t scare me like that, my love.”

    Lena moved her hand towards Kara’s cheek, keeping her forehead pressed to hers, “Ah… I’m sorry for scaring you. But your safety is more important.”

    “No, no it’s not, Lena, Rao. Don’t you dare.”

    “Okay, lovebirds,” Alex’s quivery voice said somewhere at Kara’s back. “Not to break your bubble or anything but, what the hell was that?”

    “Nevermind that,” Lena raised her voice. “Did it work?”

     

     

    As Alex described, it was as if an air bomb had exploded from Kara and Lena. Not that Kara noticed it, her senses focused only on her girlfriend at the moment. Of course, Lena and Brainy had their theories.

    As for Lena’s question… It was harder to tell for the time being.

    “I did the same as when I erased Alex's memory of it,” J’onn explained. “No one has the same connection to your identity as your sister, so it should have worked.”

    “I can, however, confirm that there is no mention of Kara Danvers and Supergirl being the same person on the web.” Brainy assured them almost an hour later.

    “Well, at least that part of the spell worked.” Lena mumbled, rubbing her forehead.

    “I think it’s better if we all go home.” Kara was quick to propose, worriedly looking at her girlfriend. 

    “I am fine,” Lena replied, looking Kara in the eyes, “Stop worrying.”

    “Never.”

     

     

    Kara waited for the man to sit in front of her. The plexiglass keeping them separate was smudged full of old handprints. When he saw her he frowned, but picked up the phone and put it over his ear, regardless. 

    “You seem familiar.” He rumbled, curious.

    “Kara Danvers, Catco Magazine,” was the only explanation that she gave. 

    John Kiley tilted his head towards the phone, confused. “To what do I owe the visit?”

    Kara looked over her notes, taking her time before answering. “I have it on good account that you hired Chien Na Wei, aka China White, to assassinate Lena Luthor, care to comment?”

    “I don’t have the faintest idea about what you are talking about.” He condescendingly replied. 

    “Figured as much,” Kara commented writing on her notepad. “What about Kane Wolfman? Don’t know him either?”

    “The name doesn’t ring a bell, Ms. Danvers.” He looked at her directly. “What is this really about?”

    “I have the receipts of your payments to both China White and Kane Wolfman, both are in the custody of the feds, and they are willing to sing for some leniency in their sentences.” Kara whispered towards the phone, looking at the man. “I don’t presume you know me, Mr. Kiley, you are too self-centered to really care about others. I am sure that your long list of crimes from which the FBI arrested you in the first place are only the tip of the iceberg, but I just want you to be aware, I am a Pulitzer winning reporter, I can see a story from miles away, and I’m relentless in the pursuit of the truth… and now you have my attention.” 

    “What’s that supposed to mean?” He asked, and Kara took some satisfaction in the uptick of his heartbeat.

    “I am sure the FBI will also be very interested in the offshore bank accounts you have under the name Wayne Roberts, and how your company was responsible for the water poisoning in Amnesty Bay five years ago.”

    “What’s your play here, Ms. Danvers?” he asked, her name hissed between his teeth.

    “Lena Luthor is off-limits.” Kara declared, her eyes fixed on his through the glass. “Try to harm her again, and I'll make sure they throw you in the deepest hole known to man and then forget your existence.”

    “Who do you think you are?” He hissed, a fierce grip on the telephone speaker. 

    “If you go against Ms. Luthor again, I'm your worst fucking nightmare,” Kara smiled. “Which reminds me, sending someone to stalk her, which you paid Mr. Winston Smith to do, also a crime. Mr. Smith has been picked out by NCPD, on private property, and he said he was told to go into the LL Foundation by you. Your lawyer may visit about that one soon.” She shrugged. “Did I make myself clear?”

    “Apparently she has more bodyguards than any other billionaire in the area.”

    “Seems like it. Are we clear?”

    He didn’t say anything, but given the look he was giving Kara, she presumed that the message had been received.

    “Glad we understand each other, Mr. Kiley. Have a good day. I would keep away from Paul Devlin, though. He lost someone in Amnesty Bay, made him a little murderous.” Kara hung up the phone and stood up with a smile. She picked up her stuff, and left without saying another word.

     

     

    It had been three days since the spell. No one outside the superfriends, her family and other friends knew Kara’s identity. It had been an absolute success, as per Lena and J’onn’s consensus. Somehow every public mention of her identity had disappeared as if it had never happened. Lena had been sleeping a lot since they confirmed that it worked. Alex hypothesized that the magic she used drained her and left her tired. Still, Kara was taking her superhero duties slow. No flying around unnecessarily, no stopping petty thievery, no breaking and entering to clear her girlfriend’s name. So she had been going old school to find the dirt on Kiley, and it was enough. Sadly, that also meant no flying to Paris for food, either.

    Regardless, she stopped at Noonan’s to pick up coffee, bear-claws, and eclairs. And bought some flowers before going home. Well, Lena’s penthouse. Her own loft was still kinda not habitable, basically the only tangible evidence of what had happened, but she had started with some repairs and hoped that it would be spotless in a week's time.

    Lena was still in bed when she got to her apartment, but Kara could tell by the telltale beating of her heart that she was about to wake up in the next few minutes, she made an effort of getting out of the button up and slacks and put on more homey clothes, before sitting down next to Lena, leaving the coffee and pastries on the nightstand. She let the midmorning sun filtering through Lena’s heavy duty blinds, recharge her for a quiet moment.

    She didn’t realize when Lena finally woke up, only figuring it out by feeling her arms snake over her chest, and Lena’s jaw resting over her shoulder. “You went out.” Lena declared, not in reproach but not as a question either.

    “I got you coffee, and some eclairs,” Kara smiled, caressing her girlfriend's arms. “Sadly, plumerias are really hard to find without flying to the Caribbean or ordering them weeks in advance, so I got you some gardenias instead.” She finished, pointing to the bouquet of red, white and yellow flowers. 

    “I don’t need plumerias, gardenias work just fine,” Lena whispered, kissing Kara’s jaw. “Thank you. Did you know gardenias got their name from a Scottish physician, botanist and zoologist? His name was Alexander Garden.” Lena mumbled.

    “Glad to know you are back to being your usual nerdy self.” Kara smiled. 

    Lena laughed, low in Kara’s ear, “What’s got you so contemplative today?”

    “It kinda happens when your girlfriend risks losing every memory of you together to keep you safe, I guess.”

    “I’m sorry,” Lena said, still hugging Kara, still with her jaw on her shoulder. “It was very possible that the spell would bounce back to me, but it was a risk I saw necessary to take.”

    Lena hummed, “I think it did happen, actually.”

    “Are you kidding?”

    “You know the expansive wave Alex and Brainy described?” Lena waited for Kara to assent, before continuing talking. “I think that was the spell breaking.”

    “With a kiss? Are you serious?” Kara asked, turning around to look at her.

    Lena shrugged, grabbing her coffee and taking a sip. “Stranger things have happened between us, if we are being honest.”

They had been walking the line between friendship and something more for years, and now, Kara didn’t want it to go back to that. She wanted to wake up everyday with Lena at her side, and she wanted to share her accomplishments with her, and see Lena triumph, and change the world for good, and for her to share her accomplishments with Kara. She wanted an entire life with Lena and she didn’t want to keep waiting on the sidelines for it. She wanted to stand on the Jewel of Truth and Honor and ask Lena to spend the rest of their life together. And she wanted to make her a bracelet and a ring and… not everything was certain in their lives now, but this… this was something Kara was sure of.
