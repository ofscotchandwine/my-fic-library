Reach me if you need to speak about L-Corp matters, but other than that, I don’t think we have anything to discuss.”

It was silent, the thumping behind her ribcage could be heard, and Lena was having a hard time deciphering the emotions riffling through her mother’s eyes. It was a range of hurt and sadness, sprinkled with remorse.

“Your father didn’t just leave you.” Her mother’s voice was small, the smallest Lena had ever heard it. “I loved your father. He was my mate”, Lillian whispered, her eyes lowering. “It hurt when he left and this scar”, she pointed at her mating bite. “It’s a daily reminder of what I had, a daily reminder of the rejection. The reason I protect you and Lex so much is because I don’t want you to feel that sort of heartbreak.”

In all these years, Lena never once thought about her mother’s emotions in all of this. To her credit, Lillian wasn’t an open book. Her mother kept her emotions close, not broadcasting them to anyone. Never once did Lena see Lillian shed a tear after her father left. Naively, Lena assumed her mother didn’t care but it was clear now, maybe it should have always been clear, her mother had been just as torn as Lena.

However, the revelation didn’t erase years of belittling. Years of her mother controlling every aspect of Lena’s life. Making her feel like it would never be enough. She would never be enough.

“You didn’t do us any favors”, she said above a whisper, not being able to stop her lip from trembling. “We needed you. I needed you.” Hot tears touched her skin. “But you weren’t around. So why be around now?”

Nothing was said. Lillian shared one last look with her daughter before walking out, quietly closing the door behind her. Once she was alone, Lena let years of disappoint run down her face. Looking down at her phone, she still managed a smile through her falling tears and typed ‘I miss you too.’

She was home.
