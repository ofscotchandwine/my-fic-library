There’s No Point Anymore

Never had anything hurt more than the parting words Lena threw in Kara’s face before exiting from Kara’s life permanently, at least it was permanent for a while. 

“I hate you, Kara.  You are dead to me.  I never want to see you again,” Lena spat with such anger in her eyes, disdain in her voice.  Kara had never heard Lena so hateful, not even when she spoke of or to her own mother.  It felt like someone had shot her in the gut with a Kryptonite laced bullet that went straight through her.  Never had she felt such anguish like this in all her life because though the destruction of her planet had been devastating, it had been an almost abstract concept since she had fallen into the phantom zone for 24 years before crash landing right into the Danvers’ lives.  Actually, Kara would have preferred a Kryptonite bullet through the brain right about now.  That might have hurt less than this unbearable wound bleeding out of her heart, searing into her brain, and seeping underneath her skin, burrowing deeply like a parasite.  No, it would definitely have been much less painful if she was shot through the brain with a Kryptonite bullet right now.  Perhaps she deserved it if a bullet shot her right now because of the angry, wounded look on Lena’s face.

“Lena, I would apologize a hundred times if I thought that would help make you feel less hurt.  I’m apologizing now, however little that means to you right now after … after everything, after lying for so long.  There are no words that can express how deeply, sincerely sorry I am that I didn’t tell you sooner, that I didn’t say something when I first realized how much I trust you, how much I care for you and believe in you, Lena.”  Kara, paused, taking a deep breath to steel herself to continue talking.  “However, I obviously didn’t and now … now it’s probably too late for me to apologize, but I am truly sorry, Lena.”

“Are you done, Supergirl?” Lena asked coldly, a sneer on her face, something Kara had never seen directed at her.  It hurt, but it probably didn’t hurt as much as Kara’s massive breach of trust did and Kara couldn’t imagine how much agony Lena was in right now.

Kara’s face fell, tears welling in her eyes that she tried to blink away as she looked at an unmoved Lena, still gazing at her stoically.  “I just … want to tell you how sorry I am one more time.  Lena, I have never been more remorseful for anything in my life than not telling you the truth the moment I trusted you to keep my secret, the moment I trusted in our friendship.”

“If you’re done, then leave, get the hell out of my office.  We’re done talking now.”  Lena’s face had smoothed out to show absolutely no emotion, but Kara could smell the saline of her tears in the air as well as hear how her heart pounded with ache.  It was difficult to know that she was the reason for all of it, for the way Lena was suffering right now was because she kept listening to everyone else instead of listening to her own heart and telling Lena, NDA forms be damned.

“Okay, Lena … I’ll leave but know that if you … if ever there is a day you might be able to forgive me,” Lena scoffed but didn’t interrupt Kara, “you have my number and know where to find me.  Until then … I’ll leave you be Lena.  I promise.”  Lena didn’t even acknowledge Kara’s words, just gestured for her to fly out of her office through the balcony door before turning around and sitting down in her desk chair.  That was the last time Kara would see Lena for nearly two years and not under the best circumstances either.

“Okay, but I was supposed to meet with her today in order to interview her for CatCo Magazine,” Kara spoke as if Jess didn’t already know she was a reporter there, but Jess didn’t comment on that and instead gave her a calculating look.  “I emailed her directly about this and someone else from LCorp emailed me back to come at this time today.”

Kara felt numb as she nodded blankly, feeling worse than how she had felt when she last spoke to Lena about her betrayal over a year ago, worse than how hollow she had been the following months without contact with her best friend, with the love of her life out of her life for such a long time.  “Why are you telling me this?”

“Right then.  I’ll just … I’ll be on my way then, Jess.  Thank you … thank you for telling me this in person when you didn’t have to … or need to let me know anything at all.”  Jess responded by nodding her head and giving her a wave goodbye before getting back to her work, after all an assistant’s job is never done no matter how far away they were from their boss.

“Hey, Kara, I brought the pizza as promised for sister … night?  What’s wrong, Kara?”  Alex came over to set the pizza boxes down on the coffee table and immediately wrapped Kara up in her arms, knowing how tactile Kara was when she wanted, or needed in this case, comfort when she was hurting over something.  For a long time, Kara was silent, just taking comfort in being in Alex’s arms and warm hug.  Kara hadn’t cried yet, nothing had really sunk in yet.  The fact that Lena was somewhere she didn’t know and couldn’t possibly hope to find her hadn’t become real yet as she sat in her dark apartment, staring at a picture frame of Lena holding her from behind, exuding happiness in her subtle smile.  Alex waited patiently for Kara to find the right words to tell her what was wrong.  She knew that Kara sometimes wasn’t sure what she wanted to say yet, how to say what she wanted to say, and so she needed the time to find what she wanted to say to her.  “Take your time, sweetie.”  Alex kissed her forehead tenderly for a long moment before leaning back, looking Kara in the eye when she made a noise.

“She’s gone, Alex, and I’ll probably never see her again.”  Alex furrowed her brows but remembered why she had come over to Kara’s apartment in the first place: Lena Luthor.  Kara had excitedly told her that Lena had accepted an interview with her for earlier today and Alex insisted that she should come over after she was done at work so that she could bring celebratory pizza over or consolation pizza if things didn’t go as planned with the interview.  “Jess, her assistant,” as if Alex didn’t know who she was from the various times she had gone to Lena’s office over the years, “was actually the one who emailed me in Lena’s place to accept the meeting to … to tell me in person that Lena left the country a long time ago.  Maybe she felt bad for me or just knew that I would keep coming if Lena never answered.  I’m not sure, but she said that the reason she asked me to come in person was so that I wouldn’t come by the office everyday looking for her because she’s not in the same state, let alone same country anymore, from how she said it anyways since she never specified if Lena was still in the country or not.  Just said that she was … far, far away from here … from me.”

“Oh, Kara.  Come here.”  Alex felt Kara bury her face into her neck, finally shedding tears at the finality of the whole situation, of the realization that Lena never wanted to see her face again and she truly meant it.  There was no way to fix this, no way to make things better without Lena coming back and telling Kara that she had forgiven her, that they could be friends again, or at the very least that she would be in the same city as her even if she never spoke to her again in an unofficial capacity.  Being acquaintances would be better than not being anything to each other at all.  Kara’s thoughts just spiraled from there.  Nothing was going to make any of this okay again.  After that Kara didn’t speak another word for the rest of the night, hardly even touched the pizza, much to Alex’s dismay and concern.

Broken Inside

Lena was glad she had found somewhere so remote without being too far out of civilization to not have decent technological resources.  Well, Lena had built herself something to magnify the output of her internet since it had been shoddy at best when she first moved into her privately built cabin in the woods to make sure no one could track her.  She made sure that the building was obscured by trees still so no one could spot it easily from an aerial view.  Clearing out trees was the dumb mistake of every rich person ever who tried to get off of the grid and hide away from whatever troubled them, or criminals for that matter.  No, Lena would never make such a rudimentary mistake like that because it would get her killed in less time than it took to apologize to yourself out loud while being murdered.

Her little cabin looked like it was a dilapidated building that no one had lived in for years so no one would try to come in should they come across it for any reason.  She had a hidden lab area underneath in which she could hide should someone try to come in to find her and the entrance to it could only be opened by her handprint alone.  In fact, unless someone knew where to look, the wood was so well crafted to look like it was falling apart without being decrepit so that no one would ever think that she was living underneath her house disguised as an unlived in cabin.  She also made sure that should someone come within 50 feet of her property she would be notified and her surveillance footage would come on to show whether they were getting anywhere near her hideaway home or not.  If someone unauthorized were to come into the cabin, for whatever reason, Lena would know about that as well and be able to monitor their movements to ensure she would not be found accidentally.  The brunette was rather proud of the fact that she was even able to soundproof everything in her secret lair so she wouldn’t have to worry about making unnecessary sound that could give her away to anyone looking.  Lena only came out of her lab and secret living quarters in the room connected to the lab when she wanted fresh air or to go to her phony apartment complex in the city to stock up on any dwindling food supplies or grab some important things that Jess would have sent to her through their system to leave as little trace evidence as possible as to where Lena was currently residing.  Everything Jess helped her set up was clever.  In fact, if it weren’t for the fact that Jess had a sibling looking to move at the time she suggested this idea to Lena, things would not have worked out as perfectly as they did.

Things were so much quieter, life so easy and simple, that Lena didn’t think she could find a reason to miss how complicated her life had been before she moved to such an isolated lifestyle with bare minimum contact with the outside world other than with her assistant and occasionally with Jess’ sister when they saw each other in passing.  Everything had been fine for nearly a year so it was inevitable that something happened to change that.  Because nothing in Lena’s life could stay the way she wanted it to stay, of course.  Today was one of the days she had gone back to the apartment she set up with Jess’ sister to pick up some basic necessities when something changed the entire course of her life.  She had been enjoying her hermit lifestyle for sixteen months now when Alex Danvers made an appearance, sitting at a table with Isla and drinking tea while waiting for her.  Lena had no illusions that Alex was there for any other reason than to find her.

“Why are you here?” Lena asked, voice cold and full of steel.

“I thought it would be obvious why it is I’m here, Lena.”  Alex sounded calm, but Lena could detect the slightest hint of a waiver in her voice.  Something was wrong then, something had to be in order to prompt this visit after all.  The fact that Alex went to so much effort meant that something had to be terribly wrong with Kara because she would never waste so much time otherwise, of that Lena was quite certain.  She knew, after all, how difficult it had to have been for anyone to track her down like this because of all of the safeguards she had put into place to ensure it would be near impossible to figure out without knowing her well enough to figure out her single, outside contact.  It was genius and near foolproof.  Not foolproof enough, evidently.

Lena gave a mirthless chuckle at that, “if I knew why you were here, I wouldn’t be asking, now would I, Alex?”  Isla cleared her throat and nodded to Lena without saying a word.  Lena was grateful that Isla didn’t give her secret hideout away, knowing that Isla was just as loyal as her sister was.

As soon as Isla had left to go about her business, Alex spoke again, sounding agitated.  “Would you cut the crap, Lena?  You know the only reason I would be here right now is for Kara’s safety and well-being, though it’s almost nonexistent these days as it is.”  Alex mumbled the last part, something Lena wasn’t sure if she was supposed to hear or not, the way Alex had muttered it under her breath.  They were barely arm’s length from each other.

“What, so Kara asked you to find me to help her, make her feel better, then?”  Lena’s tone was pure venom and derisive.  Alex was almost tempted to slap her.  Alex could not deal with having two people she needed to slap some sense into these days, but at least with Lena it wouldn’t break every bone in her hand.

“No, actually.  I’m here without Kara’s knowledge because she’s …” Alex swallowed hard, closing her eyes tightly for a moment before continuing stutteringly, “Kara is a danger to herself … these days.  Her behavior … she’s been so self-destructive lately.  Every time she comes back from a fight she has to spend several hours in the sunbed to heal because she doesn’t care how much danger she is putting herself in, what risks she’s taking, how recklessly she’s been fighting, and it’s gotten to the point that she’s nearly died almost every day for the last two months.  In fact, she hasn’t gone even three days without having to be seen for numerous wounds inflicted upon her any time she has gone out to save people or capture some wayward alien or metahuman of some sort.  She doesn’t care about her life anymore and hasn’t for nearly a full year now, Lena.”  Alex paused, too overwhelmed by emotion.  “I can’t keep watching her do this Lena, I just can’t do it anymore.  She acts like her life doesn’t matter to her anymore, she’s been so withdrawn, has had absolutely no appetite either, and it’s like the life has been sucked right out of her.  Kara used to be the sun personified, the sun in alien form, but now … now she’s more like a raincloud hovering in everyone’s peripheral, just merely existing.”  It was hard to hear, hard to picture.  “When she first found out she was just so sad.  It probably started more or less a few weeks after she found out, her being more reckless and rasher every time she was needed for something.  And that slowly progressed to her becoming injured with greater frequency by the time a month had rolled by since she found out about you leaving.  By the time it had become six months her recklessness skyrocketed to dangerous levels, constantly being brought back to the DEO almost dead, barely alive for the next time she’s needed to save National City or going after some criminals.  It’s … almost like she is … trying to get herself killed.”  Emotion welled in Alex’s throat, in her halting speech about her fears.

Lena swallowed, feeling bile rising up her throat at the thought that Kara had almost died continuously for two months straight without her knowing.  That was her own fault though.  She refused to look at the news networks centered in National City, or in general since some countries mentioned Supergirl when she ventured outside of the United States to help with major crisis’ outside when requested.  “And what does that have to do with me, then?  Why come to me rather than Kara about this behavior?”

Steel entered Alex’s eyes as she revealed the truth she didn’t want to acknowledge, “because I already did and she told me that ‘there’s no point anymore’ as if her life doesn’t matter to her anymore.  She’s …” Alex choked on the word before she could utter it, “she’s … suicidal, Lena, has been for the better part of six fucking months.  Nothing I do, nothing I say has helped make it better, make her feel like life is worth living again.”  Lena almost caved after hearing that, but she didn’t know how her being around would even help anything.  “It’s like … how some criminals know they’re caught and commit suicide by cop but instead she’s trying to commit suicide by being a Superhero, dying while saving the day.”

“And how would my being there make any difference in how she’s behaving, Alex?  What would you have me do?”

Alex looked at Lena incredulously, like she was some mythical creature come to life.  “You have got to be kidding me, Lena.  Of all the things … you think that this has nothing to do with you, that you coming back wouldn’t change things drastically somehow?  Fuck’s sake, Lena, she’s gone out drunk to save people from dangerous aliens a few times in the last four months or during an emergency of some sort.  Hell, she’s gone out hungover as well because she doesn’t care if she dies anymore and it’s … killing everyone who cares about her to see her this way because she’s been suicidal since she found out you left without a word.  If you’re not willing to save her then I’m going to have to watch her die someday and then it’ll be too late for regrets and apologies, Lena.  Do you really want that to be your future, to be your biggest regret?”  Alex looked so damn sad that even Lena couldn’t help but feel the sorrow pouring out of Alex in waves, the pain of an older sibling watching a younger sibling suffer undeniable distress and not being able to do anything about it.  When Lena didn’t answer for several minutes, Alex shook her head in disgust, scoffing at her.  “I suppose it’s my fault for thinking better of you.  The Lena I knew would never stand here instead of finding the next flight to National City and booking it with expedited tickets and flight times because she’s a billionaire with a very nearly limitless pool of resources.  Serves me right for thinking that way then.  I’m going back to watch my sister kill herself because I can’t do anything to talk her out of it, not for lack of trying either.”  Alex sighed, looking contrite for losing her temper, “look, I’m not saying you have to forgive her or anything like that.  You just need to give her closure.  No, you don’t need to give her closure, but it would better for the both of you to move on.  She’s stuck, Lena, she hasn’t moved on from this and she won’t if you don’t at least talk to her one more time or do something.  You don’t have to even talk to her in person if you don’t want.  Just … tell her that you’ve moved on or something, that you’re doing well.”

Lena was surprised by how quickly Alex reigned in her anger and composed herself.  “I don’t know what I would even say if I called her Alex and it has been nearly two years since we last saw each other.  You all knew, but none of you thought to tell me about Kara keeping a secret this big from me.”

Sorrow entered Alex’s gaze.  “There were many times that Kara wanted to tell you, especially after Lex but she didn’t want to burden you with that when you had been grieving and I talked her out of it.  In the past she kept saying she wanted to tell you but the DEO, well, J’onn wouldn’t allow it for many reasons, though … I guess none of that matters now anyways.”  Alex paused.  “You cut us all out of your life, though I don’t blame you for that, Lena.  I even thought we were friends at one point, but … here we are.  Whatever it was you were looking for here … I hope you found it.  Be safe, Lena, for Kara’s sake.  She wouldn’t want anything to happen to you without her around to … save you like old times.”

Lena watched a tear escape Alex’s eye as she said the last part, the only tear she’d ever seen Alex shed in all of the years she’d known the woman.  “Wait, Alex.”  But Alex was already gone by the time Lena came to her senses from hearing such shocking news.  And Lena hated herself in that moment for not being stronger, for not being strong enough.  If only she had been smart enough to see through Kara’s disguise.  Some genius she was for not knowing her best friend was hiding something so important from her while seeing the signs left and right but never putting any of it together despite it all being right in front of her.  A pang in her chest brought Lena out of her self-deprecating thoughts.  Perhaps a little research would help her come to a decision after what Alex had said.  Closure … to move on … move forward.

X

It had been nearly a week since Alex had come back from her mysterious trip that she wouldn’t talk about no matter how much Kara or anyone else asked her about it.  J’onn and Winn were the only ones who didn’t ask her so they probably knew for one reason or another, but they wouldn’t tell anyone else what they possibly knew about it.  At least J’onn wouldn’t and Winn might only crack under pressure if someone started hounding him, poking him like the Pillsbury Dough Boy for answers and he just might cave.  Kara gave up asking after not getting a proper answer after a couple of days, much to Alex’s disappointment because Kara normally badgered and cajoled until someone gave her what she wanted when it wasn’t something serious, like a secret someone wanted to keep.  The Kara from before wouldn’t have given in to her answer so easily, wouldn’t have given up without at least pouting to get an answer from her and the unease in Alex’s heart and mind increased further at witnessing the extent of which Kara had withdrawn herself from everyone and everything going on around her, how distant she had become.

Winn shared worried glances with Alex as they both watched Kara walk off listlessly.  It was almost as if Kara had an aura of someone who wasn’t altogether there, just from seeing the way she moved without purpose.  Anyone would be able to sense how little she cared about her life just from looking at her for even a moment.  Even someone blind would be able to see it, would be able to sense her misery.  Nothing anyone had said had caused Kara to wake up from her state of perpetual sorrow.  The saddest thing was probably seeing Kara eat pot stickers as if they tasted like nothing, just eating them because someone else made her eat food.  Kara had to be reminded to eat food these days and she was definitely eating less than half of what her usual intake of calories used to be before Lena.

“So, still nothing from Lena then?” Alex asked, making sure to whisper just in case Kara might possibly listen in, though Kara didn’t seem to notice anything even if it did hit her in the face these days.

“Nope, nothing.  Though that in and of itself doesn’t mean anything because Lena does have ways of communicating that I might not even be able to pick up or keep track of without knowing what she’s using because I still don’t know where she has been hiding all of this time, Alex.  I mean, we figured out a link to Lena indirectly.  Whatever set up she has over in that half of the world is far too complicated and well protected for me to find even a scent of information that would ping on any of my watch alerts because she’s a freaking genius.”  Alex rolled her eyes at Winn despite knowing that he was just covering all of his bases because he had learned the hard way not to leave out any details that Alex would deem important, the punch on his left arm had seriously hurt.  He was pretty sure he even had a bruise for a few seconds after.

“It hasn’t even been a week yet, but I’m still worried about whether Lena is going to come back at all to talk some sense into Kara about being more careful instead of acting liking dying to save people is actually okay.”  Alex let out a sigh that expressed all of her emotions all at once.  The sorrow, the exhaustion and worry, the desperation, and lingering hope that somehow Lena was going to help Kara were all expressed in the single sigh that Alex let out.  “Not knowing is the worst part for me right now.  If I knew for sure that Lena was coming within the next few weeks, hell, I’ll even take a month, then I wouldn’t feel so anxious about this whole thing.  Lena is an unknown factor and I wish it weren’t so, Winn.  It’s Lena’s choice whether she chooses to come back and talk to Kara or not … but I can’t help but want her here.”

“Yeah, you and me both, Alex.  All we can do is wait or see if I can get any pings before we even know whether it is a real thing or not.”  Alex just nodded, hoping she was right to put her trust in Lena and be able to see her sister finally heal, in more ways than just physically.

X

Lena had spent the last four days wallowing in misery as she read article after article, watched clip after clip, and even watched a few live video streams of Kara getting pummeled into the ground, smashing into buildings that seemed to actually break into her skin, and being beaten bloody by some aliens and metahumans with certain abilities able to harm her.  Nothing had been as terrifying as the most recent live stream that had happened just a few hours ago depicting Kara flying towards someone with an alien weapon of some sort that actually burned her skin.  It was unbelievable that something had managed to harm Kara so severely.  Lena almost cried when Kara seemed to grit her teeth against the pain but continued on her path to get the weapon out of the female alien’s hands.  Just watching all of the video footage and seeing the earliest ones where Kara, who was unused to pain, reacted much more severely than she was with the more recent ones Lena had been watching just cut Lena’s heart to pieces, really made her feel a stabbing pain in her chest that made her heart ache.

All of the things Lena was witnessing almost seemed like a lie, like it couldn’t possibly be true.  Kara, a Kryptonian, was supposed to be invulnerable to everything on this planet other than when various forms of Kryptonite came into contact with her, or certain alien weaponry with similar effects.  But it looked as if everything was harming her these days, though that was a slight exaggeration, even though nothing but Kryptonite was supposed to be able to make her bruise, bleed, and break.  No, she was supposed to be able to shrug off every blow someone managed to land on her.  She was supposed to be able to get back up without a scratch when someone threw her into a building and she was, especially, not supposed to have any DEO agents putting her in a stretcher to tend to her when she was passed out or on death’s door from a fight.  These were all things that had never happened before whenever Kara was out saving the day unless CADMUS was involved and got Kryptonite from her mother.  In fact, other than when her mother was involved, Kara had never truly been injured before.  Seeing Kara lying lifelessly on the ground after someone teamed up on her and shot a rocket launcher at her in order to escape custody, though luckily the Martian Man had intervened and caught them before they could get away with everything.

Ever since Lena had watched that last video, from just a few months ago, she had been sitting in her living room area in a nearly catatonic state.  Just seeing Kara laying lifelessly on the ground made a piercing ache lance through her heart.  Tears had come to her eyes unbidden, summoned by the thought of almost losing Kara and never knowing how close she had been to living in a world in which Kara was dead and she would never have known it because of her damn, stubborn pride.  All because she couldn’t bring herself to come to Kara and had been hiding somewhere Kara would have never gone to look for her.  Perhaps she would have never have found her either because of the lead lining she had used to ensure no superpowered beings would find her and also preventing certain other people from finding her as well.  Lena felt an extreme, all-encompassing sorrow pass over her, making her feel a strong sense of regret for not having given Kara a chance to … make it up to her, to allow her to mend their friendship and heal the wound she had caused instead of forcing her into becoming this.  She’d never felt so ashamed in her life, for allowing this to happen because it had never occurred to her that this might be the end result of her somewhat hasty departure.  It had only taken her a few months of planning after Jess had suggested it off-hand just a few weeks after Lena’s fallout with Kara had occurred and it had seemed like a brilliant idea at the time.

Lena almost wanted to grab a bottle of scotch, take a sloppy drink from it, and then smash it against a wall because her guilt was consuming her more than any other emotion had ever eaten her alive before in her life.  Never had Lena felt so much shame before for something she had caused, however inadvertent the intent had been in this case.  Had she known that this would have hurt Kara so much, maybe she wouldn’t have ever felt the need to leave National City behind.  It wasn’t as if Kara was trying to gaslight her and manipulate her into coming back, into forgiving her and forcing her to stay with guilt.  No, far from it in fact.  Maybe it wouldn’t have been ideal, but it definitely would be better than wondering if she had never left if Kara wouldn’t be so susceptible to pain like this instead, constantly becoming injured and not healing right away, or properly at all.

The worst thing about Alex’s visit was probably that she wasn’t demanding Lena to come back, hadn’t been unreasonable, or rude either.  Sure, she lost her temper once, but she was just someone who was trying to do right by the people she cared about and closure was something that Lena might need because something had been missing the entire time she was here.  The time apart had helped her heal a little, well, acting like her feelings didn’t exist was easier when she was alone other than still having meetings in her little hideout and being able to claim bad signal when she was annoyed with her board members was fun.  Having the time to herself was her way of indulging in herself, pampering herself with her me time.  It was her way of forgetting the bad and just doing whatever she needed to get to that point, to stop feeling the torturous pain in her chest and this awful feeling she couldn’t identify that felt like … heartbreak but personified.  This feeling of heartbreak almost felt like something tangible, something she could reach out and touch.  Lena didn’t know what it was, but making it go away had been her priority and she managed that by busying herself the first few months with getting her laboratory ready underneath the house.  She became lost in thought and started thinking about something that had been nagging her about the footage she had been watching.  There was something about the earliest clips versus the more recent ones that had struck her as odd but she couldn’t place what it was exactly.  Lena sat there for several moments, just thinking.

Several moments had passed into nearly half an hour when something seemed to strike a chord within Lena, something that clicked in her mind as she replayed the events she had watched within her mind’s eye.  Wondering if she was just thinking too hard, Lena decided to check to see if she was just imagining things or it there was some accuracy to what she now believed to be true.  Lena played over the oldest videos from nearly two years ago when Kara and Lena had, had their falling out, rather when Lena decided she could no longer be Kara’s friend and wanted space from her.  The more she watched from those first fights to the ones coming closer to within the last year there was something that was sticking to her brain but she couldn’t quite place her finger on it just yet.  Something about Kara’s numerous injuries over the years didn’t make sense to her if Kara was supposed to be completely invulnerable to everything on earth that could harm her without Kryptonite being a factor.  It seemed as if sometime within the first few months since their fight Kara was becoming only slightly injured during fights, like a small scratch here and there on occasion.  After nearly half a year it seemed to be slightly more precarious, some bruising that faded within a few minutes, but still noticeably different from how she almost never had any bruising without the use of Kryptonite to weaken her enough to hurt her.  Just where did these people get Kryptonite from anyways though?  It was very few and far in between, but still, seeing as more than even one criminal had gotten their hands on it was highly disconcerting.

Against her better judgement, Lena decided to watch something more recent than from four months ago depicting a major clash from just two weeks ago, not counting the one where Kara received second degree burns from a few hours ago, and she braced herself for nearly ten minutes before pressing play, the still image before the video started was of Kara flying high in the air above her, visibly enormous, enemy which looked like a large boulder with arms and legs made up of smaller boulders.  Pressing play was difficult as it were, watching Kara get hit by one of the creature’s massive arms was unbearable, more so when the audible crunch of her bones breaking was heard through her surround sound speakers as well as the nearly blood curdling scream she let out.  Lena couldn’t help but cry as she watched Kara get up from where she had fallen despite the blood now pouring from her temple where the part that would be a hand had smashed into her head, her hand was supporting her more than likely broken ribs, and flew up slowly above it again.  Tears streamed down her face as she wept silently and continued to watch Kara fight against the creature wreaking havoc and destroying her body in the process, progressively showing how hard it was for her to continue getting back up.  When she eventually defeated it and the DEO took over handling it and capturing it to transport it back to their, not so secret, headquarters, Alex helped carry Kara off to one of their signature unmarked vehicles while batting away disgusting news reporters and paparazzi trying to get close to Kara selfishly.  Lena would have found anyone who was part of CatCo and fired them if they were one of the people impeding Kara’s recovery while she looked like death after beating a monster for their sake while they watched from a safe distance.  It truly disgusted Lena that Kara protected people like that only to have them disrespect her blatantly and show how little they cared about her health after she had saved their lives.  One thing that gave her relief was seeing civilians trying to help clear a path for her and pushing reporters back to give her room, helping the DEO agents with putting a clearly marked line between Kara and the voracious reporters and frenzied photographers.

Image after image, video after video, and article after article Lena had been watching, reading, and absorbing a large amount of information without a break for more than twelve hours total in the last four days, her breaks being her passing out for three hours before waking up again to repeat the cycle of pain as she researched for more.  She found as much information as she could dig up, there were far too many news articles and video footage for Lena to sift through, ever since Alex’s visit four days ago.  Everything she found made her regret her decision from nearly two years ago because it was obvious that her leaving had a devastating and swift effect on Kara’s overall well-being.  In every picture she seemed tired, weary, as time progressed she was steadily becoming paler until recently she looked entirely unhealthy compared to how she used to look, and the worst part was seeing specks of dried up blood, or wounds still seeping, and bruises on her body.  Seeing a fading bruise on someone who was supposed to have invulnerable skin was absolutely horrifying and upsetting.  Knowing that she was the reason was even worse than Lena had ever imagined.

There hadn’t been a single day that Lena hadn’t wondered whether she had made the right choice in leaving and ensuring that Kara wouldn’t be able to find her without some serious help from someone else, like Winn.  Of course, she tried to rationalize, tried to tell herself that yes, it was the best decision to get away from Kara to heal from such a huge, painful revelation.  She needed to take care of herself first, to allow herself the time and space she needed to heal from such a scarring revelation.  Kara had been lying to her since the moment they met and it hurt that Kara had claimed to trust her, had claimed to be her best friend, had crawled past every defense she had ever built for herself to survive living with the Luthors with her sunny disposition and endless warmth and kindness.  Everything about Kara Danvers had been disarming and wonderfully charming.  Lena was sure at one point that they could have been … more than just friends, best friends.  Had things been different perhaps, if things had worked out different maybe.  But they hadn’t so there was no time for the ‘what ifs’, what could have been.

Her initial move away had been everything she had been hoping for, other than the niggling doubts that just never seemed to fade ever since she had moved from National City to China instead.  There had been something that had felt wrong, something that just didn’t feel quite right despite how desperately Lena knew she needed to get away from everything … from her.  And as time went on, the sliver of doubt never went away, but Lena had gotten quite good at ignoring that feeling and it became an almost subconscious thought, a little pest she barely noticed unless it was presented right in front of her.  She was almost completely certain it was the right move after having been away for a full year.  The thought still persisted, whether she had made the right decision, no matter how she tried to convince herself it was the right thing to do for sure.  But now, now Lena had her answer as she felt her heart break once again as she watched Kara get smacked into a metal structure and not get up, J’onn coming to her rescue once again.  The worst part was seeing how the metal was merely dented whereas it would usually have a Kara sized hole, had she been at her full strength there was no question that a hole would be present instead.

With every brutally detailed video and image of Kara’s sustained injuries becoming gradually worse Lena felt her heart splinter into two halves, one half drowning in sorrow, the other half filled to the brim with regret.  There was no denying that Lena’s actions lead directly to this situation.  Kara was becoming weaker for some reason she couldn’t possibly fathom, but there was no doubt in her mind that it had something to do with her decision to leave.  Everything had started sometime after she left and Kara found out about it, perhaps not as early as when she had first left, but it definitely started showing within several months of her leaving.  And the scientist in Lena wanted to know, wanted to figure out if it had something to do with Kryptonian biology that caused this weakness in Kara and her ability to recover was lessening.  It almost seemed as if Kara was becoming more and more human as timed passed and Kara’s body and physiology didn’t improve, didn’t become more Kryptonian.  Either way, Lena couldn’t help her curiosity over what was happening to Kara, but her most pressing concern was Kara recovering, Kara becoming strong again so she wouldn’t kill herself trying to save the world in her extremely weakened state.  Lena couldn’t bear the thought of Kara dying.  Her heart ached at the very thought as soon as it occurred to her Kara might die, can die now.

Nearly choking on her emotions, Lena prepared herself to go back to National City as soon as possible.  She was glad that she had nearly limitless funds still.  Her private jet would allow her an easier time to fly back to National City on such short notice once she took a flight to a country her pilot could pick her up from.  Plans fully forming in her mind, Lena booked the next flight to Seoul, South Korea so her pilot would be able to pick her up after she landed there and fly her back to National City.  Lena just hoped she wouldn’t be too late, hoped that Kara hadn’t managed to do irreversible damage … hadn’t managed to kill herself with all of her near suicide missions.  Her heart ached fiercely, throbbed in pain at the thought, but she thought nothing of the pain she felt tightening her chest as she got herself packed and ready to go to the nearest city that she could to take the bullet train to the airport.  The bullet train traveled at over 200 miles per hour and would beat any taxi to the airport for that reason including not dealing with China’s insane traffic throughout any major cities.  Lena could more than afford to take the most expensive mode of transportation in the country and she didn’t want to waste any time.

“Wait for me Kara, please wait for me.  I’ll figure this out … no, we’ll figure this out together and you’re going to be just fine.  Just … just wait for me.”  Lena sent a silent prayer to whoever, whatever might be out there to keep Kara safe for her.  “I hope I’m not too late.”

Leaving her hidden home was the easiest decision Lena had ever made in her life, but she could only hope and pray she was going to make it in time.  Time could only tell.  Despite her fear of flying, Lena barely paid any attention to the fact that she was, in fact, several thousand feet in the air.  Nothing mattered more than getting to Kara and seeing her for the first time in nearly two years.  Lena only felt anxiety for what waited for her back in National City, rather who waited for her.

Five days.  It had been five days since Lillian was captured and Kara had been taken to the DEO to heal and still, she was unconscious.  Lena had been pacing, biting her fingers, biting her lip, and constantly checking on Kara’s vital signs to make sure she was still breathing, still alive.  Every passing second felt like hours and the longer she was unconscious the more Lena worried she might never wake up from her injuries, from the mysterious ailment she was suffering from.  Being that Lena was still the CEO of CatCo, she made the call to put Kara on sick days until she fully recovered, much to Snapper’s surprise and, even mild, concern about Kara because she was never sick.  He swore that she had the immune system of a horse because she never called in sick in all of her years at CatCo.

Lena’s nerves continued to grow, the less progress Kara made.  Her vitals were stagnated, neither improving nor worsening, something that wasn’t necessarily good or bad but in her book no improvement was a bad thing when it came to someone’s health, when it came to Kara.  And for five days now, Kara had showed no improvement whatsoever and it was killing Lena the longer it took for Kara to simply open her beautiful blue eyes just for a single moment.  If Kara even so much as fluttered her eyelashes Lena was sure she would cry in relief of such a small sign that Kara was going to be okay, that she was going to recover fully.  Alex hadn’t been much better and she was tight lipped about the Kryptonian ailment that Kara was suffering from, not that Lena was trying to be nosy and ask questions until Alex caved in.  No, it was in the way Lena would look at Alex any time she was talking to Dr. Hamilton about the x-rays that she was being so secretive about when discussing Kara’s physical health.  She had easily told Lena that all CT scans and MRIs had come back clean other than one small tear in Kara’s shoulder that would heal easily while she was in the sunbed, something that had come back cleared when another MRI had been taken just a couple days later.  But she was very clear in saying that the x-rays had shown something that she needed to discuss privately with Dr. Hamilton.  That was easily the most effective way she could have alerted Lena to the fact that there was something wrong with Kara’s body internally and it had to do with the ailment she hadn’t said much about the other day.

Curiosity was burning Lena up inside, but more than anything her worry, love, and guilt were eating her up inside.  She was so, so worried about Kara’s well-being, felt her love for Kara so strongly since the moment Kara collapsed nearly a week ago, and her guilt over this having something to do with her, something she did more than likely caused all this.  Lena was sitting down for once, holding Kara’s hand gently and before she even realized what she was doing, she was bringing Kara’s hand up to her lips for a sweet kiss, lips lingering for a moment before she felt her breath catch because Kara was finally waking.  Eyelids fluttering with the movement of her eyes, her hand twitching in Lena’s, and her facial muscles ticking in the slightest movement, it took Lena’s breath away with anticipation for Kara’s awakening her entire body was trembling with nerves and traitorous hope.

And then Kara’s eyes fluttered open the slightest bit, no doubt groggy and sensitive to the amount of light in the room.

Everything hurt from the light hitting her closed eyes to the feeling in her entire body.  It felt like someone had repeatedly slammed a car on her body with the way her head ached and her body felt sore all over, not to mention how hard it was to feel anything passed her pain.  Several seconds passed as she tried to take things in with her senses slowly.  Her eyes registered the light in the room, noting that it was definitely the sunbed at the DEO.  Kara felt something warm in her hand and realized she could hear a familiar heartbeat thrumming next to her as well as feel it from how tightly the hand was holding her own, squeezing with tension.  There was only one person this hand could belong to, only one heartbeat that sounded like this to Kara’s ears.  It was impossible though.  There was no way.  It had to be a dream, her dream.  She must still be asleep because she might be awake but the moment Lena left, her world had changed completely.  When Lena left it was like her world was half asleep, unwilling to wake from the dream of when Lena was here.  Kara was afraid, terrified to find that she was awake but the nightmare persisted in wakefulness.  If she opened her eyes and looked to her left and found Lena, it would be a dream.  But then she heard a hopeful voice, a voice so sweet she almost couldn’t believe it.

“Kara, you’re, you’re okay.”  The sound of her voice, so angelic and beautiful, her words tasted better than ice cream and pot stickers combined, sounded better than an angelic voice singing Chasing Pavements, and it had to be too good to be true, it had to be because Lena left a long time ago.  But then Kara remembered what happened before she came to be in the sunbed.  Lillian, Lena was kidnapped by Lillian.  Kara shot up in the bed, frantic and panicked and her vitals climbed to dangerous levels as she felt a panic attack come over her as her eyes moved wildly around the room, unseeing.  The calming presence of warmth, the scent of Lena’s expensive perfume, the gentle touch of Lena’s body holding her own, the soothing sound of Lena’s calm, though slightly quickened heartbeat, and the taste of her tears as Lena held her in her arms, Kara’s head laying against Lena’s collarbone where her tears fell slowly.  Everything narrowed down to the point of their contact, narrowed to every sense that Lena took over by holding her so close.  It calmed Kara immensely, almost immediately.  Lena’s presence eased the ache, the worry, and the adrenaline that had been coursing through Kara when she thought Lena might still be in danger somehow.  Relief poured into Kara in waves just from feeling Lena’s solid presence pressing against her alone.  Her soul felt something in the moment Lena reached out to her.

Another voice entered the fray as Alex came in to see her awake finally.  “Oh, thank God you’re awake now!  I have been worried sick about you, Kara.”  Alex pressed her forehead against Kara’s and smiled with relief, happiness flooded her, and when she pulled back, she gave Kara a smack that more than likely hurt her more than it had Kara, if the small grunt was anything to go by that she let out upon feeling something solid smack against her skin like cement would.  “You scared the shit out of me this time, Kara.”  Kara and Lena still hadn’t pulled apart from the hug just yet, needing to feel each other, needing something tangible to hold on to and warmth.  It felt like forgiveness and love.  This warmth, it was something she had longed for so long but never dreamed of having ever again.  It was hard to believe she was awake right now.  Was she?  Kara dreaded that this was a nightmare where she woke up and it was all a lie, a figment of her imagination, a vision of what would never be and in her rising panic, her vital signs started going crazy.  Lena immediately grabbed Kara’s face, held her in her hands.

“What is it, what’s wrong, Kara?”  Her heartrate beat wildly, her pulse spiking, her eyes rolled back into her head and she passed out.  “No!  Damn it, Kara.  You are not doing this to me, Kara!  I am not letting you die!  Where is Dr. Hamilton?”  Lena asked frantically from where she was still holding Kara’s weight, Kara having fallen forward so her head was pressed against Lena’s shoulder and collarbone area.

“I’m right here, Miss Luthor, and I can take over from here, if you could just lay her down gently.”  Instead of answering verbally, Lena, reluctantly, laid Kara back down on the bed before getting up and standing back and just moments later Kara started coughing up blood, large amounts of blood with flower petals drowning in blood instead of the little speckles from nearly a week ago.  The horrifying, terrible sight caused Lena to nearly faint herself, but she barely managed to force herself to stay conscious.  Lena felt a warm, comforting hand on her shoulder and looked over to see Alex there.

“Are you feeling okay?  You look like you might faint, too, Lena.”  Alex looked concerned as she continued to eye Lena who couldn’t formulate an answer.  Finally, she shook her head as another wave passed and she felt like she might just collapse when Alex helped steady her.  “Okay, follow me, Lena.  I’m going to take you to the other Medical Room for humans.  Come on, this way.”  Alex was gentle and patient when guiding her because Lena was sure without her, she would have buckled already, her legs feeling like jelly, her heart in turmoil.  Every step felt like she was weighed down by a hundred pounds of … guilt, perhaps?  Her legs felt leaden, weighed down by some unknown force pushing her down, making her feel worse than Atlas shouldering the weight of the world on his shoulders.  Her heart felt even heavier, if that was possible somehow.  It was an odd feeling, like her emotions manifested in physical form.  Lena wouldn’t be surprised if there was a kernel of truth, something about what she was thinking seemed to be right along with what was happening.  There was no other explanation for the symptoms she was currently feeling.  Her emotional state was causing a physical reaction similar to panic attacks, similar to what Kara might have just gone through a few minutes ago herself.

It was more than likely what had happened and Lena felt like she couldn’t breathe for a moment.  As soon as the thought occurred to her, she knew that she was more than likely right and Lena almost wanted to sprint back to Kara.  If only her body would listen.  Alex had already led her over to a bed to sit down on when Lena’s mind came to that conclusion.  “Wait, Alex.  I just … is it just me or did Kara have a panic attack just now, before she passed out, I mean.  She was fine, her heart was calm and steady when it started picking up all of a sudden before she fainted, like she was panicking before she just couldn’t handle it anymore and fainted.”  A thoughtful look crossed Alex’s face.

“You might be right about that.  It would make sense.  Her heartrate and pulse both elevated pretty quickly, like a panic attack would, before she collapsed.  I didn’t even know Kryptonians could have panic attacks like that, Lena.”  Alex sighed heavily, her mind working over everything that had happened just moments ago in Kara’s recovery room.  “I need to check in with Dr. Hamilton, but I need to take care of you first, Lena.  Sit and let me take your blood pressure and vitals.”  Lena had been standing next to the bed, lost in thought before realizing that.

“Right, okay.”  Lena sat down, mind still whirring about everything that was going on, the possibilities for what was happening to Kara, how nothing seemed to make sense.  Kara was not becoming human, as Lena had thought of as an option, that was for sure considering Kryptonite was still deadly.  But there was still the connection between them as a strong working theory because Kara had to linked to Lena somehow, with when her powers started to have some kind of issue.  Like, her being less vulnerable started sometime around when she had left but had solidified when Kara discovered she had left long ago already.

Alex taking her blood pressure brought Lena out of her thoughts, having been lost in the many possible reasons for Kara’s sudden collapse into extreme vulnerability.  Her head and heart ached at the thoughts running through her mind.  “Hey, your blood pressure’s a little high, Lena.  I clocked you in at 135/88.  Your pulse is also a bit high too.  Are you experiencing any symptoms such as lightheadedness, dizziness, or feeling out of breath, like you were having trouble breathing?”

“Um, yes.  I had some trouble breathing for a moment earlier when we were walking here and I had also felt lightheaded earlier when you first said I looked like I might pass out.  This was … it was strange … Alex.  How I was feeling … it didn’t feel like me … experiencing these symptoms.  I felt like someone else was feeling it because though I had been worried about Kara, it wasn’t until after she started panicking that I felt myself panicking.  There’s, I don’t know how to explain it exactly, but that’s the closest I can get to explaining what it was I had felt.  Everything I felt almost seemed like an out of body experience, like seeing someone else experiencing it, but it’s really me who is feeling like that.  Does that make sense at all?”  Lena’s tone was hesitant and halting, as if relaying it as she felt it and remembered how it had felt earlier when it was happening but looking at herself, watching as it happened instead of experiencing it herself.  It was odd, indeed.

“I don’t really know what to make of all of this, but I do know that Supergirl might be the only one who knows what is going on.”  Alex was mindful of the fact that there were other agents milling about and couldn’t risk another incident like the betrayal of an agent like before.  Though most of them died in the ensuing fight, she could never trust anyone else completely other than Vazquez and Dr. Hamilton with Kara’s life and secret identity within the DEO, however unfortunate that had become.  She would not repeat the mistakes of the past and come to regret it, never again.  “All I can really tell you is that I’m sure Supergirl knows what is happening and that she holds the answers to this but maybe the survivors in Argo City might know more than Supergirl even.  It is … difficult for her to speak to her family after what happened there, the things she learned when she spoke to her mother when things fell apart here with Reign.”  Something in Alex’s expression told Lena that Kara was heartbroken about something.  All of the implications were there.  Of course, Lena knew her departure had been devastating, more than likely, but something that happened in Argo City had caused her unbearable grief.  Lena could see in the way Alex’s gaze shift, the sorrow in her own gaze as she remembered whatever it was that caused Kara to become so brokenhearted.

“Why don’t you go check on her now?  I’m, I’m fine to just stay here and rest maybe.”  Lena did look tired, exhausted even.  “I didn’t necessarily get much rest while my mother was holding me captive for the better part of a day.”  Rolling her eyes here.  Even Alex managed a small chuckle in agreement.  “She needs you more than I need you here right now anyways.  I’m sure it’d be a comfort to have you there with her when she finally wakes up, Alex.”

“Yeah, I’ll turn the lights down for you.  Do you need a better pillow?  Some blankets or anything?”

Lena shook her head.  “No, this is fine.  I’ve slept in worse conditions before.”  That sounded like a story for another time, so Alex merely nodded.

“Just let me know if you need anything or if you want someone more familiar to you, Winn can help you out.  He decided to stay here after … he came back from helping out The Legion of Superheroes because of Supergirl.  She needed him and I needed his help too, to find you.  I never told you this because at the time I was too upset over everything, but if I ever need help disappearing, you’re the first person I’m asking.  You’re far too brilliant at that.”  Lena could hear the undertone of affection coming from Alex, the slight teasing in her tone.  It made Lena’s heart melt from everything she had been feeling before.  The hurt that Alex never so much as hinted to Kara’s secret just melted away at the signs of true friendship and care Alex showed her.  Leaving all of her friends in the first place hadn’t been easy, not at all, but seeing this, the clear fondness Alex was displaying, made it even harder.

“Thank you, Alex, but that won’t be necessary.  I appreciate the kindness, truly, but this will be fine, I’m sure.”  Lena looked like she might blush.

“Always, Lena.”  That made the ache in Lena’s chest intensify, but it almost felt like a balm soothing her soul.  Alex had conviction in her voice, a warmth and strength so familiar to her.  Something she had lost when she left all those months ago, nearly a year and a half ago.  At the time she hadn’t felt that loss.  She had only felt relief and a sense of determination when she had left, working towards something new to help her heal.  This sense of accomplishment as everything fell into place the way she expected it had given her a peace she had not felt since she found out from Lex that Kara and all of her super friends had been lying to her, lying for Kara’s sake.  It had been from a place of hurt and inability to heal that Lena had left.  Never had any of it been for the sake of hurting Kara, Alex, Winn, J’onn, James and Maggie, though briefly.  She had cut them all off though, and that was a mistake.  Lena had never had these kinds of people in her life before, true friends.  People who would defend her over and over again after they got to know her, though Kara defended her the first time they met in contrast.  Kara … she was … is the only person who had ever given her the benefit of a doubt after they had spoken for the first time when Clark had gone to interview her.  And she had defended her vehemently, she was sure, against everyone else’s judgements about her character, her morals.

Lena sighed as she relaxed back into the bed, her thoughts running through her head would give her no rest.  Instead, to soothe herself, she recalled the way Kara’s voice sounded when she sang, the voice of an angel came to mind.  The memory of that voice singing to her soothe her to sleep so quickly she didn’t even remember falling asleep.

“So, how is Kara?  What happened?”  Alex almost started biting on her nails after coming over to discuss Kara’s health.  It was irritating that she had had some things to do before she could come over and check on Kara’s health earlier.  Of course, being director meant she had duties to perform, but paperwork and mission sanctions should be able to wait until she’d had a chance to check her sister over and make sure she was going to die in a ditch somewhere.  Is that too hard to ask for, really?  Granted she was in a sunbed at the moment and not in a ditch while it was pitch black outside, but still.  The principle still stands.  After nearly two hours of grueling paperwork and reading over documents carefully before signing, Alex said to hell with it and went to check on Kara and Dr. Hamilton’s progress.

“Supergirl, she experienced a panic attack, it seems, but there was something else going on that caused it.  I don’t think it was because of what happened before she came in earlier this week.  The cause is something only Supergirl can tell us, but I did take her x-ray again earlier and it looks like the flower is much more advanced since we last saw it.  Before it had been steady but whatever happened caused it to advance.  Again, it’s something only she can tell us.”  Dr. Hamilton looked grave.  “I’m not sure how long she has exactly.  There isn’t any medicine I have that can keep it from growing, that can keep it from blooming.  Perhaps … should we … contact Argo City and ask Alura for help in this matter?  It is her daughter’s life at stake here.”  Dr. Hamilton didn’t look optimistic about what could be done to help Kara at this point.  Alex wasn’t sure she had any optimism for it either.  This wasn’t an ideal situation and Kara wasn’t exactly that forthcoming with answers about this part of it.  It made things so much harder and more complicated than it had any right to be.

“We can only wait for her to wake up then?”

“I’m afraid so, Alex.  There’s nothing more I can do for her.  Having her in the sunbed does seem to abate some of the symptoms she’s experiencing but not enough, not nearly enough, Alex.”

Alex sighed heavily, “that’s what I’m afraid of.”

Before either woman could comment on it more, Kara woke up again, less groggily and seemingly more aware of her surroundings now.  “Was it a dream, or was Lena really here earlier?” Kara asked, voice croaky and hoarse.

“Yeah, she was here, holding you.  She’s resting now though because she was exhausted and hadn’t really slept since she was, you know, being held captive by her mother and that doesn’t really make for a good night’s rest for anyone in her situation.”  Alex looked Kara over critically as she spoke.  “How are you feeling?  Any better?”

Kara shrugged listlessly, like the life and energy had just drained out of her.  “I’m fine, I guess.  Well, as fine as I can be anyways.”

Alex hesitated.  “Do you want me to have Lena come over and talk to you as soon as she wakes up, maybe?  It’s been a few hours since I left her alone.”

When Kara looked over to Alex to respond, she saw and heard a familiar person standing in the doorway, “I don’t think you need to do that because she’s right there,” Kara said, nodding behind them.

There Lena was, looking ashen and more than likely had overheard part of what Alex and Dr. Hamilton had been discussing about Kara’s health.  “Perhaps we should leave you two alone to talk privately,” Dr. Hamilton said, gently grabbing Alex’s arm and leading her away, having noticed the intense looks Kara and Lena were giving each other, something she thought they needed.  “Let us know if you need anything when you’re done … talking.”  She had no illusions that there might be some yelling and possibly some tears.  Dr. Hamilton made sure that the doors were closed and even the windows were dimmed so no one could see anything inside.  She even activated the sound proofing technology that Winn had been developing so that their voices weren’t distinct and everything would sound like murmuring.  That was, of course, a secret project that no one knew about other than Alex, Winn, and Dr. Hamilton herself. 

Alex looked worried, but didn’t disagree that they needed to talk to each other in private.  Why couldn’t anything be simple anymore?  She missed the quiet days of game night, happiness, and lightness before Lex came in and ruined everything nearly two years ago.  Kara would have told Lena when she was ready and Lena would have been able to hear it from Kara rather than from Lex.  It would have changed everything, she was sure of it.  Things would have … could have … might have been easier now.  Only those two could salvage this.

Kara was quietly gazing at Lena, longing apparent in her eyes as she looked at Lena with such heartbreak in her eyes.  Lena didn’t know how she felt, what she looked like right now.  There were burning questions she wanted, no, needed answers to.  “Is it true, Kara?” she asked, her gaze burning into Kara’s weakened form lying on a sunbed that made her look so, so small.  “Are you … dying?” Lena’s voice waivered and trembled over the single word that held so much power.

Eyes squeezed shut tightly, Kara answered without looking at Lena even after she opened her eyes.  “Yes, I am dying.”

Lena let out a sharp gasp at that.  She didn’t know what to feel other than an overwhelming sense of hurt over hearing that.  “How, how can this be happening.  You’re … you’re Supergirl.”  Kara flinched when Lena said it and Lena noticed right away, of course she noticed it.  “There’s … you shouldn’t be able to be killed by anything but Kryptonite, right?”

“Well, that’s not true.  There are a … few different ways to kill me.  Kryptonite is just the most immediate and easiest way to kill me.”

A storm raged in Lena’s eyes.  “It started two years ago, just about, didn’t it, Kara?  Why didn’t you come to me then, Kara?  No, why didn’t you come to me at all when you started getting weaker?”

“I promised to never come near you again, didn’t I?  That was the last thing I promised you, after all.  And I keep my promises, Lena.”  Kara didn’t sound angry.  She looked exhausted and so ill.  Lena could see how small Kara truly was this way.  She was achingly thin compared to before, her skin was paler than she had ever seen her, and her eyes … she looked like she hadn’t slept well in a long time.  Lena swallowed hard at the truth behind Kara’s words, felt nausea coming back to her in droves at the truth in Kara’s words because those were the last words she had spoken to Lena.  I promise.  And in this very moment it pierced through Lena’s heart like an arrow, like a bullet wound, and it felt like a hot iron poker in her skin, blood oozing from the wounds she felt over what she had done.  She had caused this.  “Lena, it’s not your fault I’m this way.  No, it’s my fault.”

“What?  Kara, no, this is … it has to be my fault.  I know you didn’t just start becoming ill for no apparent reason.  Was it Harun-El or … something else that happened that caused this?  Did my mother … was she lying about not having anything to do with this, Kara?”  Lena was looking for a reason frantically as her mind raced with the possibilities.

“Stop, no.  No, Lena.  It wasn’t Harun-El, it wasn’t your mother, none of that.  I …” Kara looked away for the first time since they started talking and it made Lena’s throat close up with nerves.  Whatever Kara was going to say next … it was definitely important.  Lena could tell that she wouldn’t like whatever Kara said next, a feeling in her gut told her she wouldn’t like it.  “I am … dying, but it’s not any of the reasons I’m sure you’re thinking about in that beautiful brain of yours.  The reason is my Kryptonian biology and how we bond with the person who is our soulmate for life and it has nothing to do with anything you did before.”  Kara’s eyes were glistening.  “When I was with Mon-El,” Lena instinctively felt herself cringe at the mention of the supposed ‘Mike, of the interns’, “I was only running away from how I felt about someone, my bond mate.”  Kara clammed up, couldn’t admit to Lena that it was her, had always been her and would always be her.  “Enough about that though.  Not telling you my secret, it was the hardest thing I had to do.  Every time someone said something about you, I told them that it wasn’t you.  My friends, my family, they kept giving me reasons not to tell you, but I shouldn’t have listened!”  Kara’s voice waivered as tears finally started falling, her breath hitched as she took a deep breath to continue, her voice sounding wet with her tears.  “I should have said, ‘Lena is trustworthy and I am going to tell her whether I have to get NDAs signed, whether you approve or not, because I love her and I trust her with my life, with everything I am and I don’t care what you think.  She is my best friend and she knows me, knows everything about me, except for this.  She is my perfect partner at our game nights, but I still can’t tell her about this one thing, just this one big thing.’  I … I should have done that, all of that.”  Kara sobbed again, her voice thick, “but I didn’t and that is why we’re here now, isn’t it?”

Lena felt like she couldn’t breathe.  It was like she got sucker punched in the stomach.  “Why didn’t you tell me any of this before Lex did, Kara?  Things could have been better than this, we could have … there’s so much that would have been different but this is where we are now.”

“Everyone just, they kept telling me reasons why I couldn’t be sure whether you were good or not despite you always showing them, always giving them proof, but nothing was ever enough.  You went behind your mother’s back, you saved me when your mother left me on Rhea’s ship, you helped defeat the Daxamite invaders, you saved Sam from Reign, and still nothing was ever enough.  You were always enough for me, but they just couldn’t see past Luthor, not really anyways.  It took a long time for James to come around, but Alex and Winn, they started seeing you and realizing that you’re not like your family first after I did.  Winn … his parents were murderers and he still couldn’t see it until later.  Alex … she was just trying to protect me, but she came around when she realized that I was … that I love you so much, that you are such a good person and she started to see why I feel the way I do about you, Lena, why I trust you so much.  She was … before Lex told you, she was trying to help me build up the courage to tell you and it was around the time you killed him that I wanted to tell you but then it had been too late, I guess.  Because you decided to leave and I couldn’t make you stay if you didn’t want me to be there and so I didn’t tell you even when I started having … symptoms.”

It was a hard pill to swallow, the realization that Kara would have died without Lena ever knowing.  Kara was willing to do that.  “Were you really trying to kill yourself for the last three months?”

Kara, again, looked away.  “I … wasn’t trying to die, I was just willing to not fight for my life if it came down to it.  Had anyone been strong enough to kill me … I would have been okay with letting myself go.”

A sob escaped Lena and Kara was on her feet and had Lena’s shaking frame within her arms immediately at the sound.  Lena’s entire body shook with the force of her sobs and Kara felt her hot tears trailing down her cheeks in rivulets.  Everything hurt.  But something within Kara felt better, like Lena’s soul was reaching out to her own and some of the tear was mending.  Not truly, but it was just a little bit easier to breathe.

“How am I supposed to live without you in my life like that, Kara?” Lena asked in between her hiccupping breath, her whole body wracked with painful, sobbing breaths as she attempted to speak in between.  Kara’s heart broke for Lena, but she kept her strong, warm frame around Lena.  “You should have told me, Kara, no matter what you promised me.  I would have … I didn’t want you dead, Kara.  Never.  I just … I needed time, I needed space, I just needed to heal, not for you to die and never tell me.”

“Lena, I’m so sorry.”

“No, stop it, Kara.  I’m sorry.”  And they stayed like that for what felt like hours until Lena fell asleep in Kara’s arms and Kara held her, never letting go.

The Reason

Lena hadn’t slept this well in over a year and it felt like heaven waking up from a good rest.  A quick glance at the clock showed that it was just after ten in the morning and Lena had never slept passed eight since she became LCorp’s CEO.  It was strange, really.  There felt like a time when Lena had been fine on her own before she met Kara because being alone meant not having to worry about anyone taking advantage of her or hurting her in anyway.  It was a relief, being alone.  She didn’t have to wonder where she went wrong, what she did to cause someone to use her, and she certainly didn’t have to worry about maintaining friendships at all.  Things had been simpler then, easier.  She didn’t have to worry about missing lunch with her friends, didn’t have to wonder if someone just wanted something from her, whether they were being genuine or not, she didn’t have any of these worries.  Not until Kara came into her life like someone calling for help and realizing that she had been lonely in spite of the ease it brought to her.  Truly, lonely.

There was someone to talk to, to share in her worries and her excitement, to feel a sense of belonging with, and to smile and laugh with over anything and everything and that had meant everything.  She had someone who was genuine and kind, who would tell her she was brilliant and … good.  That had taken some getting used to every time it came out of Kara’s mouth as both Kara Danvers and as Supergirl.  Perhaps that is why it hurt so much more when everything had come crashing down, the illusion of someone who trusted her had gone away, had turned into shattered glass.  Lena was absolutely devasted.  Distraught, despondent, overcome with her despair.  Her truest friend, someone she loved dearly, had betrayed her in the worst way possible and Lena had felt herself break, like she had splintered into two different parts of herself with one from before Lex had twisted everything into the darkness that he had embodied and the part of herself now.  It was hard to know that something had changed drastically, but they hadn’t gotten to that part yet.  Kara was, she needed to heal.  That had to take precedence over everything else.  She was weaker than she should be and Lena wanted to know why, but more than anything, she wanted to help her.

All of these thoughts plagued her.  Lena wanted to know.  She needed to know.  Kara wasn’t supposed to be like this, was supposed to be indestructible.  Whatever happened to her … Lena needed to find out for herself.  If only Kara would tell her so she wouldn’t have to dig around for answers, grasp at straws.

With a start, Lena sat up and realized that she was in Kara’s bed now and not at the DEO.  She wondered when that had happened.  The last thing she remembered was being at the DEO and crying horribly in Kara’s arms.  And maybe feeling herself calm in those strong, warm arms.  She must have fallen asleep in Kara’s arms and then Kara must have brought her here.  Looking around, Lena didn’t see Kara anywhere and the other side of the bed looked completely untouched, the bed smoothed out on the other side with no signs of anyone having slept with her.  In the bed next to her.  Lena shook those thoughts away and got up to look for Kara only to find no signs of her anywhere and her heart started to pound in her chest, beating like crazy.  Where was Kara now?

Having heard Lena’s heartbeat spike up, Kara came flying through her window, opening it to make sure she didn’t have to replace it … well, again.  “What’s wrong?  I heard your heartbeat going crazy just now!”

At the sight of Kara standing in her civilian clothes, Lena came running up to Kara only to pull her into an anxiety filled hug that immediately calmed her racing heart and her nerves.  “I just … I woke up and I couldn’t see you anywhere and I just needed to know that you were okay.”

Kara gave Lena a small smile that warmed Lena’s heart immensely, “I left a note but you must not have seen it.”  Lena didn’t even feel embarrassed by her panic and Kara’s need to soothe her.  She needed this, had missed this.  “You’re okay.  I’m okay.  Everything is fine now.”  Kara assured her gently.

“But is it really?  You never told me what it was that was harming you yesterday.  Not once.  Kara, I just … I need to know,” Lena pulled back to look Kara in the eyes, “what is causing your illness?  I had so many conjectures, but nothing seemed right the more I thought about it and I still haven’t the faintest clue.  You’re not becoming human, you’re not completely weak, you still have your powers, but certain things can harm you now, and it seems like you have some kind of internal ailment because you were coughing up … blood and flower petals last week when my mother captured you.”  Kara remained silent throughout all of Lena’s observations.  “Will you please tell me what is happening to you?  Alex told me that only you can tell me, Kara.”

With a small nod, Kara gestured to her couch, something she had slept on now that Lena had taken the time to notice because she was no longer in her panic mode.  Kara had neatly folded the blankets and set her pillow on top of the one end.  Though they sat on the same cushion, Kara was as far away as she could be without sitting on her blankets and pillows and Lena felt hurt that Kara left that much space between them, whereas before they always sat so close their bodies touched everywhere.  Lena shook her head as Kara started haltingly, “right, so, um … this isn’t easy to talk about.  At all.  It’s something that only happens on Krypton, though Daxamites had a similar program for searching out mates, though they also allowed er, well, looser definitions for those interested in having someone else.  That is to say, mates were matches made for political gain and those who weren’t interested didn’t have to necessarily remain faithful and nobody really cared.  Daxamites also have it easier than we do, though some truly cared about their mates and didn’t have problems with bonding with a Kryptonian.  We do not have that outlook so we have exclusively monogamous relationships with others.  Some of us even had bonds with those outside of our galaxy, from neighboring planets other than Daxam.”

Lena nodded patiently and laughed a little at that, remembering Kara recounting the horror of finding Mon-El and Eve having a tryst at work.  “Mike, of the interns, was certainly an example of that, I’m sure.”  Kara smiled at that, but didn’t laugh, something that struck Lena in the gut.  She could see what Alex meant by how subdued and muted Kara had become since Lena left and an ache in her heart throbbed for a moment as she met Kara’s gaze.

“Right, just like that.  But anyways, our programming matrix makes it so that we know who our intended mate is through a special bond.  It is called a soul bond because the bond is so deep and connective that even our souls are bound together in this way.  Through this bond we can experience everything they are experience in a muted way … well, not muted, not really.  More like … we can feel what they are feeling when they feel something very strongly.  Like, if I were to have devastating news, the person I am bonded to will feel a sort of sympathetic response somewhat similar to fight or flight as humans have named it.  But instead of fight or flight the person I am bound to can sense what it is I am feeling and reciprocate, they can feel it too, as if they are there with me when something like that happens and it is due to our link, our bond.  It is through this bond that I have become weakened because I caused a rift, a tear, in the very bond that is between the one I love and me through betrayal, through breaking our trust.  There is a program written within the matrix that helps us find this person, the one person who is our soulmate, our bond mate for life.  A single look and we know who this person is.  Though I found my bond mate, my soul bond with them has had a severe fracture because of something that happened between us and I’m not sure if I can heal from what happened, if we can ever heal from what caused the fractures.”

“Kara, who is this bond mate of yours?  It wasn’t … Mon-El, was it?”  Lena sounded almost nervous about that.  And Kara almost wanted to laugh.  It was that ridiculous to her.

“No, it wasn’t him.  He had merely been an attempt to go against my programming because I was running away from my feelings, from this person.”  Kara sighed, looking away.  “I knew who it was as soon as I met them.  The first moment we met, when our gazes landed on each other … I knew.  And it was a magical feeling.  That moment was everything I had heard growing up, everything I had dreamed of, because they were the most beautiful person I had ever seen in my life.  A voice so sweet asked me for my name and I stuttered, just like my heart did in my chest.  It was something I had genuinely never felt before in my life.  And that terrified me.”  Kara clenched her jaw, the muscle moving in an attractive way that made Lena glance at it momentarily.

“Will you tell me who it is, Kara?  You don’t have to if you don’t want to, but I’d like to know so I can help you with this, Kara.”  Lena seemed open but at the same time, Kara could hear the note of curiosity in her voice.  There was no doubt in Kara’s mind that she was also testing her theory about who this person was, the way she was asking, it seemed like she might already know the answer anyways.

“I will, but I, there’s more you need to know.”

“Of course, Kara, take your time.”

Taking another deep breath to calm herself, Kara nodded and continued slowly.  “Our bond isn’t an immediate connection that becomes solidified after just moments of knowing each other.  In the cases in which someone has bonded with another species or with someone on a neighboring planet without the same kind of programming, things happen more slowly.  The bond takes time to become strong enough to connect the two individuals in a series of checkpoints in their relationship, milestones.  A first date, a first kiss, getting to know each other, falling in love, and becoming … intimate.”  Kara blushed slightly there.  “Those are all points in which they both say that they want this verbally, emotionally, and physically at times and every yes at these milestones causes the bond between souls to grow.  In this way, they are confirming their feelings, their emotions, their love … for each other.  There are some who wait a long time for anything intimate physically, and in those cases, other things can make the bond grow because love is not just a physical expression of feelings, it is also the actions of the other person.  Like going against one’s family and their beliefs for the other person, saving each other from certain harm, being willing to put yourself in harm’s way for your soul bond.  It’s things like that that makes this bond so strong, so powerful.  But with everything there is a consequence, a need for balance in the relationship.  One of the strongest things about the bond, is having trust for one another.  Should one half of the soul bond cause something in the trust to shatter or break entirely, it can kill the one who caused it eventually.”

Lena felt herself shaking.  It was a terrifying prospect, that Kara was dying because of this.  “And … you caused this by breaking their trust … in you?”

“Yes.  I did, Lena.”

The feeling of dread and trepidation Lena felt as Kara continued increased at the look on Kara’s face.  “And, um, what is it that kills them, how does it kill them exactly?”

“You know the flower petals I keep coughing up with blood by now, Lena,” with Lena’s nod Kara continued sadly, “it is this very flower blooming within my chest, right next to my heart, that is killing me.  Very slowly, but surely.  I am … dying.”  Kara’s chest felt tight at the moment and perhaps it was because she knew she was going to die because there was no way Lena reciprocated her feelings anymore after everything happened.  “The flower was a mere seed the moment our trust broke, thus causing breaks within our bond and fracturing my soul in the moment they renounced me, renounced our bond.  And since that moment, this flower has been growing steadily within my heart, becoming dangerous and capable of killing me.  I have been coughing up flowers for a long time now, several months actually.  And I’m not sure how much longer until it fully blooms and kills me.  Honestly, I know I don’t have much longer now, Lena.  I don’t want them to want me out of pity, out of fear I will die.  All I can say is … she is the most beautiful person I have ever met, her soul is pure and kind, just like her, and I will never love anyone the way I love her.”

Lena’s fear increased tenfold.  “Kara, you have to tell her about this, regardless of her decision.  Whether it sways her choice, she deserves to know how you feel.  It’s only right for her to make an informed decision.”

“There is … there is a way for me to remove my soul bond, however there are known risks and cases of death because the soul bond was too strong, their love and attachment too great.  Only the Kryptonian died, but their love was driven mad with grief, and they died, too.  I might … I might take the risk because I don’t think that she would, that she will go crazy.  She must hate me after I betrayed her trust, Lena.  There’s no way she cares for me anymore.  She left me a long time ago and I haven’t really seen her since.”  It was true in a way.  The Lena sitting with her now was not the same Lena she once knew.  Before there was an ease between them, a familiarity and comfortableness in which they relished how close they were to each other.  When they sat together, they were always pressed together like couples even though they never formally dated each other before.  That sense of intimacy was gone.  Kara could tell that it was.  Lena had made no move to touch her since they hugged earlier.  And it had only been because she was worried about her dying.  If she wasn’t dying … would Lena have ever returned.

Kara realized that Lena had been staring at her when she became lost in thought and shook herself off mentally.  “Where did you go just now?”

“It’s nothing.  Just thinking about how she must hate me now because the bond has not repaired even the slightest bit, though I am decidedly less … vulnerable.  I think it’s just spending so long in the sunbed.  Her returning my feelings … I don’t think that is going to happen any time soon.  Maybe, never.”

“Well, why don’t you talk to her and build up your relationship with her again, Kara?”

A sad, tired smile appeared on Kara’s face and Lena hated it.  It reeked of hopelessness and misery.  “She made sure I knew that she wasn’t ready to see me again after how I betrayed her trust and we hadn’t seen each other since that day.  It has been several long months.”  Over a year, Kara thought to herself privately.  “When I saw her last, I told her that I was going to wait for her, but it hasn’t gone anywhere because she has not contacted me since.  No email, no text, no visits, and she made sure to let her work know that I am not allowed to bother her there.  I only tried going there to see her one time.  That one time was enough to know she doesn’t want me near her anymore.”

Every word out of Kara’s mouth, but not a name, not confirmation.  Maybe Kara wasn’t talking about her after all.  Something in Lena’s heart felt a twinge at the very thought and somehow Lena thought it had to be her.  Why else wouldn’t Kara say a name, but had just revealed a gender at the least.  “Kara, do you not want to tell me who she is because you have only been saying that she is a female but nothing else about her identity.”  Lena swallowed passed the lump in her throat, “if you just don’t want to tell me … I understand completely.”

“Lena, I just … I don’t want you to try to fix this by confronting her.”  And that threw Lena off entirely.  Maybe it wasn’t her after all.  Kara had said she hadn’t been in contact with this person for some time.  Everything else Kara had said sounded like it lined up with them though, with what happened nearly two years ago.  Something wasn’t adding up.  “She is … she is very smart, kind, too kind, and it’s so hard for her to trust other people.  I just … I kind of love that about her, that she wants people to earn her trust like that.  The people she does trust … they’re so lucky to have her friendship, kindness, and love.  It’s so hard for her to let her guard down, but when she does, it’s such a beautiful thing, truly.”  A pained, melancholic smile graced Kara’s features as she thought back to that horrifying confrontation two years ago.  “I lost that with her and I’m afraid I will never have it again.  She will never trust me or love me.  Not like she used to, anyways.”

“So, you’re just going to give up on her, Kara?  Is that it then?  Never tell her, never get better, and just … die?  Is that how it’s going to go, Kara?”  Lena’s heartrate spiked with her emotional distress and her breathing hitched for a moment.  “You’re just going to let yourself die without ever telling her how you really feel, Kara?  And you’re going to leave me all alone.  I’m not sure I’d survive not having you in my life permanently like that.”

Again, that said smile appeared and Lena hated that smile more than anything in the world.  “You were doing just fine without me for nearly a year and a half Lena, so, I think you’ll be okay when I’m gone.”

“When, not if …” Lena’s voice cracked, “you’re not going to give your soul bond a chance to make things right between the two of you?”

Kara looked tired, exhausted.  “There’s nothing to fix.  I’m sure that she doesn’t need me, doesn’t want me anymore, Lena.  She didn’t need me for more than three months now, hasn’t wanted or needed me from long before that even.  When we had our falling out … it was the worst pain I had ever left in my life, Lena.  Things were already bad before that point, though.  She had withdrawn for a few weeks and I tried to give her space.  One day, a few weeks later, she finally snapped and told me that she didn’t want me to be her friend anymore because I went behind her back to do something I thought would protect her.  She clearly didn’t see it the same way and told me that I was just a liar, always lying to her in the name of protecting her and she was sick of me lying.”  A lump formed in Lena’s throat.  Kara wasn’t talking about the day she told her she never wanted to see her again.  No, this sounded more like the day Lena had confronted her about going behind her back and having James snoop around when Lena had created Kryptonite in an attempt to deal with Reign’s strength.  “And then she told me we weren’t friends anymore and that she didn’t want me anywhere near her again for any reason.”

Lena wished she didn’t need to do this, but she had to know and she had every right to know.  “Kara, is it me?”

Looking passed Lena, Kara refused to meet Lena’s gaze straight on, “is what you?”

Letting out a sigh, Lena tried again, “am I your soul bond?”

“Why would it be you, Lena?”

“Kara, look me in the eyes and tell me your soul bond isn’t me.”

“I can’t do that.”  Kara still wouldn’t look at Lena.

“Why don’t you want me to know that you’re my soul bond?”  Kara didn’t say anything, didn’t look like she’d heard Lena.  “Is something wrong?”  Still, she remained silent.  “Kara?”  In the blink of an eye Kara was gone, but Lena could hear the sound of someone coughing and she followed the sound to the bathroom where bloody and flower petals just spewed from Kara’s mouth.  The sight was gruesome and bloody like a murder scene in a tv show.  “Oh, my lord, Kara!”  Lena immediately held onto Kara’s arm to support her as she continued to cough up blood.  After several minutes, it finally ebbed away to nothing as Kara’s body sagged against the sink.  “Kara, can you walk?”  A nod.  “Come on.”  Lena lead Kara over to her bedroom where the sunlight was filtering through her large windows.  They sat in silence for a long time, however it wasn’t uncomfortable.  Many moments in their friendship they had sat in a comfortable silence, able to just find comfort in each other’s presence and somehow that hadn’t changed.  But Lena felt conflicted.  “Is it so bad that I am your bond mate?”  Lena sounded so insecure that Kara felt a surge of protectiveness overcome her and fill her with compassion.

“No, Lena, that’s not why I didn’t want to tell you about being soulmates!”  Kara interjected vehemently, sounding insulted by the very idea itself.  That brought Lena some measure of comfort at least and eased her worries slightly.

“Then why … I mean, you said that part of it was because you didn’t want to influence my decision … but you’re dying!  How can I not … when you might?”  Lena couldn’t finish either sentence because it all ended with Kara dead.

“And this is why I didn’t want you to know, the reason, well the main one, is because you feel guilty, you feel like it’s your fault and every time something happens you just blame yourself because of how other people have treated you in life.  Somehow, it’s always your fault and this isn’t you fault.  At all.  Seriously, Lena.  It isn’t.”  Lena kept shaking her head, doubtful that she didn’t have at least something to do with Kara’s condition.  “Why don’t you believe me, Lena?”

“Because why else would you hide it and why else would you be this way now?  You said it’s because you’re Kryptonian but you also said that it’s because I stopped trusting you, no matter that I felt wholly betrayed by you not telling me you’re Supergirl after three years of friendship.  It’s still my fault.  That tear, that fracture you were talking about in the soul bond, that’s my fault, right?  It has to do with my lack of trust for you and that’s how it shattered our bond.  Tell me I’m wrong.”  Lena was convinced that it was her fault, always shouldering the blame.

“You are wrong, Lena.  Not in the way you think.  Of course, because I betrayed your trust, I caused a rift in our bond.  Not the other way around, Lena.  So, I’m at fault.  Not you.  In fact, despite you not feeling as strongly about it as you did when it first occurred, our bond hasn’t mended because of me.  When I was kidnapped by Eve, that very day, I was considering a procedure in Argo City, one that would … heal me of this affliction, however, to devastating consequences more often than not.  It also has very serious side effects if the one removing the bond survives the ordeal.  That was … I was ready to … J’onn would have acted as me while I was away.  Possibly have me die in battle for appearances’ sake if I died from it.”  When Kara spoke about the bond, she was emotional, but as soon as she was talking about her death, she was stoic again, speaking as if she was talking about the weather and not her death.  Lena felt pain bloom within her own chest.

“Kara, does that mean you are actively fighting our bond?  You don’t want to be bonded to me?”  Lena felt a swirling mix of emotions as she waited for Kara to answer.

“Yes and no, to be honest.  I don’t want to be a burden to you, I want to set you free of this bond.  But at the same time, I want you, I want to be with you, so much.  I guess you can say I am conflicted.  The desire to be with you is quite strong, but the part of me that wants you to be happy is stronger and that part of me is rejecting this bond because you are … you can have anyone you want and I know that you don’t want me in your life, not anymore.”  Hurt and sorrow took over Kara’s features again and Lena hated it.  She caused this, no matter what Kara said.  Lena left and Kara started to fall apart.

“What if I … what if I didn’t hate you back then, what if I don’t hate you now, Kara?  Is that … does that change your mind or anything?”

Kara looked at Lena sharply, gauging the truthfulness of her words, trying to see if she saw anything that resembled pity in her gaze.  The way Lena was looking at her now, it didn’t seem like she was lying or being anything less than truthful in the least.  Lena was looking at Kara so openly she didn’t know what to do with herself, having not seen that expression on Lena’s face in nearly two whole years.  Bile rose to Kara’s throat as she thought back to the last time Lena had directed her gaze at Kara before she had become sick and it was all anger and coldness.  She felt a build-up and just barely managed to find Lena’s trashcan before a few petals came up.  Lena was there in an instant, rubbing her back soothingly.

After a few more minutes, it stopped and Kara quickly zipped out of the room to rinse her mouth out before reappearing after a few seconds.  “I’m fine, I swear.”

Lena looked at Kara dubiously, “are you sure you’re okay?  You coughing up flower petals and throwing up blood isn’t exactly reassuring me Kara.”

Blinking her eyes to try to hold back tears, Kara responded haltingly.  “Why don’t you hate me, Lena?  You said you didn’t hate me then, but you told me you hated me.”  Kara was confused about how Lena couldn’t possibly hate her.

“I … did resent you at the time, but I didn’t really hate you.  Not that it makes it any better, but I was just trying to hurt you the way you hurt me.  I wanted you to know that I was being serious, that I didn’t want to be near you for just a while.  And when we hadn’t spoken for some time, I thought maybe you didn’t want to talk to me anymore either.  Jess was a godsend and Sam, Sam helped me through a lot when I went to Metropolis for a business trip to check up on LCorp there a few months after our … fight.  She talked through a lot of things with me and helped me work passed my anger, but I was still in grief.  When I came back, Jess made an off-hand comment about me going away for a bit.  And then that thought, the very idea, consumed as I thought about the possibilities.  It sounded amazing, being able to just … get away and still be able to run LCorp from a distance.  And an added bonus was being able to hang up work meetings with white old men who think they know better than I do.  Having spotty wi-fi to blame was the best part because they couldn’t tell me I was lying without any proof.”

“So, you don’t hate me now?”  Kara asked, her voice breathless and just a little hopeful.

“No, I don’t hate you, Kara.  I am still angry and confused, but I’m not … I never hated you.”  The hope in Kara’s face was too much.  Lena stumbled, “and we can work towards being friends again.”  As soon as the words left Lena’s mouth, Kara’s hope shattered and her face fell and Lena felt like an idiot for putting that look on Kara’s face.  She tried to say something but Kara was already getting up and making excuses.

“Right, um, I’m just going to go and um, I should go, Lena, leave you to uh, yeah.”  Kara was walking out of her own bedroom door when Lena grabbed her arm, but Kara just gently slid her arm out of Lena’s hold even as Lena tried to protest and bring her back in.

“You’re at your apartment Kara, where are you going to go now?”  Lena asked.

“I’m just going to go um, just go somewhere else for a bit, Lena.  I’m fine.  Totally fine.  Yup, okay?”  And Kara didn’t stop her stride for even a moment and just walked over to her window and jumped out, much to Lena’s horror.  Of course, she knew that Kara was Supergirl now, but her heart beat out of its chest at the sight even as she heard Kara break the sound barrier just moments later.  The sight of her former best friend and someone she still cared about dearly jumping out of a window several stories high wasn’t any less frightening at all.  Lena let herself calm down and wondered what the hell she was going to do now.  Kara more than likely would not return to her apartment until Lena was gone.  Lena knew all of her things had been set up at the penthouse apartment Jess had found for her before she even landed in National City and everything had been taken care of by movers and such things had all been taken care of.  Her heart was in turmoil and she felt something stirring in her own chest.  She wondered if it was this bond Kara had been talking about.

Lena had more answers now than she had before they talked, but now she had even more questions that Kara wasn’t likely to answer anytime soon.  Not after her swift departure earlier.  When had her life become so utterly complicated and messy.  She had come back to … to what?  Get answers?  To find Kara and ask her what was going on?  Maybe to get the one person who had made her feel more whole than she had her entire life and had left a gap so large no one else could fill it?  To reconnect with all of the friends she had left behind in National City?  What was it she was trying to find here so badly she came without any plans?  Kara was, of course, a large concern because she was … vulnerable?  Weakened unexpectedly?  Lena still didn’t quite understand the intricacies of Kara’s affliction.  Of course, she knew that her heart had a flower growing right next to it, not that Kara had explained much about that part itself really.  Just that the cause was the fracture of their relationship.  They had been on their way to one before everything … well, maybe they had been.  Right now, Lena just wanted to save her ex best friend from certain death.

She stood there for a long time, not sure where to go.  Lena looked down at her watch and noted that it was now just pushing noon and knew she needed to do something.  Maybe get some lunch?  Lena was now in a city she felt like she no longer knew because things were different now.  She hadn’t been here in over a year.  Kara … her heart ached at the very thought of the look on Kara’s face when Lena had said they could work towards being friends again.  Her chest felt tight with emotion.

Maybe Kara just needed some time.
