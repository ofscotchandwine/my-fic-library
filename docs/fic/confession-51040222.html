    Angelica is a herb originally from the Nordic countries but over time it spread throughout Europe. It has many uses in magical lore, but the most common is healing, purification, and protection.

    Mugwort is another common herb, though it has hallucinagin properties, and it's uses are also oriented around protection and purification.

    Finally, Fennel is a common herb used mostly as spice, but also has a shit ton of uses in magical practices ranging from protection and purification to courage to fertility.

    I chose the three plants mostly due to their relation to purification and protection, but fennel in particular with how it can boost courage and confidence seemed like a good third to round out the trio.
