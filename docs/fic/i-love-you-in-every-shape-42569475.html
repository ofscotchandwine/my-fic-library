Meow!

 

“Where are you?I’m shortsighted.The glasses don’t work that well sometimes.”She lifted a throw pillow on the couch for inspection.She scanned all round her living room.It was close.She dropped down to the floor, looking under the couch.

 

When she straightened, a black tiny head appeared behind the easel by the window.Green orbs stared at her.Defiant yet reserved.Curious and cautious.Narrow pupils indicated that the owner was fearful.They looked at each other for so long.A complete conversation seemed to be conducted.Two animals observed one another.

 

“Hi.”She made it official.“I’m Kara.”She didn’t know why she said that and was even clueless about why she offered a hand to shake.Small head became a full body.Careful, slow, elegant.Paws touched the sill one by one.Tail kept perfect balance.The cat was now watching her awkward palm.They actually still had a bit of distance between them since Kara was near the couch and the cat stood next to the window.“Lucky that I forgot to close the window, right?You’re all soaked.Let me get you a towel.”She jumped up and so did the cat.“SORRY!Sorry.”She held both palms up.The cat’s eyes fixed on her open hands.“Real quick, promise!”She went backward to her bedroom and turned to accelerated the process of procuring a towel, crossing her fingers that the black cat didn’t leave.

 

She sighed in relief when she came back to find the cat still froze on the spot.“I don’t think you’ll want a stranger to dry you so…I’m just gonna leave it here.”She spread the towel on top of a cabinet.A neutral ground for both of them and plenty of space for the cat to work.Backing away to her previous seat on the couch.“I’ll give you some privacy.”She turned around, still not knowing why she treated the cat this way.She heard some commotions but that was it.She didn’t know how long she had to wait.The cat decided for them, landing on the love seat in front of her.Tail curled in way for greeting.“Feeling better?”She swore the cat just nodded.“Great.I’m gonna ask you a rather personal and a bit invasive question, it’s like asking a human their age but I don’t want to call you an ‘it’ in my head so…, are you a boy or a girl?”The cat huffed.She was about to apologize when the cat twisted the body to make a very…seductive move?At least, that was what Kara perceived.She lowered the line of sight as the cat spun to look at her over the black tiny shoulder.A quick check-over learned from her days in animal shelter.“O-Okay, a girl.Thank you and nice to meet you.”She blushed for no reason.

 

“Are you lost or did you just need a place to wait the rain out?”The cat mewled at the pouring rain outside.“Sure.It’s nice to have some company if you don’t mind it’s me.”The cat cast a glance at her like a ‘fine’.“Okay.Do you want anything to eat?”She backtracked.“But I don’t have cat-appropriate food.What about water or milk?Meow once for water, two for milk.”She looked at the cat expectantly.The cat didn’t make a sound at first.She began to curse her own silliness when two meows flew across the room.“Coming right up!”She perked up and remembered not to cause big movement.“I only have whole milk.Would it be okay?”A louder mew followed her.She turned back in time to see the cat walked toward her tentatively.“Okay, just a moment.”When she finished pouring milk in a shallow bowl, she found that the cat sat on the kitchen island with waiting eyes.She only paused a bit before recovering.“Here you go.”

 

She decided to make a cup of hot chocolate.Rainy day with sugary, warm beverage was a sweet combination.“Mind if I join you?”The cat only lifted her head up so Kara took it as yes.Leaning on the kitchen island on her elbows as she watched her furry guest.The cat went back to the milk.She noticed the cat drank in small licks.Not a spill.“Are you somebody’s?Because you seem very educated.”The cat snapped her head up, looking offended.“Did I say something wrong?”The cat mewled lowly, almost angrily.“Sorry.This is our first meeting.Apology for weird questions.Although, I didn’t see any collar.”The cat stared at her.If she had any human-like eyebrow, she would’ve raised one in perfect arc.“Fine.A mysterious background with refined manner.”The cat honest-to-god delivered a smug smirk.At least, that was what Kara’s brain told her.

 

“You are very sassy.”The cat let out a near hiss.“Sassy is great.Women these days still have a lot to fight.Am I right?”The cat looked at her pensively.Kara just smiled and continued consuming her chocolate.“I’m a reporter at CatCo.I was Cat Grant’s assistant for two years before finally promoted to a reporter.My new boss is a man who has an extremely short temper.He likes to call me Ponytail which I dislike.Ms.Grant yelled at me with wrong name but she called me the real one when I did something right and she does this to everyone.He just yells that nickname for no reason while calling others by their real name.He always writes all over my articles in red about the things needed to be corrected.Which is practically every word.But I know Ms. Grant doesn’t promote someone she doesn’t deem has potentials so this is what I must go through.I think my boss called me Ponytail less this past week.It’s a good sign, right?”She really needed to go out more because she just ranted on about her life with a cat.“Sorry to make your milk taste bad with my boring story.”She murmured and drank her now cold chocolate.

 

She was startled by a paw on her forehead.The nails slightly stung.But other than that, it was oddly comforting.She didn’t dare to move, not even to look up.Afraid that any sudden move would scare the cat away.She shut her eyes to revel in the tiny warm point.The paw disappeared as quick as it came.“Thank you.”She sighed.

 

She was doing the dishes when a shadow popped up beside her.“Yes?”She followed the cat’s eyes.The rain stopped.“Oh!You’re…you’re leaving.”She didn’t know why she felt so disappointed.This was a cat.An animal who seemed to have intelligence equivalent to a human child to say the least.But this was the least lonely she had felt for the longest time.She felt seen.She felt heard.She felt understood.A simple gesture calmed her almost entirely.She reminded herself not to get attached, like many things in her life.“Okay then, I guess this is goodbye.”She waved once, looking down at the sink.

 

The cat meowed to get her attention.She looked up to see a black paw raising.Pink toe beans soothed her stormy mind.The overcast weather migrated from outside to Kara’s brain before cleared the next second.The cat walked very slowly back to the open window.Hesitation.Kara smiled at the thought.“Maybe…we’ll meet again?I’ll leave the window open for you.”She knew the promise might be empty but she was certain that she would keep up her end of bargain.The cat threw her one last glance and disappeared into nightfall.

 

 

Another crappy week at work, she was lounging in her living room and basking in the sun.

 

Meow.

 

“You came!”She should be cringing at her eagerness but she was too happy to care.The black cat jumped down the window sill gracefully.“Did I tell you that I think your movements are really elegant?”The cat paused and meowed, satisfied by Kara’s praise.“Come lie here with me.The sun is really good.”She put a blanket on the floor to lie on top of it.Strategically arranged right under the sunlight.She resumed her previous position, lying on her back with her fingers crossed on her belly and eyes closed.She listened.The cat rewarded her after some prudence.Feeling something appeared in her proximity.She peeked from one eye to see the cat gingerly situated herself at the edge of the blanket.Kara’s grin widened before going back to her sunbath.

 

The human and the cat enjoyed the sun together.Kara had to try very hard not to laugh out loud at the quaintness.Unknown amount of time passed when a warm body brushed against her.Fur to be precise.Fuzzy, tickling, thrilling.She opened her eyes to an intrigued little face.The cat cowered back a bit.She pushed her luck and reached out.Stopping in midair to give the cat a chance to say no.The cat stared at her hand.The slimmest possibility was granted.The cat didn’t go farther, she moved closer instead.This time was a fluffy, fury head touched the back of her hand.She didn’t rub on Kara’s skin like other cats.Just a simple press.Yes, Kara admitted that she had been scouring the Internet for cat videos since the black cat visited her.The touch last more than a minute.The cat moved away first.

 

“You’re so warm.”She blurted out.Blushing.“I like it.Thank you.”To her amazement, the cat closed their range and curled right next to her side of body.She lifted up her head to take in the astonishing sight.She giggled.“Can I pet you?”The cat huffed but didn’t protest.She started small.A finger on the head.A few pettings later.She used first knuckles of her three fingers to stroke the cat’s body.The swirl of black fur squirmed.And then, she purred, almost inaudibly.The cat was purring!Kara was beyond exciting.She wanted to scream on top of mountain.To say she somehow earned somewhat of trust from this peculiar cat.They snuggled for the rest of afternoon.She thought that she was ready for another week of Snapper’s yelling.

 

 

“I was going to buy some cat food but I didn’t really know what you liked to eat.So I bought treats instead.Many of them.”She whipped out five bags of various snacks.“They are all good for your health.Animal-friendly, organic, natural and whatever that needs to be taken into account when feeding a feline.”She presented them on the kitchen island.The black cat walked back and forth, examining them.“You don’t have to try.I just thought we could snack together.You eat yours, I eat mine.With a movie or talk or just lie there in the sun.”Great, now she upgraded to making plans with a cat.Her furry friend glanced up at her and Kara blushed.The cat finished pacing and nudged at the cheese ball pack.“You like your dairy, don’t you?Let’s try it!”She opened it and took out one ball, placing it on the counter.The cat smelled it for the longest time, like a connoisseur.A small lick later, she began to eat.“Thank god!I was SOOOO afraid that you would hate it and stopped coming or worse, I might’ve poisoned you.I looked up on the Internet and went to several pet stores.This is more expensive than I thought but the staff recommended.And I checked, it has 4.8 stars.It’s gotta be delicious, rrrr…?”A paw on her lips.An unimpressed facial expression, the kind like every other cat would have.But this cat had more human in her.Kara couldn’t place it but she was sure it was there.The paw left her and Kara had this weird lost feeling.“Sorry for the rambling.Bad habit, can’t shake it.”The cat just continued devouring the cheese ball like fine dining.

 

 

The cat meowed really loud when she barged into her apartment.“Sorry!Sorry!We rescheduled our Sunday brunch to today so I ran a little late.Ready for snack?”The cat stood right beside the open window, ready to leave in any second.Threatening.“Please don’t be mad.Let’s eat two this time?I brought Noonan’s sticky buns so you can smell it.”She lifted up a paper bag.The cat caved after an excruciating moment.She made Kara suffer on purpose.Kara still took it as a win because she stayed.They ate in peace.“Sunday brunch is a thing we do.Alex and I.”The cat stiffened.Kara sensed something was wrong.She tried to decipher.Is that jealousy or…?“Alex is my sister.”The cat physically relaxed, acting nonchalant while licking on the round treat.She really wanted to ask.But it was ridiculous.Asking a cat if she was jealous of Kara being with others.

 

Kara made sure to cancel a blind date forced on her later at that day.

 

 

The black cat trusted her bit by bit.Their distance shortened gradually.Their warm-up took less and less.Therefore, Kara decided to take a step forward.It could end badly or really well.She still wanted to try.This was one of her favorite activities after all.

 

“Do you want to cuddle?We can do it like when we sunbathe or if you don’t mind, we can cuddled on my belly.”She opened her arms for the cat to come to a decision.The cat looked at her weirdly.Eyeing the open window.Kara cursed inward, the cat was considering fleeing.At this point, Kara had already given up figuring out why the cat could understand what she said.She could only assume the cat lived in National City so English was probably her native language like Kara.That was it but it was enough.“It’s okay that we don’t.”She was about to hug a blanket or a pillow when a warm furry ball sat next to her.Then, a paw stepped on her thigh.One by one, the cat was on top of her belly.Kara slid her body down a bit to create a more comfortable flat surface for her friend.

 

In this close range, Kara was finally given the opportunity to admire the cat’s fur.It was black yet shiny.An ink had a kind of sleek texture.Every hair was visibly healthy.She gave a featherlike touch.The body on her shivered.She didn’t leave so Kara ventured out.Fingertips first to the back of her hand and finally, the whole palm.Head, body, tail and one of front leg.She held the paw gently and brushed tiny beans.The cat turned to look at her.“Sorry.I’m just…really curious.I once took home a stray cat.He was black, too!I named him Streaky.Eliza said we couldn’t keep him so our family friend took him.He died years ago.I still visited his grave when I go home.”The cat gazed at her.“I don’t know why I told you that or any other things but I…um…I’m grateful that you listened.Thank you.”She squeezed the paw once in gratitude.

 

The cat remained on her belly until it was time to go home.

 

 

The cat didn’t visit for the next two weeks.Kara was so worried and Snapper yelled at her nonstop.He called her to his office on a Wednesday.“You’re interviewing Lena Luthor.”

 

“What?”She was taken aback by the sudden statement.

 

“That’s what I said when Ms. Grant told me.You’ve been assigned.More specifically, you’re the only one she’s willing to talk.Go see Ms. Grant now.”Snapper tossed her some documents.

 

She knew about Lena Luthor.Everybody in this line of business knew about her.They all wanted a piece of exclusive.Nobody had succeeded.The Luthorcorp moved to National City weeks ago and was announcing a rebranding.A ceremony would be held in near future.However, the elusive youngest Luthor member was making people’s job harder than before.Lex Luthor died a year ago.The same cause of death as his father, liver cancer.Lillian retired to somewhere like The Bahamas.Leaving the company to the remaining family, Lena Luthor, who was a mystery until she had to step up to the plate.She worked in R&D in Luthorcorp where her multiple degrees were put in a good use.Lena was a result of an affair.The scandal caused a sensation back in the day.Kara concluded that might be why Lena kept herself out of limelight.The same old gossips went uproariously again when Lena became the CEO.

 

“You didn’t tell me you knew Lena Luthor.”Cat Grant said as soon as she walked in the ostentatious glass office.The door didn’t even have time to click shut.

 

“I-I didn’t.I don’t.”She adjusted her glasses.

 

Cat regarded her for an eerily long time.It reminded of her cat friend.Both cats but her fluffy friend was definitely more lovable.She already looked forward to their next meeting.That was if the cat would show up.“These are the questions I want you to ask.You can add some of yours but not before running though with me.I assume you know the basics?”Cat looked over her lowly perched reading glasses.

 

“Y-Yes.”She accepted another pile of papers.

 

“Prepare yourself.Your appointment is tomorrow at 11:30am.If it runs long into lunch, eat with her.You can use the company card but if she offers to buy, let her.Make her happy.Don’t let me down.”Cat pinned her on the spot.She felt like a prey.

 

“Yes, ma’am.”Cat huffed.Big ferocious cat!Kara ran out of the office before she said anything stupid like her mind just did.

“Hello, Ms. Danvers.I’m Lena Luthor.Welcome to L-Corp.”A pale hand outstretched.She swallowed and took it.Following the arm, shoulder and reaching to a pair of striking green eyes.They looked so familiar.What is it?

 

“Ms. Danvers?”A head tilted to one side.She looks like a…

 

She shook her head.This was her first big story.She could not mess it up.“I’m s-sorry.Hello, Ms. Luthor.Thank you for having me.”

 

The woman let out a strange laugh.“Please call me Lena.”

 

“If I’m calling you Lena,…”She extended the same invitation.

 

“Kara it is.”She really liked the way Lena pronounced her name.Slight accent.Beautiful, alluring just like her face.Kara really needed to get a grip of herself.

 

They sat on either side of Lena’s pristine desk.A notepad, a pen, a recorder, a list of questions and an hour later.“I like the last one.Doens’t sound like Cat Grant.”Lena looked at her through long eyelashes.

 

“Because it’s Kara Danvers.”She answered sheepishly, fidgeting with her glasses.

 

“My favorite.”The woman in perfect silky blouse and pencil skirt smiled devilishly.Like a cat!She finally concluded.What’s it with women in charge and cats?

 

Her hand shoot her glasses again.“Thank you.I…um…I should get going.”

 

“Would you mind having lunch with me?It’s well past lunch time and you happen to be a very enjoyable company.I’m buying.”God, this woman was a total tease.A heartbreaker for sure.At least, she saved Kara from going back without Ms. Grant’s demand of a business luncheon for more possible scoops.

 

“It’d be my pleasure.”Kara was Cat Grant’s dutiful employee after all.

 

They ate in quietness at first.Kara tried to find something to talk.“You like Big Belly Burger?”

 

“When I feel stressful or want to share with somebody who can really enjoy the pleasure and forget about the guilt.”Lena winked.

 

“So…it’s both now?”She shoveled some fries to keep her from talking more.

 

“Unfortunately, yes.”Lena sighed.

“I should get back to finish the article.”Her voice hoarse because the idea just hit her made her throat dry as Sahara.

 

“I can’t wait to read.”Lena sounded equally husky.

Meow.

“Let’s take a break.”She took their snacks and her drink to the couch.The TV was still playing Lena Luthor’s face and her latest statement.“She’s what I’m writing.It’s hard.To depict a living breathing person to perfection.I don’t know why I want to make it perfect.There’s no perfect story, you can only write your story in the best way you can give.Close to the truth is the only way.”She cuddled with her feline buddy, palming the soft fur.“You know, her eyes reminds me of yours.It’s weird, I know.”The cat froze completely.She jumped out of Kara’s embrace and went straight to the window.

 

Kara wanted to call her back but realized that she didn’t know the cat’s name.

The cat listened to her patiently for the entire duration of her nonsense.She leapt across the gap to land on the nearest furniture and arrived at the coffee table.Front right paw stepped on the TV remote to bring it back to life.It opened to news channel.Another piece of story regarding Lena Luthor was running.The cat turned her back slowly to Kara who shifted her attention between the screen and the cat.

 

“What?You know Lena Luthor?”The cat meowed.

 

“Are you her cat?”The cat hissed.She didn’t really like to be described as someone’s property.Kara held up her hands.“I didn’t mean to offend you.So what?You’re her friend?Are you secretly begging treats from door to door?Or should I say window to window?”Why Kara felt jealous, she had no idea.The cat meowed, annoyed.Kara felt the same irritation so she spoke her heart.“I know you don’t like the idea of being owned but I will have you know that I thought about adopting you.You can come and go as you please and I will give you the best home and care that a cat can have.I really really like you and I enjoy your company so much.To a degree that even I myself don’t understand.You’re the first living being that I feel connected since I was adopted.Alex and Eliza are great but you, you are different.”She let out an angry sigh to cover her urge to cry.“And I’m arguing with a cat.I don’t know If I’m crazy or what.I rather think I’m not because I can see that you’re really intelligent in our very short span of time together.”She finally threw her dignity out of window and brushed off an escaping tear.

“Please let me explain.”Lena said.

 

“What the hell?”Even though she guessed first, the situation still made her scream and bounce up.Running to stand behind the couch.

 

“Please.”Lena pleaded, fingers still twisted.

 

 

Lena was the one making them tea this time.She knew where the mugs and tea bags were.From their numerous encounters in this very apartment before.Kara had to remind herself.

 

“I inherited my magic from my birth mother.An Irish woman.The one Lionel had an affair with.I assume you knew about this?”Lena took a sip of her tea, jaw clenching.

 

“Yes.”Kara was reduced into a single-answer machine in the past 15 minutes.She was proud of her ability to recover enough for Lena to start her story.
