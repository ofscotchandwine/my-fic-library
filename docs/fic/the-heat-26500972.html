1. The Assignment

Special Agent Lena Luthor brushed a perfectly manicured hand down her dark blue suit jacket, her fingers playing over the buttons as she squared her shoulders. She adjusted her matching skirt, checking there wasn’t a single wrinkle in sight as the automatic doors of the Metropolis FBI Headquarters opened before her and she stepped through.

Agent Luthor walked with purpose, her heels clacking loudly off the concrete and echoing around so those nearby glanced up curiously as she passed by. With her head held high, not a hair in her tight bun out of place. Her ID badge clipped impeccably straight to the pocket of her jacket, she exuded confidence and authority.

“Good morning, Agent Anderson,” Lena greeted an agent in passing, her tone dismissive as if she addressed him not because she wanted to but because she knew it was appropriate to do so.

Agent Anderson merely raised his chin slightly in greeting, indifferent towards Agent Luthor but said nothing in response. Not that Lena noticed, as she hadn’t waited to catch his response, marching onwards through the large busy lobby.

She approached the security guard, withdrawing her leather bound ID from her jacket pocket and presenting it importantly.

“Special Agent Luthor,” she said loudly, waiting for the security guard to allow her through, though the guard did not appear to be standing in her way at all.

“I already know who you are, Agent Luthor. You don’t need to do this every day.” 

“I’m just following correct procedure, Cliff, as everybody here should,” she said pointedly.

Cliff gestured exaggeratedly with his hand for Lena to pass and she gave him a small nod and continued her quick pace to the elevator.

Getting off on the third floor only moments later, she walked down the corridor and paused at the office with the name plate, A.D J'onn J'onzz.

She knocked, waited to hear J’onzz’s, Come in, before entering the office.

“Assistant Director J’onzz, I heard about your promotion last night. I came to congratulate you, Sir.”

J’onzz stood from his desk with a smile, running a hand down his dark tie as Lena held out her hand.

“Thank you, Agent Luthor.” He said as they shook.

“I assume your successor here as Assistant Director is yet to be ascertained.”

J’onzz was nodding before Lena had finished speaking, “Before you ask Agent Luthor, you are being considered for the position.”

“Well, I must admit I had already assumed-”

“Look, Agent Luthor...” J’onzz waved his hand dismissively, cutting her off.

He gestured for Lena to take a seat as he sat back down at his desk.

“What is it, Assistant Director?” Lena was perfectly calm, only a slight eyebrow raise. She sat perfectly straight in her seat.

“Agent Luthor... Lena, you’re good, you know you are. We obviously both know that. And you get results, how can I complain when you always get results?”

“Right.” Lena nodded.

“Its… It’s common knowledge, Agent Luthor, that you’re not exactly… adored by your fellow Agents.” J’onzz looked uncomfortable, a slight frown. He brought his hands together on the desk as he seemed to be considering his next words carefully.

“I don’t have many friends, that is true, but this is my place of employment, it is not a social gathering and I’m not here to make friends, Assistant Director. I’m the best agent you have. I’ve closed more cases than every other Agent on your staff. I caught the Cedar Falls Killer my sixth day on the job.”

“I know and that’s almost unbelievable, Agent Luthor. And you are right, you’re one of the best agents I have.”

“I am the best Agent you have,” Lena corrected him.

“A few other agents have described you as arrogant, not a team player-”

“Assistant Director, I must stress that I’ve closed every case I’ve ever worked on-”

“Lena, I’m not saying no, okay? It's just…" He breathed a sigh. “Okay, how about this… There's a case in National City. I'm going to assign you to that case.”

“National city?” She frowned.

“We’ll set you up with an apartment-”

“Apartment? How long exactly do you expect me to be in National City?”

“For however long it takes, Agent Luthor, it’s a big case, with a lot of players. But if you close this one, Agent Luthor, I’ll see what I can do about that promotion.”

“Really?”

J’onzz nodded.

After a moment, he pulled a case file across his desk, opened it and slid a surveillance photo towards her. It was a man who looked to be in his late 50s with small eyes, a bold head and dark tattoo indeterminable on his right cheek and down his neck.

“I know who this is,” Lena said, relaxing back in her chair. “His name is Viktor Sokolovskii, he is also known as the Russian Butcher. He first came to the US in 2008, started drug rings in Star City and here in Metropolis. He went off the grid, last seen in 2014.”

J’onzz blinked, obviously impressed by her awareness, “The guy stays so hidden, he’s like a ghost. But here’s the good news, Agent Luthor…” He pressed the tip of his index finger against Sokolovskii’s photo.

“This photo was taken in National City just six days ago…”

Lena met J’onzz gaze as she started to understand her assignment.

“Something is happening, Agent Luthor. And I’m assigning you to find out what. Six dealers in National City turned up dead last week. I need you to find out if this is his work.”

“Sir, I will have Sokolovskii in a maximum security prison by the end of the week.” Lena said with absolute confidence, rising from her chair.

J’onzz followed, also standing, “Listen, if it’s him then something big is happening. Get ahead of it. He’s here and then he’s gone, this won’t be easy. Good luck, Agent Luthor. Consider this a final exam for the job.”

“You can count on me, Sir.”

Rojas slipped lower in his chair, chuckling quietly to himself.

Who was this woman? Kara had only been in her company for mere seconds and she was furious and totally flustered, though she did her best to hide that. There was something absurdly attractive about this FBI agent, she had an authoritative presence about her that Kara wanted to strip away and see what lay underneath. But somehow, she was also annoying as hell, she’d ruined four days work for Kara. Rojas was her collar. How dare she step in and start making calls on a guy she hadn’t even had the chance to question yet.
