She dreams about having to plan a funeral, instead of a wedding - except there isn't an actual body to cremate or put in a casket. And in her dream she struggles to remember Kara's face, desperately clinging to every memory she has of her as they slowly slip away, until every thought she ever held dear is gone. 

By the time the sun peeks through the curtain, Lena is an emotional train wreck. She drags herself out of bed and stumbles into the shower, hoping it will help clear her head and wash away the terrors of the night. She turns the water as hot as she can tolerate, standing under the spray until her skin turns red and feels as raw on the outside as she does on the inside. Flashes of her nightmares keep running through her mind, and she can't shake the gnawing pit in her stomach telling her it could all happen. That in fact, the likelihood of Kara - and the rest of the crew as well - dying on this mission, is actually very high. They all knew this going into it, but now that the launch date is literally around the corner, suddenly the reality of it feels like a mountain crushing her under its weight. Her knees buckle under her and she sinks to the tile floor, gasping for air against the sudden tightness in her chest. Her whole body trembles and she starts to sob, salty tears mingling with the water running down her face.

She's not sure how long she sits there, but eventually she manages to stop crying. There's a brief moment of levity when she thinks about how grateful she is to live in a higher end apartment where she never runs out of hot water. Her stomach growls, so she sets herself a new game plan of breakfast and coffee, hoping some food and caffeine will help her pull herself back together, get her head on straight. She just needs to hold it together a little longer, for Kara's sake. She doesn't need her fiancée launching into space on a world-saving mission with her mind unfocused and distracted because she's thinking about Lena's emotional turmoil.

She makes it through one piece of buttered toast and half a cup of Colombian roast before she loses it again.

Kara has finally settled into a dreamless sleep when the alarm on her phone jolts her awake. With a disgruntled groan, she buries her face in her pillow and blindly swats at her phone to turn the alarm off. After a few minutes, she reluctantly rolls over and stretches out her sore muscles, trying not to think too hard about the fact that in just a few hours they'll be on their way back to Houston. Back to a terrifying reality where a team of oil-drillers is literally the only thing standing in the way of a giant asteroid completely destroying the Earth. No pressure or anything. Kara breathes out a heavy sigh and tries to shake the negative thoughts out of her head. She has a few more hours left to spend alone with Lena and wants to make the most of it.

Speaking of Lena…Kara rolls over and realizes her fiancée is already up, and as she glances across the master bedroom to the en suite, she can see the bathroom mirror is still slightly fogged up. Which means Lena probably only woke up a little while ago, hopefully better rested than herself, Kara thinks, stifling a yawn. A shower does sound nice after last night, but when she catches a whiff of coffee from the kitchen, she quickly decides caffeine is a higher priority right now. She can shower after breakfast. She throws on some gym shorts and an oversized t-shirt before she ambles down the hallway.

To her surprise, both the living room and kitchen are empty when she gets there. A half-empty "Geology Rocks" mug (courtesy of Sam) sitting next to a cold piece of toast on the counter is the only evidence of Lena's presence. Kara looks around the apartment and notices the patio door is open - of course. Lena has always loved the view she has of the hills from there, Kara does as well. One of the perks of living on the outskirts of National City, actually being able to see something other than just another building out the window.

The sun has already started making its way up into a bright blue morning sky, but the haze creeping through the hills has managed to trap some colors of the sunrise, the vibrant reds and oranges appearing almost like fire on the horizon. Lena's normally dark hair seems to glow in the colored light as Kara stops for a minute to just stand and admire her beauty. It's only when she gets closer that she can see the other woman's shoulders shaking, and she feels her stomach drop when she realizes Lena is crying.

Concern creasing her brow, she walks onto the patio where she gently wraps her arms around Lena's waist from behind and kisses the back of her shoulder, before asking, "Baby, what's wrong? Are you okay?"

Lena immediately spins around and throws her arms around the blonde as she buries her face in Kara's shirt and sobs. "I can't do this Kara," she croaks, her voice thick with tears. "I-I've been trying to be strong, but I can't do it anymore…I can't - I can't pretend I'm not terrified." Her hands clutch desperately at the fabric of Kara's shirt, maybe if she just holds on tight enough…"Please don't go," she pleads in a broken whisper, "They don't need you. I need you more than they do. I don't care how selfish it is, just stay here with me…please…"

Kara feels her heart shatter at Lena's words, the shards lodging themselves firmly in the pit of her stomach, one particularly sharp one finding its way into her throat. How can she answer that? She would do anything for Lena, but this? No, she has to keep Lena safe - even if it costs Kara her own life.

She squeezes her eyes shut against the tears forming there, and pulls her beloved tighter against her, one hand gently cradling the back of Lena's head as she continues to cry into Kara's shoulder.

"Lena…" Her throat constricts around the words like it's trying to force them back down. Oh, how she wishes she could, that she didn't have to have this conversation. "You know I can't do that…believe me, I'd never leave your side if I could. I don't want to go anymore than you want me to." Drawing a deep breath in, she lets it out in a long, shaky exhale. "But the world needs us - needs me. I-I couldn't save my own parents, and I couldn't do anything to save Jeremiah either... but now, I have a chance to save everyone - someone else's parents, someone else's loved ones."

"Then let me go with you," Lena says, looking up at Kara with her jaw set and her tear-swollen eyes suddenly filled with the fire of fierce determination. "You know that I know how the equipment works as well as anyone else."

"What? No, absolutely not. Even if it was up to me - which it's not, and you know damn well Lex and J'onn would never allow it - but even if it was up to me -" Kara shakes her head, her eyebrows furrowed. "Look, I know you could build a drill motor with your eyes closed and you've tested plenty of prototypes, but you've never had to use the equipment under pressure. And that's just on an oil rig, here, on Earth. It's going to be a lot different up there. We've all been training for weeks now just so we can be even the tiniest bit prepared for it - because heaven help us, you know none of us are actually cut out to be astronauts."

Staring down into the mesmerizing green-blue of Lena's eyes, Kara feels a fresh wave of protectiveness wash over her. She brushes a strand of dark brown hair out of Lena's face and tucks it gently behind her ear, then draws her hand back slightly to cup the side of Lena's face, her thumb caressing her cheekbone. "Look, I'll be alright. I'm tough — I mean, I've literally survived a war already. But you…if you went up there and something happened to you, I could never live with myself. Hell, I think I'd probably let them blow me up along with the damn asteroid, just so I wouldn't have to live without you."

Lena immediately jerks her head back and fixes her fiancée with a furious glare. "Don't you even fucking joke about something like that!"

Kara is quick to reply, "Well, then don't you go and suggest stupid ideas, like trying to come on a dangerous mission you're not even remotely trained for."

She watches Lena open her mouth to respond, then close it, the brunette's shoulders drooping with a heavy sigh of resignation. She ducks her head and quietly burrows her face into the crook of Kara's neck.

Kara lets out a sigh of her own. "It will be okay, I promise. I'm tough. We're all tough. It takes a whole helluva lot to take a roughneck down. So, we're gonna go up there, blast the damn rock to pieces, then we're gonna come home, and you and I are gonna get married. Okay?"

Lena nods into Kara's neck, not daring to look at her in case she starts crying again. "Okay."

For a while, the two women just stand there, wrapped in each other's arms. Memorizing the way it feels to be held, the way they fit together so perfectly. Willing time to stop, so maybe they can stay here like this just a little longer.

When they finally break apart, Kara reaches up and unclasps the silver chain with her mother's pendant from behind her neck, taking one of Lena's hands, and gently placing the necklace in her palm. "I need you to keep this safe for me."

Lena looks incredulously from the blue gemstone in her hand to Kara, a fire growing in her eyes. "Don't you dare -"

"I can't wear jewelry under the spacesuit," Kara interrupts Lena before she gets carried away, as she explains, "It's just going to end up sitting in my duffel bag. I'd much rather have it here where I know at least a little piece of me will still be with you, even when I'm a thousand miles away."

Lena seems to accept her explanation, giving a small nod as she contemplates the precious treasure in her palm. She purses her lips and looks back up at Kara with a stern expression. "You'd better come back for it, because if you don't…if you go and die on me, you know I'm going to kill you, right?"

A surprised laugh bubbles out of Kara's chest, and despite everything, she can't help but smile at the woman in front of her. "Yup. I wouldn't expect anything less."

She's met with a soft smile and a small eye-roll in return before Lena holds up the chain so Kara can help her secure it around her slender neck. The blue stone sits right over Lena's sternum as she puts her hand over it, holding it close to her heart. "I'll keep it safe for you," she promises, pushing up on her tip-toes to plant a tender kiss on Kara's lips. "I love you, Kara Zorel Danvers."

"And I love you, Lena Kieran Luthor."

"Promise?"

"Always."
