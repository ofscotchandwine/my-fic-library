    Kate knows something is wrong when Yelena is late.

She really needs Yelena to keep a semi-permanent emergency line. One for Kate Bishop only. Kate emergencies. Kate Calls? Like cat calls but—

She needs a way to reach Yelena when things like this happen, when her brain is running a mile a minute and she can’t seem to figure out what’s a legitimate source of anxiety and what’s completely ridiculous. Yelena is probably fine, right? She does this all the time. In fact, Yelena has been doing this much longer than Kate has. She’s a fucking professional. A very talented, incredibly lethal professional.

    Kate is making a big deal out of nothing.

    Hmph. She’s not needy, okay?
