She bows her head, close to Lena but not quite touching as she says, “Nahn rrip khap shesur urvish. You are the harmony of my soul.” 

“Nahn rrip khap shesur urvish, Kara Zor-El,”   Lena whispers to her, perfectly, as if she was born with the words in her mouth. 

When Kara kisses her again, the sweetness on her tongue tastes like joy. 

Like home.
