Lex.

Season 6 episode 8 (The length of the match) was the episode Kara came back from the phantom zone. Felt it made sense and also passed the Nadal-Djokovich final that neared 6 hours. Kara’s and Eve's post game state was based on their game as well.

I debated a lot regarding the outcome of the match, with Kara losing almost being in the final draft, but I eventually realised it would be far more satisfying for her to win. And not just for her, but also for Eve who was kinda painted as a main antagonist, but at the end of the day she is still human and I really wanted to showcase that side of her. I wanted you to see some of her vulnerable and confused parts. She is an amazing player and despite losing her ‘purpose’ and mentor, she could still have a future.
