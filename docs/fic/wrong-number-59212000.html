    Chapter 1

    “You better have a good reason for calling me on my day off, Lewis!” Yelena shouts through her phone. “If you do not, you and I are going to have a problem.”

    There is no response for a moment. It makes Yelena pause. She moves her phone away from her ear to look down at her screen. She checks if it’s the hospital number. It is.

    “Darcy!” Yelena practically shouts. Her voice causes Fanny to stir. Her American Akita tilts her head at her.

    The blonde shakes her head at her dog. “You are okay.” She says softly, moving the phone away from her.

    “Dr. Belova, is that you?” Darcy’s voice finally calls outs, sounding completely confused. Yelena rolls her eyes. “Of course it is me. You are the one who called me.” Then she adds, “On my day off, which is very rude of you by the way.”

    Darcy hums. “I may have called the wrong number.” She says, papers crinkling through the phone.

    Hearing that has the doctor perk up, “Okay good.” Yelena states. “I will be going back to my shows now.” She says, already unpausing the third episode of The Walking Dead’s season two.

    “Right, sorry about that. Enjoy your day.” The nurse chirps before hanging up. Yelena tosses her phone onto the opposite side of the couch and burrows further into her couch cushions.

    The scenes flashforward on her screen, each passing second keeps the blonde on the edge of her seat as to whether Shane and Otis will make it back to Carl on time. Just as Rick and Lori decide to proceed with the surgery, her phone rings yet again. Yelena dives for her phone, finding the hospital’s phone number on her screen yet again. “Lewis, what the hell!” Yelena shouts, pausing her show.

    Whatever the issue is at the hospital, Yelena knows that there are other doctors currently on shift. Her sister, Dr. Natasha Romanov, is head of oncology. She is supposed to be supervising a group of interns who are terrified of her. She should be available. Then there’s her friend, Dr. Sonya Lee, who is also an attending trauma surgeon like herself. Her friend is more than capable of handling whatever it is.

    The nurse exhales. “I know. I’m sorry Belova but it turns out I dialed the correct number before.”

    “What do you mean? You dialed my number without knowing.” Yelena states, shaking her head. “Do I have to come to the hospital or not?” She asks, looking at time on her computer screen. “I am trying to be lazy. And, if I do not have to, I would rather not venture into the cold.”

    “I am sorry to do this to you, Dr. Belova.” Darcy starts. “We technically don’t need you here-”

    “Then get Natasha or Sonya-”

    Darcy rushes to say, “But there’s a patient we just admitted into the ER who continues to give us your phone number for her emergency contact.” Then she adds, “Because of that, you might want to come in.”

    Yelena furrows her eyebrows. “What? Who is it?”

    “I can’t really understand her.” Darcy explains. “She’s mumbling and slurring her words.” She sighs in exasperation. “That's why I didn’t call back straight away. I tried to get more information from her.”

    “Oh.” The blonde says, completely confused at the turn of events.

    Darcy groans. “If I’m being completely honest the only coherent thing I got out of her is ‘hawkeye’ which I doubt is her name but that’s all I’ve got.” She shrugs. “The girl is freaking me out.”

    Yelena frowns. “And you are positive that she gave you my number, da?” “Well, I did call this number twice and you picked it up twice.” Darcy answers. “I tried getting Dr. Lee to help but she’s not really having any luck either.” She sighs, continuing. “When this chick finally said something coherent, she said to call someone named Clint and then gave me this number.”

    “Well, she definitely must be confused.” Yelena mutters. “Is she drunk?” “Oh without a doubt.” Darcy confirms. “Kind of early in the afternoon but who am I to judge?”

    The blonde takes a deep breath in and exhales out slowly. “And this woman does not have anyone else with her?”

    “Nope.”

    Yelena stands up, resigning herself to going back to the hospital. She quickly changes out of her sweatpants and changes into a pair of jeans and a crewneck sweatshirt. The nurse continues speaking as Yelena gets dressed. “Honestly, we're pretty swamped right now.” She explains. “I probably could keep an eye on her until she sobers up, I guess." “No, no, I am on my way in.” Yelena interrupts her. “Just to be safe in case I actually do know her."

    Darcy sighs in relief. “Thank you Dr. Belova.”

    “See you sooner than I would like, Lewis.” Yelena says dryly before hanging up. She looks at Fanny who perks her head up again.

    The akita whines at her owner.

    “I know.” Yelena coos, walking over to pet her puppy. “I need to go to work, sweet girl. Be good, da?”

    Fanny’s only response is to lick the blonde’s hand.

    Quickly, Yelena grabs her jacket, helmet, keys and wallet. For the entire ride to the hospital, the blonde tries to rack her brain as to who this mystery person might be. It cannot be Ana as Sonya is at the hospital and would not need help identifying their mutual friend. It is not Wanda since everyone at the hospital has met her sister’s girlfriend. Yelena swears that if it is one of her previous hookups in a moment of desperation, she’ll turn her motorcycle right back around.

    Regardless, no matter who it is: a friend, an ex, or even a drunk, confused stranger, Yelena is not happy. She should not have to go into work on her day off.

    “Yelena?” Natasha questions as she watches as her sister walks through the entrance doors. “Isn’t it your day off?”

    The blonde rolls her eyes. “Do not remind me, sestra.” She huffs as she gestures to her left. “I must go to the ER.”

    The redhead raises an eyebrow. “What’s going on?”

    “I am trying to figure that out.” Yelena says with a shrug. “I will let you know what is going on once I know more.” She says. “All good with your interns?”

    Natasha laughs. “They’re terrified.”

    Yelena smirks. “Good.” Then she clicks her tongue. “And with that, I am off to figure out this mystery.” She winks at her sister. “Tell Dr. Maximoff I said hello.”

    “Yeah, yeah.” Natasha mutters, rolling her eyes at her sister.

    The blonde chuckles, making her way to the emergency room. She finds Darcy at the nurse’s station.

    “She’s behind curtain number one. I don’t think she’s speaking in tongues anymore.” The brunette says wryly. “So, good luck with that.”

    Yelena shakes her head. “Oh wonderful. Thanks Lewis.” With that, she ventures over to the first cot and moves the curtain.

    The woman, who is a completely confused and drunk stranger, looks at the blonde like she has three heads.

    “You’re not Clint.” The woman states, eyebrows furrowed in confusion. Yelena hums. “Apt observation.” She says, scrutinizing the woman in front of her. This brunette seems to be a few years younger than her. Her hair is drawn back in a ponytail and there’s butterfly bandages scattered across her arm, as well as her left eyebrow and right cheek. Her right ankle is also wrapped and elevated on the bed.

    Despite being sweaty and overall just a mess, Yelena cannot deny the fact that this stranger is very attractive.

    “You’re a girl.” The woman says, swaying slightly to one side as she sits on the bed. “You’re definitely not Clint. You’re too pretty to be Clint.”

    The blonde chuckles. “Well thank you.” She smirks. “You are neither Andrew Lincoln nor Jon Bernthal but you do not see me complaining.” She counters. “Seeing as I would prefer to be laying on my couch with my dog, I guess we will both be disappointed tonight.” Not waiting for the other woman to respond, Yelena picks up the chart at the end of the bed. “Hey!” The woman almost shouts. “You can’t read that.” She huffs. “It’s confidential.” Yelena rolls her eyes. “Oh is it?” She says, not even sending a glance in her direction. “Yes, it’s mine.” The brunette tries to lean forward and grab the clipboard. Yelena easily moves it out of her reach. “It is fine.” She waves her off. “I am a doctor.” The woman squints at her, slowly processing her words. Yelena does not know if the brunette is a skeptical drunk or if she’s just more aware of her surroundings than she lets go because she looks suspicious of Yelena.

    “So your name is Hawkeye, da?” Yelena teases, raising an eyebrow at the other woman. The woman doesn’t answer her question. “Are you actually a doctor? Because you don’t look like the woman who helped me before.”

    The doctor smirks. “I will answer your question if you answer mine.”

    With a dramatic sigh, the woman flops back onto the hospital bed and mumbles something. Now Yelena has good hearing but she still couldn’t make out the nonsense spewing from this woman.

    “Excuse me?”

    The woman repeats, “I said Kate.” She says. “My name is Kate Bishop.” Then she adds, “Now where is the dinosaur?”

    Hearing that makes Yelena pause. “Do you only have alcohol in your system?” She asks, quirking her eyebrow. “Or are you on something stronger?"

    “The dinosaur.” She repeats again, exasperated. “It is green and purple.” Yelena raises an eyebrow. “Are you talking about Barney?” She teases, a small smirk on her face.

    This woman, Kate Bishop, rolls her eyes. “No, it’s an Apatosaurus.” She states plainly, as if Yelena is the one in the wrong for not knowing what kind of dinosaur she is talking about. The doctor blinks once. Then twice. “Right, duh.” She says, a deadpan look on her face. “So where is it?”

    “I do know what you are-” Then the doctor pauses. Biting back on a smile, she says, “Wait, one second.”

    Before the brunette can respond, Yelena steps out into the waiting room. She finds the stuffed animal in question lying on one of the patient chairs.

    This is one of the stuffed animals they offer to hurt children who come in. All the doctors tell the kids who come in with broken bones that they can squeeze the dinosaur as tight as they want and it will take on all their pain.

    Yelena has no idea how Kate Bishop got her hands on it but that’s the least of her worries at this point.

    When she walks back through the curtain, Kate brightens up. Yelena offers the brunette the stuffed animal, setting it on the other woman’s lap like a real pet. Kate holds the plush like it’s the most important thing in the world.

    Yelena quirks an eyebrow. “Better?”

    For the first time that night, Yelena sees this woman smile. “Much.”

    “Good.” Yelena chuckles. “Now, can you tell me everything that is in your system?” Kate grumbles. “That’s kind of gross.” She says. “Why do you want to know everything I ate?”

    The blonde rolls her eyes. “I did not ask that.” She sighs. “What drugs did you take?” She asks bluntly.

    “No drugs!” Kate says vehemently, looking completely taken aback by the insinuation. She covers the dinosaur's head, as if to stop it from hearing the accusation. Yelena tilts her head. “So this is just you normally?”

    Kate shakes her head. “Me on fucking tequila shots and rum.”

    The blonde clicks her tongue against the top of her mouth. “Never mix the hard liquor, Kate Bishop.” She admonishes. “It is never wise.”

    “Now you tell me.” Kate mumbles, petting the dinosaur as if it were a cat. Yelena shakes her head, the corners of her mouth tick up as she wills herself not to laugh. “Anyway, now that we both are aware of your wrongdoings, can I call someone for you?” “The scary lady called Clint.” Kate answers.

    “The scary lady.” Yelena repeats, smirking. “Dr. Lee?”

    Kate nods.

    “Right of course.” The blonde says with a wide smile. “What was the number you gave them? To make sure they have it correct."

    Without hesitation, Kate rattles off numbers slowly. It’s absolutely not this Clint person’s number.

    “Unfortunately Kate Bishop, that is my phone number.” Yelena sighs, holding out her hand. “Where is your phone?”

    Kate thinks for a moment. Slowly, she answers, “Pocket.” Then she twists herself to grab it out of her pants pocket. Then she holds out the device as if it were a prize. Yelena shakes her head, taking the phone from the woman. She presses the home button but, unsurprisingly, it does not turn on.

    “It is dead.” Yelena states. “I will go charge this.”

    Without another word, she turns on her heel. But, not before hearing the woman shout, “Hey! The hot doctor stole my phone!”

    Yelena rolls her eyes as she approaches Bucky and Sonya who are sitting at the nurse's station. Darcy seems to have gone elsewhere. The blonde sends them a nod before digging around in a drawer for a spare charger.

    "Flirting with drunk patients again, Belova?" Bucky teases, a smirk on his face. The blonde flips him off before she finds what she’s looking for. She quickly plugs the woman’s phone into a charger. “Shut up, Barnes.”

    “Need any help?” He asks, not looking even remotely interested in moving. Yelena rolls her eyes at him. “I got it.”

    Sonya quirks an eyebrow. “Did you get anything out of her?” She asks. “Because she gave me nothing and it was infuriating.”

    The other woman laughs. “Yes, she was not speaking in tongues anymore like Darcy had mentioned. She spoke to me a lot actually.”

    Her friend huffs, shaking her head. “Got more than me.”

    “Yes she referred to us as ‘scary lady’.” Yelena smirks. “Should I call you that from now on?”

    “You’re hilarious.” Sonya says dryly, lightly punching her friend in the shoulder. Yelena laughs, not bothering to move away. “For some reason she keeps repeating my phone number but claims it is some Clint person’s instead.” She shrugs. “Whatever.” “Did I see you grab the stuffed dinosaur in the waiting room?” Bucky asks randomly. The blonde nods. “Yes, she was asking for it. It seemed to appease her. I got more information out of her when it was in her lap.”

    Bucky laughs. “Wonderful to know it works on children and grown women.” Yelena rolls her eyes before looking at Sonya. “Also, I figured out that her name isn't Hawkeye because obviously.” Then adds, “It is Kate Bishop.”

    Her friend raises an eyebrow. “You have no idea who she is?”

    “Not one.” Yelena says. “Hopefully, as soon as this phone turns on, we will be able to track down this Clint person."

    Sonya grimaces. “I know it was your day off, Yelena.” She says. “I’m sorry Lewis called you down.”

    Yelena shrugs, all that annoyance had dissipated after speaking with the brunette. “I was annoyed at first but it is fine.” She looks down at the phone before turning back to Sonya. “I will wait with her until we can phone someone she actually knows.”

    Bucky nods. “Good luck with all of that.”

    “Yes, on that note, I better return to this woman.” Yelena nods. “See you.” When the blonde is back in Kate’s view, she glares at her.

    “Just because you’re hot, does not mean you can steal my phone.” Kate says petulantly, arms crossed over her chest.

    Yelena smirks. “Good to know you think I am attractive, Kate Bishop.” She says as she drags a chair over. She sits down before asking, “So, who is this Clint?”

    “He lives in Iowa.” Kate opts to say.

    The blonde raises an eyebrow, tilting her head in confusion. “What do you think I asked you?”

    Kate shrugs.

    Yelena shakes her head but goes along with the new piece of information. “If this Clint lives in Iowa, why do you want us to call him?”

    “He keeps ignoring my calls.”

    The doctor rolls her eyes. “I am a trauma doctor, not a therapist, Kate Bishop.” She says dryly. “Is there someone else we can call for you? Perhaps someone in the same time zone as us? Preferably the same city.”

    The brunette takes a deep breath and sighs. “Parker.” She shrugs. “He’ll make fun of me for this but he lives here.” Then she pauses. “Well, not here in the hospital but like here.” She clarifies as she gestures to the entire area.

    Yelena smiles in spite of herself. "Your friends have interesting names, Kate Bishop.” “You say my name a lot.” Kate states, squinting her eyes at the blonde. “What is your name?”

    “Yelena.”

    “That’s an interesting name.” Kate mumbles. “Doctor Yelena.”

    The blonde smiles. “Yes, Doctor Yelena,” She agrees. “So, Kate Bishop, what did you do to get all the scrapes and scratches on your face and arms?” She asks. “Wrestle with a raccoon, did you?”

    Kate grumbles. “Doctors should not judge patients.” She huffs. “And it was not a raccoon.” Then she adds, “I fell into a pricker bush.” She mutters under her breath. “And then twisted my ankle falling into a ditch.”

    Yelena chuckles, shaking her head. “Hate to break the news to you but doctors judge all of the time.” She shrugs. “Just not to their face.”

    “Rude.” Kate says, sulking as she plays with the tail of the dinosaur. “Where are your scrubs?” She asks suddenly.

    The blonde smirks. “At my house.” She answers. “It is my day off.”

    “Oh, right.” Kate hums. “I took a taxi here.”

    Yelena halts, completely thrown at the sudden turn in the conversation. “Huh?” “Getting a taxi is hard when you see two taxis.” Kate says, eyes on the dinosaur. The blonde raises an eyebrow. “Did you take a taxi alone while drunk and injured?” When Kate looks up at her, she gives her a look that says duh. “I could not drive myself.” She states. “You cannot drink and drive, Doctor Yelena.”

    Yelena smiles. “Very true.”

    “Also I don’t know how to drive so that would’ve been really bad.” Kate explains. Before the brunette can continue, Bucky steps through the curtain holding out Kate’s phone. “It has about ten percent battery.” He says. “You could probably call somebody now and then we’ll charge it up more after.”

    Yelena nods, “Thank you.” She takes the phone and hits the home button. “What is your passcode, Kate Bishop?”

    “Date I got Lucky.” Kate answers immediately.

    The blonde looks up, confused. “I do not know what that means.”

    “Lucky is my dog.” The brunette says, her eyes scream ‘obviously’.

    Yelena shakes her head. “Good to know but that does not clarify the date.” “December second.”

    “Great.” Yelena says as she unlocks the phone. She scrolls through the woman’s contact. “Is Parker the contact with the odd emojis as the first name or is it Mary Parker?” Kate stares at her for a second. Then she answers slowly, “The first one.” She watches as Yelena brings her phone up to her ear. “What are you-”

    “Hush.” Yelena cuts her off, listening as the phone rings.

    Bucky casts an amused glance between the two. He plugs in the charger next to Kate’s bed before heading back to the nurse’s station.

    The phone rings a few times before someone finally picks up.

    “Dude, are you okay?” The voice sounds. “You know when call it freaks me out because-” “This is Dr. Belova at Manhattan Medical."

    “Oh no.” The guy, Parker, must walk into a different room. It’s much quieter on his end now. “So this is an actual emergency?”

    Yelena hums. “Yes, somewhat. Kate Bishop stumbled into the hospital after falling into a bush then a ditch. Then she thought it was wise to take a taxi while drunk.” She shakes her head. “It is nothing life-threatening, but if you or someone else could come pick her up, that would be greatly appreciated. She says she has not taken any other drugs but her behavior says otherwise-”

    “Hey! I’m not high on anything!” Kate’s voice interrupts.

    The blonde doctor ignores her. “At the very least, she is still pretty drunk with a sprained ankle.”

    “Got it.” Parker says. “Tell her that MJ and I are on our way."

    Yelena nods. “Will do.” Then she hangs up and plugs Kate’s phone back in to charge. Kate huffs as the blonde’s attention falls back on her. “You didn’t need to call him.” She mumbles. “I could take a taxi back to my place.”

    The doctor shrugs. “Maybe,” Then she adds with a smirk, “But who am I to deprive your friends of the opportunity to make fun of you?”

    The brunette shakes her head, grumbling. “Hot but mean doctor.” She huffs. “You like to point out how hot I am frequently,” Yelena says smugly. “It is very flattering.” Kate rolls her eyes and changes the subject. “Why are you here if it's your day off?” Yelena smirks. “Because some idiot got incredibly drunk, took a cab to the hospital after falling into a pricker bush and spraining her ankle. Then she gave her doctors my number to call.”

    For once the brunette looks bashful. She grimaces. “Right, sorry.” Then she tags on, “I really thought it was Clint’s number.”

    “Who is this Clint?” Yelena asks again, curious as to who this mystery guy could be. “Is he your boyfriend? Or maybe an ex-boyfriend, if he's not speaking to you.” Kate blanches. She makes a face. “Gross, no.” She shakes her head adamantly. “He's old.” She shudders. “He’s more like my mentor.” Then she sighs. “He thinks I’m too reckless, so he's gone radio silent.”

    Yelena bites back on a smile. “Is that why you got so drunk?”

    “No, I was out with some coworkers for lunch after taking a half day and had way too many shots.”

    “Yes, it seems so.”

    Then Kate changes the subject again. “I’m bored. Do you have any games on your phone?” Yelena chuckles, shaking her head at this weird yet oddly charming woman. “I have many games on my phone.”

    It’s just thirty minutes later when Kate Bishop’s friends arrive. They walk in to find the pair huddled over Yelena’s phone as they plan a strategy to destroy all the pigs on the screen. Angry birds can get quite intense.

    Despite being completely annoyed when she first arrived at the hospital, Yelena has completely forgotten about her couch and her The Walking Dead marathon. “KB!” One of her friends calls out, smirking as she looks at the brunette in the hospital bed. Kate smiles at her. “MJ!”

    Yelena shakes her head and stands. “Thank you for coming to pick her up.” Parker nods. “Of course.” Then he holds out his hand to Yelena. “I’m Peter Parker and this is my girlfriend MJ.” He adds unnecessarily. “We’re Kate’s friends.”

    “I gathered.” Yelena quips, a friendly smile on her face. “Dr. Yelena Belova.” She greets as she shakes their hands. “While I am sorry we had to meet under these circumstances, at least you will have a fun story to tell her when she is sober.”

    MJ laughs. “Oh yes, we had to see this.” Then she claps Kate on the back. “Come on, we can call Clint on the way back. He answers my calls.”

    Kate scoffs. “Rude.” She mutters.

    Peter turns to look at Yelena. “You're the doctor who called me, right?” Yelena nods. “Yes.” She smirks. “I wanted to make sure we called the correct number this time.”

    Kate huffs, “I was really sure that I remembered Clint’s number.” She says. “But I was wrong.”

    Peter shakes his head at his friend, a fond smile on his face. “Oh you are going to regret so many things in the morning.” He teases.

    Yelena laughs. “I think she's sobering up enough to start regretting them right now actually.” She shakes her head. “Are you both okay to take it from here?”

    MJ nods. “Yes, we got her.” She says. “Thanks for calling.”

    Kate smiles. “Yeah, thanks for your help.” She says, a small smile on her face. Yelena nods, sending the brunette one last smile. “See you around, Kate Bishop.” “Bye, Doctor Yelena.”

    Despite most of her day off being at her place of work, Yelena cannot help but wake up the next morning in a good mood.

    Yes, she did not get as far in her Walking Dead marathon. And yes, she did not get to cuddle up with Fanny on the couch for as long as she liked.

    But, she did meet an incredibly odd but charming woman. So, everything kind of balanced out in the end.

    The blonde arrives at the hospital and is quickly swept up in surgeries, paperwork and a few patient meetings. She takes lunch with Natasha and Sonya, the latter filling her sister in on what had happened yesterday.

    “Wait, so this random woman just recited your number as her emergency contact?” Natasha asks, completely shocked. “What are the odds?”

    Sonya shrugs. “Who knows? But I have never seen Yelena so quickly charmed.” Then she smirks. “You should have seen her with this girl.”

    Yelena rolls her eyes, trying to get a handle on her burning cheeks. “Cyka.” She mutters. Her friend laughs. “See.”

    “Wow sestra, who knew all it took was some brunette disaster to sweep you off your feet.” “She was not a disaster.” Yelena argues, not helping her point. “Well not a total disaster.” She shrugs. “It was endearing.”

    “You thought she was hot, right?” Sonya asks, wiggling her eyebrows.

    Yelena shakes her head. “And with that, I am leaving.”

    “Aw come on Yelena.” Sonya groans. “It’s awesome that someone’s caught your attention.” The blonde shrugs. “We will see.” She stands. “For all I know, I may never see this woman again.

    Natasha smirks. “Well, we all know she has your number memorized.” She says with a wink. “Goodbye sestras.” Yelena says, ignoring their laughter as she walks away. Later, the blonde is filling out some patient paperwork in the ER when she hears someone clear their throat. When she looks up, Yelena finds Kate Bishop standing before her. The brunette shifts her weight back and forth, offering her a sheepish smile. The doctor allows herself a moment to fully take in the other woman. Kate’s face is clear of any bandages but there are still a few lingering scratches. Most of them are faded. And, it does not seem like her ankle is wrapped anymore.

    “Hi.” Kate greets, a small smile on her face.

    Yelena nods, smiling back at the other woman. “Hello, Kate Bishop.” Then she smirks. “What feels worse, the hangover or all the scratches from that mean pricker bush?” She adds. “Or is it the ankle?”

    Kate laughs, looking down at her shoes for a moment. “Definitely the hangover.” She answers, her shoulders relaxing as she takes in the blonde. “I honestly don’t know why I came to the ER for my injuries. They weren’t that bad.”

    The blonde shrugs. “Better to be safe than sorry, da?”

    “Yes, that’s true.” Kate agrees. “I came back because I wanted to apologize for-” Then she pauses. “Well for a lot of things but mostly for ruining your day off.” She rubs the back of her neck with one of her hands. “Babysitting a drunk mess probably wasn’t on your to-do list.” Yelena smiles. “Do not worry, Kate Bishop.” She states. “I have had worse days off, sadly.” Then she chuckles. “It was entertaining to say the least.”

    Kate laughs. “That’s fair.” Then she smiles shyly at her. “Could I make it up to you?” She asks. “With a drink sometime?”

    The blonde raises an eyebrow, pleased at this woman’s forwardness. She smiles, “My next night off is Wednesday.”

    “Perfect!” Kate chirps, her smile somehow getting wider. “I look forward to it.” Then she eyes the papers in front of the doctor. “I should probably let you go so you can get back to work.” Her eyes meet Yelena’s. “I’ll call you later?”

    Yelena smirks. “Yes, you have my number.”

    Kate smiles. “Yeah I do.” She chuckles. “And, I’m not forgetting it anytime soon.”
