    “Where are the girls?” he asked.

    “They got talking about archery, so we brought Lucky in to keep him out of the way of any arrows,” Cooper explained.

    “And because he got embarrassed that he didn’t know what they were talking about,” Nate added.

    “Listen, not all of us got to inherit Dad’s archery skills,” Cooper defended.

They were stood a few metres away from the target, Kate giving the younger girl tips on her form as Lila aimed at the target. Clint watched the pair as Lila released the bowstring, the arrow sailing cleanly through the air until it hit the target. A perfect bullseye.

    His daughter grinned as she looked over at him. “Thanks, I had a great teacher… I’m talking about Kate, just so you know.”

    “I can do it.”

    “No, you can’t.”

    “He can,” Lila interrupted. “I’ve seen him.”

    “And you’ll be able to do it too,” Clint added, turning back to Kate. “It just takes a little bit of practice. And we’re staying out here until you do.”

    “She split the arrow,” Clint answered. “Because it isn’t impossible.”

    “Fine, maybe it isn’t impossible,” Kate admitted.

    “That’s great,” Laura replied. “Now, go clean up before dinner.”

    “It’s nothing,” he replied.

    “You know, if she’s going to do this then we should get her a good first aid kit as a late Christmas gift,” Laura suggested.

She moved around the car to open another door. “Also, I thought your dog might want some company.” A large and fluffy dog jumped out from the back of the car, obediently coming to sit at Yelena’s side. “Her name is Fanny.”

It’s just the same as the last time she was here.” She wiped her eyes as a few more tears trickled down her cheeks. “I just really miss her.” Kate came over to lie down next to Yelena on her bed. “What are you doing?”

See you later?” “See you later Kate Bishop.”

    “I’ve done this every year and nothing bad has happened yet,” Clint replied.

    “Emphasis on the ‘yet’,” Kate muttered. “You’re really going to do this?”

    “No, you’re going to do this,” Clint answered.
