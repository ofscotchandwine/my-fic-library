“I think so, thank you.”

Hope is her destiny. It has always been. 

 

Her destiny was hope when her parents sent her after Kal-El, to keep Krypton alive. 

 

Her destiny was hope when she finally became friends with Alex and decided to live a good life on Earth, no matter all the bad that had happened to her before. 

 

Her destiny was hope when she put on her suit for the first time and started to be a message of hope for everyone, too.

Hope that it’ll all be okay. Hope that she’ll be okay. Hope that her family will be okay.

When she followed Kal-El, her mission was to help. When she started to love Alex and Alex started to love her, she knew the feelings of compassion for the first time. When she put on the suit, she extended that compassion and that help to the whole world.

Kara nods, knowing where her sister is going.

I just… I think that I started associating the hope and compassion and help with Supergirl instead of just… me,” she explains. “In the totem, I couldn't be Supergirl. I couldn’t be Kara Danvers either, because neither of them existed. And it just left me, just Kara. And like you say, I still did what I had to, I gave them hope and compassion and help.”

“I think… I don't want them to be separated anymore. Kara and Supergirl. I just want to be me and not be limited by what I’m wearing. I don’t want to be split in half anymore.”

 

Lena looks at her with tears in her eyes, an excited gleam in her eyes. Alex looks at her with a small smile, Kara can see the worry in the back of her eyes but over them is something much better— pride.

 

“Then maybe you shouldn’t be,” Lena says.

And somehow, having never used magic, she knew exactly what to do to dim Nyxly’s power and be able to take her to the tower. Leading the phantoms back to the portal and closing it felt like she was simply flying— overpowered by the allstone, everything felt easy. 

 

“I will help you,” Kara tells her, standing in front of her, hands on her hips. “I have a friend who will take you home.”

 

Kara chuckles when, after whispering Mxyzptlk three times, Mxy shows up in front of her— wearing khakis, sandals and a floral shirt with a margarita in his hand. Once he realizes he’s not on the beach anymore and finds Kara’s eyes, he smiles. 

 

“My love,” he exclaims, dramatically, “Have you missed me so much you’ve called my name? Have you finally realized we’re destined for each other?”

 

“Not quite, Mxy,” she says, crossing her arms but with a smile still on her face. “I need your help.”

 

She points towards Nyxly, who’s frozen in place, looking at Mxy. Surprisingly, for the first time ever, Kara sees something she never thought she would; Mxy, quiet. 

 

“Princess,” he says, placing a hand on the middle of his chest, his thumb hiding under his palm and his fingers separated in pairs, and bowing. “You’re still alive.”

 

“I am,” she says, “I’d like to return home.”

 

Mxy nods. He looks at Kara. “Her powers are dampened.”

 

“Yes,” she answers, “We had some trouble… coming to an agreement.”

 

“I can take you back home, princess Nyxlygsptlnz,” he says, slowly. “But how do I know you’re not planning to kill King Bryxly?”

 

Nyxly’s mouth falls open and tears form in her eyes. “My brother is king, now?”

 

“Oh, I see,” Mxy walks towards her until only the glass is separating them, he places a hand on the glass. “You’ve been away far too long. It’s time you return.”

 

“Why are you doing this?” she asks, seeing kindness for the second time after ages. “You were nothing but a wild card when we were younger.”

 

“Kara,” Mxy answers. “She taught me to be… better. And since I got a second chance, I believe everyone deserves one, too. You made one mistake, princess, it shouldn’t keep you away from home for the rest of your life.”

 

Nyxly smiles and for the first time Kara sees what she used to be— free, at peace, out of anger. When Kara opens the glass door to let her out, Nxyly walks out slowly, as if almost waiting for Kara to push her into the cell again.

 

“Your powers will stay dampened,” Kara tells her, “consider it a warning to not return to Earth.”

 

“I think less power will be good for now,” Nxyly says. “You have my word, I do not plan to return to this planet.” She takes out her hand from her pocket and extends it to Kara, once Kara shakes it, she says, “Thank you, Supergirl.”

 

Mxy takes Nyxly’s hand and as fast as Mxy had shown up, with a puff, they disappear. Kara hopes it's the last time he sees them. 

 

 

Even after all these years of catastrophes and fights, Kara still can’t describe what it feels like to walk through her door and find everything exactly as she left it. 

 

Her bed, unmade. The dishes she didn’t have time to clean, still dirty. The papers and research she was looking at, still spread across the table. 

 

It sort of feels like leaving the movies and suddenly it’s night outside— nothing’s changed, not really. But Kara feels different. Changed. Tired. Depleted. All at the same time. 

 

The damage to the city wasn’t as extensive as Kara thought it would be. There had been a couple of losses, mostly people either brave or stupid enough to think they could defeat a phantom. The impact was mostly broken windows, doors and cars— whether the team managed the phantoms well or Kara closed the portal quickly, she doesn’t know but she’s glad either way.

 

Kara took the allstone to the fortress of solitude, placed it among the rest of hers and Clark’s weapons— the ones that are too powerful to be around humans and the ones that are too dangerous to be used constantly. She locks it away in a crystal box and sighs in relief when she feels the amount of power of it slip away from her too. 

 

And she sighs, happily, when she walks through her apartment door and is greeted by the sight of Lena, in Kara’s National City University hoodie, cooking for two.

 

Lena is softly and very off-key singing along to one of the indie bands Nia got her into, and she’s stirring a pot, her back to Kara. 

 

Kara’s still wearing her suit, it’s dirty and ragged and her boots are still somewhat wet, marking the floor with each step she takes but none of that stops her from walking towards Lena and wrapping her arms around her waist. 

 

“How’s the girl of steel doing?” Lena asks, turning her head to leave a soft kiss on Kara’s cheek. 

 

“There’s food and a gorgeous woman in her apartment so I’d say she’s doing pretty good,” Kara smiles and leans in to kiss Lena. She feels Lena smiling into the kiss and placing her hand on Kara’s cheek, pulling her closer. 

 

Lena keeps moving her thumb back and forth on her cheek even after they pull away. There’s a question in her eyes, one that Kara already knows she’ll ask. “Are you alright?” She asks, softly.

 

Kara thinks it through for a moment, not wanting to instantly lie like she always does. But she realizes, maybe this time, she actually wouldn’t be lying. “I think I am,” she says, honestly, “I think for the first time in forever I know what I want to do next.”

 

“So, you were serious?” Lena asks, “About the coming out thing?”

 

“Yeah,” Kara breathes out. She bites her bottom lip when the sudden and intrusive thought of Lena leaving her for it shows up in her mind, “Is that— Is that okay?”

 

Lena’s eyes soften and she smiles that kind and small smile Kara loves so much. “Of course it’s alright,” she says, “I’m with you, whatever comes next.

 

“Okay,” Kara says and kisses her again. “We’ll figure it out, then. We’ll figure it out.”

 

 

As soon as Kara sits down in Andrea's office, the moment is uncomfortable. 

 

The room barely looks like it used to when Cat was in charge— perhaps that was always Kara’s mistake with Andrea, hoping she’d be like the CEOs before her. Perhaps it's Kara’s difficulty with change. Perhaps it’s the small resentment Kara feels at Andrea after Lena told her what had happened between them. 

 

Perhaps it’s none of it at all and Kara had simply outgrown Catco years ago and it just took her a drastic change to notice that.

 

“Honestly, Kara,” Andrea says after a long sigh, “I don’t know what to do with you. I’ve tried being patient and kind because you're a talented writer but… this will not work,” she explains, pointing at Kara and moving her finger around. “You don't come to work, you do things halfway—" 

 

“—I know,” Kara says, nodding, “I’m sorry. I am.”

 

“I’m afraid your apology does not do much,” she raises an eyebrow. “Your apology does not bring back the time your coworkers have run around doing the articles you decided not to deliver or the money we wasted printing articles you later had to completely rewrite at the last second because of your flimsy excuses and heroic findings.” 

 

“There’s always a reason for that,” Kara says, clenching her jaw.

 

“And there’s also workplace policies.”

 

Kara breathes in and out, knowing it’s not really something she can win. “Then you’ll be glad to hear the reason why I came today,” she says, “I want to quit.”

 

Andrea laughs. Loudly, right to her face. “You can’t quit,” she says, chuckling.

 

“I don’t want to work here anymore,” Kara says, trying not to raise her voice but knowing the anger is being heard in it anyway. “I don’t like the Catco you’ve created. I understand you want to make money, Andrea, I do but I don’t respect it. Journalism is not about that.”

 

Andrea’s laugh lines leave her face and her eyes darker at each word coming out of Kara. She pulls out a file from under her desk without saying a word— Kara’s contract, signed by her, and a red post-it arrow on a single line. 

 

“You can’t quit,” she snaps, “You signed your contract again once management was given over to me. You’re expected to work at Catco, exclusively , and not any competitor of us for at least two more years. But if you want to break that clause, go ahead, Miss Danvers, I’ll make your life a living hell and sue you into oblivion.”

 

“No, you won’t.” 

 

“And why wouldn’t I do that?”

 

“Because I’ll give you the exclusive on Supergirl’s real identity,” she says, clenching her fists. Andrea’s eyes instantly go wide and her breath itches. “When she’s ready,” Kara adds, “and only if you let me quit and go to another job without issues.”

 

Andrea looks her up and down— Kara’s not sure if she’s suspecting the truth or wondering how the hell did Kara Danvers find out Supergirl’s real identity. It’s a good deal. It’s a good enough deal that Lena agreed would be a good enough reason for Andrea to let her go if she didn’t accept her resignation letter. 

 

Kara doesn’t hate it, really. Supergirl started at Catco, she’ll end up in Catco. She just wishes it had a different CEO for it. 

 

“And how do you know you’ll actually give me the exclusive?” Andrea asks.

 

“I might have been bad at my job here,” Kara answers, shrugging her shoulders, “but I always keep my promises.”

 

“Well,” Andrea stands up and gives out her hand for Kara to shake, when she does, she says, “consider yourself fired, then.”

 

 

They figure it all out. They do.

 

It takes way longer than Kara thought it would. She couldn’t just do a livestream and announce who she is to the world, instead they had to plan.

 

They talk around the possible issues coming up with her outing herself for months. 

 

Kara talks about it with Alex— they make plans for the moment anyone tries to hurt Alex or her family. Especially for Esme. They do backups of each plan and spend a lot of sister nights going over every single thing that could complicate their life in the future. 

 

And they stop as soon as Kelly would walk in on them still talking at 3AM and say doom thinking will help no one and go into a rant about how terrible it is for their mental health.

 

Kara lets Brainy and Lena poke her with needles and measure her a million times so they could make a complete set of new supersuits ready. She watches as they make one for everyone else in the team, too.

 

Kara discusses with Nia how her Supergirl interview will go, how they’ll handle superheroes quotes from now on, how articles together will work now that they don’t work in the same place. They’re tiring days, sometimes, but she holds each moment Nia gives her and each adjustment in her life she does for Kara very dearly.

 

Kara and J’onn go see a friend of his and figure out most of the things the government will ask of Supergirl, now that they’ll know who she is. 

 

She’ll have to fly from airport to airport and sign her arrival— except in a world crisis. Kara Danvers would be held responsible for any and all broken things during a Supergirl save. She’ll have to revalidate all her credentials to her real name— Kara Zor-El Danvers— and a house of El emblem would show up in the corner of it, so everyone that sees it would be aware of who they’re talking to. 

 

“You wouldn’t be an entity anymore, Supergirl,” the man says, “You’d be a person . And a person is reachable by the government.”

 

She nods. And even though it all feels too big, too much, too heavy— there’s a thrill of excitement in her heart. 

 

 

Kara’s seen this view a million times. It is, probably, the first view she truly saw of Earth— not the desolate desert her pod landed in but the greens of the Midvale trees and the blue of its lake and the yellow-ish of the sand on its shore. She’s leaning against the railing of the balcony, her fingers aimlessly moving along the telescope. 

 

She hears the sound of the door unlocking and she looks up to see Lena walk into the porch, adjusting one of her knitted winter cardigans around her frame. She smiles at Kara and leans into her— Kara instantly wraps her arms around her, pulling her closer and keeping her warm. 

 

“How are you feeling?” She asks.

 

“Surprisingly good,” Kara says, closing her eyes and taking in the moment.

 

“Yeah?”

 

“Yeah,” she places a kiss on the crown of Lena’s head, “It’s gonna be insane from now on, for a while. But… I think it’ll be amazing.”

 

“It will,” Lena confirms, placing her hands on Kara’s cheeks, “because everything about you is amazing.”

 

They smile, lost in each other’s eyes. Lena looks away when she hears Esme shirk in excitement and the following laughter in the living room. Kara chuckles. 

 

“I guess Alex’s beach proposal went well,” she says. 

 

Lena rolls her eyes with a smile. “Kelly was obviously gonna say yes,” she says, “she told Nia and I she’s been suspecting since we handled Nyxly.”

 

“Yeah, Alex was really excited so she wasn’t very subtle,” Kara laughs. She moves her hands to Lena’s hips and clears her throat. “So, Miss Luthor, would you like to be my date for the wedding?” 

 

“I’d love to, Miss Zor-El,” she says, smiling so big Kara hopes the little wrinkles forming on the side of her face never go away, just to prove Lena is happiest with her. Lena kisses her, deep and strongly, her hands on the back of Kara’s neck. 

 

“Would you two stop kissing?” Nia says, showing up on the door, “The interview is about to start.”

 

They walk into the living room, hand in hand. Alex, Kelly and Esme are sharing the big couch— Kelly’s brand new engagement ring shining just as much as their smiles. J’onn and Eliza are on the chairs, talking while they share a cup of tea. And Nia is pulling Brainy away from Alex’s and Kara’s Star Wars films collection and sitting down in one of the armchairs, leaving the other for Kara and Lena.

 

They’re all watching the TV as Nia and Kara show up on screen, on Catco’s living room— just two chairs and the two of them. And they all smile at Kara when she starts speaking on the screen.

 

“Hello everyone,” Kara says, with a smile, “you might know me as Supergirl or Kara Danvers but I am here to introduce myself to you, properly, truthfully. My name is… Kara Zor-El.”
