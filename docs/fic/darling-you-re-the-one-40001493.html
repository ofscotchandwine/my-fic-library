    Darling, you're the one

    Dating Lena had been as easy as breathing.
    Well, no that was a lie. Telling Lena about her feelings, the lead up to it at least, had been tortuous. Because she had already lost Lena once, and the idea of losing her (again), felt like losing Krypton (all over again). But she forced herself to do it because Lena deserved the truth and Kara could not keep avoiding her best friend because of unrequited feelings. She wanted Lena in her life, whatever capacity she saw fit. So, she confessed her feelings, (she poured her heart out, really), and the outcome was absolutely worth it. In that sense, dating Lena Luthor had been as easy as breathing.

    Except, you know, when Lena became restless everytime Kara went out to fight, and ignored her girlfriend’s more strategic approach about something, and instead went all guns blazing into battle (and more frequently than not, ended up with more injuries than necessary).

    Or when Kara didn’t quite understand why Lena needed to spend so much time at the office, or go on international trips for over a week and not let her go too, (“You have responsibilities here, Kara. It’s only for two weeks”). 

    The main thing was, that dating Lena didn’t really change the way they treated each other, worried about each other, and protected each other. But they could kiss, caress and touch each other, and that was a great upgrade in Kara’s opinion.

     

    Asking Lena to marry her, had been as easy as flying under the yellow sun. There wasn’t an elaborate plan, there were no big gestures and no flying around the world to pick up Lena’s favorites. She was simply watching Lena read on their couch one day, (the moving in together had also been a blur, she had simply stopped sleeping on her loft. The security detail of Lena’s building afforded them some privacy after revealing her secret identity, and then Lena’s apartment was their apartment). 

    Anyways, she was simply watching Lena read on their couch, a sunday afternoon, Sol bathing her with it’s light, her hair unbound, past her shoulders, thick black rimmed glasses perched on her nose, one of her socked feet under her tight, and the thick leather book on her lap, and Kara could not help herself.

    “We should get married.” She said, sitting across from her, a laptop open in front of her, the cursor blinking in the middle of an article. She saw the moment Lena registered what she’d said.

    “Is there a question in there somewhere, Ms Zor-El Danvers?” Lena teased.

    Kara smiled and stood up. She walked up to Lena, took her hands in hers and then bent her knee right in front of her girlfriend.

    “I was, you know, working, and I just saw you here. On our couch, reading your mother’s grimoire, with your glasses, your Star Wars socks, and my sweater, and it just hit me that I don’t want to spend another day without officially calling you mine, and you calling me yours. I want us to grow old together.” Kara kissed the inside of Lena’s wrist. “I want us to go to Argo and visit my parents, and I want you to show off that beautiful brain of yours, and I want to combine our houses on the remains of my planet, and then come back here and combine our houses in the eyes of the people of earth. Or the other way around, doesn’t really matter. I want to wear your crest, and you to wear mine, or maybe combine the two. Do what we always said we could do. A Luthor and a Super, changing the world, but this time as one and the same.” Kara kisses the inside of Lena’s other wrist. “So, I’m here in one knee, without ring nor bracelet, asking if by chance you might want something similar. Asking if you, Lena Kieran Luthor, would do me the absolute honor of marrying me?”

    “My darling Kara,” Lena said, freeing her hands from Kara’s and grabbing her face. “Of course I’ll marry you. Here and on the moon if you want.”

    “Is that a yes?”

    “Yes, Kara. It’s a ye–” Kara had immediately kissed her, with all of her being, just like their first kiss, trying to say everything she could not with words, because they were not enough.

     

    If she thought the public interest in her after announcing her identity was a lot, after the media got a hint that they were engaged (because Kara gave Lena an engagement ring and they got pictures of it), it was clear to Kara that her identity had been nothing in comparison. Her and Lena had to either fly or portal everywhere, to try and avoid people taking their picture. Couldn’t go out with their family for a few weeks, paparazzi were posted in front of their home and their place of work.

    But they managed to plan a small wedding ceremony in Midvale. And Kara had never been happier. She cried when her soon-to-be wife walked down the aisle and cried some more when she said her vows in Kryptonese. And she laughed when both Alex and Brainy, somehow, gave the best man's speech talking about how miserable they had been ‘ seeing the two most genius idiots they had ever met not figure out that they were in love with each other’. And Lena looked just as happy, or at least Kara hoped so. They danced, and they laughed and they kissed and it couldn’t have been more perfect.

     

    The first and only interview after their wedding was with Nia, of course.

    "Hey, I don't think I've ever asked how you two met." The young reporter said.

    "Oh, she was shadowing this reporter..."

    "After the Venture explosion, she was a passenger but didn't show up," Kara further explained. "And the origin of the explosion was this thing that LuthorCorp had manufactured. We were following a lead."

    "Anyways, I went on a long rant about making a name for myself outside the Luthor's, and she was listening to me like I was preaching to her God," Lena continued talking, looking into Kara's eyes. "Actually, I think part of me knew right then and there."

    Kara's face and shoulders practically melted, and the movement was picked up by the camera clearly, adoringly looking at her wife.

    "Really?" She asked in a whisper, but it was Nia's most potent voice what the mic actually picked up.

    "That you were going to end up together?"

    Lena smirked then, so pleased with herself.

    "No. That I was gonna climb that cardigan wearing beefcake like a tree."

    "LENA! Oh, my --." Kara spluttered, her cheeks crimson red. 

    The clip ended up becoming viral. And her family was not gonna let her live that one down.

     

    It's been almost a week since the viral clip that had everyone and their mother teasing Kara, because who knew that the girl of steel could end up redder than her cape?

    Anyways, Kara is not thinking much about it, and she loves her wife very much, even if she gets a kick of embarrassing her on the internet. So, it has been a little over a month since their wedding, and they're still on that honeymoon phase on a midmorning Sunday. They’re planning a trip to Argo, Lena wants to study the matrix, ‘Just in case’ and Kara wants to spend time with her parents and for them to get to know Lena. 

    Kara has been banned from the kitchen because she's eating pancakes quicker than Lena can cook them, and she's sulking on the couch when a text from Nia on the group chat pops up on the screen of her cell.

     

    Nia

    'DUDE, YOU GUYS ARE INSUFFERABLE.'

     

    It’s what the text says, and then she also links the article that Clark had written about the Venture explosion. Kara is a little confused about why she's supposedly insufferable for a few seconds until she looks at the date on the article. 

    "Hey, babe," she says with a little smile.

    "Yes, darling?"

    Kara's smile grows just a little, like everytime Lena calls her that.

    "Nia found Clark's article about the Venture."

    "Is she still teasing you about it? Do you want me to talk to her?" She asks without turning to her, still cooking bacon to perfection.

    "Nah, it's okay. But she called us insufferable because the date on the article is October 11, 2016."

    Lena visibly stops, her back rigid, and then turns around, surprise coloring her face.

    "That would make the interview… The day we met, October 10th."

    "The same day we got married, yes. Talk about coincidence, uh?"

    "Well, Kara Luthor-Danvers, you've always been my destiny, so that's hardly news."

    “Destiny implies that it has ended. Lena Luthor-Danvers, we’re just beginning.”
