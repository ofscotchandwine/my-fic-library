    Your eyes look like comin' home

    If she were bet, Lena would have a long list of things that could take away her sanity, but a baby was definitely not one of the options. Actually, it wasn't until a few weeks ago, because at 3am on a Friday with your almost 2 month old daughter crying, this was definitely her top one at the moment.

    Taking care of Lori was probably the hardest thing Lena had ever done, and it's coming from someone who had seen people die and stopped the end of world. The child would cry because of hunger, then for colic, she'd sleep for a few minutes only to cry again to have her diaper changed, then when it was nothing like that the baby would just scream because of a problem that her mother can't figure out. To say Lena was exhausted would be an understatement, all she wanted was to sleep for two hours without interruptions.

    "Lori, you're okay. You've already eaten, you have a clean diaper, I tried singing, reading stories, I've done everything. Please be quiet!" The raised tone only made Lori cry louder and she moan of frustration. "I just want to sleep!"

    Lena felt terrible, pathetic for trying to discuss with a newborn and just horrible for get so angry with her own daughter. Maybe that voice that is in her head since she found out about her pregnancy telling her that she would be a terrible mother was right, maybe she was just like Lilian, the monster everyone expected her to be. Lena Luthor being a mother could only be a kind of sadistic joke of the universe, because clearly Lori would be better with anyone else in the world than with her.

    With the free hand that wasn't holding the baby against her chest, the woman grabbed her cell phone and without thinking much looked through her contact list, only then noticing the tears that blurred her vision and ran down her face. Ridiculous, an adult who should be in control acting exactly like the child in the situation.

    "Hello, Lena?" Kara's sleepy voice sounded from the device, she hadn't even noticed she made the call. "What is happening?" The question resonated in her head, what was she doing bothering her friend in the middle of the night?

    "Lena, can you hear me?" Kara insisted with more urgency in her tone.

    "I'm sorry... I didn't mean to wake you up."

    "Don't worry. What do you need? Are you alright?"

    "I-I don't know, I just..." A sob interrupted her when she could no longer hold back her own tears.

    "Darling, breathe. I'm coming there now, just tell me if you and Lori are safe."

    "Yes, I'm sorry. I d-don't...sorry, I can't do this."

    "It's okay, don't apologize. I'll be there in a few minutes and we'll fix whatever is going on, I promise." Her answer was just a muttered "okay" that could hardly be heard over the crying.

    When Kara walked through the balcony door 5 minutes later, she found Lena wandering the room while trying to comfort a very annoyed Lori.

    "She've been crying for hours, I can't hear her screams anymore." The brunette said freaking out before Kara could say anything.

    "You already have..."

    "I've tried everything, everything! Please just make her stop."

    Kara simply took the baby without asking any more questions as her friend was clearly in no condition to explain. The blonde hurried to the bedroom and Lena could have cried even more with relief that the noise was no longer close to her ear. What kind of mother gets happy to be separated from her child? How horrible did someone have to be caring more about themselves than a baby? How could she do this alone for years if she was freaking out after a few weeks? Lena fell in the couch with thoughts about being the worst mother in the world haunting her mind. So much time with incessant noise was too much to handle, she was sure banging her own head against the wall would be more relaxing.

    But then, suddenly, everything went silent. As if magic nothing could be heard coming from the apartment, Lena instinctively held her breath for fear that the slightest noise would break the spell and the screams would return. She only dared to move when she heard footsteps towards her and Kara sat down in the couch.

    "She's sleeping in her crib. Are you okay?"

    "How did you do that? I tried for hours." Lena chose to ignore the question because she couldn't handle Kara's worry and affection at the moment without starting to cry again, she blamed it on hormones and tiredness, but her stupid romantic feelings for her best friend were probably the real reason.

    "Kelly said babies feel safe when they're wrapped in blankets like burritos. But she looked exhausted and would probably fall asleep at any moment."

    Lena felt like an idiot for not thinking of something so obvious, she had made her daughter uncomfortable for so long when the solution was simple, but before she could fall into another spiral of self-deprecation Kara put her arm around her shoulder and pull her close.

    "And you look exhausted too, darling."

    "I don't remember the last time I slept a whole night." She laid her head against the blonde's shoulder and took a deep breath for what seem to be the first time all day, feeling Kara's perfume that she loved so much.

    "Just rest now, I'll make sure everything is fine when you wake up."

    Danvers' hand went to her hair, making such a comforting caress that it was inevitable to close her eyes for a moment. She knew she couldn't just sleep there, Kara definitely wanted to go home and she should get to her own bed before Lori needed something again, but just a few seconds enjoying that moment of peace wouldn't hurt anyone.

    [...]

    When Lena woke up the sun was already breaking through the curtains of her room, how she had gotten there was a mystery that her sleepy brain couldn't solve at the moment. But it wasn't the light that woke her up, it was Lori.

    "I'm sorry for wake you up, but I think she's hungry." Turning to the side, Luthor saw Kara sitting on the bed with a guilty smile on her face, while holding the baby who fortunately was more whimpering than screaming at the top of her lungs like last night.

    Lena sat down and picked up her daughter to feed her. "What time is it?"

    "Almost 10 a.m. I wish you could sleep for a little longer, but I couldn't find any bottle to give her."

    "Wow, that's our new record for consecutive sleep time, I can't wait until it's an all night." The brunette had a small smile as she watched Lori that Kara thought was adorable.

    "Actually, she woke up once. I changed her diaper and put her back to sleep, I hope it's not a problem."

    "Problem?" Lena gave her friend an incredulous look, with a raised eyebrow and all. "Kara, this is the best thing that's happened to me in the last week, thank you."

    "It wasn't a big deal, you don't have to thank me."

    "Of course I have to, and not just because of that, you answered my call and made her sleep too. You didn't need to bother with these things, I'm the one who should be taking care of everything, yet you come and do even more than requested, thank you."

    "It really wasn't bother me, I love being with you both." Looking into those blue eyes that always made your heart skip a beat, it was impossible not to wonder if those same words couldn't carry a different meaning.

    "And I love that you're here." The smile Lena received in return didn't just give her butterflies in her stomach, it was more like a complete fireworks show.

    "You know you don't have to do everything alone, right? You can always tell me when you're tired or just want some company."

    "I don't want to put that responsibility on you."

    "I'd be happy to help more, really. I'm sure even Alex and Kelly would be willing to spend the weekend with her if you need a day off." Lena was about to disagree saying that she couldn't bother everyone like that, but she thankfully held back to let Kara finish. "Maybe then could we have a movie night or go out to dinner? It's been a while since we've done anything just the two of us."

    "I'd love to, maybe tomorrow night?"

    "I'm going to call Alex right now." Danvers said with a big smile and jumping out of bed.

    Well, maybe Lori wasn't the one who would take away her sanity – at that moment looking at her cute and calm face, Lena even felt that it wasn't an impossible task to give her a decent childhood. But Kara, with her sunshine smiles and mesmerizing blue eyes, would probably drive her crazy in the very near future.
