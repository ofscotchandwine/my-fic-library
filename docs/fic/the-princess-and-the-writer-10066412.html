The Princess and The Writer

 

 

When Kara Zor-El’s kingdom got destroyed and she was left with nothing to call home, she never could have imagined that a few years later she would find herself in the most amazing place she could ever think of.

As she stepped inside a huge room, Lena Luthor’s hands covering her eyes as not to peek, excitement and anticipation was flowing through her veins. Once Kara opened her eyes and realised that Lena guided her into the biggest library she has ever seen, the blonde took a moment to remember and be thankful to all that brought her there.

 

 

After the unfortunate event that led to Kara losing her home, she was lucky enough to find a new kingdom named Gaia and an adoptive family that took her in. Kara was thirteen at the time and adapting to new customs would have been extremely hard if not for her adoptive sister Alex. The two became inseparable really fast, and as the years went by and they had to start contributing to the society a lot of things changed, but never the bond between the two sisters.

Growing up in a new society was not easy. Kara was always being mocked for being an outsider and most of the people considered her to be different. That only led the blonde to find her love for books and stories, for the worlds where everything seemed possible. Her passion stirred some more mockery from the other children her age, but her sister was always there to fight anyone who was mean to Kara, and to defend her no matter what.

That brought the two sisters to the capital of the kingdom some years after, each one pursuing her dreams. Despite her love for the healing arts that Alex picked from her mother, or maybe because of it, the older Danvers became a member of the royal guard, tasked with preventing threats to the kingdom.

Kara’s dream always was to be a writer, but before she could be a successful one she had to pick up a different profession to provide a living. The closest thing to what she really wanted to do was to become a scribe, so that was how she got to be a member of the royal court. Her profession was more than a decent one as she was not conscripted, did not have to pay taxes. Her daily tasks included things that ranged from keeping historical and juridical records for the royal family and various noble ones, to copying books and documents or other secretarial duties.

The biggest advantage was that she had access to the kingdom’s main library any time she wished while it was open, the biggest disadvantage being the fact that she had to put up with her superior’s love to interrupt her small pleasures. The master-scribe, Lady Grant, loved using Kara as her personal apprentice and have her run the most ridiculous of choirs every day.

Even though she complained about her job, Kara managed to meet two of her best friends because of it. Winn started as a messenger, even though his passion was to work as a blacksmith. He became one of the best messengers of the royal court and soon enough, because of his diplomatic skills and contacts, he was assigned to work close to Alex. Her other friend, James, was an aspiring knight that served as the seneschal of the royal house.

It was with their and Alex’s support that Kara started following her dream and writing a story. It was one of the days when she finished the third chapter when she lost track of time at the public library and was late to return to Lady Grant for whatever job she had for her. Realising her mistake, Kara picked up the latest book she was reading, the parchment with her story, some other papers she had and hurried out of the main hall. In her haste she did not see the person coming from around the corner and she ran straight into the brunette that was approaching. It was a miracle that neither of them fell to the ground, and that the worst thing Kara did was to drop her stuff and the stranger’s, leaving what she thought was the worse first impression she could create.

Mortified by the accident she caused and pressured by the fact that she was being late, Kara did not pay attention who the beautiful brunette was. With her face red and her voice trembling the blonde ensured that the other woman was safe, helped her pick up her things and apologised before she continued running out of the library.

The brunette was left behind shouting, “You forgot your… papers,” but to no avail, the clumsy blonde was already gone. Before the brunette could pick up Kara’s things, she heard the librarian approaching in a haste, wanting to ensure that she was not harmed.

“Princess Lena,” the grey-haired woman said out of breath. “Is everything alright? Do you want me to call the guards and apprehend the terrible woman who did this to you?”

Lena was still too lost in her thoughts, thinking at those beautiful cerulean eyes and those locks of golden hair, to pay attention to the old woman.

“Your Highness, are you hurt?” The librarian tried again, this time pulling Lena out of her fantasy.

“No, that won’t be necessary, I am unharmed.”

“Is there anything else I can do for you then? Fetch you another book, perhaps?”

Lena considered the woman’s words for a moment. The expected response should have been ‘yes’, that was why she was at the library, but a quick look at what she was holding in her hand, a manuscript of some sorts, made her decide against it.

“No, I have everything I need right here,” the princess said with a smile. “That would be all.”

After the librarian took her leave, Lena slowly made her way into the main room. Most tables were occupied by various people of all ages, and a couple of tables were completely empty. Lena started walking towards the closest one until she saw something that made her change her mind. A single piece of parchment was left on the second table, and at a closer inspection the brunette proved her theory right. The writing from that parchment was identical with the one from the papers she had in her hands, and belonged to the rest of the manuscript the blonde dropped. Without wasting any more time, Lena started reading it, hoping that she would find a clue to who was the owner, so she could return it. Or that was what she told herself in order to deny that she was about to satisfy her curiosity by reading a stranger’s work without her consent.

Reading the blonde’s story did not take her too long, but soon enough, Lena found herself spending hours on it. She was so enraptured by how beautiful and sensitive the story was that she read it several times, and even found references and connections to other books she read. Realising the late hour, Lena settled for writing a letter to the writer and then leave it with the manuscript for the librarian, under strict orders that it should be picked only by the blonde when she would most probably return after it.

 

 

The next day, before her work hours could start, Kara was at the library, hoping to find the only copy of her story. When the librarian saw her enter, the old woman gave her the strangest and the most disapproving look she ever received, most probably due to the fact that she left in a haste the previous day without being certain that the stranger was alright. At first, the blonde thought that her story was lost forever, but the librarian relented and reached for her familiar looking parchments. The thing that was out of the ordinary was an additional letter attached to her work.

Slightly confused, but curious to see the content of the letter, the blonde settled down at a table and opened the mysterious piece of paper. In the moment that she started reading, Kara was awe-struck, not expecting to be from the brunette she bumped into the previous day. But more than that, what the stranger had to say really impressed the blonde.

 

“To the brilliant writer of this story,

I need to apologise for scooping into your things, but in my defence this was really great. I read a few stories in my life, but I was truly captivated by yours. The way you managed to portray each character and give the story an air of gentleness and sensitivity is remarkable.

What I loved even more was the wonderful way in which you used Ovid’s first letter in ‘Heroids’ to make a parallel between Penelope’s struggle with the absence of her lover and those of your own characters. It is not only a great point to make in the story, but it was delightful to see an author that pays tribute to the great poets and writers that came before her.

I really enjoyed reading your story over and again, and if you would not be opposed, I would like to read more of your writing. I understand that this arrangement might be odd, and that you might not want to share your work with a complete stranger, but if you do and you wish for an opinion, know that you have a faithful reader in me.

If you would like this exchange to continue, we could communicate through letters that we can leave inside Ovid’s ‘Heroids’ book. I think that would be fitting, no?

I do hope to hear from you again.

Your faithful reader”

 

Without wasting any more time Kara agreed to what the stranger was proposing, so she started writing her own letter and left it in the book the other woman mentioned.

 

 

As a few days went by, the two women continued writing letters to each other and leave them in their own safe place each day. When they were not in the actual library neither of them could stop thinking about the other and their little arrangement.

While at work one day, Kara could not stop thinking about her ‘faithful reader’. She was lost in her thoughts all day and the only thing she could think of was what if she could take a day off and spend it in the library, just to be able to see when the brunette would show up to leave another letter. As wonderful as their interaction was, she wished to be able to share and do more with the person she was certain she started falling for.

It would have taken the blonde a long time to realise that on her own, but a couple of days before, when she was transcribing an official letter from the royal house that needed to be sent to the neighbour kingdoms, Kara kept making mistakes. It also happened that Alex was having a really quiet day and she thought of stopping to see her sister, who lately seemed a little too busy all the time.

When the older Danvers tried to see what was wrong, if Lady Grant was giving her sister a hard time, Kara kept finding excuses to change the subject. Having an idea about why the blonde wanted that, Alex kept probing until she drove Kara over the edge and confessed.

“Fine Alex, you win! I… met someone?” The writer then narrowed her eyes when she saw what was playing on her sister’s face. “She’s just a friend, alright?”

“A friend. Right…”

“What are you trying to say, Alex?”

“That this new woman in your life is all that you thought about for the past three weeks, if I’m correct?”

“Three weeks and four days,” the blonde admitted with a blush.

“You’d been counting?” Alex said with a knowing smirk.

“I did no such thing!”

“Mhmm, right Kara. But there’s nothing wrong with that. Do you think you might be having feelings for her?”

“No?”

“You’re telling or you’re asking me, Kara?”

“Isn’t it too soon, Alex? And what if she doesn’t feel the same?”

“So you do feel something for her. Why would it be too soon?”

“Because,” the blonde hesitated. “The situation is a bit different.”

“Different how?”

“We uhm, didn’t meet yet? I mean we’ve bumped into each other once and that’s how we started talking, but it’s not like… I wish we’d…” Kara shied away from her sister, burying her face in her hands.

Realising that the blonde needed her support, Alex went around the desk and laid her hand across Kara’s back. “Then why don’t you tell her that?”

“Tell her what?” Kara asked, looking up at her sister with red eyes.

“That you would like to meet?”

“That’s… I don’t know if that’s a really good idea, Alex. I mean…” The blonde trailed off once more.

“It’s alright, Kara. Whenever you are ready.”

Realising that Alex was right before and that she really wanted to meet Lena, Kara wrapped out her things and went to the library. It was a late evening, the place was almost deserted – save for a few people she came to recognise as regulars – but nothing could have stopped the blonde from writing her message, and that also included her own insecurities. 

By the time she finished, at least a couple of hours later, it was already night and the library was closing down in a few minutes. Kara read her final version of the letter once more, throwing all of the others away, took a deep breath and then folded it, ready to place it into their spot. When she opened the book something unexpected happened as a letter fell down, a letter holding Lena’s own beautiful handwriting.

Realising that she did not check if she had anything expecting her before starting her own letter, Kara eagerly opened the piece of paper Lena left for her and quickly read it. Even more shocking was the fact that the brunette was asking her the same thing, if she would want to meet up, two days from now in front of the private library of the royal family.

The two women often had the same opinions and similar ideas, but this time it could not have been a coincidence. Confident that it was some sort of a sign, Kara opened her letter and quickly explained what happened to the brunette, also writing the answer Lena was hoping for, which was a big ‘yes’.

 

 

Lena got to their meeting place earlier that she should have. She picked the spot because she knew how much Kara loved literature and stories. The royal family had a massive library inside the castle where they were holding a vast number of books, including original works from some of the authors Lena knew were the blonde’s favourites. That was why she knew that it would be a perfect spot for a first date. The reason why she arrived earlier was to have the time to calm her nerves, considering the fact that she never told Kara that she was a Luthor.

Apparently Kara had the same idea as the brunette because the writer was early as well. When the princess saw Kara approaching, she panicked and turned with her back, not ready to let the other woman know who she was. The princess did not agree with the way the king and the queen were ruling the kingdom, giving the fact that they did not like or approve of the outsiders from several kingdoms. There were mixed opinions about the royal family because of that, and Lena was scared that Kara would judge her for who she was.

The brunette could hear the writer coming closer and closer until they were just a few metres away. What surprised her though, was hearing the same hesitancy that was flowing through her veins in Kara’s voice.

“H-Hi. Lena, right? I am… I am Kara Danvers, but you already knew that,” the blonde rambled, fact that eased Lena’s emotions. “I am glad that we are finally meeting,” the writer made a pause to assess the situation. “Unless you don’t want us to meet anymore, that’s why you don’t want to see me, right? Oh, I should go-“

“No,” Lena said instantly and slightly turned to face the hesitant blonde. Taking a deep breath to calm her nerves, the princess continued. “I don’t want you to leave.” She was still a few metres away from the blonde, taking advantage of the angle the shadow was falling at to somewhat mask her face. “It’s just that I… I’m afraid that you will regret meeting me when you’ll find out who I am.”

“I already know who you are,” the writer said confidently.

“You-you do?”

“Yes, because I know you. I know you are the most amazing and precious person I know. I know that you didn’t have an easy life, but that never stopped you from doing your best to help other people. I know that even though you had all the reasons in the world, you didn’t let anything change who you wanted to be. That also didn’t stop you from finding beauty in everything around you: in the nature, in the way the sun rises each morning, in the way the rain makes the world stay still and gives life to everything, in the way the earth hums. I know what tea you like, how you take your coffee, how you like to sleep, the fact that you are easily tickled and that you are a huge tease. I might not know the way your lips curl up when you smile, I might not know who was your childhood best friend or what was the first word you spoke... I never looked deep into your eyes, I never had the chance to hold you in my arms like I dreamed thousands of times before, I never listened to the rhythm of your heartbeat, but I do know you, Lena!”

The princess could not believe what was happening. It was like her brain shut off all the other functions in order to be able to focus on offering all her attention to Kara and carve each word on the walls of her heart. That was why it took her a few moments to register the fact that the blonde stopped speaking and that she was able to come back to her senses. When she did, she realised that her eyes were teary and that a joyful drop was crawling down her cheek because Lena knew that Kara loved her and that they were connected even though they never met. But even so, to be finally greeted with how much love the writer actually had for her was overwhelming. Before that moment Lena never could have imagined that such a love existed and now she did not simply found that out, but that kind of love was only for her.

With her thoughts still fuzzy, the only thing she could think of were Kara’s last words and the things the writer did not know about her. Searching in her heart, all that fear and all the insecurities she had before were gone. Now the princess was confident and impatient to share more of herself with the woman who loved her.

With a big smile adorning her face, Lena spoke again. “Would you like to know all those things?”

“Only if you are ready.”

“I am,” the princess said confidently and fully turned towards the blonde. “I think we should start with this: my name is Lena Luthor.”

“Luthor as in,” the blonde stopped when the brunette stepped into the light. “You are the princess,” Kara simply stated, not allowing her surprise to drip in her voice.

But the princess misinterpreted the writer’s reaction and she was already regretting the decision to come forward. “I-I knew this wasn’t a good idea. I’m sorry, I-“

Seeing the other woman panicking Kara knew that she had to do something. Without hesitating she stepped forward, leaving only a few inches in between the two of them. “Hey, it’s okay. There’s nothing to worry about. This was what you were concern about, how would I react finding out you are the princess?”

“Well, my family… I mean we aren’t exactly the most likeable people.”

“Lena, I meant all that I previously said. The fact that you are a Luthor does not change that. You are who you want to be. That is not set in stone just because of the family you were born and raised in.”

The brunette considered Kara’s last words and everything the blonde told her and decided that she was right. “I… Thank you, Kara. You believing in me means a great deal.”

When Kara smiled back, her whole face lighting up, Lena’s first thought was to hug the blonde and before she could catch herself she was leaning in and the writer was responding to it, closing the last distance between them and returning the hug. They stood like that for several moments, no words passed between them. When they pulled back they were both reluctant to let go, but the brunette remembered about the surprise she had prepared for the other woman.

“Now, what do you say we go in?”

“Wha-what? Go in there? Are we even allowed?” Kara asked incredulous.

“Well, I am the princess after all. Who would say no to me?”

Seeing the blonde overjoyed by the prospect of getting access to the private library, Lena knew that she made the right decision.

 

 

The first time they met went really great and they were both left more taken with each other than before. Because it would have risen suspicions to have Lena meet Kara on a daily basis, the princess asked for a trained person to have as a personal assistant to entertain her love for the stories. Several people were deemed good for the job, but the brunette asked the writer she already loved to take that role. That was how they were able to see each other more often without people asking questions.

It was one of those days when the two women were in the royal library late at night, Kara writing a new instalment in her story, while Lena was reading a philosophy book. Or at least she tried, but she could not focus on St Augustine’s ‘Confessions’ while Kara was next to her, lost in creating beautiful stories. The princess tried several times to go back to reading her book, but she only ended up repeating the same paragraph several times. The way Kara’s tongue was sticking out when she was concentrated was a sight that Lena could not ignore.

The princess closed her book and got closer to Kara, who was still busy writing. She waited for the right moment, which was when Kara pulled her eyes from her papers and looked into nothingness trying to find the missing piece. Eager to know how the blonde would respond to what she had in mind, Lena called after her. “Kara?”

When the writer looked at the brunette, she was taken by surprise when Lena’s hand touched her left cheek. The brunette did not do anything else apart from asking for consent from the blonde with a silent plea and a focused look at Kara’s lips.

Realising that she desperately wanted to know how the brunette’s gorgeous lips tasted, Kara nodded her approval and made the first step to close the distance between them. Before their lips could touch though, Lena stopped and touched her forehead to Kara’s, letting their noses brush together. The princess did not want to break contact with Kara’s deep blue eyes, but her desire to kiss the woman that came to mean so much to her was definitely telling her to stop prolonging the wait.

The brunette searched Kara’s eyes for any sign of hesitancy. When she found none, she dipped in and pulled Kara’s lips between hers. The feeling was better than any time they did imagine the moment. Kara tasted sweet, like she just ate a pack of doughnuts, while Lena had a lingering taste of coffee. When the kiss ended, they were both left feeling content and wishing to do that again because of how close they got in those moments.

Before they could do that, though, Kara’s mind decided that she should ask the question that was nagging at her. “Wow, that was… wow! But uhm, I have to ask, where does this leave us? Are we… together?”

“Would you like that?” The brunette replied with a smile that put Kara’s nerves at ease.

The blonde returned the same smile before she spoke. “Very much so.”

“Hmmm, that means I can do more of this,” Lena said as she leaned in and kissed Kara again.

Before their night ended and they separated to go each to their homes, Lena had something to ask her girlfriend.

“Kara, as we are girlfriends now, I would like if you could come to the ball tomorrow. I would love to have you there.”

“As your girlfriend? Everyone will know.”

“For now, as my personal tutor and friend? I need to find a proper way to come out about us to my family before we can act as girlfriends in public. Are you okay with that?”

“Of course I am, Lena. The most important thing is that I will be with you.”

With a gentle smile and another kiss towards Kara, Lena bid her girlfriend goodbye.

 

 

The next day at the ball Kara was dressed in a simple and modest dress, but which was falling amazingly on her and it was making her look otherworldly. Lena had a beautiful dark blue gown that captivated everyone’s attention. Luckily for the blonde, her girlfriend had eyes only for her from the moment she stepped down the stairs.

Unfortunately, Lena could not spend as much time with her girlfriend as her wanted because her mother had other plans for her. She forced Lena to meet a prince from another kingdom. Mon-El of Daxam was visiting their kingdom much in the same way Lex was visiting theirs, in order to forge an alliance between the two kingdoms. The prince was quite taken by the brunette and asked her for several dances, while Kara was left on the side to watch how the guy was doing the thing that she was supposed to do, dancing with her girlfriend.

The only thing that distracted Kara and stopped her from going into the middle of the ballroom floor was Alex, who managed to keep her temper – and jealousy –  in check, reminding her that Lena chose her not some spoiled guy.

In the end, neither women could wait for the ball to be over and meet back to their place, as Lena suggested halfway through the night when she got wrapped up in entertaining the prince. Before the brunette could take her leave, thought, the queen herself stopped her.

“Lena?” Lillian called after her daughter.

The princess was already exasperated to be met with even more interruption, but she hid that well from the queen. “Yes, mother?”

Lillian pointed for a place on the ottoman next to her, and Lena had to take the seat. “Tell me, what did you think of Mon-El?”

“Well, he spoke well of our kingdom and of this alliance. Whatever Lex is doing seems to be working, as I think that the prince realises that this is for our mutual benefit.”

“It’s not Lex’s doing, Lena. As good as he is at politics and diplomacy, what tipped the scale towards this alliance is the prince’s fascination with you. He came earlier to your father and I and asked for your hand in marriage in the exchange of our kingdoms working together. We accepted.”

In the moment her mother finished talking, Lena was shocked beyond recognition. She could not believe what was happening or that her parents betrothed her to a stranger. Seeing her daughter’s sombre and horrified expression, Lillian continued.

“Come on, Lena. He seems like a nice young man and you know the importance of this. You have a duty to your people to uphold. Plus, it could always be worse. He is at least your age, good looking, he seems to like you, and with the years you will grow to love him too.”

When Lena finally spoke, she could not keep the anger and disappointment out of her voice. “And I am what, just some bargaining chip for you? What I feel doesn’t matter if it solves all your political problems? Well, he can go to hell and take his alliance with him because I.WILL.NOT.MARRY.HIM!”

“You don’t have a say in this, Lena. It’s already been decided.”

“Like hell I don’t!” The brunette stood up and started walking out of the door.

“Where do you think you are going, young lady? Come back.”

“None of your business,” the princess shouted back as she stormed out of the room.

By the time she got to the library, Lena was all red with anger and every bone in her body was shaking. When Kara saw her approaching, she rushed to her girlfriend, worry pulling at her face.

“Hey, hey, Lena… What happened? Are you okay?”

“I am-,” were the only two words the brunette could speak before she collapsed into the strong arms of her girlfriend.

Realising that she had to help the princess calm down first, Kara held her in her arms, running soothing circles on her back, whispering gentle and reassuring words in her ear, occasionally interrupted by small kisses.

“Shhh baby, I’ve got you. I’m here. It’s all going to be alright. I’m not leaving you, honey. I’m not leaving you…” Kara kept repeating like a chant, thing that managed to relax Lena slightly.

The brunette’s crying receded to occasional sobs and her breathing almost evened up. When she felt the blonde’s gentle fingers brushing the tears from her cheeks before they could stain, she managed to unclench the hands that were gripping at Kara’s shirt and most probably already left deep marks on the writer’s back.

Without wasting another moment thinking, Kara scooped Lena off her feet and carried her inside the library, where she laid her in top of her own body on the available divan. After the sobbing stopped, Lena managed to gather her thoughts enough to speak, her voice cracking on the words. “Thank you, Kara.”

“Oh honey, you don’t have to thank me. I told you I am always here when you need me or feel ready to talk.”

“I don’t think I’ll ever be ready to talk about this, but you need to know.”

Kara’s response of encouragement came as a tighter hug, and Lena took that as a sign to continue, her heart feeling slightly lighter. “Do you remember the prince of Daxam being present at the ball tonight?”

“Is he the guy who danced with you the whole night and didn’t give me the change to dance with my girl?”

“You wanted to dance with me?” Lena left all the talk about Mon-El and focused on more pleasant things.

“Of course I wanted to dance with you. There was nothing more that I wanted to do tonight. Oh, maybe apart from this,” the blonde leaned in and kissed her girlfriend’s forehead.

That put a smile on Lena’s face. No matter what the world threw at her, she always felt comfortable and safe around Kara, and especially in Kara’s arms. Being around the woman made Lena feel hopeful about a future together, and despite all that happened that night, for some unexpected reason the brunette was convinced that they will figure out a solution together. That gave her the strength to continue. “Yes, that was the prince. Who, after the ball ended, went to my parents and asked for my hand in marriage. Of course they didn’t even think asking for my opinion and considered that their precious alliance was at stake, so they made the decision for me. I am to be wed with Mon-El of Daxam.”

Hearing Lena’s words, the blonde’s own got stuck in her throat and she was left speechless. Seeing the panic in those beautiful blue eyes, the princess continued.

“I don’t plan on marrying him, Kara.”

The writer managed to look down at the beautiful woman in her arms and meet her gaze before speaking. “Do you even have a choice?”

“They can’t force me to say yes to him, Kara. I simply won’t do it!”

Kara spoke several moments after considering all the options and the consequences of all the possible decisions. “But the alliance with Daxam is at stake, Lena, and if you don’t do this, you have no idea what your parents will be willing to do to you. They will force you to marry him, so you might as well-“

“DO.NOT finish that sentence! You can’t actually believe that I would do that, do you?!” Lena suddenly pulled back from Kara’s arms.

The man stepped on the hidden door and Kara had to gulp out of fear to be discovered.

“Were you out in the woods all day?”

“Most of it, yes.”

When he heard Kara’s answer, the guard turned towards her, getting away from the place Lena was hidden.

“Did you happen to see a woman, slightly shorter than you, long dark-brown hair?”

You have to let me go, Kara.”

With a last kiss on the blonde’s forehead the princess was gone, and Kara was frozen on the spot, emotionless, not able to stop her girlfriend from leaving.

When Lena stepped outside of the door she collapsed on her knees and broke down crying. After she gathered enough strength to walk, she headed for the main road to turn herself in.

 

 

Two weeks went by and both women were back in the castle. Lena did not require the skills of a certain scribe anymore, so the two did not meet since their goodbye back into the woods. “Winn, what are you doing here?” Kara asked confused by the sudden visit.
