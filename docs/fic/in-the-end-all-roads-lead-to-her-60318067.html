    Chapter 1

    What a very interesting word, which holds so many meanings, so much culture, so much weight. So much fear. It is fascinating how such a small word own so much power. Death is not for everyone and when you are surrounded by it, it is even more difficult to find someone willing to stay by your side. Until one day, after wandering for centuries alone, with everyone terrified by your presence because you are the personification of death, you find someone for whom death is as common and fascinating as it is for you, and everything changes.

    She was young, beautiful even with her face bathed in guilt for the atrocity she had just committed. Free from the bonds to which the woman she once trusted subjected her, she approached her lifeless body.

    “It was not your fault” I said, interrupting her farewell. A serious mistake, I had a job, a method, collect souls without intervening. Simple. For a second I thought she hadn’t heard me, she turned around and the pained expression was gone, giving way to a cold mask as her eyes met mine.

    “I know. She was always ashamed of me”

    “Why?” I asked with genuine curiosity. One I wasn’t used to.

    “Because I’m different. She hated me from the moment she found out”

    “I can see that”

    “You’re not scared”

    “Should I?”

    “Why are you here?” she asked me starting to understand.

    “I think you know”

    The girl cautiously stepped forward. “I’ve heard stories about you. I never thought you were real”

    It could be the effect of the lack of company or my sense of humor was completely lost, because I couldn’t help but laugh at her comment. “You’re a witch, who just murdered her entire coven absorbing their powers. And you think Death doesn't exist”

    “I'm more of those who need to see to believe”

    “Then it's lucky that I decided to materialize in front of you”

    “Oh, really?” her lack of fear was impressive.

    “Yes, Agatha Harkness. I like what's different. I'm different too”

    I broke eye contact, I shouldn't stay long, I had already lost too much time. It was also the first time I allowed someone to observe my work. I saw curiosity in her eyes.

    “Good luck, Agatha”

    “Wait! What's your name?”

    I laughed again. “Is that what you ask?”

    “You know mine. It seems fair to me”

    “Life is unfair” I looked into her icy blue eyes for the last time before disappearing.

    “True. Luckily we are the opposite of life” the young woman smiled with her gaze fixed on the place I had occupied.

    Time.

    It's something I'm not used to. Time doesn't pass for me the same way it does for humans. How long had it been until I saw her again? I couldn't say for sure. What I was sure of was that it was too long.

    “This is what it took to get your attention,” she said without turning around. A similar scene to the first time we meet at her feet. The rest of the coven's witches lay lifeless around her. What are you up to, Agatha Harkness? I asked to myself.

    “Did you want to see me again that badly?”

    “Please,” she replied sarcastically. “I don't even know your name.”

    “You do know my name,” I began. “I'm Death.”

    “Nonsense. I want a real name. Something like, Rio Vidal”

    “Rio Vidal” I repeated amused at her name choice. “That’s totally opposite to me”

    She shrugged. “I like it”

    “Sorry to disappoint you, my name is Death. Throughout history I have been called many things, none of them are what you are looking for”

    “Too bad. I hope the next time we meet you have one that suits me” she replied with a mischievous smile on her lips. “I’ll let you do your job”

    You already had my attention, Agatha. There was no need to perpetuate it. I thought to myself.

    “I was starting to get worried” she strike, her power so big now she can feel my presence.

    “I’ve been busy”

    “Death never stops” she commented sarcastically. “I’m glad you find time to visit me”

    “How long has it been?”

    Witches are almost similar to me when it came to time perception.

    “About half a century since the first time. Why? You missed me” she asked raising her eyebrows mischievously.

    “No” I lied maybe a little. “I’m curious, why you do it. Absorb power, so often. What do you seeking, Agatha?”

    “I was wondering if you already had a name for me” she asked instead.

    “You ended the life of your coven just for that? To see If I had an answer for you”

    “Maybe” she replied in a cold voice.

    “Don’t lie to me”

    “They found out. Who I am, what I can do with my power. They tried to destroy me” her expression of total indifference returned. “The good thing is that I get to see you” her expression softened accompanied by a small smile. “You’re the only one who doesn’t fear me. You’re the only one who doesn’t run away from me”

    “What do you want, Agatha?”

    “I want someone who will stay. By my side. Someone who understands me”

    “Anybody?”

    She smiled halfheartedly. “Fine, Death. I want YOU to stay.”

    The statement boosted my ego, I won’t deny it. However, the feeling of victory didn’t last long.

    “I’ll take that as a no.”

    “Death is my job, Agatha. I can’t stay in one place for long.”

    “It doesn't matter. We can figure something out” she waved her eyebrows with those beautiful blue eyes, I knew in that moment I could never say no to her.

    So we did, for over a century. It was funny, how I understood, time is an illusion.

    Happiness.

    Was a feeling I didn't believe it really existed. It was different from the thrill of knowing I was gonna see Agatha again. It was certain of knowing that from time to time I could go home, that I have a place to call home, and she was expecting me.

    For the first time since my first steps in this world I could say I was happy.

    We live together, we traveled together. We even bound to each other sacred by earth and blood. That's when it happened. You see, Agatha always want more. She wants everything.

    She had power, she had me, just one thing was missing. I swear I could never say no to her. Not even a petition for a child. But because of our nature, her desire was impossible to fulfill. A child from Death herself. That was crazy. We did it anyway, knowing the risks, knowing that I couldn't do anything to stop it from happening. We thought we will have more time, but we didn't. So she start running from me.

    But you can't run away from death. She begged me not to. It was my son too! I was in pain and I couldn't do anything to stop me. Maybe one thing, I knew it was wrong, I knew that if more time passed the pain and grief would be so much worse. But I couldn't stole it from her. From us. So I gave us time.

    You should had meet him. Nicholas Scratch. Beautiful boy, just like her mother. Brown wide eyes, dark hair, a small pretty nose. He loved bed time stories, music and was obsessed with bunnies.

    Even if she didn't want me close to him because she was worried he could hate me and she already hated me, I was the one who was gonna take him away. He didn't. He had so much love in his heart. I will always be surprise for that.

    But when death is your job, when you are the personification of death, feelings have no part in the deal. There was not an easy way to do it.

    Death sobbed.

    I'm sorry, I got sentimental for a second. One day my biggest fear come true. We messed with forces bigger than us. Even for me. And I'm a cosmic been! Nick die and it was my job to pick up his soul. Agatha was sleep, they both were, I think it was for the best. It wasn't.

    And as she promised, she has hate me ever since. Because in the end, all roads lead to me.

    “Listen, weird lady. You are pretty and all” The blonde snap her empty drink on the bar table for the bartender refilled it. “but I said I'm not interest in you stupid story”

    “Ugh” Death gasp. “I liked you when you were a child murderer. I worship you in your flirting assassin era. But now you are just sad and depressed”

    “Good. Now leave me alone” Yelena tried to occupy another sit far from the strange lady but fail tripping over her own feet. She was too drunk to find a new place to continue drinking, and killing the lady wasn't an option either.

    “And drunk” Death added sarcastically.

    “How is this relevant? You are drunk too. Can't you be drunk and quiet?”

    “Sit Yelena. Before you fall” the blonde assassin was ready to leave again, or at least tried. “Sit, Yelena and listen” Death's voice came as a sharp order. Yelena obeyed immediately.

    “At least pay a round”

    “I don't carry money with me. You're paying for both”

    “Great” She took another shot.

    “I met you when you were seven. I was doing my job while you finished a man's life. I was shocked, such a good technique at such a young age. The clean cut on his throat. I was amused. Truly. So I followed you. I had nothing better to do anyways. I couldn't found my wife because she was using the darkhold dark magic to hide from me” Death declared with anger.

    “What?” Yelena frowned her eyebrows lost by all the new words.

    “We are witches, little assassin, keep up”

    “Are you even real?”

    Death ignored her. “It was a shame you had to work for that horrible man. Dreykov. Amazing job killing him by the way. My heart stopped when you used your stick to blow off his jet. Luckily your sister was there and saved you from that fall. It would had hurt me pick up your soul after that performance and even more because of what happened next when she showed up” Yelena froze knowing what part of the story they were getting to. And what she Death was referring. “First I lost track of you for a while, I'm guessing you blip. Don't worry I don't blame you, Hideous Thanos is the only I blame for my boredom”

    ”I'm glad to hear it“ Yelena replied ironically. ”Do you... Oh God I most be going mad. Did you pick my sister's soul?“

    “Good question. No. Oh, what a soul to possess. Sadly she died out of my jurisdiction. Her soul belong to the infinity stone”

    “Right. This is crazy. What are we doing?”

    “Oh, no. It was beautiful. All the pain. The lies. Being hired to kill you sister's murderer. I remember like it was yesterday. The tension, the hate, the seek for revenge. And there she was. A clean shot. No matter what you say she is right she could had kill you if she had made the shot. I even whispered to her 'Do it'. I'm pretty sure she hear me. But then you looked at her” Death moaned scaring Yelena.

    “What the fuck. You're sick”

    “Oh. I which you would have seen the look on your face. I thought for a second you were gonna kill her. But no, my little assassin was in love!” Death waited. “You are not gonna denied it”

    “What for? I'm probably dead”

    “You're very much alive”

    “Then what's the point of all this suffering?”

    “I am getting to that!”

    "Ok. I get it We are the same. We are death, we are doom. Happy"

    "No. We are not the same. Death is my job, If I don't fulfilled balance will be lost. For you death is a task" Death stared at her drink before finished. "I found her" Yelena frowned her eyebrows. "My wife. She is in a small town under the spell of a dead witch. It's stronge. It's not my territory so I can't break it. And maybe it is for the best. A lot of dangerous people are after her. At least I can pay her visits, because she doesn't remember me. Even though, she still hates me. She doesn't remember why exactly. We are not the same, Yelena. You can choose. You choosed not to kill the old archer. You can change. You can be saved"

    "Death herself came to give me advice. Should I feel important?" Death sighed standing up. "Where are you going"

    "To visit my wife. Today is our anniversary, I maybe bring her flowers and with a bit of luck we will fight as we used to"

    "You are crazy"

    "Perhaps. The thing is Yelena. For Death, all roads lead to her" Death shrugged. “For me it is my pookie misunderstood witch. For you is a sexy archer with an extreme fascination for pizza and the color purple“

    ”What if she never forgives you? She hates you“

    ”What is love without hate. That's the thing between you two. She doesn't hate me, she hates herself for falling in love with the one who hurt her. She is afraid of facing her fears. She is afraid of facing Nikky. You are afraid too. Afraid of a life you think you don't deserve. Afraid of hurting the one you love. Maybe it's time for you both to face yours fears“

    Yelena raised an eyebrow. “More like the Goddess of Death you should be the Goddess of Love”

    “Not a goddess that's someone else's title. And believe me, you don't want to meet her. But what can I say, I'm the romantic type. And what's more romantic than death itself. I hope the next time I see you You are in better company than a vodka shot”

    “I hope you and your wife work things out”

    Death nodded. "Te veo"
