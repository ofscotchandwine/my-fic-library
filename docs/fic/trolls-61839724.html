    original post: https://x.com/sobercatnip/status/1874011132572385455 It was on the morning of December 25th that Agatha found Rio curled up on the bathroom floor. The former baseball player, now a successful entrepreneur in the sports world, had been complaining of pain since the 24th. However, things worsened in the early hours of Christmas. Agatha put on her warmest clothes and drove to the Westview municipal hospital. A few hours later, Agatha waited in a hospital room; the case had already been assessed. It seems that the former athlete had eaten a lot of low-quality food and drank very little water since she retired, which caused gallstones and the absolute certainty of their uselessness. Rio was 45, no longer so young, and it seemed that these things were normal.

    — Mrs. Harkness Vidal? called the doctor. Agatha lifted her face from the gossip magazine, anxious for an answer.

    — The surgery went well, she’s stable and is on her way to the room. It’s important to maintain a regular diet from now on, as people without a gallbladder have a higher chance of gastrointestinal problems.

    — This will be monitored. I appreciate it so much, Agatha said. Before she could finish, two nurses entered, carrying Rio on a stretcher. Gently, they placed her in the bed. Rio’s face was serene, and she was in a deep sleep. Agatha walked over to the bed and caressed her lover’s face, tucking a strand of hair behind her ear.

    A few hours later, Agatha was engrossed in the gossip magazine more than she’d like to admit, until her attention was drawn by a moan. Rio had woken up and was disoriented.

    — My love, how are you feeling? Agatha stood up. Rio looked at her, confused, slowly stretching out her hand. She grabbed the plastic knife from the hospital food, which Agatha may or may not have nibbled on.

    — Oh, really? — Exactly, I’m married, very well married, Rio said, showing her wedding ring. Agatha walked over to her and tucked her hair back. — She... she could sue you. She’s a very important lawyer...

    — Hi, love. How are you feeling? Rio began to cry, and Agatha stood up startled.

    — My dear, what’s wrong? Are you in pain? Do you want me to call the doctor? — Sorry! I cheated on you, Rio cried. A look of confusion spread across Agatha’s face. — With the nurse! said the ex-player, crying.

As the car moved through the quiet streets of the city, Rio looked out the window, watching the lights of dawn reflect off the trees still covered in frost. She held Agatha’s hand over the gearshift, intertwining their fingers.

    — Thank you for everything, Aggie. For being here with me... Always.

    Agatha squeezed her hand and gave her a brief but meaningful smile. — I will always be here, my love.

    When they arrived home, the warm comfort of the house surrounded them.

Rio looked at Agatha, who was busy preparing a tray with tea and some fruit. Despite her exhaustion, her heart was full. Everything she needed was right there: her loving wife, her noisy kids, and a future that, though uncertain, seemed promising with that support.
