    A Lovely Day For A Tea Party

    The first time it happened, Lena smirked to herself. She knew pretty well that early in the day Kara and her superfriends had been executing a plan to capture a rogue alien. That failure was playing on all the news channels since then. So, the lone, pristine box on her office's desk at 8 am sharp only meant that Kara had woken up at an unholy hour to fly to Dublin to bring them to her. And everything was according to plan. She didn't bother to eat the scones, how delicious they were was irrelevant; they represented lies and deception, disguised as sweets and innocence.  

    Mere hours later she received Alex's call asking for help with a green martian, that only accelerated the progress of her plan. Everything was going better than expected.

     

    Kara doesn't mention her transatlantic pastry delivery that day. Neither does Lena.

     

    The pattern has repeated every day since. Everyday Lena arrives at her office and finds a pristine, somehow still warm, box of scones. It starts to unnerve Lena. It had been a too sincere comment that had slipped from her lips and now Kara had taken it as atonement, she supposes.

     

    ~~~~~~~~~~~

     

    "You brought me coffee eclairs from Cafe Rue Serret on the Champs-Elysées?" Lena asks, incredulous. 

    "I know they're your favorite." Kara quips, shrugging, as it was as simply as crossing the street to the closest noonan's.

    "And the cappuccinos, did you really go to Pave in Milan?" 

    "It didn't take me that long." Kara moves her hand in a dismissive manner, again, like it was nothing.

    "You know, I have really been missing out on the perks of having Supergirl as a best friend." Lena laughs, at this moment the phrase doesn't hurt as much.

    "Well, we have a lot of lost time to make up for." The kryptonian promises, open and sincere.

    "Yeah, but you know you don't have to go to Paris to pick up lunch, Kara." Lena rattles, her words too real, said without really thinking. "That's not what's important to me." She smiles, her heart breaking again by the reminder of years of lies and deception. "But, these scones from Dublin, oh, my God, you can fly these in everyday." They both chuckle at that. Lena lifts a pastry and takes a bite. "Mmm. So, tell me, how is CatCo?"

     

    ~~~~~~~~~~~

     

    Kara's irish imported bribing had not deterred Lena's plan to launch non-nocere as soon as possible. Humanity needed to get rid of their deceptive behavior, and she would be the Luthor to make humanity better by making them honest. It had already worked with an avenging green martian, she could do this. She only needed something to reach the global population.

     

    It all came to a head when Kara catches her trying to leave the Fortress of Solitude with Myriad.

    "Well, you caught me." She says dropping her happy act. "I have Myriad because I'm using you like you used me."

    Kara looks as if she'd sustained physical damage. "I never used you." She says, too quick for it to be true.

    "Do you remember when you finally told me you were Supergirl?" Lena is done holding back, she will let Kara know exactly how her betrayal has broken her. "You were weeping. Big crocodile tears. Well, I wept real tears, bitter tears over you weeks before."

     

    It wasn't pretty, she screamed herself hoarse, and Kara looked so distraught that it would have broken Lena's heart, if it weren't already irremediably broken. 

    She continued with her plan, using a portal to Mount Norquay, to finish what she'd started. She would make humanity better, she had anything else to lose, after all.

    "Lena." The voice interrupted her progress. She tried to not let her affect her, but then again, she's doing all of this because of Kara's lies.

    "Supergirl." She answers coldly.

    Kara begs.

    "Save your breath. You showed me who you really are, and I believe you." She says succinctly.

    "You have every right to say that, but don't do this." 

    "Do what?" Lena asks, ready to defend her estance, against the super-powered alien.

    The blonde had crocodile tears all over her face, again, and it pissed Lena even more. She hates that tears sprout on the corners of her eyes. 

    Kara's hologram was incredibly uncanny, it was suffocating Lena with its proximity, even when it wasn't the real deal. Kara does her entire speech, all hope and compassion. To Lena is just an admission of guilt. Lex's words echo in her mind, how foolish she'd been not only not seeing past glasses and a ponytail, but also how many things she had done only because Kara asked with a smile. And what others she had done only to not be seen like evil incarnated by a solar powered goddess, only for none of it to matter at the end. She would always be the untrusted Luthor, even to her supposed best friend.

    "I am good, and I always was. Lex didn't change me and you didn't change me either. You just exposed me to the ugliness of humanity." She says instead. "You did me a favor, Kara. I learned what kind of deceitful person you really are, and that's what inspired me to do this."

    "Lena…" 

    Kara says her name like it's a last resort prayer, and she has to turn around and stop looking at Kara's handsome face if she wants to finish what she started. "Your words mean nothing to me anymore. Spare us both the drama. Leave me alone." She clicks a few buttons in quick succession to cut communication with the Super.

    "Wait–." 

    The silence is loud, and a tear finally makes its way to Lena's cheek. She quickly wipes it away, there is work to do and no time to waste.

     

    Her plan doesn't work. Not only had Kara deceived her planting a virus using her hologram as a trojan horse and stalled her, but she had managed to fry the whole thing. Non-Nocere is dead, and humanity keeps being as corrupt as ever. She manages to escape jail by making Hope take the blame. She just needs time to think of her next move. It's incredibly surprising when the next day, there is a new box of Irish scones in her desk, next to a still warm to-go cup of coffee. Also new is the very succinct two worded note next to it. 

    'I'm Sorry.' 

    The familiar handwritten words were stamped on one of her own post-it. And Lena could not decide if that was more or less insulting. Besides the fact that she had no idea what sin was Kara apologizing this time. The lies? The new deception with the virus? Not actually catching her?

    Lena couldn't actually reach a conclusion, because the entire world was at stake and as usual the superfriends needed her help. Of course she helped, without even touching the scones again.

     

     

    As soon as she opened her eyes she knew something was wrong. First of all she was on her couch, she would only sleep there by accident. Even more worrisome was the fact that the last thing she remembers is the actual end of the world. It all comes crashing down when Lex's slimy voice floats to her. She simply can't believe it after everything she's done to crawl from under his shadow, his power over her, here he is. As if Lena had not put two bullets in his chest.

    "What matters now is that a very powerful person brought me back to life to help." Lex says, one run-on sentence from monologuing. "But I refused to do so, unless he promised that you would make it through okay, and you have, with your noggin intact."

    Lena doesn't believe that was really his only stipulation, but she lets him have that. There are more pressing matters. "You gambled the fate of the entire multiverse to get your own way?"

    "It gets better. In this new world, we work side by side at Luthor Corp and the DEO to make the world a better place." Her brother is so full of himself is revolting.

    "So I'm in hell."

    "No, no, you're in a new reality. To the outside world, we're billionaire philanthropists. It's the perfect cover."

    "I want nothing to do with you." And she means every word.

    Sadly it's easier said than done.

     

    The kicker comes that same day, a little closer to noon, when a repentant looking Supergirl lands on her office's balcony. Lena can hear her brother's voice in the back of her head like a radio, beckoning her to not listen to whatever thing the kryptonian had to say, and listen to him instead. So she does her best to at least try to do whatever Lex wouldn't want her to do.

    "What did you think would happen when you came here?" She asks, when Kara tries to explain crisis to her. Her hurt being more prevalent than her distrust of Lex. "That you'd tell me everything in a fit of selflessness, even if it meant that I knew how you betrayed me, and then I'd just keel over and forgive you."

    "Lena, I hurt you. I– I know I hurt you." Kara has her hands on her back, and it's weird not seeing her fidget with them. "And I'm never ever going to do it again. And I know how much good you want to do in the world. Just think of what we could do together to help people."

    Lena quirks a challenging brow "Funny. Lex gave me the same pitch."

    "He asked you to help him?' Lena says nothing, and Kara sighs. "You're not seriously considering it, are you?" Lena glares at the question, and Kara grimaces when it finally dawns on her that it was a terrible question to ask. "Please, just be careful." She says, low and timid. Then she gets closer to Lena and brings forward a brown bag. "I suppose I don't have to explain this one, then." When Lena doesn't take the bag, she deposits it on the desk. "The cafe doesn't exist on this earth, but these scones are even better in my opinion." Kara tries to smile, but it's not but a shadow of her usual one. "Hope you like 'em." 

    Kara leaves the same way she arrived. The only trace of her presence were the wind that ruffled Lena's hair and the insistent, warm bag of Irish scones.

     

     

    She starts tentatively working alongside Lex, because he's predictable and she knows how he works. At least for once in their lives he's been upfront about something.

    "When will you turn on me?" She asked, sitting in her office.

    Lex didn't hesitate, with the baby truth seeker latched on his arm, he had no other option than answer honestly. "As soon as this partnership inconveniences me. 

     

    She avoids Kara. And yet, the very next morning the bag of fresh, Dublin made scones are waiting for her on her desk, 8am sharp.

    It's still unnerving. She still doesn't eat them, giving the bag without any explanation to Jess.

    This earth's frequency is different, hence the Q Waves' are different too, so she's back to square one. She tells as much to her brother, and she's surprised to see him work with Brainy, who looks completely different to his usual self, to eliminate Leviathan. She decides to lock the worry for Brainy into a new box and promises to try and find out whatever she can about Leviathan from Andrea. In exchange Lex will figure out the Non-nocere problem.

    Facing Andrea, is just a reminder of all the betrayals, so even if she smiles at her, she's determined to keep that friendship at bay too. She leaves Catco, with the promised information and avoiding bumping into familiar faces. And Lex gives her her answer in the form of an alien and time traveling cube. Everything goes according to plan.

     

    The routine repeats itself again, the bag of freshly made scones waiting for her on her desk. 8am sharp. She gives them again to Jess. Everything goes just as expected.

    Except she needs a distribution method, which requires Andrea's tech. She tried to convince her to work together by creating a minor issue within Obsidian, but apparently this world's Andrea is extremely sentimental, and rather's not mix business and friendship. 

    Lex says he will figure it out, and Lena… Lena's suspicion rises to new levels. He's too content to help her,without actually doing the work in it. It's unlike him, unbecoming of him. But whatever he does, it works and gives her access to the lenses, and she could work with that.

     

    It's close to 9PM, Lena is winding down at home, making some tea, when a familiar thud makes her look up to her balcony. "Let me guess, you're here to tell me once again that I should forgive you." She tells Kara, forgetting all about her tea, and walking to her. "Or maybe that I shouldn't work with my brother?" 

    "Not this time." Kara is stoic, very different to the last few times she'd seen her. "I recognize I made a mistake in hiding my identity from you for so long, but the past is the past, and I can't change it." She doesn't enter the apartment, keeping her post on the balcony. And Lena stays inside. "Forgive me or not, that's your choice. Just like it's your choice to work with Lex. I'm done blaming myself for your bad decisions."

    The phrase makes Lena cringe. She tries her best to keep a neutral face. "Then why are you here?"

    "To tell you that from now on, you're accountable for your own actions. If you decide to forgive me, I will be there for you." Lena doesn't breathe, it's like the planet has stopped spinning. Kara keeps talking. "But if you continue to work with Lex. If you go through with whatever it is the two of you are planning, I will do everything in my power to stop you just like I would any other villain."

    It freezes Lena's blood too. Kara leaves, like she hadn't destroyed Lena in one swift swoop.

    She couldn't sleep, not even the calming chamomile and valerian root tea could help her. Mulling over Kara's words, and Lex behavior, and even her own actions, in this world and in the previous one. She got to her office earlier than usual. She got there earlier than Jess, something that hadn't happened since the very beginning of her move to National City, in this world and in the previous one. She distracted herself with paperwork she had been neglecting since working on Non-Nocere, still thinking about Kara. When she looks up, she catches the time on her computer, and she stops. 8:23 am, and there was no bag of scones on her desk.

     

     

    Lex was predictable, and that's why she started working with him. When he used the baby truth seeker, he was clearly only lonely. That didn't mean that he hadn't found a new goal. And him constantly checking on her… it was a really big, really red, red flag. Was Lena willing to keep working on Non-Nocere, and risk even the barest possibility of Lex using her tech to his benefit?

    Would she had even considered working with him if she weren't as hurt by Kara's omission? 

    The answer to both questions was a clear and rotound no.

    She told Jess to cancel all and any meeting she may have. And if her brother asked for her, to tell him that she was working from home.

     

     

    She knocked at the door, and braced herself. When Kara opened, her face was one of clear surprise. But said absolutely nothing. Took a deep breath and got back into her apartment, leaving the door wide open.

    Lena took it like a good sign, and followed her inside, closing the door. "I have made a terrible mistake." She says, fighting the tears that pooled around her eyes. "I was hurt. I was so hurt. And…" She takes a deep breath. "I thought I could get rid of the hurt. I thought that I knew better, that I could make the world a better place. But I was wrong."

    Kara was still silent. Stoically looking at her. "That hurt was taking me down a dark, dark path, where I was blind to what I was really doing. To what I was one step away from becoming." It was weird to see Kara as silent. But the words just keep spilling out of her like a river. "I never wanted to help Lex, Kara. But I know Lex, I know how he works. You? You're an enigma. And I don't think my heart could take another heartbreak."

    Lena is a few seconds from turning around and running away, when Kara finally says something.

    "Are you okay?" She quietly asks, crossing her arms over her chest. 

    "I have been better, not gonna lie." Lena almost immediately winces by her own turn of phrase. Kara either doesn't pay attention to it or simply ignores it, by searching something on Lena's face.

    And the brunette is not sure what or if she found it. Instead Kara asks; "What do you have there?"

    Lena had almost forgotten about it. She forces a smile. "They're definitely not from Dublin. And I know you prefer sticky buns, but I was… hoping that we could eat some scones." She takes a step towards the blonde, lifting the Noonan's bag. "I think it's my turn to apologize."

     

     

    Things are not easy. They still work to figure out Lex's plan, and how to get rid of Leviathan. There is no inherent trust, but there is a tentative truce between them. 

    They talk. A lot. Lena airs all of her grievances, her insecurities. Kara tries to explain herself the best she can. Sometimes they get into screaming matches when the emotions are high, until one of them reveals too much and then silence surrounds them.

    Kara then reveals stuff she'd hidden to Lena in her fear of exposing her alien status. About her planet exploding, about the phantom zone. Alex's kidnapping by someone who knew her identity. How both Lillian and Lex had used the people closest to her as leverage. She talks about an imp, and Lena understands then Kara's most basic fear. Losing. Especially losing people.

    And Lena comes to understand how most of the people who knows about Kara's powers, often forget that she's not actually made of steel.

    How, without knowing, Lena was a reprieve; someone who Kara could be just Kara. Just as Lena was just Lena with her. Not another Luthor, or a billionaire, or a CEO.

     

     

    After Lex, and Leviathan, and another not planned trip to the phantom zone, Kara brings a bag of scones from Dublin.

    "What's the occasion?" Lena asks, lifting an eyebrow.

    "I think I figured something out."

    "Do tell."

    "People don't cross an ocean to find the favorite stuff of someone who is just their friend."
