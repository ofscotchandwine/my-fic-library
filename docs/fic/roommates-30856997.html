1. Chapter 1

Kara opens the front door and enters her apartment, Lena tails behind her with a suitcase and a duffel bag - she looks lost.

"Thanks again for letting me stay in your apartment, Kara." Lena expressed.
"Of course, I wouldn't have it any other way." the blonde responded "I can't believe that Lex had you kicked out of your apartment." she continued
"I can... Me leaving Luthor-Corp means that he lost, and he hates loosing so now he's getting even by cutting me off financially"
"Can he even do that?"
"There's a lot of things my brother does that he shouldn't be allowed to do..."
"Yeah well, his lost is my gain" Kara smiles.

Kara smiles at Lena reassuringly, and Lena smiles back.

"Here, let me take those for you."

Kara takes Lena's bags and places them in her bedroom, afterwards she walks back towards Lena.

"So I mean you pretty much know where everything is, I guess the only thing to go over is..."
"Sleeping arrangements." Lena interrupted
"Yeah... So I was thinking you can take the bed and I'll take the sofa"
"Kara - I'm not taking your bed" protests Lena
"I know, I'm giving it to you" the blonde argued
"But Kara, this is your apartment and your bed - you should sleep in it not me - I don't want to be an inconvenience."

Kara moved closer to Lena.

"Lena you are never an inconvenience, you are my best friend and I want you to feel at home here, besides I wouldn't offer if I wasn't okay with it. So please, I want you to take my bed."

Kara began to pout and Lena found it incredibly hard to say 'no'.

"You know it's really hard to say 'No' to that pout of yours."
"That's why I do it" smiled Kara cheekily.

4. Kara darling, I'm gonna need full sentences.

Summary for the Chapter:

Kara is unable to keep her cool demeanour around Lena,and Lena notices.

When Kara had invited Lena to stay at her apartment she hadn't really thought it through, she was just thinking of the unfortunate position her friend had found herself in and how she wanted to change that - she really hadn't taken into consideration that she would be around Lena twenty-four seven.

Not that she minded of course - she loved spending time with Lena, it was just that she was becoming more and more unable to keep her cool demeanour around her and by that I mean Kara would often catch herself making googly eyes at Lena or rambling longer than she needed to.

It started the night Lena asked Kara for advice on the outfit she was wearing, Kara was having her fortnightly sister's night with Alex and Lena was getting ready to meet Kelly and Nia at the alien bar for drinks. Both Alex and Kara were settling in on the sofa, multiple snacks at their disposal although a few may or may not have already been in Kara's mouth.

And that's when it happened.

Lena, who had walked out in a tight fitted black dress with six inch heels, her hair down and curled, had simply asked "Kara, does this look okay?"

Kara who had half a mouth full of potato chips, looked up casually - not expecting what was about to happen but the moment she saw Lena in that dress her jaw instantly drop to the floor along with the chips in her mouth.

"Eww Kara" Alex said in disgust, pulling her out of wherever her mind had gone "Close your mouth for god's sake" her sister demand, and she did.

Lena couldn't help but smirk a little, almost as if she had planned this whole situation - almost as if she knew that this dress would make Kara speechless.

"So, does it?" asked Lena, waiting for a response and getting nothing in return "Kara?" she called out and Kara's eyes snapped onto hers "Does my dress look okay? It's not too much is it?"

Kara took another moment to respond, just looking Lena up and down in the process.

"For the love of Rao Kara, speak" demanded Alex
"Uh - uh...... No - I mean yes - well I mean yes and no." rambled the kryptonian

Lena raised an eyebrow in confusion while Alex just chuckled to herself, because her sister was failing - she was failing hard.

"Yes and No?" asked Lena, admittedly confused "Kara darling, I'm gonna need full sentences."

'DARLING???' What the hell was Lena trying to do to Kara.

"Right, sorry... you look amazing Lena, it's definitely not too much." Kara finally said.
"Thanks Kara" Lena smiled as she looked at the time on her phone "Well I better get going the girls are waiting for me, have a fun sister's night you two." Lena continued as she made her way to grab her coat and purse on the kitchen counter and headed to the door.
"You too" answered Kara immediately but realising what she said and instantly turned to face Lena "I mean have a good night - not sister's night, although I guess in a way it is kinda like a sister's night like I'm having with Alex"

The rambling was back, and although Lena found it adorable she also knew if she didn't stop it - it would go on forever.

"Kara?" laughed Lena
"Yeah?"
"I'll see you later."
"Okay, have fun" smiled Kara and Lena smiled back and off she went.

When Kara turned back to face the TV all she could see was her sister dying of laughter.

"You've got it bad, sis." laughed Alex trying her hardest to contain it
"I know" sulked Kara

5. I'm usually better at controlling my strength

Summary for the Chapter:

Another scenario of Kara being unable to keep her cool demeanour around Lena.

The second time Kara found herself unable to keep her cool demeanour around Lena was when Lena had offered to teach Kara how to make a Lasagna knowing that the Kryptonian could only cook simple dishes that were less likely to burn.

And to Kara's surprise everything had been going well, she was crushing it.

Or so she thought... About halfway into their cooking lesson Lena had realised that the meat sauce needed more chopped tomatoes, so she picked up another can and searched for the can opener that she was certain she had just seen but now could've easily been misplaced under all the mess on the counter.

"Kara, would you be a dear and open this for me?" she asked, handing Kara the can "I seem to have misplaced the can opener."
"Sure thing Lena." replied Kara innocently, happy to help in anyway

Kara put down the spoon she was using to stir the Bechamel sauce, and took the can from Lena and began to try to find a safe way to open it.

Sure she had super strength and could just rip it open but there was liquids and softly chopped tomatoes inside and the last thing Kara wanted was a repeat of 'thanksgiving 2014' with the cranberry sauce.

So Kara continued to try to find a safe way to open the can, and Lena couldn't help but laugh at what she saw.

"If I had known it was going to take you this long Kara, I would've looked for the can opener" chuckled Lena.
"It's a lot harder than you think Lena, I have to use the right amount of strength."

Lena laughed.

"Well I believe in you Supergirl" stated Lena, placing a kiss on the kryptonian's cheek.

And that's when Kara's cool demeanour evaporated .

Completely caught off guard by Lena's unexpected kiss Kara ended up squeezing the can of chopped tomatoes way tighter than she should've and it had exploded, going all over herself and Lena.

"Oops" Kara said, feeling more than slightly embarrassed.

Kara looked over at Lena to see her drenched in tomato juice.

"Oh gosh Lena look at you, I'm so sorry..." she apologised "I'm usually better at controlling my strength"
"It's okay Kara" Lena reassured "It's fine besides it's my fault - I distracted you."

Kara blushed embarrassingly, knowing exactly what Lena was talking about.

"Now I know not to distract you when you're using your powers especially if you're opening something." Lena laughed, looking down at herself and then at Kara. "I'm gonna go get cleaned up, do you want me to bring you back a towel?"

"Yes please" nodded Kara, trying not to die from embarrassment.

Lena smiled as she left the kitchen and Kara stood and watched her, when Lena was completely gone Kara facepalmed herself hard enough to knock a normal human out.

"Seriously Kara? What is wrong with you?" she asked herself "Pull it together."

6. Chapter 6

Kara had always had trouble sleeping at night; she would often have nightmares of Krypton, see glimpses of her home planet exploding and her parents dying. And when she had first gotten to earth they had been horrendous, but luckily Alex had found a way to help her through them. Alex would often slip into her bed when she had her nightmares and sing her little lullaby's until she fell asleep again, but now that Alex was living with Kelly it wasn't necessarily an option anymore and Kara desperately needed it to be an option because ever since she had come back from the Phantom Zone she had been having the nightmares again but this time they were worse, and she didn't want to burden anybody so she had been keeping them to herself or at least she thought she was.

On this particular night the nightmares had been beyond horrendous and Kara was literally tossing and turning as she screamed out into the quiet apartment, which caused Lena to jump out of her sleep and head towards the kryptonian to see if she was alright.

"Kara?" Lena called out cautiously.

But there was no response just Kara tossing and turning, so Lena walked closer.

"Kara, darling?" Lena called out again.

"No! No! Please I don't want to go - don't make me - please" Kara cried out in her sleep and Lena's heart broke because it became clear to her that she was having a bad dream.

She carefully made her way towards Kara making sure not to startle her because she knew what would happened with Kara's powers if she was caught off guard.

"Kara, darling wake up." Lena called out gently stroking the blonde's hair

Kara's eyes slowly opened and they were filled with tears and Lena's heart broke even more.

"Oh Kara" she called out as she immediately went to hug her friend. "It's okay, it's just a nightmare"
"Lena..." Kara cried "It was horrible... I was being forced into my pod but not from Krypton from Earth" she continued "I was being deported back to Krypton but there was nothing there just the Phantom Zone and I didn't want to go Lena - I begged them to let me stay but they didn't listen."

Kara burst into tears and Lena just hugged her tighter and wiped her tears.

"You're safe now Kara, I promise" Lena reassured her "I won't let anyone take you"

Lena opened the duvet covers and made her way into Kara's makeshift bed on the sofa.

"What are you doing?" Kara asked curiously, sniffling up tears.
"I'm gonna lay with you until you fall asleep, so move over."

Kara did as she was told and moved over to make space for Lena, she then laid her head on Lena's chest and closed her eyes. Lena began to sing her a little lullaby that her birth mother would sing to her after a bad dream.

7. Lena’s bad day

Kara always knew when something was wrong with Lena; it had become somewhat of a sixth sense over the years and she was always right even when Lena tried her best to hide it Kara always knew, that's how Kara could tell something was wrong from the moment Lena stepped through the door.

"Hey" responded Lena, sounding completely defeated as she placed her handbag on the counter and began to take off her shoes. "Hey, you're home." Kara stated, using her warmest voice.

Kara immediately stopped what she was doing.

"What's wrong?"
"Nothing, I'm just tired"
"That's not it" Kara argued "I mean you do look pretty exhausted..."
"Gee thanks"
"What I meant to say is" Kara stopped in her tracks not knowing the best to explain what she meant, so she got straight to the point "What happened?"

Lena pondered telling Kara for a moment, but opted not to.

"Nothing"
"Lena... I thought we spoke about this!" protested Kara
"Spoke about what?"
"About not keeping secrets anymore, you know not saying we're fine when really we're just bottling up how we feel and sneaking off at two in the morning to drink scotch and cry"

Lena looked at Kara with a questionable expression, and Kara knew exactly what it meant.

"I have super hearing remember."

Lena rolled her eyes, because she had genuinely forgot that about her friend.

"So what are you not telling me?"

Lena let out a deep breath.

"I met with the board today."
"At Luthor-Corp? But you don't work there anymore"
"I know, but it turns out me walking away from the company wasn't enough."
"How do you mean?"
"They're opening an investigation."

Kara was confused.

"I don't understand, what for?"
"To see if during my time as CEO I used the company's resources in an ill-mannered way."
"What? That's ridiculous! They can't do that!"
"Apparently they can." Lena stated sounding beyond fed up and reaching for the whisky bottle.

Lena poured herself a triple portion of whisky and downed it in one gulp and Kara watched, slightly concerned as she went to pour another.

"Okay maybe we sho-" Kara began before Lena cut her off
"Kara, I've had to endure grumpy old white men telling me what I can and can't do all day in a company I don't even work at anymore" she began "please just let me enjoy this judgement free" she pleaded as she finished pouring the second scotch.

Kara nodded and thought of other ways that she could help then an idea came to mind.

"Do you want me to handle it for you?"
"How?" Lena asked as she looked up at Kara
"I could go talk to them."
"As Kara or as Supergirl?" Lena asked suspiciously.
"Whichever you think will be more effective, but I was leaning more towards Supergirl." she stated "Maybe I could leave them on a shipment container like I did Morgan Edge."

Lena laughed slightly and Kara smiled.

"I never did thank you for that."
"It's no bother, so what do you think? Because I reckon twenty four hours on a shipment container should bring them some clarity."
"As much as I would love to see that I think I have to handle it my way, but thanks for offering. I know who to go to when Alex is annoying me."

They both laughed.

"There must be something I can do to make you feel better."

Lena thought about it for a moment.

"Can we watch a movie and snuggle on the sofa?"
"Absolutely, what movie do you want to watch?"
"Princess Diaries?"
"I thought you hated that movie?"

Lena looked away sheepishly.

"You totally love that movie now don't you?"
"Maybe... okay yes it's so good!"

Kara laughed

"Well then Princess Diaries it is! You get changed and I'll load it up on Disney plus."

Lena nodded and headed into their room to get changed, Kara switched on the tv, put it on Disney plus and selected 'Princess Diaries'. She then headed to the kitchen and got some snacks for the pair of them which included Lena's comfort snack; Strawberry Laces and placed them on the coffee table.

Afterwards she picked up the blanket she kept on the back of the sofa and fluffed it out then sat down on the sofa waiting for Lena who returned moments later.

"I see you got snacks." Lena stated
"What's a movie without snacks Lee."
"Very true."

Lena noticed the strawberry laces in the midst of the snacks and Kara noticed.

"I thought you might want them seeing as you had a bad day."

Lena smiled.

"Have I ever told you you're my favourite person."
"A million times, but I never get tired of hearing it." Kara smiled and Lena smiled back.

Kara patted to the seat next to her and Lena sat down, she then snuggled into Kara's side as she placed the blanket over them and handed her the strawberry laces, before kissing her on her forehead and pressing play on the remote control.

Lena laid there watching what had now become her favourite movie with her favourite person and felt so grateful to have someone like Kara in her life.
