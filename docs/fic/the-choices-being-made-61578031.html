    Both women had been so caught up living in the moment, enjoying their reinvigorated teasing dynamic. In the process, they both completely forgot how this day, and how their characters’ relationship, would end.

    The Kiss of Death.

    Now, as they stood on either side of the destroyed yard, there was a tether between them. The tension could be felt meters away and their eyes kept flickering over to each other. The intimacy coordinator finally wrangled them together.

    She clapped her hands enthusiastically. “Alright ladies. It’s the big finish! I’m sure you’re both excited but I wanted to check in to make sure you are both still comfortable with the proposed plan for the scene. Are there any boundaries you’d like to stipulate that you didn't have when we last discussed this?”

    Kathryn looked at Aubrey, listening and searching her face for any signs of protest. She only saw honey-brown eyes looking back at her fondly.

    “I don’t” the younger woman stated. She raised an eyebrow expectantly at her co-star. Kathryn took a big breath in. “Me neither.”

    The intimacy coordinator nodded. “Perfect! Don’t hesitate to speak up if you need anything during filming alright? Your comfort is the most important thing. Good luck!” And with that, she was off. There was a beat of silence as the two actresses stood side by side.

    “You sure you don’t want to chicken out again, Hahn?” Aubrey asked with her tongue poking against her cheek. It was said jokingly, but there was a hint of insecurity behind it. Without hesitation, Kathryn answered. “Not a chance.” There was no teasing lilt in her tone. No mischievous smile. She wanted to make sure there was no doubt. She was in. “Are you sure you’re still ok with it?” She asked, her voice soft, fearful of what the answer might be.

    Aubrey took a small step forward and placed both hands on Kathryn’s shoulders, making sure her eyes were on her. “I’m so sure.”

    Kathryn’s face lit up and her chest felt warm. “Well then let’s make this the best breakup scene ever.”

    “ACTION!”

    Billy came forward. “I’ll go with you! That’s my choice, take me!” He pleaded. Agatha spat and wiped her bloody mouth on her sleeve. This was her chance. The road led her here, time to finish the job.

    She stood up confidently and spoke. “Take him. You heard him. The boy,” she pointed at the teenager as if presenting a gift. “As promised.”

    Rio laughed in disbelief, shaking her head at the audacity of her ex-lover. Agatha lifted her head high. “It’s over.”

    The purple witch took a few smug steps forward. With a dismissive tone, she gave out orders. “Now, put my yard back. Why don’t you plant a few azaleas on your way out?” Hearing the whimpering protests of the boy she left for dead, she turned around, hair laid perfectly over one shoulder. Kathryn had to make an effort to look at the boy and not at Aubrey’s hooded gaze taking her in.

    “What can I say?” Agatha shrugged. “I’m a covenless witch” she finished, flashing a cheshire grin and laughing brightly, turning to walk back into her house as if it was nothing. Kathryn stopped herself in her tracks. The words of the script flashed in her mind. Billy would be speaking to her telepathically. “Is this how Nicky died?”

    Her hand came up slowly, her smile fading, fingers pressing to the center of her chest where her pendant should be. Grief, guilt, longing. It was all too much.

    Then she felt it. The tether. The magnetic pull. Her body screamed for her to take action. All she could do was listen.

    She turned around slowly, hand still on her chest. Everything around her faded. All she saw was her rival. Her ex. Her love. Rio.

    Aubrey.

    Her feet began to move before she even realized. Each step was heavy with anticipation. Aubrey heard the crinkle of the grass behind her. She flinched as if she had just been brought back to reality. Like she didn’t know how she got there.

    She turned around as the steps grew closer but was unprepared for what she saw. Kathryn looked unimaginably stunning. Long brown hair and the flowy hem of her dress tossed in the breeze. The dark color of her outfit accentuated the delicate porcelain quality of her skin. Her pale blue eyes shined despite the sorrow they were trying to convey. Aubrey couldn’t breathe. Suddenly, hands were reaching for her. She couldn’t help the feeling of deja vu when Kathryn held her face, careful to push Aubrey’s hair back as she did, alleviating any obstruction like the professional she was. All for the camera.

    But Aubrey felt those same confident hands tremble. Instinctively, she latched on to Kathryn’s waste. Grounding her.

    Brown met blue for a faint moment of reassurance before both sets of eyes fell to the other's lips. There was no more doubt. No further permission needed.

    Kathryn pulled Aubrey’s head forward, her eyes fell shut and she finally closed the distance. Their lips met gently at first, like neither of them could believe it was real. Aubrey had her eyes open for fear that if she blinked, it would all disappear. But another stable caress of her hair relieved her of that fear.

    Aubrey’s hands came up between them to cradle Kathryn’s jaw, attempting to pull her impossibly closer and deepening the kiss. She felt Kathryn’s tongue glide along her lower lip, asking for entrance and there was not a bone in her body that could deny her.

    The younger woman felt frantic, unable to stop her hands from moving, from grabbing, from holding everywhere she could. On the other hand, Kathryn was constant, holding them both in place, not wanting to miss a single moment.

    Their tongues met tentatively, exploring each other as if savoring the first taste. However, exploration quickly grew into desperation, their tongues dancing, teeth clacking from how close they were. Neither dared to break their lips apart, only pushing further and further into each other. The smack of their lips pressing together was audible but they didn’t care. Nothing else existed at that moment.

    Until Kathryn felt the pull on her waist as wires began to lift her slowly. Aubrey felt it too and grabbed onto the older woman’s wrists instinctively, trying to hold her for a little bit longer. But alas, it was futile. She was Death. And Agatha was her witch.

    Still, she held on, placing her hands on the side of the purple witch’s neck to savor the last moment they’d have together. Her lips still lifted, searching for Agatha’s as she floated higher and higher. Rio pressed her nose to her love’s cheek before their faces were finally separated. Agatha’s hands never left their place. The last thing anchoring her to this world was Rio and she held on until the last possible second. As she caressed her chin for the last time, those familiar honey-brown eyes looked up at her, now glassy. Tears threatened to spill from her own, but Agatha still managed a sorrowful grin. Part satisfaction, part despair, she succumbed to her inevitable fate as Death’s only love.

    It was overwhelming in the best way. It was both an end and a beginning.

    “CUT! Amazing work ladies! Let’s get the next shot!”

    Kathryn was lowered back down, and found herself holding onto Aubrey’s shoulders for stability. As the cheers and laughter erupted around them, all they could do was look at one another. Two hearts hammered away, wondering silently if they were feeling the same about what had just happened.

    After they kissed, everything was a blur. Kathryn had to shoot Agatha’s death holding the camera rig instead of Rio. She felt the phantom heat of Aubrey’s skin in her hands as she was hoisted up and then gently laid flat on the ground. It wasn’t the same but she tried to tap into the intense emotions she was still feeling after the scene with her co-star to get her facial expressions right. Aubrey couldn’t watch. Seeing how Kathryn held the rig so gently, she couldn’t help running her hands along her own jaw and the back of her neck, where Kathryn's had been placed moments ago. The touch made her shiver.

    The last shot of the day was Rio looking down at Agatha’s body which would soon become her flowery grave. Aubrey found herself tearing up with very little motivation as she stood over Kathryn lying limp on the ground. Her nostrils flared as a single tear rolled down her cheek. Final cut was called and the crew rejoiced after a hard day. There was an immediate buzz of conversations breaking out in all directions. Kathryn opened her eyes and saw Aubrey still standing above with her hand outstretched. Kathryn took it, and as their fingers intertwined a spark ran through them both.

    They stood facing each other, hands still held together. Aubrey broke the silence. “So, that was our last scene together,” she murmured shyly. “We went out with a bang, huh?” Her tongue poked the inside of her cheek.

    Kathryn giggled softly. “I think that’s an understatement.”

    They both smiled and Kathryn nudged her co-star gently. “Wanna get changed and grab a drink?” “That sounds amazing,” Aubrey quickly answered.

    Off in her changing room, Aubrey put on her comfy clothes and reached into her bag to grab her hair clip. What she found was the poetry book she had started reading at the beginning of the project. The object she thought had cursed her had actually been the catalyst for the amazing potential that her relationship with Kathryn now had.

    Opening it to her marked page, the one she had angrily ripped out, she read through a few, short and beautiful poems. And then one jumped out at her. It was passionate, warm and expressed everything she didn’t have the words to say about the hopes she had for what was to come. She ripped the page from the book, laughing to herself that if she kept this up, soon she would only be left with the cover.

    Aubrey walked over to Kathryn’s dressing room and knocked lightly. “Come in” came from the other side and she opened the door.

    Kathryn was wearing a plain white t-shirt and light blue jeans. She seemed to be taking her last bit of makeup off with a wipe.

    Fresh-faced she turned to Aubrey, “Oh, hey! I’m almost ready, just give me a sec.” The brunette tied her hair up into a ponytail, shooting a glance at Aubrey in the mirror. The younger woman was far too affected by the casual appearance she had walked in on.

    A faint blush made its way onto her cheeks before she could stop it. Kathryn noticed and just smirked before rummaging through her bag and putting on her large framed glasses. Aubrey let out a small whimper before she could stop it.

    Kathryn finally turned around to face her head on and asked, “Is everything ok?” There was no real concern in her voice.

    “Uhhh,” Aubrey stumbled as she ran her hands through her hair nervously. “Y– yeah, everything’s fine. You… you look really good in glasses.” She wished she could take Agatha’s place and be absorbed into the ground at that very moment.

    Kathryn held her lower lip between her teeth, stifling a giggle as she watched her co-star fidget slightly. But she didn’t want to tease. This was a time to be vulnerable and after everything they’d gone through, she wasn’t going to risk anything by not being direct.

    So she took the leap. “So, where are we Plaza?”

    The question hung in the air for several beats. It seemingly caught both of them off guard with its honesty. For a moment Kathryn wanted to take it back, worried she had broken the spell they had been under for so many months by talking about it out loud.

    But then, Aubrey walked forward. She didn’t have the words. She didn’t need them. Placing her hands gently in the same spot Kathryn had during their scene, she caressed the brunette's cheeks before pressing their lips together. Not for the camera. Not as Agatha and Rio. Not to save anyone’s life.

    They kissed simply because they wanted to and there were no more questions left unanswered. The paper Aubrey had been holding slowly fell to the floor, forgotten. Because in this moment, actions spoke far louder than words.
