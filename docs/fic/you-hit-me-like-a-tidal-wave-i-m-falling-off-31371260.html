Kara has done a lot of side jobs in the past year. What else is she to do in a gap year? Travel the world? Yeah, she should have done that. But instead she decided she wanted a car, so now she doesn’t have any money to even travel anywhere. On top of that, Kara’s car, McQueen, has a lot of… complications. So she needs even more money to repair the love of her life. 

 

That’s how she ended up waitressing, doing deliveries, babysitting, and tutoring until she got fired. She just isn’t that good with people. She gets nervous around them. They talk too much, too little, or are just straight up rude. That’s how she ended up applying for a job at the Luthor Institute for Marine Conservation (or as Kara likes to call it: L-Marine), an aquarium owned by the Luthor family. Since she already has her diving license and some work experience, she could start right away. 

 

She enjoys working with the animals, even though she can’t pet them (not that that stops her from trying). It calms her and makes the long hours a lot more tenable. She doesn’t even mind the wrinkled fingertips when she’s spent half of her day in the tanks or the fishy odor that causes Alex to throw her under the shower the second she comes home. Kara doesn’t even mind the people watching her that much. She can’t hear what they say, so it can’t hurt her. 

 

So yeah, she loves it, every minute, even when she gets cleaning duty. Okay, maybe that’s a bit of a lie. After having worked at the aquarium, sorry, Institute for Marine Conservation (the Luthor’s are really pressed on using that name, sounds more professional, apparently) for over a month, the cleaning duty is most definitely the worst part of working here.

 

Kara just tries not to think about the fact that she is wiping fish poop and pee off of the huge glass windows. However, she can’t ignore the humans watching her and pointing at her from the other side of the glass. They are always a bit too close, even with a thick wall of glass between them. She can read their expressions, and that says enough, definitely, when they’re laughing at her.

 

But a job is a job, and she gotta do it. That’s how she ends up three feet underwater, wiping glass with some swiper, and ignoring the eyes staring at her. She is just grateful it’s early in the morning, and there aren’t a lot of people, yet. 

 

She greets some of the fish who scurry away when she reaches out. The fish are always more skittish, doesn’t mean that Kara loves them less tho. But if she has got to be honest, she does have favorites. Well, one favorite.

 

Dave.

 

Her little Zebra Shark. Her enemies to friends storyline. It started with him biting at her diving gear, actually destroying some of the tubes which provoked Kara to swim up fast before she could drown. After some other bumps in the road (or rather say, current), they had agreed on being friends. Well, Kara started petting him, and he fell in love with her. 

 

So now they are best buddies. He always comes to visit her when she is cleaning, so when she takes a small break, she can pet him, her gloved hand stroking the bare, white belly of the great predator of the sea. 

 

Today is no different. He greets her with a small bump against her shoulder and then annoys her until she is forced to take a break and pet him. So she directs her full attention to the tame shark and let’s her hand slide across the greyish stripes across his back. Until he scurries away, spooked by something Kara has yet to discover.

 

As soon as she snaps her head up she sees it , and it’s less frightening that she would have expected, but it still manages to freeze Kara up.

 

It being two green orbs staring at her through the glass. They are as clear as the just cleaned glass wall dividing them. And they belong to an even more beautiful girl with raven colored hair and pale skin hued by the blue reflection of the water.

 

Kara’s mind just goes blank.

 

Nothing is going on up in her brain, just this girl. This girl looks at her with a coy smile and a raised eyebrow. Green (more aquamarine in this moment) eyes filled with surprise and amusement stare at her, as she floats, seemingly lifeless, in the water.

 

When Kara keeps playing dead (maybe in the hope that the reason for her racing heart and quickly oxygen-deprived tank will leave and let her move again) the girl breaks eye contact. The ravenette grabs her phone, starts typing away, and when she holds her phone up, Kara is scared that she is going to take a picture.

 

She doesn’t. She just presses the screen against the glass for Kara to read. Without thinking, Kara leans closer, trying to read the letters on the screen. They are a bit blurry through the slow-moving water, but at least they are big and readable. 

 

Are you okay? Should I get help? 

 

“No!” That is what Kara wants to say, but the only thing that comes out of her mouth is some weird gurgling which creates little air bubbles in the water. So instead, she just shakes her head vigorously, causing the girl on the other side to chuckle.

 

The brunette takes her phone away and starts typing again. 

 

Kara patiently waits, floating in the water and trying not to look all too awkward though that ship has probably sailed.

 

I just saw you petting a shark. How? 

 

She wants to smile and answer, but the regulator in her mouth barricades her words. Instead, she just raises her shoulders, her elbows bent and palms facing up. And to finish it off, she tilts her head to emphasize her I-don’t-know look.

 

The girl shakes her head, frowning slightly, and starts typing again.

 

Isn’t it dangerous? It’s a shark.

 

The blonde just grins and points at herself, makes a heart with her hands and points at Dave swimming nearby. She starts typing again.

 

You love sharks? 

 

Kara nods, maybe a bit too fast, and the girl mimics her motions in a slower manner, still looking a bit confused.

 

The girl starts typing again and Kara checks her gauge. She got around five minutes left which means she has to hurry up. Kara presses her hand against the glass, causing the girl to look up in surprise.

 

Then once again, she starts another game of charades.

 

I have little oxygen left , she points at her gauge.

 

So I have to go to the surface again , she points up.

 

But you , she points at the brunette.

 

Stay here , she points at the girl’s feet, please , and puts her hands together as if she is about to pray.

 

The brunette seems to understand and gives her a thumbs up.

 

Give me five minutes, she holds up her hand with the palm facing the girl and then a thumbs up. She gets a pearly smile in return, making her stomach flutter. 

 

--

 

Five minutes turn into ten. That’s what she gets for almost ripping her suit when trying to hurriedly take it off. Kara has to take a quick shower (after which she still smells like fish), put on her clothes, and brush her hair which has dried almost immediately. Somewhere along the way, she has to find her glasses, after she’s taken out the contacts that are stinging her eyes (not her smartest moment). Then she walks past a mirror, spooking herself with the bushy and tangled hair of her reflection. She looks like a sheep, and has to quickly tame her hair into her famous ponytail. 

 

Yeah, ten minutes.

 

And what does a desperate girl do when she is late to a meet up with a cute girl? Run, run for her life, faster than Forest Gump. For once she doesn’t mind the confused eyes watching her run by.

 

Her pace falters when she sees the girl standing at the end of the hall, facing the huge windows, mesmerized by its inhabitants. Golly, she is beautiful, even more when they are on the same side of the glass. 

 

Kara breathes in deeply and jogs toward her. “Hey,” even though she has great stamina, she still sounds a bit out of breath.

 

The girl spins to face Kara, her eyes big and surprised. “Hi…” The hi sounds off, and Kara immediately starts to panic.

 

Kara is not what the girl had expected? The girl didn’t recognize her? Is she mad that Kara is late? Oh did she already fork it up?

 

“Sorry,” the girl chuckles, shaking both Kara and herself out of a daze, “I just expected you to look different behind all that…” She waves her hand around her face, indicating to the swimsuit and goggles that Kara had worn.

 

Kara flinches, “Yeah,” her hand rubs her neck awkwardly, “sorry to disappoint.”

 

“Disappoint? No, it’s the opposite,” the brunette chuckles, the sound of it warming Kara’s heart already, “I’m Lena by the way.” Lena, Lena, Lena. 

 

Kara eyes the outstretched hand and grabs it firmly, offering her a bright smile. “Kara, nice to meet you.”

 

“So, Kara,” the way she says it almost makes Kara’s knees buckle, “you love sharks?”

 

“Oh golly, yes. The shark you saw me petting is Dave. He is my best buddy.” The tenseness in her shoulders disappear as soon as she starts talking about her partner in crime. “When I get a stupid task, like cleaning the glass, he keps me company.”

 

Lena just smiles warmly at Kara. “That’s adorable.” She turns to face the aquarium. But Kara just keeps on looking at Lena, studying her face.

 

“It is beautiful isn’t it.” Lena sighs when they have been silent for a bit too long.

 

Kara can only sigh, “Yeah, you can say that.”

 

Lena glances to the side, meeting Kara’s gaze, and blushes brightly under Kara’s heavy inspection. “I was talking about Dave.” She winks at Kara. This time it’s the blonde’s turn to blush.

 

“Yeah, Dave.” She tears her gaze away from Lena to the huge tank in front of them. “You can say whatever you want about L-Mar-” She huffs, “the Luthor Institute for Marine Conservation, but they do have gorgeous animals.”

 

“You really call it that?” The corners of her lips turn downward as if she’s trying to hold in laughter.

 

“Uh,” Kara frowns, “Yes?”

 

“My brother and I always call it the Luthor Lagoon. My mom and dad are too obsessed with upholding a professional view of the aquarium, but the name is just plain ridiculous.” 

 

Her what? “Your mom and dad?” 

 

“Yeah, Lionel and Lillian Luthor?” Lena watches as Kara’s eyes widen with realization, and Lena’s eyebrow raises, “You didn’t know? The name Lena didn’t ring a bell?”

 

“Gosh, no,” Kara chuckles, feeling her face redden, “Aren’t you supposed to be in Ireland?” It sounds confused and rude. Yeah, Kara really isn’t a people person. 

 

But Lena doesn’t seem to mind. She just smiles at Kara. “Yeah, but it’s the summer break, and I always spend them at home.” 

 

“Ah... Still, you went to Ireland, that’s so cool!” Kara chuckles, still a bit surprised but nonetheless amused.

 

Lena’s eyes seem to twinkle at that, and she turns to face the tank again, “You think so?”

 

 “Heck yes! I have never been out of this damned state. I would love to go to Ireland.” She steps towards Lena. Now she only has to move her arm, and she’d be touching Lena. “Tell me about it?”

 

Lena grins, “Another time, over lunch maybe?”

 

Is Lena asking her on a date? Is she on a date now? Is she just being nice? Are they friends? Did she hear Kara’s stomach grumble? Oh no she has been silent for too long. Say something. 

 

“Oui!” Oui? Why the heck did she say oui? 

 

“Ho, et elle parle français...” The brunette grins, her eyes challenging.

 

“Oui?” What is she even saying yes to? 

 

“Quand est-ce que tu termines, Kara?” She is speaking French like a natural, not that Kara knows because she can’t even understand French, but it sounds natural, fluent. Even her name sounds French now. Oh man, that’s hot.

 

“Oui?” 

 

This makes Lena slip out a short but warm sound, “You can’t speak French, can you?” 

 

“Non.” Kara chuckles, brushing a strand of her behind her ear.

 

“I just wanted to ask when you are done with your shift.” 

 

Well, that Kara can understand. “Oh, I’m done at six, but I got a half an hour break at twelve!”

 

The answer seems to disappoint Lena. She furrows her brows and studies the wall of glass with a thoughtful look in her eyes. “No.”

 

“No?” 

 

“You’ll have half an hour break at twelve, and after that, I need your help with something, Luthor Lagoon related, of course.” 

 

Kara’s grin grows till it matches Lena’s. “If the boss says so.” The blonde tries to sound as serious as is possible, as she watches the fish swim in front of her.

 

“Great.” Lena’s smile disappears when she shivers. 

 

It draws Kara’s immediate attention, “Are you cold?” It wouldn’t be surprising since Lena is barely wearing more than a loose (and probably thin) sweater. 

 

“A bit.” The brunette admits, not looking up at Kara. 

 

So Kara, being the charming person she is, softly pulls Lena close, wrapping her arm around Lena’s waist, a bit higher than most people would have, just to avoid herself from having a heart attack. 

 

“Thanks,” Lena leans into Kara’s jacket and lays her head down on her shoulder.

 

Kara is going to explode. Lena Luthor, the daughter of her bosses, has her head resting on Kara’s shoulder. She is holding her. She can see the small details in Lena’s focused eyes, the few specks of blue and brown mixed with the green. It’s gorgeous.

 

“You missed a spot.” Lena interrupts her thoughts.

 

“I- What?” She frowns and looks at the aquarium. 

 

On the wall of glass there is one, small, miniscule, micro, spot. 

 

“Damnit.”
