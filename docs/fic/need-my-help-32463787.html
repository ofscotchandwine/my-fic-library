What the fuck...

After leaving Metropolis, Lena found an apartment on the rent-controlled side of National City and took a chance. She moved to the city for a fresh start and away from her manipulative mother. Now, she is plopped on the couch, surrounded by boxes filled with her belongings, watching The Big Bang Theory when her stomach growls. 

“Shit, I forgot to eat before the drive.” Lena gets up, wanders into the kitchen, and opens the fridge. Only to find the most terrible, horrible, just awful thing in there.

“An empty fridge. Great,” Lena says sarcastically as she shuts the fridge door close and scrolls through Google maps looking for the nearest grocery store. She finds one, to her surprise, only two blocks away. She grabs her coat, keys, and purse and heads to her car. Once she leaves the building, she notices how frigid the temperature is in National City and quickly buttons up her coat. She grabs a pair of earbuds from her coat, plugs them into her phone and ears, and enters her car. Once she reaches her destination, Lena realizes she has never been to a grocery store since she was brought into the Luthor manor all those years ago.

“It can’t be that hard.” 

Oh, how wrong was I…

Lena thinks as she looks around the store and is utterly confused about her surroundings. She quickly makes her way over to what she believes is the vegetable aisle and stops dead in her tracks. There, restocking the tomatoes, stands a tall blonde with arms that can tear her shirt in half. With glasses perched up her nose, a ponytail laid to one side, and the bluest eyes Lena has ever seen. Lena quickly looks away as she sees the blonde look up and searches around the aisle, feeling Lena’s eyes bore into her head. She shakes her head and goes back to restocking the tomatoes. Lena quickly grabs a basket near her and makes her way over to the red peppers. She picks out the ones she thinks are the best and places them in her basket and is about to move where the lemons are when-

“Need some help?”

Lena jumps at the voice and squeaks as she turns around and sees the blonde looking straight at her.

“Sorry, sorry. I just noticed you were putting your vegetables into the basket without a bag and I came to help. Oh, my name is Kara, by the way. Kara Danvers.” The cute blonde fixes her slightly slanted glasses and offers a handshake to Lena. Lena takes the offer and straightens herself out before speaking to the goddess in front of her.

“Hi, my name is Lena. Lena Luthor. And what do you mean a bag, don’t people bag their things at the counter?” Kara looks at Lena with a confused look and grabs a bag from the top of the crate of peppers. 

“No...people place their fruits and vegetables into these little bags and then place them into bigger bags at the checkout counter...are you new here?” Kara opens the bag up and places Lena’s peppers into them. “Some people use the scales around the store to measure how many pounds they have but others kind of wing it.” Kara then ties the bag up and places it back into Lena’s basket.

“Oh, right, right. Sorry, I’m new here. Um...would you help me by any chance?”

“Uh...sure. It will help me pass time here until my shift is over. So where did you move from?”

“Metropolis. Why? Is it so obvious?” Lena asks with nervousness in her voice and looks up to Kara, hoping to God she is not making a complete idiot of herself.

“Little bit, but it’s cute. So city girl, how are you liking National City so far?”

“It’s nice so far, not a lot of people like in Metropolis and it is quieter than the Big Apple. How long have you lived here?” Lena asks as she notices how cute and strong the blonde next to her looks. She quickly blushes and turns away as many unholy thoughts run through her mind. Kara doesn’t seem to notice this as she is distracted picking the freshest apples on the crate in front of her.

Ok, what do I do now?

“Yes, please,” Lena says with a plea and makes puppy dog eyes toward Kara.

But I just quit because I couldn’t take it anymore, my last name is infamous due to my brother,” Lena says as she looks back at her memories with Lex. One of his mistakes caused hundreds to die at the company, and since then the Luthor name has been dragged through hell.

“You live in 17A. I live right above you. Looks like we will be seeing each other often, Lee.” Lena almost drops the bags in her hands as her heart beats faster and she feels a bit shaky for Kara’s nickname.

“That’s my nickname for you. I hope you don’t mind.” Kara looks at Lena with uncertainty and Lena is quick to reassure the blonde.

“No, no. You are completely fine. I like it.” Lena gives Kara a smile and begins to unbag her items. An hour passes by as the two women put the groceries away and talk to each other. They are currently watching tv, debating if Joey is hotter than Ross, when Kara jerks up from her place on the couch.

The group collectively said their hi’s and shook hands with Lena.

“Hi, I’m Lena. Lena Luthor. I live downstairs from Kara.” Lena fidgets in her spot and glances toward Kara for reassurance. Kara smiles and gives Lena a thumbs-up to boost her confidence.
