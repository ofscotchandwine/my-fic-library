Hawkeye VS the Black Widow

by Chargedlion

    Chapter 1

    Also don't worry, I'm still working on Love is Blind. Writers block, you know?

    "Natasha! Thank God!"

    The Widow spun around on her heel, stunned to see young Kate Bishop running towards her.

    "You gotta help me; I really messed up!" Kate frantically exclaimed as she glanced over her shoulder as if she was expecting a sneak attack.

    Natasha tilted her head back and raised an eyebrow. She knew that Yelena and Kate had planned to practice sparring together, so where was...? Oh no. "Kate." Natasha pinched her nose and shook her head, her mind racing with endless possibilities. "What happened?"

    "I didn't- Okay, okay." Kate paused to take a deep breath and collect her thoughts. "So, I was sparring with Yelena, right?"

    -10 Minutes Previously-

    "I'm surprised you agreed to this, Kate Bishop," Yelena admitted, her voice low and her smile pointed. "I thought you were afraid of me or something."

    Kate scoffed, holding back a remark about Yelena using her full name again. "I'm not afraid of you! Believe it or not, I have met scarier people than you."

    Yelena's smile widened, now flashing an almost menacing grin. "Oh really? Well, I'm about to make you wish you were fighting them rather than me." She adjusted her fingerless gloves in an effortless attempt to further intimidate the younger Hawkeye. "But don't worry; I won't hurt you. That badly."

    Kate hesitantly followed her lead and needlessly began fixing her own gloves. "Um, you know this is just a practice fight, right? You're not actually supposed to hurt me."

    "Oh, right, right." Yelena slapped her forehead and scrunched her face up. "Guess that little detail slipped my mind. Oh well. Go on then, Bishop, put your training wheels on; I ain't got all day."

    "Hey, I am very skilled, you know!" Kate wagged her finger at the assassin before they fist- bumped to initiate the "friendly" fight. "You should see my trophy collection," she continued, allowing herself to give the Russian a smug smile. She was proud of her accomplishments, and there was nothing Yelena could say that would-

    "Bah!" Yelena waved her off with an exaggerated flourish and shrugged. "I've seen that dinky collection of yours. I was not impressed, Kate Bishop."

    A small fire lit under Kate's feet. Odds are Yelena was trying to make her mad on purpose, but she didn't care. Besides: she's more accurate when she's aggregated. "You know what, Belova, I've had enough of your- Whoa!" The dark-haired girl dodged Yelena's hasty punch with her mouth open in shock. "Hey, I was in the middle of a sentence!"

    "You talk too much," Yelena teased, a pleased smile plastered on her face as she circled the woman. "Come on and fight me, Hawkeye Jr."

    Kate had agreed to spar with Yelena because she was ready for a change after all of the grueling and mostly humiliating spars she'd have with Clint. She'd tease him about being an old man, but he never did lose his spry nature. Clint was great, don't get her wrong. He was still the best Avenger; she just wanted a change of pace. But not like this.

    "You can do better than that," Yelena urged, throwing another loose punch to wake her friend up. For once, she was glad Natasha had encouraged her to hang out with Kate more. "You won't hurt me, Katie. I'm too good for that to happen."

    "Aw, come on!" Kate finally fought back, but much to her dismay, her hit was blocked by a haphazard arm and a bored expression.

    "No, no, Kate, not like that. It goes a little something like this!" Yelena tensed her shoulders and smirked before she performed a move Natasha had shown her. She thought it was foolproof, but shockingly, Kate was able to counter it with only mild stumbling. The assassin jerked back in surprise, and Kate flashed a cocky grin.

    "You know that's Clint's move, right?"

    Yelena only gave her an aggravated huff.

    Their fight continued for a short while, Kate keeping up better than Yelena assumed she would. A weird part of the Russian was proud of Kate's progress, but she still needed to make sure the archer knew her place on the food chain.

    With more force than necessary, Yelena grabbed Kate's arm while it was out, then, using it for leverage, kneed her in the stomach.

    With a definite 'Oof' Kate stumbled back, clutching her abdomen with a grimace. "Is that even a legal move?!"

    "A real enemy wouldn't pull their punches." Yelena raised her head in triumph. "You still have a lot to learn, Kate Bishop."

    Kate was growing more agitated by the second. Punch after punch was either blocked or countered with skill and precision Clint didn't even have. Needless to say, she was jealous of the blonde's skill set.

    Yelena grabbed Kate's wrist when the woman threw a sorry attempt at a punch and twisted it back, effectively throwing the raven-haired girl off balance. The ordeal ended in Kate sprawled out flat on her back on the training mat. The Russian grinned, and Kate felt her face grow hot.

    Before Yelena had time to react, Kate performed a kip-up just like how Natasha had taught her and faced the assassin with a burning fire that could wipe out the Amazon.

    Yelena's eyes widened for a moment before she laughed and gave the girl an approving nod. "Atta girl, that's what I'm talk-"

    WHACK Time moved in slow motion. Kate hadn't meant to do it. She just got so frustrated and-

    The assassin slowly turned to face the archer, a near-perfect handprint spread across her right cheek. Yelena smiled in such a way that made Kate take a few steps back. "What was that, Kate Bishop?"

    "I don't- I don't know! I," Kate stuttered, looking her friend up and down as she mentally mapped out an escape plan. "I didn't mean to! Please don't- Oh boy..."

    Yelena reached up to touch the mark on her face, then locked eyes with Kate and gave her one of the scariest looks the archer had ever seen. And the Russian barely had to move a muscle. "Ten."

    "What are you doing?! What does that mean?!" Kate asked feverishly, backing up further into the corner. She had really done it now.

    Yelena looked up at her and squinted slightly, the corner of her lip tugging upward in an intended smirk. "Nine."

    "Oh sh-!" With a quick turn on her heel, Kate hopped over the ropes of the ring they were in, landing hard and taking off across the room as she heard "Eight!" being called after her. She needed to hide and fast. Kate had managed to piss off one of the many ex-assassin's in this compound. Just her luck.

    In the process of trying to escape the wrath of Russia's best weapon, Kate brushed past Clint, who merely watched her go while scratching the back of his head. Whatever this was, he wanted absolutely no part of it. He was too old for this.

    -End Flashback-

    Natasha laughed when Kate finished her story, much to her surprise.

    "Come on, Natasha, I'm being serious! She's going to kill me!" Kate insisted fearfully, almost gripping the woman's shoulders to add to her frantic begging. "You've gotta help me!"

    "Alright, alright," Natasha gave in, wanting to at least try and keep the peace in the compound. Her head hurt enough to begin with. "Do you know how far she was behind you?"

    "I don't know!" Kate hastily looked over her shoulder again. "Um, she was counting down from 10, so probably not far."

    "Okay. Just-" Natasha nodded her head to the side. "-duck around the corner. I'm sure she'll come this way, and I'll try to keep her calm. And if I can't, well." Natasha shrugged nonchalantly. "At least I tried."

    "Uhh..." Kate raised her eyebrows for a moment, but Natasha reassured her with a small smile. "Okay. Thanks, Natasha, I'll owe you. I'm so sorry."

    "Don't be sorry." Natasha's smile widened ever so slightly, a playful glint Kate had never seen before appearing in her eyes. "I'm honestly impressed. I won't let her live this down so quickly; I promise you that."

    Kate let out an awkward chuckle and nodded, relieved that her rival's big sister didn't hold her mistake against her. And she even thought it was funny. The Widow sisters never failed to surprise her. "So I just- Hide over here."

    "Yep." Natasha looked down the empty hallway. "Better be quick; I think she's coming. I'd recognize those stomps anywhere."

    A small squeak passed through Kate's lips before she could stop it before she ducked around the other side of a conveniently placed vending machine. She held her breath as the sound of heavy footsteps drew closer. She wondered if Natasha had some sort of enhanced hearing she never told her about. Or maybe it was some sort of wicked sister sense. Kate wished she had that.

    "Natasha! Where is she?!"

    "Where is who?" the redhead asked, her voice tired and very unlike the person Kate had just been talking to.

    "Kate Bishop!" Yelena yelled, making the hidden woman press her back further against the wall. "I know she came down this way, and you're just standing there like a weirdo. Don't tell me you're colluding with the enemy."

    "Enemy?" Natasha laughed by expelling air from her nose. "What could she have possibly done to earn that nickname?"

    Yelena huffed, now embarrassed with having to admit that Kate had bested her in combat. Or, at least, ended the spar with a fiery finish. "Well- See... She slapped me. Like-" She mimicked the slap, lightly tapping her own cheek. "And nobody gets to do that. Ever. She needs to pay, Natasha."

    Natasha had to forcefully swallow the laugh creeping up her throat. Never had she seen someone be so childish yet so serious at the same time. "I know the kid, Yelena. She wouldn't just attack you, especially you, without a very good reason."

    Kate fought back a smile. She knew Natasha already knew what had happened. So that meant that Natasha was trying to defuse her little sister by basically embarrassing her into submission. This only made her respect and admiration of the Black Widow grow.

    "Come on, Nat," the Russian whined, stepping forward and clutching to the collar of Natasha's jacket. "I'm not going to hurt her! Just tell me where she is so we can... talk." "Uh-huh." Natasha gave her a disapproving look, and Yelena instantly let her go. "What? Are you jealous that she got off a shot you weren't expecting?"

    "No!" her sister answered way too quickly.

    Bingo.

    "Aww." The Widow reached out and cupped Yelena's face where her cheek was still slightly red, Yelena grumbling but still leaning into her touch. Her eyes softened as she stroked her skin, but her tone still delivered an insulting punch. "Russia's best can't handle a little competition from a younger, newer recruit?"

    Yelena looked away; her jaw clenched as the rest of her face turned the shade of her cheek. "She's really not all that..."

    "Hey, that's my best friend's protege you're talking about here." Natasha tilted her head to the side to try and initiate playful eye contact with the assassin. "Are you sure there's not something you're not telling me?" Yelena muttered something under her breath the Avenger couldn't quite make out. "What did you say?"

    "She's good, Nat!"

    Kate's eyes widened.

    "But I can't tell her that! She'd get too big of a head! Enemies won't hold back. I'm just trying to show her what it will be like out there. Then she slapped me, and I felt..."

    The archer swallowed hard.

    Natasha drew back, wondering if this whole ideal had been deeper than everyone had expected.

    Yelena suddenly smiled and shook her head. "I guess I underestimated ol' Kate Bishop. But I won't make that mistake again."

    Natasha gave a fond hum before she swiftly kissed her cheek. "I bet you won't. But try and be a little easier on the poor kid, okay? She's not an assassin like the rest of us, believe it or not." "Trust me, I know." Yelena looked at her big sister for a moment, observing Natasha's relaxed features before she found herself wrapping an arm around the Widow's neck and burying her head into her shoulder. She didn't know why, but this felt like something she was supposed to do.

    Natasha kissed her temple and began gently swaying them. She did not doubt that the slap had brought back some memories of Yelena's abusive past, whether the younger Widow knew that or not. She wasn't the least bit surprised at this outcome but knew that now was not the time to bring it up. Especially when- "Kate. You can come out now."

    "Wait, what?!" Yelena looked up just in time to see a sheepish Kate stepping around the corner, her hand on the back of her neck as she looked at the two sisters. "You traitor!" The blonde attempted to wiggle free of her sibling's grasp, but all she managed to do was spin them around so that they both faced the younger woman, Natasha now wrapping her arms around her waist from behind. "You were there this whole time?!"

    "Uh... yeah?" Kate admitted slowly, shifting back on forth on her feet. When she clasped her hands behind her back, she noticed Natasha giving her a slight wink, and that alone gave her the confidence to face her rival and friend. "I'm sorry... I didn't mean to hit you like that." Yelena pursed her lips as she observed the kid standing in front of her. Her general demeanor was fine, having taught herself how to hide her emotions. But not well enough. She was scared of her. Yelena didn't mean to scare her that badly. Only enough to prove a point.

    Wait a minute. If Kate was there the whole time, that meant... "How much of that did you hear?"

    "Oh, like..." Kate flashed an awkward and cheesy smile. "All of it?"

    The assassin's jaw dropped, and Natasha pressed a teasing kiss to it, immediately receiving a hand to her face pushing her away. She only laughed and ducked away from the misplaced assault.

    "Kate Bishop, if you tell anyone about this, I swear-"

    "I won't." Kate cocked her head and allowed herself to smirk, trusting that Natasha could hold the Russian back if it came down to it. "But I appreciate how much you care about me, Yelena Belova."

    Natasha nearly snorted at the tone Kate adopted. It was clear that the two rivals had been hanging out a lot more recently. She only wished Yelena was a better influence.

    Yelena gritted her teeth but didn't deny the statement. There was no use anyway, now that she had heard the entire "private" conversation. "I hope you know I'm going to get you back for this. You won't know when or how, but I will. I promise you that."

    "Alright, alright." Natasha finally let her sister go, stepping up to her side. "Don't make threats, rooskaya. We're all on the same team here."

    Yelena gave her a look and earned a flick to the nose. She let out a short whine and huffed her annoyance. "Okay, okay, fine. I'll call a truce, Kate Bishop." The assassin stepped forward and offered the archer a hand to shake.

    Kate stared at it for a moment before she looked to Natasha for guidance. She gave a short nod, so Kate accepted her offer and shook her hand. "Truce. I gotta say, I-" Before she could finish her complement, she found herself being pulled forward and her feet being swept out from underneath her. Prior to the inevitably painful hit to the ground, a hand grabbed her wrist and tugged with just enough force to ease the fall. Kate, now lying on her back, looked up at the now grinning Russian standing over her, the wide-eyed Widow not too far behind. "Yelena! It's dangerous to do that out here!" Natasha scolded, swatting her sister's shoulder before she drew her attention to the new recruit. "Are you okay, Kate?"

    "I'm good, Natasha!" Kate quickly reassured, propping up on her elbows. "But it looks like someone doesn't follow simple directions very well," she followed up under her breath, avoiding the eyes of her rival.

    "In my defensive." Yelena crossed her arms and lazily tucked one foot behind her ankle. "I was told not to make threats. But I didn't." She tilted her head back in a cocky gesture. "I made a promise."

    Natasha scoffed, but surprisingly, Kate found herself laughing.

    "Touche, Belova. Touche."
