    Chapter 1

    Eleanor’s body crumpled to the floor, and Kate’s world crumbled.

    Kingpin looked back up at Kate, the gun still in his hand, standing over her mother’s body, but Kate couldn’t take her eyes off of the rapidly spreading bloodstain on the rug.

    Clint’s voice was yelling in her ear, so was Yelena’s, but all she heard was a high-pitched ringing and Kingpin’s dark chuckle.

    “Sorry about your mom, kid,” he said coldly, not sounding sorry in the slightest. “Collateral damage. A sacrifice, if you will.”

    Kate looked up, trembling. Her last arrow was still notched, waiting in her hands, but she couldn’t move.

    She couldn’t breathe .

    Because Eleanor was dead, her mom was dead and it was Kate’s fault, it was all her fault -

    Kingpin was still speaking but Kate’s head was still spinning and she didn’t know if she’d ever recover from this, she didn’t know if she wanted to recover because what did she have to live for, really?

    Clint didn’t even like her, he would retire and leave her, Yelena still had her Widows to save and she’d leave her, and Pizza Dog wasn’t even her dog.

    Kate had nothing to live for and if she had nothing to live for, then what better way to die was there than avenging your mother’s death?

    Kingpin’s goons would be here soon enough and when they arrived Kate would still be here, standing over Kingpin’s lifeless body because she was going to kill him, she was going to fucking kill him.

    Clint and Yelena’s voices were still loud in her ear but the buzzing in her head subsided a little bit and Kate looked up, meeting Kingpin’s eyes as he spoke to her, oblivious to her internal anguish.

    “So,” he sneered, loading his gun.

    “Any last words, kid?”

    Kate felt a wave of pure fury wash over her and she scowled, her eyes darkening.

    “Yeah,” she snarled.

    “ Fuck you .”

    Faster than Kingpin could blink, Kate drew her bow back and released the arrow, watching as it flew through the air and nailed Kingpin right in the eye with a slight squelch .

    She saw the life in his other eye flicker and fade, and watched his body sway and collapse to the floor, beside her mother’s.

    Kate saw the arrow sticking straight out from his eye socket, buried as deeply as it could reach, skewering his brain.

    Kate fell to her knees.

    Her bow clattered to the floor next to her. Her arms were limp at her sides.

    She could hear Clint and Yelena in her ear but now there were gunshots and grunts and thumps, though Kate couldn’t tell if they were coming from the earpiece or next door.

    “ Kate! ” Clint yelled. “ Kate, get out of there, the Tracksuits are here .”

    “ Kate Bishop ,” Yelena spoke next, sounding out of breath. “ You need to leave, you’re not safe. Kate, you have to- “

    Kate reached up and plucked the earpiece from her ear, letting it fall to the floor beside her bow.

    It was quiet, now.

    Well, quieter .

    The gunshots Kate heard just moments before were definitely nearby, but that didn’t bother her.

    It’d all be over soon.

    Kate may have partially avenged her mother’s death by killing the man who pulled the trigger, but Kate was really the reason she was dead, so why not finish the job?

    That sounded better than facing a lifetime of guilt and loneliness.

    And yeah, it may the coward’s way out, but Kate was done being confident and brave and naive.

    If she hadn’t been, things would be different. If she had just ignored her gut and left it alone and stayed blissfully unaware of the crime happening in her family then things would be different, a lot of people would still be alive, her mom would be alive.

    So Kate was done.

    She wasn’t crying yet, she didn’t think. Her cheeks were dry when the raised her trembling hands to her face.

    She felt like she should be… doing more . Crying, screaming, clutching her mother’s lifeless body, etc. Like they did in the movies. Kate had always thought most people acted a little melodramatic in those sorts of scenes, but there was always a touch of realism to them, right? But Kate couldn’t find it in herself to do anything . Move, make a noise, cry.

    She felt paralysed, sitting and staring at her mom’s corpse, which had likely already begun the process of decomposition-

    Nice, Kate, that’s a real nice thought.

    Kate felt as lifeless as her mom.

    She might as well be.

    Noise suddenly exploded to her right and she didn’t bother turning to see who’s footsteps were stomping through the doorway, but she wouldn’t have to wait long to find out it was Clint and Yelena, looking worse for wear.

    Clint was crouching in front of her, a bleeding cut on his forehead, a bruise blooming on his cheekbone, his eyes worried, his mouth moving and forming words.

    “ Kate! What’s wrong? Are you hurt? ”

    No, she wasn’t hurt. She was alive, and her mom wasn’t.

    Kate could hear the words, but she wasn’t really hearing them.

    She gazed at Clint blankly, and saw his brow furrow. He was pushed out of the way by Yelena, who’s face replaced his in front of Kate. Her nose looked broken, she had a black eye, her lip was split. She looked concerned, almost scared , and Kate wished she could tell her she was okay.

    “ Kate Bishop ,” Yelena’s voice was firm, steady. “ Tell me what happened. Kate? Kate? “ She turned to Clint.

    “ What’s wrong with her? ” she demanded.

    “ She’s in shock ,” Clint murmured, pursing his lips.

    “ I don’t blame her. ”

    “ C’mon, get her on her feet. ”

    Yelena stuck her hands underneath Kate’s armpits and suddenly she was being lifted into the air, leaning heavily against the Widow. Yelena grunted slightly underneath her weight.

    Kate heard a distant thwip and clang , and then Yelena’s hands were being replaced with bigger, firmer ones and she was being held up by Clint.

    “ You go first ,” Kate heard Clint instruct. There was a faint sort of zipping noise, then a yell, then Clint was strapping something from Kate’s suit to his own, and his arms were now fully wrapped around her.

    “ I don’t know if you can here me, Kate ,” he grunted. “ But hold on if you can .”

    And she tried, really, but for all her effort she still hung all but limp in Clint’s arms as they flew through the air, landing on some sort of rooftop no more than 5 seconds later.

    Kate was silent during the whole thing. Her mind was still elsewhere, vaguely aware of the world around her but unable to hold onto it, to be apart of it.

    It was a bit of a blur after that. Kate quietly registered both Clint and Yelena holding her upright, leaping over rooftops and slinking through alleyways and climbing stairs, but she wasn’t there .

    Her mind was still at her mom’s mansion, in the room with Kingpin and her mom and the gun and arrow and her mom was dead .

    They reached Kate’s aunt’s apartment with no delays, and when they stepped through the door she felt a little more like herself.

    She was carried through the apartment and eased onto the couch, and then Clint was in front of her again.

    He had tears in her eyes, and he looked infinitely more worried than when they were at the mansion.

    “ Kate ,” he pleaded. “ Can you hear me? I’m sorry. I’m so, so sorry. ”

    Kate just stared at him. He placed a hand on her cheek, and it was warm and firm and secure and Kate almost leaned into it. But she didn’t.

    Clint looked to the side, shaking his head slightly, and moved out of the way so Yelena could crouch in front of Kate.

    “ Kate Bishop ,” she started, her voice strained. “ I know you’re in there. I know you can hear me but you can’t talk back right now, and that’s okay. It’s okay. ”

    Yelena cupped Kate’s face with both her hands, gently stroking her thumbs over her cheekbones.

    “ You are hurting ,” she continued. “ Your mother is dead, and you think it’s your fault, but it’s not. You think you’re alone, but you’re not. You think life isn’t worth living anymore, but it is .”

    Kate started trembling harder than before, her blank stare filling with tears.

    “ I know you’re hurting, ” Yelena said. “ But I need you to come back to me and Clint. To right here, right now. Because we want to help you, but we can’t help you from in there.

    Okay? Come back to me, Kate. Come back to us. We’re here for you. ”

    Kate blinked.

    Her gaze focused, and she was really looking at Yelena, she could feel her hands on her cheeks, she could see the concern and relief in the Widow’s hazel eyes.

    She turned her head slightly to look at Clint, sitting beside her and looking at her worriedly. She didn’t even notice he was holding her hand.

    Kate blinked again, and the dam broke.

    For a moment the tears were silent, cascading down her cheeks and dripping onto her suit, but then she let out a whimper and it was all over.

    She hunched in on herself, sobbing into her hands before she felt Clint wrap his arms around her again and she was falling against his chest, and she was crying in that ugly way that no one wants to see.

    Yelena was still there, a comforting hand resting on her thigh, but Clint was hugging her and holding her and he was running a hand through her hair, and he was whispering to her gently and he was rocking back and forth and he was being such a father , and god Kate missed her father.

    And her mother.

    “It’s okay,” Clint murmured against her hair, pressing light kisses to her head.

    “It’s okay, Kate, you’re okay . You can cry. Let it out. We’re here for you. I’m here .”

    And Kate did. She cried, loud and ugly and raw and hurt , and she knew she must sound hysterical but she didn’t care , because her mom was dead and nothing else in the world mattered to her right now.

    And she felt pathetic, crying like a baby into the chest of her idol , an Avenger , but crying was all she could do right now.

    She cried, but soon the air was getting hard to breathe and Kate’s head was spinning and she was gasping weakly against Clint’s chest.

    Clint immediately noticed her struggling, and gently pushed her away.

    He looked her in the eyes, his own still teary, and gave her a soft look.

    “Hey, hey ,” he murmured.

    “None of that, okay? I know you’re hurting but you need to breathe. Breathe with me, Kate. In and out. In, and out. In, out.”

    He grasped one of her hands and brought it to his chest, holding it against his lungs and exaggerating his breathing.

    “That’s it, come on, in and out.”

    Kate shakily attempted to copy his breathing, her chest rising and falling rapidly, but Clint shook his head softly and raised his other hand to her cheek.

    “Too fast,” he said gently. “I need you to slow it down, okay? Copy me. Feel my chest, my breath, my rhythm. Can you copy that for me?”

    And Kate could.

    It took a minute, of course. Clint held her face and her hand and kept looking at her softly and breathing deeply, and Yelena was holding her knee, and soon Kate was breathing steadily, and she may have been seeing things, but Clint looked almost proud of her.

    Tears were still streaming down her face, but Clint just smiled sadly and wiped them away with the pad of his thumb.

    Then her chin trembled and she squeezed her eyes shut and she let out another whimper, and Clint just looked at her and gathered her into his arms, holding her against his chest until she was practically in his lap.

    “You’re okay, Katie,” he whispered, and his dad side must really be taking over because he’s never called her Katie before.

    “I’m here. I’m here, honey, you’re okay. You’re okay.”

    And Clint’s words must have been magic or something because Kate was feeling okay .

    She wasn’t fine yet, and she wouldn’t be for awhile, but it was okay because she was in Clint’s arms and she was okay .

    Yelena was still next to her, only trembling slightly and keeping her firm yet gentle grip on her knee.

    Clint was still holding her, whispering to her like a father and stroking her hair and making everything feel alright.

    And Kate was still crying, but that was okay because it was normal to cry, and Clint and Yelena were there for her while she did, and they would be there for her once she stopped.

    Clint and Yelena were there for her, and Kate felt okay.
