come a little closer

If you’d asked Kara whether it was possible to be obsessed with something that you had never tried or experienced, her answer would have been something along the lines of probably not. And yet, here she was, trying to focus on finishing her article but finding herself unable to do so. 

When you try to get your mind to stop thinking about something but that only makes you think about it more. So you stop trying to fight it. And so you go through the same thing in your head so many times that it starts feeling real when you imagine it. Until that moment is gone and the stinging reminder that you can’t have it only makes you want it more. When even the slightest of reminders or triggers is enough for it to consume your mind with need. When something makes you feel powerless. And the worst part? You love how you feel. You wouldn’t get rid of the feeling if you could.

If Kara could compare this feeling to something it would be to how she felt after she’d come into contact with Red Kryptonite.

She shut her laptop down with a sigh after what was probably her hundredth failed attempt at focussing on her article, whipped off her glasses with a little more force than necessary and took off into the sky through an open window. She circled the city a few times, stopping a few petty crimes on her way until she came to a stop in front of the shining LuthorCorp logo without even really intending to. It was times like these when she wished she could just shut her brain off for a few moments of blissful silence.

She didn’t even realize she’d touched down on Lena’s office balcony until the CEO whirled her chair around with a smile lighting up her face that instinctively pulled one from Kara.

 

“Hey”, said Kara, her tone almost all breath as she entered through the already open door.

 

“Hey, yourself” and the low, sexy way in which her voice wrapped around the words made Kara’s heart feel like it was trying to win the Olympic gold medal for Gymnastics. And if Kara was being honest with herself, everything Lena did made her feel the same way. At first, she’d just put it off as a natural reaction to Lena being a Luthor. But now, with all of their secrets laid out between them, the way even remembering how close she’d been to losing Lena to Lex made her shudder. The crippling blow of loss she’d felt when she’d thought she had and the intense and bone-deep relief when she realised she hadn’t. When the intensity of her feelings made her feel so out of control that she couldn’t ignore them anymore as she had from the very beginning.

 

“Are we still on for tonight?”, Kara asked, knowing the answer but not knowing what else to say while simultaneously having to remind herself to not stare at the way the setting sun lit up Lena’s pale skin and dark hair.

 

“Of course, I just have to sign a few documents then I can leave.”

 

“Can I fly you back?”, asked Kara, fully expecting no for an answer.

 

“Actually, nevermind, you don’t have to say yes if you don’t want to,” she added hurriedly sensing Lena’s immediate hesitation.

 

“No Kara, yes, you’ll just have to wait for me to finish signing these.”

 

“Oh, that’s fine, no worries.”

As Kara tried to contain her excitement to a minimum, she was also painfully reminded of the way every interaction of theirs felt so forced and formal. Where earlier Kara would have cracked a corny joke causing Lena to throw the stress ball she kept on her desk at her head and calling her a dork , and the big, dopey grin that would immediately light up Kara’s face, now there were only pleases and thank yous. 

 

Kara must have been staring at Lena for a while because what broke her out of her stupor was Lena’s teasing, “Something on my face?”, sending Kara right back to blushing and ducking her head before saying, “I’ll just wait outside,” in the quietest of voices. Kara wouldn’t admit it if you asked her but she was thankful for Lena’s teasing, if nothing else it served as a reminder that they were going to be fine.

 

She went outside and leaned on the balcony as she waited for Lena to finish her work, watching the last glimpse of sunlight leave the sky. 

 

After they’d somehow managed to miraculously defeat Lex and not die in the process, Lena had announced her intention to return to Metropolis, prompting their worst argument till date that ended in Kara on the floor on her knees next to where Lena had been seated, crying and begging Lena to stay. She’d watched Lena’s cold mask fall to be replaced by tears that Lena never let fall, instead, she said that she didn’t want to leave either but there was nothing left for her here.

 

“Please Lena, not you too, I can’t lose you, I can’t let you go,” she said in a strained voice between sobs.

 

“What if I’m already gone?” Lena asked in a quiet voice.

 

Kara shuffled forward, still on her knees and took hold of Lena’s hand and said, “No,” in a petulant, childish tone that made Lena’s heart melt.

 

“No? Kara, what do you mean no?”

 

“No. You’re not leaving.”

 

“Kara,” Lena said as she shifted forward on her seat and pulled her hand out of Kara’s grip to bring both of them to the sides of her face, holding it like it was the most delicate thing in the world and forcing Kara to look at her directly, “You deserve better, I can’t hurt you again.”

 

“You’re not leaving,” Kara repeated somehow, willing Lena to listen and saying,” Please,” in the most broken pleading tone Lena had ever heard her use. And that did something to her. The fact that this adorable dork of a superpowered alien could have anyone she wanted and anyway she wanted them, and yet here she was, on her knees, begging Lena to stay. Kara was crying again, tears streaming down her face as she shifted herself impossibly closer to Lena and brought her hands up to wrap around her waist, burying her face in Lena’s chest. And Lena let her, all the while dropping soft kisses on her forehead and rubbing her back gently. Neither of them knew how long they stayed like that but it must have been a while because they laughed softly when they separated and Lena promised to stay.

 

Kara smiled at the memory. Turning her head when she caught the sound of Lena’s heels clicking against her balcony floor.

 

“You would have missed this view if you’d left.”

 

“Yeah, I would have,” replied Lena, while looking directly at Kara, “Shall we?”

 

“Right. Yeah,” Kara said, stepping closer to Lena, “Can I?”, she asked holding her arms out in invitation briefly before Lena nodded and looped her arms around Kara’s neck just as Kara picked her up in a bridal carry.

 

It’d been a while since they’d done this and both of their thundering heartbeats were a testament to that. Well, at least she can’t hear mine, thought Kara as she took off towards Lena’s penthouse.

“So you move the queen’s pawn two spaces forward to which your opponent should predictably respond by doing the same. And then you take the queen-side bishop’s pawn and move it two spaces forward too,” Lena explained and Kara answered by nodding dutifully.

 

“This is one of the most common openings, it’s called the Queen’s Gambit. And from here your opponent has three choices, they can either protect their pawn called Queen’s Gambit Declined or they can take your pawn also known as Queen’s Gambit Accepted or they can play the Albin Countergambit, where you move the king’s pawn two spaces forward. This was Lex’s favourite opening.”

 

“The Queen’s Gambit?”

 

“Yeah. Though it’s not truly a gambit since black cannot hold the pawn. It’s used by aggressive players to control the centre very early on in the game.”

 

“Why?”

 

“Because it allows them to continuously launch attacks at their opponent and leaves the opponent with no other option than to defend the attacks without launching any of their own.”

 

Kara expressed her understanding by nodding.

 

“Thank you for doing this,” Lena said quietly.

 

“I’m the one who asked,” said Kara with a smile.

 

“Have you really never played Chess?”

 

“We had a game similar to this on Krypton, though it wasn’t nearly as complicated. Chess has a lot more to it than I gave it credit for. When I came here I gravitated towards studying, I couldn’t participate in the more physically challenging sports because I couldn’t control my powers as well as I can now and I didn’t really have any friends to play with. But even with the academics, I had to hold back and keep myself from answering in class sometimes. Krypton was several centuries forward in terms of science and technology than Earth. We were introduced to calculus in what would be the equivalent of the fourth grade here on Earth,” Lena’s eyes widened at that, prompting a laugh from Kara.

 

“I wish you could have seen it,” and it took every ounce of Lena’s willpower to stop herself from crying at that. 

 

“I wish I could have seen it too.”

 

“Do you want to play a game?” Kara asked suddenly, breaking them out of an intense moment and leaving Lena to wonder if she was relieved or disappointed.

 

“Why? Do you want to lose?” asked Lena with a teasing smile, even as she moved to put the pieces back into place.

 

“I’m just here to learn,” said Kara, smiling back, “Why do you love Chess so much?”

 

“I like winning,” Lena said, with a shrug as if stating something that should’ve been obvious.

 

“Okay, yes, but being good at something can’t be the only reason to love doing it,” Kara said, encouraging Lena to continue as she leaned forward and developed her queen’s knight.

 

“When I was adopted by the Luthors, I was constantly trying to find a way to fit in, trying to gain Lillian and Lex’s approval. I found that in Chess. Though I only came to love it once I realised even they couldn’t defeat me in it. It made me feel powerful, knowing I could plot several moves ahead, knowing that I’d won the game even before I started playing, knowing no matter what anyone did I would always come out on top. Plus Chess is a lot like real life. Especially when you try to relate it to the world of business,” Lena said before she countered Kara’s opening move by moving her king’s pawn forward by two spaces.

 

“Really, how so?” Kara said before moving her knight to the centre, making Lena smile at her fondly before she moved forward and showed her the correct move, “If you do that my pawn has the chance to advance and reach the centre of the board on your side more quickly. When a pawn moves two spaces like that you should block it with one of your own.”

 

“You didn’t answer my question,” when a raised eyebrow was all she got as a response she added, “How do you relate Chess to real life and the business world?”

 

“Well, there’s a king that’s supposed to be the most important piece on the board, but can only move one space at a time. Then there’s a queen, who’s actually the most important and powerful piece on the board, she does whatever she wants to,” Lena punctuated her explanation with a smirk before continuing, “ but even she can’t deliver a checkmate by herself without at least one other piece. I win most games by taking my opponent’s queen. You corner your opponent early on and don’t give them a chance to strategize and plan for anything more than the next move. The best defence is a strong attack,” Lena finished with a smile.

 

“And when all else fails?”

 

“You force a stalemate or a draw. A stalemate is when you or your opponent has no legal moves left but the king is not in check. And a draw is when there’s no way for you or your opponent to checkmate.”

 

“And how do you do that exactly, I feel like I’m gonna be needing the information?” Kara asked, prompting a laugh from Lena.

 

“Don’t worry darling, you’ll get better with practice.”

 

“I have full confidence in my teacher,” said Kara, making Lena’s smile widen.

 

“As you should.”

 

Lena turned her attention back to the board as Kara made her next move.

 

“Not too bad, not bad at all,” Lena commented before leaning forward and moving her queen in place, “Checkmate,” she said with a disarming smirk. And just like that Kara was reminded of how powerless Lena made her feel. How she would have to resist the urge to get down on her knees and worship Lena Luthor like the goddess she is.

 

“Can you….maybe like, stay the night?” Lena asked, all confidence from her victory seeming to have evaporated.

 

“Yeah, but only if you let me have all the chocolate chip ice cream.”
