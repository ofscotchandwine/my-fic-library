Lena was used to people asking her out.

Whether it was her fancy boarding school, or MIT, she was no stranger to date requests. Even after Lex snapped and turned the sun red, the requests didn’t slow down. 

The people just got… weirder.

She hadn’t dated many people after that - one, in fact. And maybe that was less out of mutual chemistry, and more out of solidarity. James was one of the few people on Earth who understood what being kidnapped by Lex was like.

Only once had she ever been in a relationship where she wasn’t asked out, but she hadn’t done the asking either. It’s hard to say how things happened with Andrea. One could only say that they had happened, until Andrea suddenly pulled away, shortly after their trip to Costa Rica. And, well, that wasn’t an ending Lena wanted to dwell on much longer.

Therefore, Lena was completely at a loss, inches away from the blonde reporter, as they sat on the cozy Danvers couch, watching the Mummy for the first time (well, Lena hadn’t seen it before - Kara’s viewings were probably in the triple digits). It wouldn’t be the first time her courage had failed her, if she were to walk away from movie night having intended to ask Kara out, only to hug her goodnight and promise another movie night soon.

It drove Lena crazy, sometimes thinking that Kara was broadcasting clear signs of interest, other times getting the whiplash of Kara emphasizing that they were good friends. Lena couldn’t be sure that Kara was interested in women at all, let alone specifically interested in Lena.  

But no, tonight was going to be the night. Kara was going on vacation the next week anyway. If things went south… they’d both have an excuse for distance, at least for a little while. And if Kara wanted it to be a long while… well, Lena knew what a slow fade was like.

“Would you go out with me?”

Kara blinked in surprise, looking up at Lena with a startled - or maybe outright shocked - look on her face. Her mouth opened, as though she were about to say something, then closed slightly, then opened again - every gap of silence causing Lena to want to melt into the floor. So, she hasn’t thought about it before.
