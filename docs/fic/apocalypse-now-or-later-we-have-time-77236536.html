Kara had apparently underestimated how slow she was without her powers, the scale of the situation hitting her anew, with their new reconnaissance mission.

    "Look at you being strategic and not running immediately into danger," Lena teased. "Maybe losing your powers isn't so bad."

And in the meantime, you can give me tight hugs without having to worry about breaking my bones, which is also nice, right?" Lena mused with a gentle nudge at Kara's shoulder.

    "You're right, there's no time to lose. I'm sure there are a lot of things in storage here you could use somehow. Do you want to make yourself a cup of coffee before we go exploring?"

    "Coffee can wait a bit more," Lena said, biting her lip. "We better make sure nothing surprise us here. Plus you've said something about my bad sleep habits? Maybe coffee before bed is not so wise."

They'd done this so many times by now that it barely took Lena half a minute on average. "Best aunt my ass, I'm going to build a rechargeable battery for this and I will forever be Esme's favorite." Lena instructed and flicked the power button on the Gameboy to see if it still had juice. With the signature crackle, it turned on, so Lena flicked the button again, not wanting to waste anything.

She didn't dislike it, what's more, sometimes she even enjoyed to turn the tables and say something outlandish enough that made Lena turn a delightful shade of pink. It was a rare occurrence before the end of the world. But Kara was happy to report… to whatever higher being that existed now, that it was something that seemingly happened more and more frequently.

And I was wondering, if maybe when we manage to reverse the end of the world, you would like to go on an actual date?"
