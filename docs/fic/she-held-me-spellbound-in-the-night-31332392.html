Lena learns that despite being the crown princess of the kingdom, Kara eats like it’s her last meal. But instead of being disgusted, she finds herself smiling adoringly at the blonde who was filling her cheeks up with bread. 

Finally, after they finish their dinner, Kara stands up and offers Lena her hand, “Would you like to dance?” her face open and hopeful. 

How can Lena say no?

They twirl slowly around the small space. Kara stares admiringly at Lena’s graceful movements, her dark hair flowing against the evening wind. The light from the moon shines on her as if it’s naturally drawn to her, making her look like an ethereal being. 

Kara learns that Lena tends to avoid the sun whenever she can because she burns so quickly due to her pale skin. 

“Is that why witches wear that wide-brimmed pointy hat?” the blonde curiously asks. 

Lena laughs, “No, that ‘pointy hat’ is usually for special ceremonies. And no, you can’t wear it.” she adds, making Kara pout. 

They share another quiet moment, just idly swaying and holding each other. Then a thought suddenly comes to Kara, “Does your mother know that we’re getting married?”

“Kind of late to ask that now, darling.” Lena comments, “But no. I’ll tell her as soon as she’s back from her travels. That woman is enjoying her retirement way too much.” 

“Do you think she’ll like me?” 

“Yes. To be honest, she wanted me to settle down for a while now. Too afraid I’d end up alone,” Lena rolls her eyes, “I’ve always been independent but she would be glad that I’ll finally have someone.” 

“Is that why you agreed to marry me?” Kara asks tentatively. She knows that she has strong feelings for Lena, nearing the point of love, even. But the witch is still an enigma to her, and Kara can’t help but be a little saddened by the possibility that Lena is only agreeing to this for practical reasons. Like a transaction.

Lena doesn't answer her, instead, she asks a question back, “Do you remember the first time we met?”

Kara doesn’t know where Lena is going with this but she nods. 

“As you know, I saved you from that horse carriage. But there’s a reason why I pulled you away so quickly,” Lena says, then taking a deep breath, “I had been eyeing you across the market, I was trying to muster up the courage to approach you and talk. I don’t even like apples that much but it was the closest stall to you and I was waiting for the right time.” 

Kara can't believe what she’s hearing. Lena continues to ramble, too nervous to stop. 

“Then I saw that carriage coming towards you and I just acted. But then I found out you’re the princess so I panicked and ran. I know how the kingdom thinks of us, I knew what was going to happen if you found out who I am.” Lena laughs mirthlessly, “But then you kept appearing for some reason. I thought you were scouting me, but you didn’t do anything, you just hovered around so I mostly pretended that I couldn't see you.”

“Then you suddenly appear at my doorstep asking me to marry you. I thought it was a joke or an elaborate plan to capture me. So yes, I kind of reacted negatively.”  Kara remembers Lena fiercely wielding her wand on her face. It was both beautiful and terrifying. 

“I understand,” Kara says, holding up Lena’s chin so she can meet her eyes, “I’m sorry for springing it on you so suddenly. But just know that I never saw you as anything else but the amazing woman you are. I don’t care about those tall tales and rumors. What I care about is you.”

Kara has more things to say but she’s interrupted by Lena’s soft and warm lips on hers. Her brain takes a few seconds to catch up, but after a few moments, she wraps her arms around Lena’s waist and deepens their kiss. 

It was magical and better than anything she ever imagined. When they separate, both of them have giddy smiles on their faces. The musicians who are still playing in the background are studiously avoiding their gaze on the pair, trying to give them privacy.

Finally, they resume dancing, Lena’s head cradled comfortably on Kara’s shoulder. 

“So were you really going to turn me into a rabbit?” the blonde asks.

“...Let’s just keep dancing, darling.”

Kara also begins to escort Lena to her cottage. She doesn’t need to, Lena knows the Dark Forest like the back of her hand and Kara is usually the one who needs help navigating the large woods, but she enjoys more time learning new things about Lena. 

She learns that Lilian Luthor is not actually her mother, that she just found her in the forest as a baby and took her in. Lilian saw that she had magic and took it upon herself to train Lena and care for her as her actual daughter. 

But when Lena was old enough, Lilian told her what happened to her actual mother. 

“She was the head of the coven, before Lilian. But then something happened. And Lilian was next in line, so she took over. Then she found me.”

Kara wants to ask more but she sees how much it pains Lena to talk about her mother, so she simply hugs her and talks about her antics with Alex until she sees Lena start to smile again. 

“So you don’t do magic often?” Kara asks as they continue their walk. She’s proud of herself for starting to memorize the Dark Forest bit by bit. Yesterday, she only got lost five times!

“No,” Lena says, gracefully hopping over a fallen branch. “Magic always comes with a price. The bigger the spell, the bigger the price. So I tend to only use them when necessary.”

“What kind of price?” 

“Most of the time it’s your strength. I used to get so tired after casting a spell but that faded away when I got older, now I only get tired when I do particularly complicated spells.”

Then her brows furrow, a tight frown on her lips, “But there are some that go insane. They become addicted to it. The magic starts to control them instead of them controlling it. The price for their magic is their sanity.” 

Kara hesitates for a moment, wanting to ask the question that’s been at the back of her mind, “Is that what happened to your mother?” She really needs to control her mouth one of these days. 

Lena stops in her tracks, the blonde worries that she offended her, and starts to form an apology but Lena shakes her head and continues their trek.

“She was powerful and brilliant, or that’s what they told me. She always dreamed of a family, but somehow she couldn't. So she gambled with forces that were bigger and stronger than her. And exchanged her life for another one.” she laughs, ruefully. 

And with a sinking heart, Kara understands. 

Lena’s mother sacrificed herself so that Lena could live. 

Kara doesn’t know what right words to say, so instead, she wraps her arms around Lena. The brunette exhales a shuddering breath and melts into her arms. 

Kara is no stranger to talking to important people. 

Being the sole heir of their kingdom, she grew up talking to leaders, lords and ladies, kings and queens. She’s used to it. 

But there’s nothing more nerve-wracking than her first meeting with Lilian Luthor. 

The woman has a strong, elegant air around her. She holds herself in a way that is so much like Lena that makes Kara’s head spin. 

Lilian takes one look at Kara, then at her hand that’s tightly clasped around Lena’s, and smiles.

“I must say dear,” she says in a casual drawl to Lena, “I didn’t think you’d be into the happy, sunny type. Princess Kara here looks like she woke up with singing birds helping her get dressed.” 

Kara makes an affronted sound, “I dressed myself! I didn’t need any birds’ help at all!”

Lilian hums, “Do you sing, dear?”

“Yes?” Kara answers, confused. 

“Wished upon any stars, lately?” 

“Well, there was one shooting star the other night that—hey!” Kara finally catches on, Lilian starts to laugh good-humoredly, Lena to her benefit tries to hide it behind her hand.

Alex, however, is laughing at her full on, “She got you good Kara! Remember that one time in the Dark Forest when the animals just started randomly following you everywhere? The deer even started to eat your dress!”

Kara huffs but Lena gives her a sweet kiss on the cheek that makes her smile goofily. 

Lilian finally stops snickering, opening her arms up to Kara which the blonde happily accepts. They share a warm hug, but then Lilian whispers to Kara’s ear, “Try and hurt my daughter and I will turn you into a slug.”

Kara laughs quietly, “You’re too late, Lena already threatened to turn me into a rabbit.” but then sincerely adds, “but I’m not worried because I’ll never hurt her.” 

Lena just smiles, watching them.

The king and the queen, however, took a bit longer to warm up to Lena. They weren’t hostile towards the witch but often gave her wary looks across the dining hall whenever she and Kara planned for the wedding. 

Lena ignores it but if Kara notices, the princess' protective instincts would immediately take over and she would fiercely defend Lena. Yelling at how she was so disappointed by them believing in old tales and prejudices. And she wouldn't stop until the witch pulls her away and silencing her with a kiss and quiet reassurances. 

Then one night the castle got attacked by an assassin from a rival kingdom. The masked man silently infiltrated Kara’s sleeping quarters but was stopped by one of Lena’s freezing spells.

After that, any trace of wariness completely disappeared from the king and the queen. They even ignored the fact that Lena was sleeping in Kara’s bed that night.

Since then everyone in the castle knew to respect Lena as much as they respect the royal family. 

However, one time a young new servant who had just moved from a different kingdom, only knowing of the rumors of witches and their evil magic, made the mistake of trying to “accidentally” trip Lena. 

Lena almost fell down the steep stairs but was luckily caught by Kara, who had turned red with rage, seeing the events unfold before her eyes. She quickly unsheathed Alex’s sword from her guard’s waist and aimed it towards the now cowering servant. 

Both Lena and Alex had to hold back a raging princess, “Kara, put the sword away!” 

Everyone was surprised that they chose to get married in the Dark Forest.

Lena herself expected the wedding to be in the castle, in front of a large crowd filled with royals from all over the land. But she should've known that Kara always knew what she needed even before she herself knew. 

Kara wanted it to be in the Dark Forest as a symbol of them uniting as two people from the contrasting realms. She wanted everyone to know that she is very much willing to be part of Lena’s world as Lena is to hers. 

She also knew how Lena detested large crowds but was willing to sacrifice that to make Kara happy. And that fact only solidified the princess’ decision. 

“I love you,” Lena says after hearing Kara’s plan for their wedding. Her smile is as radiant as the sun and Kara again wonders if Lena is a gift from Rao himself. 

“I love you too,” Kara says. She probably is. 

Their wedding is a private affair, with Alex, the king and the queen, and Lilian in attendance. As well as the royal council that serves as a witness to their marriage. 

“You wore the pointy hat!” Kara exclaims happily, after the initial breathlessness she experienced in seeing Lena at the makeshift altar made of flowers. 

Lena laughs at her ridiculous wife-to-be, “Yes, darling. And no, you still can’t wear it.”

Kara and Lena exchange silent vows only whispered towards each other, but everyone can see and feel the palpable love between the pair. 

Then when they finally kiss, the forest comes alive with fireflies. Kara wraps her arms around Lena’s waist and spins her around, the flurry of fireflies dancing around them. Lena giggles happily while firmly holding onto her wife’s shoulders. 

Princess Kara Zor El, now Queen Kara was born in the early dawn. Lena Luthor, now Queen Lena was born at midnight during the full moon.

They were known all throughout the land as the most unlikely pair, very different but also the same. 

But everyone that sees them with their own eyes knows that the two women share the fiercest never-ending love that poets and composers would write about for generations to come. 

After all, it’s not every day where the fair princess falls in love with the witch.
